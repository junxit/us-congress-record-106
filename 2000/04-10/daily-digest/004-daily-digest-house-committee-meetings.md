# Daily Digest/House Committee Meetings

```
Committee Meetings
OVERSIGHT--INS: COMMISSIONER REPORTS

Internal Revenue Service: The Commissioner Reports''. Testimony was

Treasury: Charles O. Rossotti, Commissioner; and W. Val Oveson, 
National Taxpayer Advocate; Margaret Wrightson, Associate Director, Tax 
Policy and Administration Issues, GAO; and public witnesses.
```
