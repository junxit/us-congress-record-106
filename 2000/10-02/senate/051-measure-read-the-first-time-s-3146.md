# MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--S. 3146

```
                  MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--S. 3146

  Mr. KYL. Mr. President, I understand that S. 3146 is at the desk. I 
ask for its first reading.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report the bill by title.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (S. 3146) to preserve the sovereignty of the United 
     States over public lands and acquired lands owned by the 
     United States, and to preserve State sovereignty and private 
     property rights in non-Federal lands surrounding those public 
     lands and acquired lands.

  Mr. KYL. Mr. President, I now ask for its second reading and object 
to my own request.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Objection is heard.

                          ____________________
```
