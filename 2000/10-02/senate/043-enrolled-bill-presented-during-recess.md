# ENROLLED BILL PRESENTED DURING RECESS

```
                 ENROLLED BILL PRESENTED DURING RECESS

  The Secretary of the Senate reported that during the recess of the 
Senate, on September 29, 2000, he had presented to the President of the 
United States, the following enrolled bill:

       S. 1295. An act to designate the United States Post Office 
     located at 3813 Main Street in East Chicago, Indiana, as the 
     ``Lance Corporal Harold Gomez Post Office.''
```
