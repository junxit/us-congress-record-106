# HONORING THE KARNES ON THEIR 50TH WEDDING ANNIVERSARY

```
         HONORING THE KARNES ON THEIR 50TH WEDDING ANNIVERSARY

● Mr. ASHCROFT. Mr. President, families are the cornerstone of 
America. Individuals from strong families contribute greatly to 
society. I believe it is both instructive and important to honor those 
who have taken the commitment of ``till death us do part'' seriously, 
demonstrating successfully the timeless principles of love, honor, and 
fidelity. These characteristics make our country strong.
  For these important reasons, I rise today to honor Dorothy and Eddie 
Karnes, who on October 7, 2000, will celebrate their 50th wedding 
anniversary. My wife, Janet, and I look forward to the day we can 
celebrate a similar milestone. The Karnes' commitment to the principles 
and values of their marriage deserves to be saluted and 
recognized.●

                          ____________________
```
