# ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, OCTOBER 3, 2000

**Speaking:** Kyl, Jon (K000352) [R-AZ]

```
                  ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, OCTOBER 3, 2000

  Mr. KYL. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate 
completes its business today, it recess until the hour of 9:30 a.m. on 
Tuesday, October 3. I further ask unanimous consent that on Tuesday, 
immediately following the prayer, the Journal of proceedings be 
approved to date, the time for the two leaders be reserved for their 
use later in the day, and the Senate then begin final remarks on the H-
1B visa legislation under the previous order.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. KYL. I further ask unanimous consent that the Senate stand in 
recess for the weekly party conferences to meet from 12:30 to 2:15 p.m.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
