# Mrs. JONES of Ohio and Mr. JACKSON of Illinois changed their vote from "nay" to "yea." So (two-thirds having voted in favor thereof) the rules were suspended and the bill was passed.

```
                              {time}  1836

  Mrs. JONES of Ohio and Mr. JACKSON of Illinois changed their vote 
from ``nay'' to ``yea.''
  So (two-thirds having voted in favor thereof) the rules were 
suspended and the bill was passed.
  The result of the vote was announced as above recorded.
  A motion to reconsider was laid on the table.
```
