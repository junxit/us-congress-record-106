# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Miller, Gary G. (M001139) [R-CA]

**Measures:** S. 613

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. GARY G. MILLER

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, March 1, 2000

  Mr. GARY MILLER of California. Mr. Speaker, on Tuesday, February 29, 
2000 I had to delay my return to the Capitol in order to attend to 
personal business in my district. During my absence, I missed rollcall 
vote 26.
  Had I been present, I would have voted ``yes'' on the motion to 
suspend the rules and pass the Indian Tribal Economic Development and 
Contract Encouragement Act (S. 613).
```
