# PROGRAM

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. COVERDELL. Mr. President, for the information of all Senators, 
the Senate will resume consideration of the Hatch-Mack amendment No. 
2827 regarding the marriage penalty tax at 9:30 a.m. tomorrow. 
Following 30 minutes of debate, at approximately 10 a.m., the Senate 
will proceed to a vote on or in relation to the amendment.
  The managers are actively working on scheduling the remaining 
amendments that need to be acted upon. It is possible the bill may be 
completed as early as tomorrow evening. Therefore, Senators can expect 
votes throughout the day and into the evening.
```
