# MEASURES PLACED ON THE CALENDAR

**Measures:** H.R. 1725, S. 2267, H.R. 3081

```
                    MEASURES PLACED ON THE CALENDAR

  The following bills were read the second time and placed on the 
calendar:

       H.R. 3081. An act to amend the Internal Revenue Code of 
     1986 to provide tax benefits for small businesses, to amend 
     the Fair Labor Standards Act of 1938 to increase the minimum 
     wage, and for other purposes.
       S. 2267. A bill to direct the National Institute of 
     Standards and Technology to establish a program to support 
     research and training in methods of detecting the use of 
     performance-enhancing substances by athletes, and for other 
     purposes.

  The following bill, received previously from the House of 
Representatives for concurrence, was read twice and placed on the 
calendar:

       H.R. 1725. An act to provide for the conveyance by the 
     Bureau of Land Management to Douglas County, Oregon, of a 
     county park and certain adjacent land.
```
