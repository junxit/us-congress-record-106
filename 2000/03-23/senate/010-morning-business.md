# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, there will now be a 
period for the transaction of morning business not to extend beyond the 
hour of 11 a.m. with time to be equally divided between the Senator 
from Idaho, Mr. Craig, or his designee, and the Senator from Illinois, 
Mr. Durbin, or his designee.
  Under the previous order, the Senator from Idaho, Mr. Craig, shall be 
in control of the first half of time.
  The Senator from Colorado.

                          ____________________
```
