# MILLENNIUM DIGITAL COMMERCE ACT

**Speaking:** Thompson, Fred (T000457) [R-TN], Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

**Measures:** S. 761

```
                    MILLENNIUM DIGITAL COMMERCE ACT

  Mr. THOMPSON. I ask that the Chair lay before the Senate a message 
from the House to accompany S. 761.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there objection?
  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I object.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The objection is heard.
  Mr. THOMPSON. I ask unanimous consent that the Senate disagree to the 
amendments of the House, agree to the request for a conference with the 
House, and the Chair be authorized to appoint conferees on the part of 
the Senate.
  Mr. REID. I object.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The objection is heard.
```
