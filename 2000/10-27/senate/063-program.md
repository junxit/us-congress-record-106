# PROGRAM

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. HATCH. For the information of all Senators, the Senate will vote 
on the continuing resolution at 9:30 a.m. tomorrow. Further, the Senate 
will convene on Sunday at 4 p.m., for those Senators who want to make 
statements, and we will vote on another continuing resolution at 7 p.m.
  As a reminder, votes on continuing resolutions will be necessary each 
day prior to adjournment. The appropriations negotiations are ongoing, 
and it is hoped that the Senate can adjourn by early next week.

                          ____________________
```
