# RECESS UNTIL 9:30 A.M. TOMORROW

```
                    RECESS UNTIL 9:30 A.M. TOMORROW

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, the Senate stands in 
recess until the hour of 9:30 a.m., tomorrow, Saturday, October 28, 
2000.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 7:54 p.m., recessed until Saturday, October 
28, 2000, at 9:30 a.m.
```
