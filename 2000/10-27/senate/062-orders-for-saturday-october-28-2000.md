# ORDERS FOR SATURDAY, OCTOBER 28, 2000

```
                 ORDERS FOR SATURDAY, OCTOBER 28, 2000

  Mr. HATCH. I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate completes its 
business today, it recess until the hour of 9:30 a.m. on Saturday, 
October 29. I further ask consent that on Saturday, immediately 
following the prayer, the Journal of proceedings be approved to date, 
the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the 
day, and the Senate then proceed to a vote on the continuing 
resolution, as under a previous order.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
