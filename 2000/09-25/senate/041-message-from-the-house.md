# MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

**Measures:** H.R. 940

```
                         MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE


                          enrolled bill signed

  A message from the House of Representatives, delivered by one of its 
clerks, announced that the Speaker has signed the following enrolled 
bill on September 22, 2000:

       H.R. 940. An act to designate the Lackawanna Valley and the 
     Schuylkill River National Heritage Areas, and for other 
     purposes.

  The enrolled bill was signed subsequently by the President pro 
tempore (Mr. Thurmond).
```
