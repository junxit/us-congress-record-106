# APPOINTMENT AS MEMBERS TO TICKET TO WORK AND WORK INCENTIVES ADVISORY PANEL

```
 APPOINTMENT AS MEMBERS TO TICKET TO WORK AND WORK INCENTIVES ADVISORY 
                                 PANEL

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Without objection, and pursuant to section 
101(f)(3) of the Ticket to Work and Work Incentives Improvement Act of 
1999 (42 U.S.C. 1320b-19), the Chair announces the Speaker's 
appointment of the following members on the part of the House to the 
Ticket to Work and Work Incentive Advisory Panel:
  Mr. Steve Start, Spokane, Washington, to a 4-year term; and
  Ms. Susan Webb, Phoenix, Arizona, to a 2-year term.
  There was no objection.
```
