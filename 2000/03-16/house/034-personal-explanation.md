# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

  Mr. BERMAN. Mr. Speaker, I was unable to cast a vote on the Boehlert 
amendment to H.R. 2372. However, had I been present, I would have voted 
``yea.''
  Also, I was unable to cast a vote on the motion to recommit H.R. 
2372, Private Property Rights Implementation Act of 2000. However, had 
I been present, I would have voted ``yea.''
  Also, I was unable to cast a vote on final passage of H.R. 2372, the 
Private Property Rights Implementation Act of 2000. However, had I been 
present, I would have voted ``nay.''
```
