```

adjourned to meet at 12:30 p.m. on tomorrow for morning hour debates.

  Accordingly (at 2 o'clock and 1 minute p.m.), under its previous 
order, the House adjourned until tomorrow, March 21, 2000, at 12:30 
p.m., for morning hour debates.
```
