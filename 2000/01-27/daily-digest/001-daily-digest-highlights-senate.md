HIGHLIGHTS

      Senate and House of Representatives met in Joint Session and

Chamber Action
Routine Proceedings, pages S107-S119

2013.
  Page S113
Measures Passed:
  Joint Session: Senate agreed to H. Con. Res. 241, to provide that the 
two Houses of Congress assemble in the Hall of the House of 
Representatives for the purpose of receiving the President's State of 
the Union message.
  Page S107
Joint Session Escort Committee: The President of the Senate was 
authorized to appoint a committee on the part of the Senate to join 
with a like committee on the part of the House of Representatives to 
escort the President of the United States to the House Chamber for a 
Joint Session.
