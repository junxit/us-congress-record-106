# HONORING NANCY HOFFMASTER, POWER OF ONE HONOREE

```
            HONORING NANCY HOFFMASTER, POWER OF ONE HONOREE

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. SCOTT McINNIS

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, January 27, 2000

  Mr. McINNIS. Mr. Speaker, it gives me great pride to now honor Nancy 
Hoffmaster who has been awarded the Colorado Woman 2000 Power of One 
Honor in recognition of her service to her community and the State of 
Colorado. Nancy is a true heroine in her community and she deserves our 
highest thanks and praise.
  Nancy, who is currently battling breast cancer, has been volunteering 
in the Jefferson County Public School District for twenty-five years. 
She is the past president of the Jefferson County School's Parent 
Teacher Association and has served on the Jefferson Foundation.
  Nancy founded the program ``Serving Kids from the Inside Out.'' The 
program provides clothing for needy children in the district. She also 
coordinates the Jefferson County School's ``Support for Homeless and At 
Risk People'' (S.H.A.R.P.) program.
  It is with this, Mr. Speaker, that I would like to honor Nancy 
Hoffmaster and thank her for all the outstanding efforts she has put 
into making her community a better place to live. She is a most 
deserving recipient of the Power of One Honor.

                          ____________________
```
