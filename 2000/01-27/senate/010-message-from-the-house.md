# MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

```
                         MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

  At 8:35 p.m., a message from the House of Representatives, delivered 
by one of its reading clerks, announced that the House has agreed to 
the following concurrent resolution, in which it requests the 
concurrence of the Senate:

       H. Con. Res. 241. Concurrent resolution providing for a 
     joint resolution of Congress to receive a message from the 
     President on the State of the Union.
```
