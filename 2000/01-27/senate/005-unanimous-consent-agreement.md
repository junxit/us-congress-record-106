# UNANIMOUS-CONSENT AGREEMENT

```
                      UNANIMOUS-CONSENT AGREEMENT

  Mr. LOTT. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the President 
of the Senate be authorized to appoint a committee on the part of the 
Senate to join with a like committee on the part of the House of 
Representatives to escort the President of the United States into the 
House Chamber for the joint session to be held at 9 p.m. this evening, 
Thursday, January 27, 2000.
  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
