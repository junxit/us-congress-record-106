            SENATE BILLS AND CONCURRENT RESOLUTIONS REFERRED

  Bills and Concurrent Resolutions of the Senate of the following 
titles were taken from the Speaker's table and, under the rule, 
referred as follows:

       S. 244. An act to authorize the construction of the Lewis 
     and Clark Rural Water System and to authorize assistance to 
     the Lewis and Clark Rural Water System, Inc., a nonprofit 
     corporation, for the planning and construction of the water 
     supply system, and for other purposes; to the Committee on 
     Resources.

     Committee on the Judiciary.

     to the Committee on the Judiciary.

     oilheat industry for the benefit of oilheat consumers and the

       S. 366. An act to amend the National Trails System Act to 
     designate El Camino Real de Tierra Adentro as a National

     Committee on Resources.

     fall within the jurisdiction of the committee concerned.
