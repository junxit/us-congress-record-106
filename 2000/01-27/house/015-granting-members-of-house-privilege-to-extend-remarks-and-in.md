# GRANTING MEMBERS OF HOUSE PRIVILEGE TO EXTEND REMARKS AND INCLUDE EXTRANEOUS MATERIAL IN CONGRESSIONAL RECORD FOR THE SECOND SESSION OF 106TH CONGRESS

**Speaking:** Armey, Richard K. (A000217) [R-TX]

```
   GRANTING MEMBERS OF HOUSE PRIVILEGE TO EXTEND REMARKS AND INCLUDE 
 EXTRANEOUS MATERIAL IN CONGRESSIONAL RECORD FOR THE SECOND SESSION OF 
                             106TH CONGRESS

  Mr. ARMEY. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that for the second 
session of the 106th Congress, all Members be permitted to extend their 
remarks and to include extraneous material within the permitted limit 
in that section of the Record entitled ``Extensions of Remarks.''
  The SPEAKER. Is there objection to the request of the gentleman from 
Texas?
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
