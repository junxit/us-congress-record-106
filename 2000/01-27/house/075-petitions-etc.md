# PETITIONS, ETC.

```
                            PETITIONS, ETC.

  Under clause 3 of rule XII,

       78. The SPEAKER presented a petition of Mr. Gregory D. 
     Watson of Austin, TX, relative to urging the Congress of the 
     United States to vote and approve legislation to make 
     employment discrimination against an applicant based upon the 
     applicant's actual, or perceived, sexual orientation 
     unlawful; which was referred jointly to the Committees on 
     Education and the Workforce, House Administration, Government 
     Reform, and the Judiciary.
```
