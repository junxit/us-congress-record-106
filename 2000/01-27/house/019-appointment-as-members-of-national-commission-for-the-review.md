# APPOINTMENT AS MEMBERS OF NATIONAL COMMISSION FOR THE REVIEW OF THE NATIONAL RECONNAISSANCE OFFICE

```
  APPOINTMENT AS MEMBERS OF NATIONAL COMMISSION FOR THE REVIEW OF THE 
                     NATIONAL RECONNAISSANCE OFFICE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to section 702(b) of the 
Intelligence Authorization Act for Fiscal Year 2000 (50 USC 401) and 
the order of the House of Thursday, November 18, 1999, the Speaker on 
Wednesday, January 12, 2000, appointed the following Member of the 
House to the National Commission for the Review of the National 
Reconnaissance Office:
  Mr. Goss, Florida.
  And from private life:
  Mr. Eli S. Jacobs, New York.
  Mr. Larry D. Cox, Maryland.

                          ____________________
```
