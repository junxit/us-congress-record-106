```
    COMMUNICATION FROM THE ASSOCIATE ADMINISTRATOR, OFFICE OF HUMAN 
                               RESOURCES

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following 
communication from Kay Ford, Associate Administrator, Office of Human 
Resources:

         Office of the Chief Administrative Officer, House of 
           Representatives,
                                 Washington, DC, December 8, 1999.
     Hon. J. Dennis Hastert,
     Speaker, House of Representatives,
     Washington, DC.

     the Northern District of Ohio.

           Sincerely,

                                                     Kay Ford,

     Office of Human Resources.
```
