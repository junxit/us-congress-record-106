# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, there will now be a 
period for the transaction of morning business, not to extend beyond 
the hour of 12:30 p.m., with Senators permitted to speak for up to 5 
minutes each.
```
