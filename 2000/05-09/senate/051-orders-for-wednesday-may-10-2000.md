# ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, MAY 10, 2000

```
                   ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, MAY 10, 2000

  Mr. BROWNBACK. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until the hour of 9:30 
a.m. on Wednesday, May 10. I further ask consent that immediately 
following the prayer, the Journal of proceedings be approved to date, 
the morning hour be deemed expired, the time for the two leaders be 
reserved for their use later in the day, and the Senate immediately 
proceed to a vote on the motion to proceed to the conference report to 
accompany H.R. 434, the African Trade-Caribbean Basin Initiative, as 
under the order.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
