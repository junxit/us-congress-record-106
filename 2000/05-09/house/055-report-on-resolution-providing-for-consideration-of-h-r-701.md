# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 701, THE CONSERVATION AND REINVESTMENT ACT OF 1999

**Measures:** H.Res. 497, H.R. 701

```
   REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 701, THE 
               CONSERVATION AND REINVESTMENT ACT OF 1999

  Mr. LINDER (during the special order of Ms. Millender-McDonald), from 
the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged report (Rept. No. 106-
612) on the resolution (H. Res. 497) providing for consideration of the 
bill (H.R. 701) to provide Outer Continental Shelf Impact Assistance to 
State and local governments, to amend the Land and Water Conservation 
Fund Act of 1965, the Urban Park and Recreation Recovery Act of 1978, 
and the Federal Aid in Wildlife Restoration Act (commonly referred to 
as the Pittman-Robertson Act) to establish a fund to meet the outdoor 
conservation and recreational needs of the American people, and for 
other purposes, which was referred to the House Calender and ordered to 
be printed.
```
