# ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

```
                ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Shimkus). Pursuant to clause 8 of rule 
XX, the Chair will now put the question on each motion to suspend the 
rules on which further proceedings were postponed earlier today in the 
order in which that motion was entertained.

  H.R. 3293, by the yeas and nays;
  H.R. 4386, by the yeas and nays;
  H.R. 4365, by the yeas and nays;
  H.R. 3313, by the yeas and nays.
  The Chair will reduce to 5 minutes the time for each electronic vote 
after the first such vote in this series.
```
