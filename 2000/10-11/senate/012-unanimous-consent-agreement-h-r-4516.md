# UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--H.R. 4516

**Speaking:** Lott, Trent (L000447) [R-MS]

**Measures:** H.R. 4516

```
                 UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--H.R. 4516

  Mr. LOTT. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that immediately 
following the vote on the adoption of the HUD-VA bill on Thursday, the 
motion to proceed to the motion to reconsider the vote by which the 
conference report to accompany H.R. 4516 was not agreed to be 
immediately agreed to, and the vote occur on the conference report 
immediately, without any intervening action or debate.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
