# ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, OCTOBER 12, 2000

```
                 ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, OCTOBER 12, 2000

  Mr. McCAIN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it recess until the hour of 9:30 
a.m. on Thursday, October 12. I further ask consent that on Thursday, 
immediately following the prayer, the Journal of proceedings be 
approved to date, the time for the two leaders be reserved for their 
use later in the day, and the Senate then proceed to H.R. 4635, the 
HUD-VA appropriations bill as under the previous order.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. McCAIN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senator 
from Arizona, Mr. McCain, be allowed 10 minutes before the HUD-VA 
appropriations bill is voted on.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
