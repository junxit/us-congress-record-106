# JUSTICE FOR VICTIMS OF TERRORISM ACT

```
                  JUSTICE FOR VICTIMS OF TERRORISM ACT

Victims of Terrorism Act, I wish to make clear that the reference to 
June 7, 1999 in the anti-terrorism section of H.R. 3244 is intended to 
refer to the case of Thomas M. Sutherland.
```
