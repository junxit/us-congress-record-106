# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF A MOTION TO CONCUR IN THE SENATE AMENDMENT WITH AN AMENDMENT TO H.R. 4386, BREAST AND CERVICAL CANCER PREVENTION AND TREATMENT ACT OF 2000

```
REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF A MOTION TO CONCUR 
  IN THE SENATE AMENDMENT WITH AN AMENDMENT TO H.R. 4386, BREAST AND 
          CERVICAL CANCER PREVENTION AND TREATMENT ACT OF 2000

  Mr. DREIER, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 106-975) on the resolution (H. Res. 628) providing 
for consideration of the Senate amendment to the bill (H.R. 4386) to 
amend title XIX of the Social Security Act to provide medical 
assistance for certain women screened and found to have breast or 
cervical cancer under a federally funded screen program, to amend the 
Public Health Service Act and the federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act 
with respect to surveillance and information concerning the 
relationship between cervical cancer and the human papillomavirus 
(HPV), and for other purposes, which was referred to the House Calendar 
and ordered to be printed.
```
