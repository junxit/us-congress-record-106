# Daily Digest/NEW PUBLIC LAWS

**Measures:** S. 1638, S. 2460

```
                             NEW PUBLIC LAWS

     (For last listing of Public Laws, see Daily Digest, p. D1013)
  S. 1638, to amend the Omnibus Crime Control and Safe Streets Act of 
1968 to extend the retroactive eligibility dates for financial 
assistance for higher education for spouses and dependent children of 
Federal, State, and local law enforcement officers who are killed in 
the line of duty. Signed October 2, 2000. (P.L. 106-276)
  S. 2460, to authorize the payment of rewards to individuals 
furnishing information relating to persons subject to indictment for 
serious violations of international humanitarian law in Rwanda. Signed 
October 2, 2000. (P.L. 106-277)
```
