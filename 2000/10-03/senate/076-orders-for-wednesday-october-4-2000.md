# ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, OCTOBER 4, 2000

```
                 ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, OCTOBER 4, 2000

  Mr. ROBERTS. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it recess until the hour of 9:30 
a.m. on Wednesday, October 4. I further ask unanimous consent that on 
Wednesday, immediately following the prayer, the Journal of proceedings 
be approved to date, the time for the two leaders be reserved for their 
use later in the day, and the Senate then resume consideration of the 
conference report to accompany H.R. 4578, the Interior appropriations 
bill.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
