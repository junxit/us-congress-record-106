# MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--S. 3152

```
                  MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--S. 3152

  Mr. ROBERTS. Mr. President, I understand that S. 3152 is at the desk, 
and I ask for its first reading.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report the bill by title.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (S. 3152) to amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 
     to provide tax incentives for distressed areas, and for other 
     purposes.

  Mr. ROBERTS. Mr. President, I ask for its second reading and object 
to my own request.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Objection is heard.
  The bill will be read a second time on the next legislative day.

                          ____________________
```
