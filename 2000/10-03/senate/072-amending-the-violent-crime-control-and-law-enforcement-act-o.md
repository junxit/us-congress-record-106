# AMENDING THE VIOLENT CRIME CONTROL AND LAW ENFORCEMENT ACT OF 1994

**Speaking:** Roberts, Pat (R000307) [R-KS]

**Measures:** H.R. 1800

```
   AMENDING THE VIOLENT CRIME CONTROL AND LAW ENFORCEMENT ACT OF 1994

  Mr. ROBERTS. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the 
Committee on the Judiciary be discharged from further consideration of 
H.R. 1800 and the Senate then proceed to its immediate consideration.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER (Mr. Allard). Without objection, it is so 
ordered.
  The clerk will report.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (H.R. 1800) to amend the Violent Crime Control and 
     Law Enforcement Act of 1994 to ensure that certain 
     information regarding prisoners is reported to the Attorney 
     General.

  There being no objection, the Senate proceeded to consider the bill.
  Mr. ROBERTS. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the bill be

[[Page S9770]]

considered read the third time and passed, the motion to reconsider be 
laid upon the table, and any statements relating to the bill be printed 
in the Record.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The bill (H.R. 1800) was considered read the third time and passed.

                          ____________________
```
