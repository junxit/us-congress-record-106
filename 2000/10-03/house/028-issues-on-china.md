# ISSUES ON CHINA

```
                            ISSUES ON CHINA

  (Mr. TRAFICANT asked and was given permission to address the House 
for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. TRAFICANT. It is a proven fact, China has missiles pointed at 
America. China bought submarines and attack aircraft from Russia. China 
has spied on America. China has illegally purchased American secrets, 
and China is now taking $100 billion a year in cash out of our economy 
in sweetheart trade deals. If that is not enough to smell the gun 
powder, a Chinese spokesman announced, and I quote, Cuba is our 
Communist ally and China will now embrace Castro.
  Beam me up. Let me caution Members, China has more soldiers than 
America has citizens. I yield back both the treason of Janet Reno and 
the blindness of the Congress of the United States of America.

                          ____________________
```
