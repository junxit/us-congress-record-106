# Daily Digest/Senate Committee Meetings

```
Committee Meetings
(Committees not listed did not meet)
NURSING HOME BANKRUPTCIES
Special Committee on Aging: Committee concluded hearings to examine the 
causes of nursing home bankruptcies, in order to inform future policy 
decisions, including proposals to raise Medicare payments to skilled 
nursing facilities, after receiving testimony from Laura A. Dummit, 
Associate Director, Health Financing and Public Health Issues, Health, 
Education, and Human Services Division, General Accounting Office; 
George F. Grob, Deputy Inspector General for Evaluation and 
Inspections, and Steven Pelovitz, Director, Survey and Certification 
Group, Health Care Financing Administration, both of the Department of 
Health and Human Services; John Ransom, Raymond James and Associates, 
Inc., St. Petersburg, Florida; and Charles H. Roadman, II, American 
Health Care Association, Washington, D.C.
```
