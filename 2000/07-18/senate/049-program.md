# PROGRAM

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. NICKLES. Mr. President, when the Senate convenes at 9:30 a.m., 
the Senate will immediately resume consideration and debate of the 
Agriculture appropriations bill. Amendments are expected to be offered 
and debated throughout tomorrow's session. As previously announced, any 
votes ordered with respect to the Agriculture appropriations bill will 
be stacked to occur sometime after 2 p.m. in order to accommodate those 
Senators attending the funeral service for former Senator Pastore. In 
addition, as information becomes available with respect to the services 
for Senator Coverdell, further announcements will be made.
  Mr. BROWNBACK. Mr. President, before we close, I ask that we have a 
moment of silent prayer for the Paul Coverdell family.
  (Moment of silence.)
  Mr. NICKLES. Mr. President, I thank my friend and colleague from 
Kansas, and I wish to reiterate the statement that all of us are 
praying for the Coverdell family.
```
