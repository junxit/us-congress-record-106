# ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, JULY 19, 2000

**Speaking:** Nickles, Don (N000102) [R-OK]

```
                  ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, JULY 19, 2000

  Mr. NICKLES. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until the hour of 9:30 
a.m. on

[[Page S7169]]

Wednesday, July 19. I further ask unanimous consent that on Wednesday, 
immediately following the prayer, the Journal of proceedings be 
approved to date and the morning hour be deemed expired, the time for 
the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the day, and the 
Senate then resume consideration of the Agriculture appropriations 
bill.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
