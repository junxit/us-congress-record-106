voted nay; and rollcall vote 318, on the DeGette amendment to H.R. 
4690, I would have voted nay.
  June 23, 2000: Rollcall vote 319, on the Waxman amendment to H.R. 
4690, I would have voted nay; rollcall vote 320, on the Davis of 
Virginia amendment to H.R. 4690, I would have voted nay; and rollcall 
vote 321, on the Coble amendment to H.R. 4690, I would have voted aye.
