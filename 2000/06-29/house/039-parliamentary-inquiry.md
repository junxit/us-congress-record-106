```

                         PARLIAMENTARY INQUIRY

  Mr. CONYERS. Mr. Speaker, a parliamentary inquiry.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Nussle). The gentleman will state his 
parliamentary inquiry.
  Mr. CONYERS. Does the Speaker have the authority to roll the votes in 
the interest of saving time tonight?
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The Chairman of the Committee of the Whole 
House will have the authority to postpone and cluster votes on
```
