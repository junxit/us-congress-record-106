```

125, 106th Congress, I move that the House do now adjourn.

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to the provisions of Senate 
Concurrent Resolution 125, 106th Congress, the House stands adjourned

  Thereupon, (at 2 o'clock and 6 minutes a.m.), pursuant to Senate 
Concurrent Resolution 125, the House adjourned until Monday, July 10, 
2000 for morning hour debates.
```
