# APPOINTMENT OF HON. CONSTANCE A. MORELLA TO ACT AS SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE TO SIGN ENROLLED BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS THROUGH JULY 10, 2000

```
APPOINTMENT OF HON. CONSTANCE A. MORELLA TO ACT AS SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE 
   TO SIGN ENROLLED BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS THROUGH JULY 10, 2000

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following 
communication from the Speaker:


                                               Washington, DC,

                                                    June 29, 2000.
       I hereby appoint the Honorable Constance A. Morella to act 
     as Speaker pro tempore to sign enrolled bills and joint 
     resolutions through July 10, 2000.
                                                J. Dennis Hastert,
                          Speaker of the House of Representatives.

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Without objection, the appointment is 
approved.
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
