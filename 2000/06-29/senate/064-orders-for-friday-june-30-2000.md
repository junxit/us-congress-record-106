# ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, JUNE 30, 2000

**Speaking:** Stevens, Ted (S000888) [R-AK]

**Measures:** H.R. 4577

```
                    ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, JUNE 30, 2000

  Mr. STEVENS. I now ask unanimous consent when the Senate completes 
its business today it stand in adjournment

[[Page S6183]]

until 9:30 a.m. on Friday, June 30, 2000. I further ask that on Friday, 
immediately following the prayer, the Journal of proceedings be 
approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, the time for the 
two leaders be reserved for their use later in the day, and the Senate 
then resume consideration of H.R. 4577, the Labor, Health and Human 
Services, and Education appropriations bill under the previous order.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. STEVENS. Mr. President, I further ask consent that following the 
votes, Senator Domenici be recognized as in morning business.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
