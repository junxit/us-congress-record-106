# PETITIONS, ETC.

```
                            PETITIONS, ETC.

  Under clause 3 of rule XII.

       124. The SPEAKER presented a petition of the Legislature of 
     Rockland County, New York, relative to Resolution No. 606 of 
     2000 petitioning the United States Congress to condemn the 
     terrorist attack on the United States Naval vessel the U.S.S. 
     Cole and urges President William Jefferson Clinton to use all 
     the resources of the United States government to speedily 
     bring those responsible for the terrorist attack to justice; 
     to the Committee on Armed Services.
```
