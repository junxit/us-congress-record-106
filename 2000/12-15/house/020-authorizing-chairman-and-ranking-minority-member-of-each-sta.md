# AUTHORIZING CHAIRMAN AND RANKING MINORITY MEMBER OF EACH STANDING COMMITTEE AND SUBCOMMITTEE TO EXTEND REMARKS IN RECORD

```
   AUTHORIZING CHAIRMAN AND RANKING MINORITY MEMBER OF EACH STANDING 
         COMMITTEE AND SUBCOMMITTEE TO EXTEND REMARKS IN RECORD

  Mr. McCOLLUM. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that the chairman 
and ranking minority member of each standing committee and each 
subcommittee be permitted to extend their remarks in the Record, up to 
and including the Record's last publication, and to include a summary 
of the work of that committee or subcommittee.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Florida?
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
