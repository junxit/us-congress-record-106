# SINE DIE ADJOURNMENT

**Speaking:** McCollum, Bill (M000350) [R-FL]

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 446

```
                          SINE DIE ADJOURNMENT

  Mr. McCOLLUM. Mr. Speaker, pursuant to House Concurrent Resolution 
446, One Hundred Sixth Congress, and as the designee of the majority 
leader, I move that the House do now adjourn.
  The motion was agreed to.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. In accordance with the provisions of House 
Concurrent Resolution 446, One Hundred Sixth Congress, the Chair 
declares the second session of the One Hundred Sixth Congress adjourned 
sine die.
  Thereupon (at 8 o'clock and 41 minutes p.m.) pursuant to House 
Concurrent Resolution 446, the House adjourned.

                          ____________________
```
