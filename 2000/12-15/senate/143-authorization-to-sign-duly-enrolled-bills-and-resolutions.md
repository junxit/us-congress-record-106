# AUTHORIZATION TO SIGN DULY ENROLLED BILLS AND RESOLUTIONS

**Speaking:** Stevens, Ted (S000888) [R-AK]

```
       AUTHORIZATION TO SIGN DULY ENROLLED BILLS AND RESOLUTIONS

  Mr. STEVENS. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the majority 
leader or Senator Abraham be authorized to sign all duly enrolled bills 
and resolutions following the sine die adjournment.
```
