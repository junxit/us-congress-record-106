# ORDER FOR RECESS

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV], Torricelli, Robert G. (T000317) [D-NJ]

```
                            ORDER FOR RECESS

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, due to the delay in consideration of the 
final appropriations bill, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
stand in recess until the hour of 4 p.m., following the remarks of 
Senator Torricelli from New Jersey.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. REID. I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will call the roll.
  The bill clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. TORRICELLI. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the order 
for the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
