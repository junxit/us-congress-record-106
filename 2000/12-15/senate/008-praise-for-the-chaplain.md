# PRAISE FOR THE CHAPLAIN

**Speaking:** Warner, John (W000154) [R-VA]

```
                        PRAISE FOR THE CHAPLAIN

  Mr. WARNER. Mr. President, I say with gratitude that we have such a 
marvelous Chaplain, one who with great skill and such strength of 
feeling and emotion is able to deliver the message of prayer and 
incorporate those historic moments of history.
  That election of Thomas Jefferson was one, fortunately, we avoided 
this time around; for Congress was involved, as our distinguished 
Chaplain and others know, and the vote in Congress was razor thin.

                          ____________________
```
