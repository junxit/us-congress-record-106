# ADDITIONAL COSPONSORS

**Measures:** S. 741, S. 2718, S. 3250

```
                         ADDITIONAL COSPONSORS


                                 S. 741

  At the request of Mr. Graham, the name of the Senator from South 
Dakota (Mr. Johnson) was added as a cosponsor of S. 741, a bill to 
provide for pension reform, and for other purposes.


                                S. 2718

  At the request of Mr. Smith of New Hampshire, the name of the Senator 
from Montana (Mr. Burns) was added as a cosponsor of S. 2718, a bill to 
amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to provide incentives to 
introduce new technologies to reduce energy consumption in buildings.


                                S. 3250

  At the request of Mr. Brownback, the name of the Senator from 
Mississippi (Mr. Cochran) was added as a cosponsor of S. 3250, a bill 
to provide for a United States response in the event of a unilateral 
declaration of a Palestinian state.

                          ____________________
```
