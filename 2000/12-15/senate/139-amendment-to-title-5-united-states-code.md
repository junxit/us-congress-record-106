# AMENDMENT TO TITLE 5, UNITED STATES CODE

**Speaking:** Stevens, Ted (S000888) [R-AK]

**Measures:** H.R. 207

```
                AMENDMENT TO TITLE 5, UNITED STATES CODE

  Mr. STEVENS. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
turn to the consideration of H.R. 207, which is at the desk.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report the bill by title.
  The assistant legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (H.R. 207) to amend title 5, United States Code, to 
     provide that physicians comparability allowances pay for 
     retirement purposes.

  There being no objection, the Senate proceeded to consider the bill.
  Mr. STEVENS. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the bill be 
read a third time and passed, the motion to reconsider be laid upon the 
table, and that any statements relating to the bill be printed in the 
Record.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The bill (H.R. 207) was read the third time and passed.

                          ____________________
```
