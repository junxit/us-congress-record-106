# RECESS UNTIL 3 P.M. TOMORROW

```
                      RECESS UNTIL 3 P.M. TOMORROW

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Senate stands in recess under the previous 
order until 3 p.m. tomorrow.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 5:15 p.m., recessed until Tuesday, October 
24, 2000, at 3 p.m.
```
