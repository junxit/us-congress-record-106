# PRIVILEGES OF THE FLOOR

**Speaking:** McConnell, Mitch (M000355) [R-KY], Feingold, Russell D. (F000061) [D-WI]

```
                        PRIVILEGES OF THE FLOOR

  Mr. McCONNELL. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent Christyne 
Bourne, a legal intern for the Rules Committee, be permitted to have 
access to the floor during the debate on the FEC nominations.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

McCormick, a legal intern on my staff, be granted floor privileges 
during the duration of the debate on the nominations that we are 
considering today and tomorrow.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
