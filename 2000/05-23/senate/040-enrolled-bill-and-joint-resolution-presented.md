# ENROLLED BILL AND JOINT RESOLUTION PRESENTED

```
              ENROLLED BILL AND JOINT RESOLUTION PRESENTED

  The Secretary of the Senate reported that on today, May 23, 2000, he 
had presented to the President of the United States, the following bill 
and joint resolution:

       S. 1836. An act to extend the deadline for commencement of 
     construction of hydroelectric project in the State of 
     Alabama.
       S.J. Res. 44. Joint resolution supporting the Day of Honor 
     2000 to honor and recognize the service of minority veterans 
     in the United States Armed Forces during World War II.
```
