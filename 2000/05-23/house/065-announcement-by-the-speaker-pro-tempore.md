# ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

```
                ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Calvert). Pursuant to clause 8, rule XX, 
the Chair announces that he will reduce to a minimum of 5 minutes the 
period of time within which a vote by electronic device will be taken 
on each motion to suspend the rules on which the Chair has postponed 
further proceedings.
```
