# CONSIDERING MEMBER AS FIRST COSPONSOR OF H.R. 1202

```
           CONSIDERING MEMBER AS FIRST COSPONSOR OF H.R. 1202

  Mr. FRANKS of New Jersey. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that I 
may hereafter be considered as the first sponsor of H.R. 1202, a bill 
originally introduced by Representative Brown of California, for the 
purpose of adding cosponsors and requesting reprintings under clause 7

gentleman from New Jersey?
```
