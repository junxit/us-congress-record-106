# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 3916, TELEPHONE EXCISE TAX REPEAL ACT

```
    REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 3916, 
                    TELEPHONE EXCISE TAX REPEAL ACT

  Mr. SESSIONS, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 106-637) on the resolution (H. Res. 511) providing 
for consideration of the bill (H.R. 3916) to amend the Internal Revenue 
Code of 1986 to repeal the excise tax on telephone and other 
telecommunications services, which was referred to the House Calendar 
and ordered to be printed.
```
