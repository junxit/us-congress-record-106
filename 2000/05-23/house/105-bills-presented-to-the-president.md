# BILLS PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

**Measures:** H.R. 3629, H.R. 3707

```
                    BILLS PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

  Mr. THOMAS, from the Committee on House Administration, reported that 
that committee did on the following date present to the President, for 
his approval, bills of the House of the following titles:

           On May 22, 2000:
       H.R. 3707. To authorize funds for the construction of a 
     facility in Taipei, Taiwan suitable for the mission of the 
     American Institute of Taiwan.
       H.R. 3629. To amend the Higher Education Act of 1965 to 
     improve the program for American Indian Tribal Colleges, and 
     Universities under Part A of title III.

                          ____________________
```
