```

                ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Calvert). Pursuant to clause 8, rule XX, 
the Chair will now put the question on each motion to suspend the rules

  H.R. 2498, by the yeas and nays; and
```
