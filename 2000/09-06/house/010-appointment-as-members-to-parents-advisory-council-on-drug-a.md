# APPOINTMENT AS MEMBERS TO PARENTS ADVISORY COUNCIL ON DRUG ABUSE

```
    APPOINTMENT AS MEMBERS TO PARENTS ADVISORY COUNCIL ON DRUG ABUSE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to sections 710(a)(2) of the Office 
of National Drug Control Policy Reauthorization Act of 1998 (21 U.S.C. 
1709) and the order of the House of Thursday, July 27, 2000, the 
Speaker, on Tuesday, August 15, 2000, appointed the following members 
from the private sector to the Parents Advisory Council on Youth Drug 
Abuse on the part of the House:
  Ms. Judith Kreamer, Naperville, Illinois, to a three-year term;
  Ms. Modesta Martinez, Bensenville, Illinois, to a two-year term;
  And Mr. Richard F. James, Columbus, Ohio, to a one-year term.

                          ____________________
```
