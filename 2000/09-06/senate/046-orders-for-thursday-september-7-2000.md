# ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, SEPTEMBER 7, 2000

```
[[Page 17130]]

                 ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, SEPTEMBER 7, 2000

  Mr. BOND. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate


completes its business today, it adjourn until the hour of 9:30 a.m. on 
Thursday, September 7. I further ask consent that on Thursday, 
immediately following the prayer, the Journal of proceedings be 
approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, the time for the 
two leaders be reserved for their use later in the day, and the Senate 
then resume debate on the Daschle motion regarding the Missouri River, 
with 10 minutes equally divided in the usual form prior to a vote on or 
in relation to the motion.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
