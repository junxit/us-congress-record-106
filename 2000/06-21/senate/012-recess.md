# RECESS

```
                                 RECESS

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. In my capacity as a Senator from Rhode Island, 
I ask unanimous consent that the Senate stand in recess until 11 a.m.
  Without objection, the Senate stands in recess until 11 a.m.
  Thereupon, at 10:22 a.m., the Senate recessed until 11:01; whereupon, 
the Senate reassembled when called to order by the Presiding Officer 
(Mr. Hutchinson).
```
