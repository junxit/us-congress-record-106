                                 RECESS

  Mr. LOTT. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate

Parliament be greeted by Senators.

2:01 p.m.; whereupon, the Senate reassembled when called to order by 
the Presiding Officer (Mr. Gregg).
