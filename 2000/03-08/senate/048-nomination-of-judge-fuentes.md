# NOMINATION OF JUDGE FUENTES

**Speaking:** Feinstein, Dianne (F000062) [D-CA]

```
                      NOMINATION OF JUDGE FUENTES

  Mrs. FEINSTEIN. Mr. President, I did not have the opportunity to vote 
on rollcall vote No. 34, the nomination of Julio M. Fuentes to be U.S. 
circuit judge, for the third circuit. Judge Fuentes is a very highly 
regarded judge, and had I been present on the floor, I would have voted 
``yea.''

                          ____________________
```
