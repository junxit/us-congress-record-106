# PROGRAM

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. LOTT. Mr. President, the Senate will convene at 9:30 a.m. We will 
have 4\1/2\ hours postcloture debate on the Berzon and Paez 
nominations. Under the previous order, the votes will occur at 2 p.m. 
The Senate will return to morning business for the purpose of bill 
introductions and statements. The Senate may also have consideration 
tomorrow of any Executive or Legislative Calendar items that are 
available for action.
  Does Senator Leahy wish to propound a request at this time?
  Mr. LEAHY. Mr. President, I ask the distinguished leader--once he has 
completed, and I realize there are others waiting--if I might be 
recognized for not more than 5 minutes to refer to the unanimous 
consent agreement on the judges. I did not want to delay earlier.
  Mr. LOTT. Thank you very much.
```
