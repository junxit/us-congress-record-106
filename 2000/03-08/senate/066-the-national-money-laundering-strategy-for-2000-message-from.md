# THE NATIONAL MONEY LAUNDERING STRATEGY FOR 2000--MESSAGE FROM THE PRESIDENT--PM 91

```
   THE NATIONAL MONEY LAUNDERING STRATEGY FOR 2000--MESSAGE FROM THE 
                            PRESIDENT--PM 91

  The PRESIDING OFFICER laid before the Senate the following message 
from the President of the United States, together with an accompanying 
report; which was referred to the Committee on the Judiciary.

To the Congress of the United States:
  As required by the provisions of section 2(a) of Public Law 105-310 
(18 U.S.C. 5341(a)(2)), I transmit herewith the National Money 
Laundering Strategy for 2000.
                                                  William J. Clinton.  
The White House, March 8, 2000.

                          ____________________
```
