# SCHEDULE

**Speaking:** Gorton, Slade (G000333) [R-WA]

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. GORTON. Mr. President, this morning the Senate will begin 1 hour 
of debate on the conference report to accompany the Federal Aviation 
Administration bill. Following that debate, the Senate will be in a 
period of morning business until 11:30 a.m. with the time under the 
control of Senators Brownback and Durbin. Following morning business, 
the Senate will begin consideration of the Export Administration Act 
with amendments to the bill expected to be offered. As a reminder, 
there will be three stacked votes at 5 p.m. The first vote will be on 
the conference report to accompany the Federal Aviation Administration 
bill, to be followed by the two cloture votes with respect to the 
Berzon and Paez nominations.
  I thank my colleagues for their cooperation.

                          ____________________
```
