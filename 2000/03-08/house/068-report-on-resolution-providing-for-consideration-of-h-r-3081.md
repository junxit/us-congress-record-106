# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 3081, WAGE AND ECONOMIC GROWTH ACT OF 1999, AND PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R.

**Measures:** H.Res. 434, H.R. 3081, H.R. 3846

```
REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 3081, WAGE AND 
 ECONOMIC GROWTH ACT OF 1999, AND PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 
                    3846, MINIMUM WAGE INCREASE ACT

  Mr. SESSIONS, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 106-516) on the resolution (H. Res. 434) providing 
for consideration of the bill (H.R. 3081) to increase the Federal 
minimum wage and to amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to provide 
tax benefits for small businesses, and for other purposes, and 
providing for consideration of the bill (H.R. 3846) to amend the Fair 
Labor Standards Act of 1938 to increase the minimum wage, and for other 
purposes, which was referred to the House Calendar and ordered to be 
printed.
```
