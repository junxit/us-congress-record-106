# COMMUNICATION FROM ACTING ASSOCIATE ADMINISTRATOR, OFFICE OF HUMAN RESOURCES

```
  COMMUNICATION FROM ACTING ASSOCIATE ADMINISTRATOR, OFFICE OF HUMAN 
                               RESOURCES

  The SPEAKER laid before the House the following communication from J. 
Michael Dorsey, Acting Associate Administrator, Office of Human 
Resources, House of Representatives:

         Office of the Chief Administrative Officer, House of 
           Representatives,
                                Washington, DC, November 15, 2000.
     Hon. J. Dennis Hastert,
     Speaker, House of Representatives,
     Washington, DC.
       Dear Mr. Speaker: This is to formally notify you pursuant 
     to Rule VIII of the Rules of the House that the Custodian of 
     Records, Office of Human Resources has received a subpoena 
     for documents issued by the United States District Court for 
     the District of New Jersey.
       After consultation with the Office of General Counsel, I 
     will make the determinations required by Rule VIII.
           Sincerely,

                                            J. Michael Dorsey,

                                   Acting Associate Administrator,
     Office of Human Resources.
```
