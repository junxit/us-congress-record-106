# POSTPONING CONSIDERATION OF COMMITTEE ON RESOURCES CONTEMPT RESOLUTION

**Speaking:** Young, Don (Y000033) [R-AK]

```
 POSTPONING CONSIDERATION OF COMMITTEE ON RESOURCES CONTEMPT RESOLUTION

  (Mr. YOUNG of Alaska asked and was given permission to address the 
House for 1 minute.)
  Mr. YOUNG of Alaska. Mr. Speaker, as many of my colleagues know, we 
were going to take up the contempt report following this vote. We have 
decided not to do that until a later time. It is not because of the 
issue. It is because of the number of people that saw fit to leave this 
body on both sides of the aisle to return to their homes. It will be 
considered next time.

                          ____________________
```
