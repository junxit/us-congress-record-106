# MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

**Measures:** H.J.Res. 84, H.R. 4986

```
                        MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

  A message from the Senate by Mr. Lundregan, one of its clerks, 
announced that the Senate has passed with amendments in which the 
concurrence of the House is requested, a bill and a joint resolution of 
the House of the following titles:

       H.R. 4986. An act to amend the Internal Revenue Code of 
     1986 to repeal the provisions relating to foreign sales 
     corporations (FSCs) and to exclude extraterritorial income 
     from gross income.
       H.J. Res. 84. Joint resolution making further continuing 
     appropriations for the fiscal year 2000, and for other 
     purposes.
```
