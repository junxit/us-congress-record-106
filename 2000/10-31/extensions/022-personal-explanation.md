have voted as follows: Rollcall 583--Technical Corrections to Minimum

Question--``no''; rollcall 581--Rule to Allow Additional Continuing 
Resolutions--``yes''; rollcall 580--Previous Question--``no''; rollcall 
579--Hour of Meeting October 31 at 6:00 p.m.--``no''; rollcall 578--
Passage One Day Continuing Resolution--``yes''; and rollcall 577--
Approval of the Journal--``yes.''
