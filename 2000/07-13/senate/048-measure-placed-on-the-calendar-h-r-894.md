# MEASURE PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--H.R. 894

**Speaking:** Roth Jr., William V. (R000460) [R-DE]

**Measures:** H.R. 894

```
                MEASURE PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--H.R. 894

  Mr. ROTH. Mr. President, I understand there is a bill at the desk due 
for its second reading.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report the bill by title.
  The assistant legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (H.R. 894) to encourage States to incarcerate 
     individuals convicted of murder, rape, or child molestation.

  Mr. ROTH. Mr. President, I object to further proceeding on this bill 
at this time.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The bill will be placed on the calendar.

                          ____________________
```
