# ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, JULY 14, 2000

**Speaking:** Roth Jr., William V. (R000460) [R-DE]

**Measures:** H.R. 8

```
                    ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, JULY 14, 2000

  Mr. ROTH. I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate completes its 
business today, it adjourn until the hour of 9 a.m. on Friday, July 14. 
I further ask consent that on Friday, immediately following the prayer, 
the Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be 
deemed expired, the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use 
later in the day, and the Senate then resume consideration of H.R. 8, 
the Death Tax Elimination Act, under the previous order.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
