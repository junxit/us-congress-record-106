# Text of House Amendment 196

**Speaking:** Boehner, John A. (B000589) [R-OH]

**Measures:** H.R. 4577

```
H.R. 4577

                        Offered By: Mr. Boehner

       Amendment No. 196: At the end of the bill, insert after the 
     last section (preceding the short title) the following new 
     section:
       SEC. ____. None of the funds made available in this Act may 
     be used for any program under part B of title IX of the 
     Elementary and Secondary Education Act of 1965.
```
