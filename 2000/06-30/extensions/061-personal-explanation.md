# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Jones, Stephanie Tubbs (J000284) [D-OH]

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                       HON. STEPHANIE TUBBS JONES

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, June 29, 2000

  Mrs. JONES of Ohio. Mr. Speaker, on June 22, 23 and 26, 2000, had 
family commitments and missed rollcall votes 315, 316, 317, 318, 319, 
320, 321, 322, 323 and 324. I ask that the record reflect that had I 
been present, I would have voted ``aye'' on rollcall votes 315, 316, 
317, 318 and 319. Also the record should reflect I would have noted 
``no'' on rollcall votes 320, 321, 322, and ``aye'' on rollcall vote 
323 and ``no'' on rollcall vote 324.
```
