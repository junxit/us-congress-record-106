# ORDER FOR MOMENT OF SILENCE

```
                      ORDER FOR MOMENT OF SILENCE

  Mr. GRASSLEY. As a reminder to all Members, on this date 2 years ago, 
Officer Chestnut and Detective Gibson were killed in the line of duty 
while defending the Capitol against an intruder armed with a gun. In 
honor of this anniversary, I now ask unanimous consent that at 3:40 
p.m. today, there be a moment of silence to honor these two officers.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. GRASSLEY. I thank my colleagues for their attention.

                          ____________________
```
