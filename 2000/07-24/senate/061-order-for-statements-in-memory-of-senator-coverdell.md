# ORDER FOR STATEMENTS IN MEMORY OF SENATOR COVERDELL

**Speaking:** Murkowski, Frank H. (M001085) [R-AK]

**Measures:** S.Res. 341

```
          ORDER FOR STATEMENTS IN MEMORY OF SENATOR COVERDELL

  Mr. MURKOWSKI. Further, I ask unanimous consent that on Thursday, the 
time from 9:30 a.m. until 11 a.m. be designated for Senators to make 
statements in memory of our dear friend, the late Senator Paul 
Coverdell.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. MURKOWSKI. Under the provisions of S. Res. 341, statements made 
on Thursday or prior to Thursday in regard to our colleague's death 
will be bound and given to Mrs. Coverdell.

                          ____________________
```
