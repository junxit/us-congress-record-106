# ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

```
                ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The Chair desires to make an announcement.
  On July 24, 1998, at 3:40 p.m., Officer Jacob J. Chestnut and 
Detective John W. Gibson of the United States Capitol Hill Police were 
killed in the line of duty defending the Capitol against an intruder 
armed with a gun.
  At 3:40 p.m. today, the Chair will recognize the anniversary of this 
tragedy by observing a moment of silence in their memory.
```
