                                 S. 279

  At the request of Mr. McCain, the name of the Senator from Oregon 
(Mr. Wyden) was added as a cosponsor of S. 279, a bill to amend title 
II of the Social Security Act to eliminate the earnings test for 
individuals who have attained retirement age.
