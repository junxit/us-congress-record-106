# REPORT ON THE NATIONAL INSTITUTE OF BUILDING SCIENCES FOR FISCAL YEAR 1998--MESSAGE FROM THE PRESIDENT--PM 124

```
 REPORT ON THE NATIONAL INSTITUTE OF BUILDING SCIENCES FOR FISCAL YEAR 
                1998--MESSAGE FROM THE PRESIDENT--PM 124

  The PRESIDING OFFICER laid before the Senate the following message 
from the President of the United States, together with an accompanying 
report; which was referred to the Committee on Banking, Housing, and 
Urban Affairs.

To the Congress of the United States:
  In accordance with the requirements of section 809 of the Housing and 
Community Development Act of 1974, as amended (12 U.S.C. 1701j-2(j)), I 
transmit herewith the annual report of the National Institute of 
Building Sciences for fiscal year 1998.
                                                  William J. Clinton.  
The White House, July 27, 2000.

                          ____________________
```
