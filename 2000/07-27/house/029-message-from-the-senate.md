# MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

**Measures:** S.Con.Res. 132, S. 2869

```
                        MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

  A message from the Senate by Mr. Lundregen, one of its clerks, 
announced that the Senate has passed a bill and a concurrent resolution 
of the following titles in which the concurrence of the House is 
requested:

       S. 2869. An act to protect religious liberty, and for other 
     purposes.
       S. Con. Res. 132. Concurrent resolution providing for a 
     conditional adjournment or recess of the Senate and 
     conditional adjournment of the House of Representatives.
```
