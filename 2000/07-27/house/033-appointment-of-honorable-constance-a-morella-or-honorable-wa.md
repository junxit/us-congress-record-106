# APPOINTMENT OF HONORABLE CONSTANCE A. MORELLA OR HONORABLE WAYNE T. GILCHREST TO ACT AS SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE TO SIGN ENROLLED BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS THROUGH SEPTEMBER 6, 2000

```
  APPOINTMENT OF HONORABLE CONSTANCE A. MORELLA OR HONORABLE WAYNE T. 
  GILCHREST TO ACT AS SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE TO SIGN ENROLLED BILLS AND 
              JOINT RESOLUTIONS THROUGH SEPTEMBER 6, 2000

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following 
communication from the Speaker:

                                               Washington, DC,

                                                    July 27, 2000.
       I hereby appoint the Honorable Constance A. Morella or, if 
     not available to perform this duty, the Honorable Wayne T. 
     Gilchrest to act as Speaker pro tempore to sign enrolled 
     bills and joint resolutions through September 6, 2000.
                                                J. Dennis Hastert,
                          Speaker of the House of Representatives.

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Without objection, the appointment is 
approved.
  There was no objection.




                          ____________________
```
