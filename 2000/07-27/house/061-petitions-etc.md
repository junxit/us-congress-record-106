# PETITIONS, ETC.

```
                            PETITIONS, ETC.

  Under clause 3 of rule XII,

       103. The SPEAKER presented a petition of Essex County Board 
     of Supervisors, Clerk, Essex, New York, relative to 
     Resolution No. 101 petitioning the House of Representatives 
     to amend the Conservation and Reinvestment Act of 1999 to 
     include a provision stating that if any county, town, city or 
     village has more than 20% publicly owned land, the governing 
     body of such municipality must approve of the acquisition of 
     any property or property rights with such municipality 
     through the use of CARA funds in whole or in part; which was 
     referred jointly to the Committees on Commerce, Agriculture, 
     and the Budget.
```
