# Text of House Amendment 25

**Speaking:** Traficant, James A., Jr. (T000350) [D-OH]

**Measures:** H.R. 4942

```
H.R. 4942

                       Offered By: Mr. Traficant

       Amendment No. 25: Page 78, insert after line 15 the 
     following:
       (d) Prohibiting Use of Funds in Contravention of Act.--No 
     funds in this Act may be used in contravention of the Act of 
     March 3, 1933 (41 U.S.C. 10a et seq.; popularly known as the 
     ``Buy American Act'').
```
