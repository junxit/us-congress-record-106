# ANNUAL REPORT OF NATIONAL INSTITUTE OF BUILDING SCIENCES FOR FISCAL YEAR 1998--MESSAGE FROM THE PRESIDENT OF THE UNITED STATES

```
  ANNUAL REPORT OF NATIONAL INSTITUTE OF BUILDING SCIENCES FOR FISCAL 
       YEAR 1998--MESSAGE FROM THE PRESIDENT OF THE UNITED STATES

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following message 
from the President of the United States; which was read and, together 
with the accompanying papers, without objection, referred to the 
Committee on Banking and Financial Services:

To The Congress of the United States:
  In accordance with the requirements of section 809 of the Housing and 
Community Development Act of 1974, as amended (12 U.S.C. 1701j-2(j)), I 
transmit herewith the annual report of the National Institute of 
Building Sciences for fiscal year 1998.
                                                  William J. Clinton.  
The White House, July 27, 2000.

                          ____________________
```
