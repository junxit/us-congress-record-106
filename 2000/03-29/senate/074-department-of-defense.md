# DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

**Speaking:** Domenici, Pete V. (D000407) [R-NM]

```
                         DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

  The assistant legislative clerk read the nomination of Rudy deLeon, 
of California, to be Deputy Secretary of Defense.
  Mr. DOMENICI. Mr. President, I further ask unanimous consent that the 
nomination be confirmed, the motion to reconsider be laid upon the 
table, and that any statements relating to the nomination be printed in 
the Record, the President be immediately notified of the Senate's 
action, and the Senate return to legislative session.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The nomination was confirmed.

                          ____________________
```
