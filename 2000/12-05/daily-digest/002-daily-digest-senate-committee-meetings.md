BASEBALL REVENUE GAP
Committee on the Judiciary: On Tuesday, November 21, Subcommittee on 
antitrust, Business Rights, and Competition concluded hearings to 
examine the impact that growing payroll and revenue disparities among 
teams in Major League Baseball have on the competition, health, 
popularity, and integrity of the game, after receiving testimony from 
former Senator Mitchell; George Will, Washington Post, Washington, 
D.C.; Allan H. Selig, Major League Baseball, Milwaukee, Wisconsin; Bob 
Costas, NBC, St. Louis, Missouri; Rodney Fort, Washington State 
University Department of Economics, Pullman; and Frank Stadulis, United 
Sports Fans of America, Boca Raton, Florida.
