# ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, DECEMBER 6, 2000

**Speaking:** Allard, Wayne (A000109) [R-CO]

```
                 ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, DECEMBER 6, 2000

  Mr. ALLARD. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it recess until the hour of 10 
a.m. on Wednesday, December 6. I further ask consent that on Wednesday, 
immediately following the prayer, the Journal of proceedings be 
approved to date, the time for the two leaders be reserved for their 
use later in the day, and the Senate then begin a period of morning 
business until 11 a.m., with Senators speaking for up to 5 minutes 
each, with the following exceptions: Senator Hagel, 10 to 10:30 a.m.; 
Senator Durbin or his designee, 10:30 to 11 a.m.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
