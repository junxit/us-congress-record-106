# UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--H.J. RES. 126

**Speaking:** Kyl, Jon (K000352) [R-AZ]

**Measures:** H.J.Res. 126

```
               UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--H.J. RES. 126

  Mr. KYL. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that at 2:15 p.m., 
the Senate proceed to H.J. Res. 126, the continuing resolution; 
further, that no amendments or motions be in order, and that there be 
15 minutes equally divided between the chairman and the ranking member; 
that following that time the resolution be immediately read the third 
time, and the Senate proceed to a vote on passage of the resolution, 
with no intervening action or debate.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Senator from West Virginia is recognized.

                          ____________________
```
