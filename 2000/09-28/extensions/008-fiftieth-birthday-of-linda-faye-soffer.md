# FIFTIETH BIRTHDAY OF LINDA FAYE SOFFER

**Speaking:** Dickey, Jay (D000312) [R-AR]

```
                 FIFTIETH BIRTHDAY OF LINDA FAYE SOFFER

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. JAY DICKEY

                              of arkansas

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, September 28, 2000

  Mr. DICKEY. Mr. Speaker, I want to recognize one of my constituents, 
Linda Faye Soffer (nee Cook) of White Hall, Arkansas, who will be 
celebrating her 50th birthday on October 15, 2000. Linda was born on 
October 15, 1950 in Memphis, Tennessee to William Allen Cook and 
Dorothy Annice Cook (nee McGill) of Earle, Arkansas. I want to join Stu 
Soffer, her husband, in wishing her a Happy Birthday with best wishes 
for the upcoming year.

                          ____________________
```
