# SCHEDULE

**Speaking:** Stevens, Ted (S000888) [R-AK]

**Measures:** H.J.Res. 109

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. STEVENS. Mr. President, I wish to make this statement for the 
leader. Today, the Senate will immediately begin consideration of H. J. 
Res 109, the continuing resolution. Under the previous agreement, there 
will be up to 7 hours for debate with a vote scheduled to occur after 
the use of the time or after the yielding back of the time. After the 
adoption of the continuing resolution, the Senate will proceed to a 
cloture vote in regard to the H-1B visa bill. Therefore, Senators can 
expect at least two votes during this afternoon's session of the 
Senate.
  As a reminder, tomorrow evening is the beginning of Rosh Hashanah. 
Therefore, the Senate will complete its business today and will not 
reconvene until Monday, October 2, in observance of this religious 
holiday.

                          ____________________
```
