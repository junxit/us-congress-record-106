# RELIEF OF JOSE GUADALUPE TELLEZ PINALES

**Measures:** S. 2289

```
                RELIEF OF JOSE GUADALUPE TELLEZ PINALES

  The bill (S. 2289) for the relief of Jose Guadalupe Tellez Pinales 
was considered, ordered to be engrossed for a third reading, read the 
third time, and passed, as follows:

                                S. 2289

       Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of 
     the United States of America in Congress assembled,

     SECTION 1. PERMANENT RESIDENCE.

       Notwithstanding any other provision of law, for purposes of 
     the Immigration and Nationality Act (8 U.S.C. 1101 et seq.), 
     Jose Guadalupe Tellez Pinales shall be held and considered to 
     have been lawfully admitted to the United States for 
     permanent residence as of the date of the enactment of this 
     Act upon payment of the required visa fee.
```
