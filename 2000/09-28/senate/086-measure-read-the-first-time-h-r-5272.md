# MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--H.R. 5272

```
                 MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--H.R. 5272

  Mr. GRAMS. Mr. President, I understand that H.R. 5272 is at the desk, 
and I ask for its first reading.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report the bill by title.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (H.R. 5272) to provide for a United States response 
     in the event of a unilateral declaration of a Palestinian 
     state.

  Mr. GRAMS. Mr. President, I ask for its second reading, and object to 
my own request.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The bill will receive its second reading on 
the next legislative day.

                          ____________________
```
