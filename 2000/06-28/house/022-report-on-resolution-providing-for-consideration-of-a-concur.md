# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF A CONCURRENT RESOLUTION FOR THE ADJOURNMENT OF THE HOUSE AND SENATE FOR THE INDEPENDENCE DAY DISTRICT WORK PERIOD

**Measures:** H.Res. 541, H.R. 3240

```
   REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF A CONCURRENT 
    RESOLUTION FOR THE ADJOURNMENT OF THE HOUSE AND SENATE FOR THE 
                 INDEPENDENCE DAY DISTRICT WORK PERIOD

  Mr. GOSS (during consideration of H.R. 3240), from the Committee on 
Rules, submitted a privileged report (Rept. No. 106-708) on the 
resolution (H. Res. 541) providing for consideration of a concurrent 
resolution providing for adjournment of the House and Senate for the 
Independence Day district work period, which was referred to the House 
Calendar and ordered to be printed.
```
