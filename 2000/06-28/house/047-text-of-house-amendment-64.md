# Text of House Amendment 64

**Speaking:** Sherman, Brad (S000344) [D-CA]

**Measures:** H.R. 4461

```
H.R. 4461

                        Offered By: Mr. Sherman

       Amendment No. 64: At the end of the bill, insert after the 
     last section, preceding the short title (page 96, after line 
     4), the following new title:

                TITLE IX--ADDITIONAL GENERAL PROVISIONS

       Sec. 901. None of the funds made available in this Act may 
     be used to allow the importation into the United States of 
     any agricultural or fishery product that is the growth, 
     product, or manufacture of the Islamic Republic of Iran.
```
