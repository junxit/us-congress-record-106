# Text of House Amendment 55

**Speaking:** Hayes, Robin (H001029) [R-NC]

**Measures:** H.R. 4461

```
H.R. 4461

                         Offered By: Mr. Hayes

       Amendment No. 55: Page 31, after line 5, insert the 
     following:

                        Administrative Provision

       Any limitation established in this title on funds to carry 
     out research related to the production, processing, or 
     marketing of tobacco or tobacco products shall not apply to 
     research on the medical, biotechnological, food and drug, and 
     industrial uses of tobacco and tobacco products.
```
