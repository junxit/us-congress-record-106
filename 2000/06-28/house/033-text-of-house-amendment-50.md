# Text of House Amendment 50

**Speaking:** Burton, Dan (B001149) [R-IN]

**Measures:** H.R. 4461

```
H.R. 4461

                   Offered By: Mr. Burton of Indiana

       Amendment No. 50: Insert before the short title the 
     following title:

                TITLE IX--ADDITIONAL GENERAL PROVISIONS

       Sec. 901. With respect to serving as a member of a Federal 
     advisory committee that has responsibilities regarding 
     vaccines, no scientist, physician, or other individual who is 
     a member or prospective member of such a committee may be 
     granted a waiver from conflict-of-interest rules that are 
     applicable to such service.
```
