  H.R. 4249, An act to foster cross-border cooperation and environmental 
cleanup in Northern Europe.
  S. Res. 239, expressing the sense of the Senate that Nadia Dabbagh, who 
was abducted from the United States, should be returned home to her mother, 
Ms. Maureen Dabbagh.
  S. Res. 309, expressing the sense of the Senate regarding conditions in 
Laos.
  S. Res. 329, urging the Government of Argentina to pursue and punish those 
responsible for the 1994 attack on the AMIA Jewish Community Center in 
Buenos Aires, Argentina.
  S. Con. Res. 57, concerning the emancipation of the Iranian Baha'i 
community, and with an amended preamble.
  S. Con. Res. 122, Concurrent resolution recognizing the 60th anniversary 
of the United States nonrecognition policy of the Soviet takeover of 
Estonia, Latvia, and Lithuania, and calling for positive steps to promote a 
peaceful and democratic future for the Baltic region.

  By 51 yeas to 48 nays (Vote No. 153), three-fifths of those Senators duly
