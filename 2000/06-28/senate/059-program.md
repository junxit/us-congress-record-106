# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Brownback, Sam (B000953) [R-KS]

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. BROWNBACK. For the information of all Senators, on Thursday the 
Senate will resume consideration of the disclosure bill at 9:30 a.m. 
Under the previous order, there will be closing remarks on the bill 
with a vote on final passage to occur at approximately 9:40 a.m. Under 
the order, a vote in relation to the Frist amendment to the Labor-HHS 
appropriations bill will immediately follow the disposition of the 
disclosure bill.
  As a reminder, there is a finite list of amendments to the Labor 
appropriations bill. Those Senators who have amendments on the list 
should work with the bill managers on a time to offer their amendments 
during tomorrow's session. Final passage on the bill is expected to 
occur by midafternoon.
```
