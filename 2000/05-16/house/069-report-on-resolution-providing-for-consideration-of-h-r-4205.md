# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 4205, NATIONAL DEFENSE AUTHORIZATION ACT FOR FISCAL YEAR 2001

**Measures:** H.Res. 503, H.R. 4205

```
REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 4205, NATIONAL 
             DEFENSE AUTHORIZATION ACT FOR FISCAL YEAR 2001

  Mr. SESSIONS, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 106-621) on the resolution (H. Res. 503) providing 
for consideration of the bill (H.R. 4205) to authorize appropriations 
for fiscal year 2001 for military activities of the Department of 
Defense and for military construction, to prescribe military personnel 
strengths for fiscal year 2001, and for other purposes, which was 
referred to the House Calendar and ordered to be printed.
```
