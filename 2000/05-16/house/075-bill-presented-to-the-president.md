# BILL PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

**Measures:** H.R. 434

```
                    BILL PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

  Mr. THOMAS, from the Committee on House Appropriation, reported that 
that committee did on this day present to the President, for his 
approval, a bill of the House of the following title:

       H.R. 434. To authorize a new trade and investment policy 
     for sub-Saharan Africa, expand trade benefits to the 
     countries in the Caribbean Basin, renew the generalized 
     system of preferences, and reauthorize the trade adjustment 
     assistance programs.

                          ____________________
```
