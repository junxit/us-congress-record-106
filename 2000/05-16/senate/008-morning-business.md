# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, there will now be a 
period for the transaction of morning business not to extend beyond the 
hour of 11 a.m., with Senators permitted to speak therein for up to 5 
minutes each.
  Under the previous order, the Senator from Alaska, Mr. Murkowski, or 
his designee, is recognized to speak for up to 45 minutes.
  The Senator from Alaska is recognized.

                          ____________________
```
