# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  Mr. BURNS. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that there be a 
period for the transaction of morning business until 12:30 p.m. today 
and that Senators be permitted to speak therein for up to 10 minutes.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. BURNS. Mr. President, I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will call the roll.
  The legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. DOMENICI. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the order 
for the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
