# PROGRAM

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. BROWNBACK. For the information of all Senators, the Senate will 
convene at 12 noon on Monday and will be in a period of morning 
business until 2 p.m. Following morning business, the Senate will 
resume debate on the education savings accounts legislation. As a 
reminder, cloture was filed on the bill today with the cloture vote 
scheduled to occur at 2:30 p.m. on Tuesday, February 29. Pursuant to 
rule XXII, all first-degree amendments must be filed by 1 p.m. on 
Monday. For the information of all Senators, the leader has announced 
there will be no rollcall votes during Monday's session of the Senate.

                          ____________________
```
