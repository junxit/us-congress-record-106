# WITHDRAWAL

```
                               WITHDRAWAL

  Executive message transmitted by the President to the Senate on 
October 26, 2000, withdrawing from further Senate consideration the 
following nomination:


            Federal Mine Safety and Health Review Commission

       Marc Lincoln Marks, of Pennsylvania, to be a Member of the 
     Federal Mine Safety and Health Review Commission for a term 
     of six years expiring August 30, 2006 (Reappointment), which 
     was sent to the Senate on June 8, 2000.



             CONGRESSIONAL RECORD 

                United States
                 of America


October 26, 2000
```
