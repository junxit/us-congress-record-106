# ENACTMENT OF CERTAIN SMALL BUSINESS, HEALTH, TAX, AND MINIMUM WAGE PROVISIONS--CONFERENCE REPORT

```
  ENACTMENT OF CERTAIN SMALL BUSINESS, HEALTH, TAX, AND MINIMUM WAGE 
                     PROVISIONS--CONFERENCE REPORT

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       The committee of conference on the disagreeing votes of the 
     two Houses on the amendment of the Senate on the bill H.R. 
     2614 ``To amend the Small Business Investment Act to make 
     improvements to the certified development company program, 
     and for other purposes,'' having met, have agreed that the 
     House recede from its disagreement to the amendment of the 
     Senate, and agree to the same with an amendment, and the 
     Senate agree to the same, signed by a majority of the 
     conferees on the part of both Houses.

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Senate will proceed to the consideration 
of the conference report.
  (The report is printed in the House proceedings of the Record (Part 
II) of October 25, 2000.)

                          ____________________
```
