# THE CALENDAR

**Speaking:** Brownback, Sam (B000953) [R-KS]

**Measures:** H.R. 4404, H.R. 4957, H.R. 5083, H.R. 5331

```
                              THE CALENDAR

  Mr. BROWNBACK. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
now proceed en bloc to the following Energy bills which are at the 
desk: H.R. 5083, H.R. 4957, H.R. 5331, H.R. 4404.
  I ask unanimous consent that the bills be read the third time and 
passed, the motions to reconsider be laid upon the table, and that any 
statements relating to the bills be printed in the Record, with the 
above occurring en bloc.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
