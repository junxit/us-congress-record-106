# TO EXTEND THE AUTHORITY OF LOS ANGELES UNIFIED SCHOOL DISTRICT TO CERTAIN PARK LANDS

**Measures:** H.R. 5083

```
   TO EXTEND THE AUTHORITY OF LOS ANGELES UNIFIED SCHOOL DISTRICT TO 
                           CERTAIN PARK LANDS

  The bill (H.R. 5083) to extend the authority of the Los Angeles 
Unified School District to use certain park lands in the City of South 
Gate, California, which were acquired with amounts provided from the 
land and water conservation fund, for elementary school purposes, was 
considered, ordered to a third reading, read the third time, and 
passed.
```
