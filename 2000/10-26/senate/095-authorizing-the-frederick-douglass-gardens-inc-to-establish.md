# AUTHORIZING THE FREDERICK DOUGLASS GARDENS, INC., TO ESTABLISH MEMORIAL IN HONOR OF FREDERICK DOUGLASS

**Measures:** H.R. 5331

```
AUTHORIZING THE FREDERICK DOUGLASS GARDENS, INC., TO ESTABLISH MEMORIAL 
                     IN HONOR OF FREDERICK DOUGLASS

  The bill (H.R. 5331) to authorize the Frederick Douglass Gardens, 
Inc., to establish a memorial and gardens on Department of the Interior 
lands in the District of Columbia or its environs in honor and 
commemoration of Frederick Douglass, was considered, ordered to a third 
reading, read the third time, and passed.
```
