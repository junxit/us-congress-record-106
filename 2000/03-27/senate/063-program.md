# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Sessions, Jeff (S001141) [R-AL]

**Measures:** S.J.Res. 14

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. SESSIONS. For the information of all Senators, tomorrow morning 
the Senate will resume consideration of the pending flag desecration 
resolution. Under the order, there will be 2 hours remaining for debate 
relating to the Hollings amendment, to be followed by an additional 
hour for general debate. At 2:15 on Tuesday, following the party 
luncheons, the Senate will proceed to two consecutive votes on the 
pending amendments to the flag desecration resolution. It is hoped that 
following those votes, the Senate will be able to reach a consent 
agreement regarding the passage vote of S. J. Res. 14. As a reminder, 
if an agreement is not reached for a vote on passage, then under the 
provisions of rule XXII, a cloture vote will occur on Wednesday of this 
week.
  I thank all the Members for their attention.

                          ____________________
```
