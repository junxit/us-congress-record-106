# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, there will now be a 
period for the transaction of morning business not to extend beyond the 
hour of 1:30 p.m., with Senators permitted to speak therein for not 
exceeding 10 minutes each.
  Under the previous order, the Senator from Illinois, Mr. Durbin, or 
his designee, will be in control of the first 45 minutes.
```
