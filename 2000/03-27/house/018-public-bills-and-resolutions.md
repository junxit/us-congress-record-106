# PUBLIC BILLS AND RESOLUTIONS

**Measures:** H.R. 4093

```
                      PUBLIC BILLS AND RESOLUTIONS

  Under clause 2 of rule XII, public bills and resolutions of the 
following titles were introduced and severally referred, as follows:

      By Mr. WEINER (for himself, Ms. Roybal-Allard, and Ms. Millender-
        McDonald):
       H.R. 4093. A bill A bill (H.R. 4093) to amend the Truth in 
     Lending Act to require a store in which a consumer may apply 
     to open a credit or charge card account to display a sign, at 
     each location where the application may be made, containing 
     the same information required by such Act to be prominently 
     placed in a tabular format on the application; which was 
     referred to the Committee on Banking and Financial Services.
```
