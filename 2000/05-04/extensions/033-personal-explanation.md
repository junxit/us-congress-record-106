# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Baca, Joe (B001234) [D-CA]

**Measures:** H.R. 434, H.Res. 488

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                             HON. JOE BACA

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                         Thursday, May 4, 2000

  Mr. BACA. Mr. Speaker, due to a speaking engagement outside the 
Capitol I was unable to cast a vote today on H. Res. 488, the rule to 
waive the two-thirds requirement for same day consideration of H.R. 
434.
  Had I been present, I would have voted ``no.''
  I share the concern of America's workers that the Caribbean Basin 
Initiative contained in H.R. 434 will jeopardize American jobs.
```
