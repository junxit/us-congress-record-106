# ENROLLED BILL AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS PRESENTED

**Measures:** S.J.Res. 40, S.J.Res. 42, S. 452

```
             ENROLLED BILL AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS PRESENTED

  The Secretary of the Senate reported that on May 4, 2000, he had 
presented to the President of the United States, the following enrolled 
bill and joint resolutions:

       S. 452. An act for the relief of Belinda McGregor.
       S.J. Res. 40. Joint resolution providing for the 
     appointment of Alan G. Spoon as a citizen regent of the Board 
     of Regents of the Smithsonian Institution.
       S.J. Res. 42. Joint resolution providing for the 
     reappointment of Manuel L. Ibanez as a citizen regent of the 
     Board of Regents of the Smithsonian Institution.
```
