# DISCRIMINATION CLAIMS AGAINST LOCKHEED MARTIN

**Speaking:** McKinney, Cynthia A. (M000523) [D-GA]

```
             DISCRIMINATION CLAIMS AGAINST LOCKHEED MARTIN

  (Ms. McKINNEY asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute.)
  Ms. McKINNEY. Mr. Speaker, I thought Southern good ol' boys were a 
dying breed. How mistaken could I have been? They must have all just 
been hiding in Lockheed Martin's management suites.
  Now, why do I say this? Just imagine a black woman having to get a 
bathroom pass from her white coworkers and then one of them having to 
escort her to the rest room to make sure she actually tinkles in the 
toilet.
  And if you are in need of Ku Klux Klan robes and membership cards, I 
know where you can find some.
  Just imagine coming to work and finding a noose hanging around your 
tool box.
  Also, seems Lockheed has found the fountain of youth. How else could 
they have so many 50-year-old black boys working for them? Not 
surprisingly, discrimination claims are being filed against Lockheed 
Martin all across the South from Alabama to North Carolina.
  Mr. Speaker, if John Rocker needs a job, I think I found the perfect 
place to hide him.

                          ____________________
```
