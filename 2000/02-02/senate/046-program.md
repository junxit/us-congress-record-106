# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Voinovich, George V. (V000126) [R-OH]

**Measures:** S. 1052

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. VOINOVICH. Mr. President, for the information of all Senators, 
when the Senate convenes tomorrow, it will immediately proceed to a 
vote on the Greenspan nomination. Therefore, Senators can expect the 
first vote to occur at approximately 10:30 a.m. tomorrow. Following 
that vote, the Senate will proceed to a period of morning business for 
general floor statements and bill introductions. Further, to 
accommodate the Democratic conference, the Senate will not be in 
session this Friday, February 4. On Monday, it is expected that the 
Senate will begin consideration of S. 1052, the Mariana Islands 
legislation, and on Tuesday the Senate should begin debate on the 
nuclear waste bill. Senators can expect votes throughout next week's 
session.

                          ____________________
```
