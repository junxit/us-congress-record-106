# SCHEDULE

**Speaking:** Grassley, Chuck (G000386) [R-IA]

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. GRASSLEY. Mr. President, for the leader, I would like to give 
today's schedule.
  Today the Senate will resume consideration of the bankruptcy reform 
bill. Senator Schumer will be recognized to debate his amendments 
regarding safe harbor and clinic violence. There are several other 
amendments remaining, and those amendments will be debated throughout 
this morning's session.
  All votes, including final passage, will be stacked and are expected 
to begin at approximately 12 o'clock noon. After disposition of the 
bankruptcy bill, the Senate is expected to begin consideration of the 
nomination of Alan Greenspan to continue as Chairman of the Federal 
Reserve Board.
  The leader thanks all Senators for their attention.
  I yield the floor.

                          ____________________
```
