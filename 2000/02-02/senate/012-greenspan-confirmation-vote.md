# GREENSPAN CONFIRMATION VOTE

**Speaking:** Boxer, Barbara (B000711) [D-CA]

```
                      GREENSPAN CONFIRMATION VOTE

  Mrs. BOXER. Mr. President, I was informed that the vote on the 
Greenspan nomination would be at 6 p.m. on Wednesday, so I had 
rearranged my schedule to return to my State. As I am unable to be 
present for the 10:30 a.m. Thursday vote, I ask that the Record show 
that if I were present to vote, I would vote in favor of confirming 
Alan Greenspan for another term as Chairman of the Federal Reserve 
Board of Governors.

                          ____________________
```
