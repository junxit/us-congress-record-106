# ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, FEBRUARY 3, 2000

**Speaking:** Voinovich, George V. (V000126) [R-OH]

```
                 ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, FEBRUARY 3, 2000

  Mr. VOINOVICH. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until the hour of 10:30 
a.m. on Thursday, February 3. I further ask consent that on Thursday, 
immediately following the prayer, the Journal of the proceedings be 
approved to date, the morning hour be deemed to have expired, the time 
for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the day, and the 
Senate then proceed to a vote on the confirmation of the nomination of 
Alan Greenspan to be chairman of the Board of Governors of the Federal 
Reserve system.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
