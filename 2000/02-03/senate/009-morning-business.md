# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  Mrs. HUTCHISON. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that there now 
be a period for the transaction of morning business, with Senators 
permitted to speak therein for up to 10 minutes each, with the 
exception of myself, and that I be permitted to control up to 30 
minutes.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The Chair recognizes the Senator from Texas.

                          ____________________
```
