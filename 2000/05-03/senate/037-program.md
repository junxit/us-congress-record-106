# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Jeffords, James M. (J000072) [R-VT]

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. JEFFORDS. Mr. President, for the information of all Senators, at 
9:45 a.m. the Senate will resume consideration of the Elementary and 
Secondary Education Act, with debate on the Abraham-Mack merit pay 
amendment to begin immediately. Following the consideration of that 
amendment, Senator Murray will be recognized to offer her amendment 
regarding class size. Votes are expected throughout the day. As usual, 
Senators will be notified as these votes are scheduled. As a reminder, 
the Senate will not meet on Friday in order to accommodate the 
Democratic retreat.

                          ____________________
```
