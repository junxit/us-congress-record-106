# NOTICE OF HEARINGS

**Speaking:** Campbell, Ben Nighthorse (C000077) [R-CO]

**Measures:** S. 611

```
                           NOTICE OF HEARINGS


                      committee on indian affairs

  Mr. CAMPBELL. Mr. President, I would like to announce that the 
Committee on Indian Affairs will meet during the session of the Senate 
on Wednesday, May 24, 2000, at 9:30 a.m. to conduct a hearing on S. 
611, the Indian Federal Recognition Administrative Procedures Act of 
1999. The hearing will be held in room 485, Russell Senate Building.
  Note: This hearing was originally scheduled for 9:30 a.m., May 17.
  Those wishing additional information may contact committee staff at 
202/224-2251.
```
