# MEASURE PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--H.R. 3709

```
               MEASURE PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--H.R. 3709

  Mr. ROBERTS. Mr. President, I understand there is a bill at the desk 
due for its second reading.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. The clerk will read the bill for 
the second time.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (H.R. 3709) to extend for 5 years the moratorium 
     enacted by the Internet Tax Freedom Act, and for other 
     purposes.

  Mr. ROBERTS. Mr. President, I object to further proceedings on the 
bill at this time.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Objection is heard. Under the rule, 
the bill will be placed on the calendar.

                          ____________________
```
