# Text of House Amendment 1

**Speaking:** Traficant, James A., Jr. (T000350) [D-OH]

**Measures:** H.R. 4392

```
H.R. 4392

                       Offered By: Mr. Traficant

       Amendment No. 1: At the end of the bill, add the following 
     new section:
       Sec. __. The Director shall report to the House Permanent 
     Select Committee on Intelligence within 60 days whether the 
     policies and goals of the People's Republic of China 
     constitute a threat to our national security.
```
