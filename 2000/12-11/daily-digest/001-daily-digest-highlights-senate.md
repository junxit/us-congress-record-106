# Daily Digest/Highlights + Senate

**Measures:** H.J.Res. 129, S.Con.Res. 161, S.Res. 387, S. 1508, S. 3276, H.R. 5528, H.R. 5630

```
                                          Monday, December 11, 2000

[[Page D1210]]

                              Daily Digest


HIGHLIGHTS

      The House passed H.J. Res. 129, Making Further Continuing 
      Appropriations.
      Agreed to the Senate amendments to H.R. 5630, Intelligence 
      Authorization--clearing the measure for the President.


                                 Senate


Chamber Action
Routine Proceedings, pages S11755-S11763
Measures Introduced: One bill and two resolutions were introduced, as 
follows: S. 3276, S. Res. 387, and S. Con. Res. 161. 
                                                   Pages S11759, S11762
Measures Passed:
  Relative to the Death of Representative Dixon: Senate agreed to S. 
Res. 387, relative to the death of Representative Julian C. Dixon of 
California.
  Pages S11755-56, S11763
  Omnibus Indian Advancement Act: Senate passed H.R. 5528, to authorize 
the construction of a Wakpa Sica Reconciliation Place in Fort Pierre, 
South Dakota, clearing the measure for the President.
  Pages S11757-60
  Enrollment Correction: Senate agreed to S. Con. Res. 161, to correct 
the enrollment of H.R. 5528.
  Pages S11759-60, S11762
Indian Tribal Justice Technical and Legal Assistance Act: Senate 
concurred in the amendment of the House to S. 1508, to provide 
technical and legal assistance for tribal justice systems and members 
of Indian tribes, clearing the measure for the President. 
                                                        Pages S11756-57
Continuing Resolution--Agreement: A unanimous-consent agreement was 
reached providing that when the Senate receives from the House the 
joint resolution funding the Government until Friday, December 15, it 
be considered read a third time and passed.
```
