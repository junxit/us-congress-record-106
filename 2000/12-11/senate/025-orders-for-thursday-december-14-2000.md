# ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, DECEMBER 14, 2000

**Speaking:** Hutchinson, Tim (H001015) [R-AR]

```
                 ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, DECEMBER 14, 2000

  Mr. HUTCHINSON. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it recess until the hour of 10 
a.m. on Thursday, December 14. I further ask consent that on Thursday, 
immediately following the prayer, the Journal of proceedings be 
approved to date, the time for the two leaders be reserved for their 
use later in the day, and the Senate then begin a period of morning 
business until 12 noon, with Senators speaking for up to 10 minutes 
each, with the time equally divided in the usual form.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Without objection, it is so 
ordered.

                          ____________________
```
