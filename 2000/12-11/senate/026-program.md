# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Hutchinson, Tim (H001015) [R-AR]

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. HUTCHINSON. For the information of all Senators, the Senate will 
reconvene on Thursday, December 14, at 10 a.m. There will be no session 
on Tuesday or Wednesday of this week in order to accommodate the 
funeral service for Congressman Dixon of California who passed away on 
Friday. Discussions will continue on the remaining appropriations 
issues, so the final votes may occur as early as Thursday or Friday.
```
