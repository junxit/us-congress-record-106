# HONORING OFFICER JOHN BRUGGER

**Speaking:** Condit, Gary A. (C000670) [D-CA]

```
[[Page E2167]]



                     HONORING OFFICER JOHN BRUGGER

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. GARY A. CONDIT

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                       Monday, December 11, 2000

  Mr. CONDIT. Mr. Speaker, I wish to recognize the retirement of one of 
California's finest. Officer John Brugger of the California Highway 
Patrol is retiring after 31 years of honorable service.
  Officer Brugger has spent 21 years of his career in Modesto in my 
district in California's great Central Valley, including the last 10 as 
the Public Affairs Officer. During his tenure, Officer Brugger has 
distinguished himself with the community. Officer Brugger is a Central 
Valley icon to those learning highway regulations and safety tips.
  His many years of service have given him a unique outlook at public 
safety and a vast resource of examples for his presentations. 
Additionally, Brugger is a familiar face in many of the community 
programs involving youth. As a founding member of the Modesto Explorer 
Scout program, John has been recognized by the California Attorney 
General for his efforts.
  I would like to take this opportunity to thank Officer Brugger for 
his contributions to the community. I also commend him for his courage 
in putting his life on the line as a California peace officer. It is an 
honor to call him my friend and I want to wish John and his wife, 
Linda, the very best as they embark on a new adventure.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to rise and join me in honoring 
California Highway Patrol Officer John Brugger.
```
