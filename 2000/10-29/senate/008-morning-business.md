# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, there will now be a 
period for the transaction of morning business, for not to extend 
beyond the hour of 6:45 p.m., equally divided between the two sides, 
with Senators permitted to speak therein for up to 10 minutes each.
  The Senator from Idaho.
```
