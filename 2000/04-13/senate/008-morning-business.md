# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, there will now be a 
period for the transaction of morning business not to extend beyond the 
hour of 12:30 p.m. with Senators permitted to speak therein for up to 5 
minutes each. Under the previous order, the time between 10:30 and 
10:45 a.m. shall be under the control of the Senator from Idaho, Mr. 
Crapo, or his designee.
  The Senator from Georgia.

                          ____________________
```
