# ENROLLED JOINT RESOLUTION PRESENTED

**Measures:** S.J.Res. 43

```
                  ENROLLED JOINT RESOLUTION PRESENTED

  The Secretary of the Senate reported that on April 13, 2000, he had 
presented to the President of the United States, that the following 
enrolled joint resolution:

       S.J. Res. 43. A joint resolution expressing the sense of 
     Congress that the President of the United States should 
     encourage free and fair elections and respect for democracy 
     in Peru.

                          ____________________
```
