# APPOINTMENT OF HON. FRANK R. WOLF OR HON. CONSTANCE A. MORELLA TO ACT AS SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE TO SIGN ENROLLED BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS THROUGH MAY 2, 2000

```
 APPOINTMENT OF HON. FRANK R. WOLF OR HON. CONSTANCE A. MORELLA TO ACT 
  AS SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE TO SIGN ENROLLED BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS 
                          THROUGH MAY 2, 2000

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following 
appointment by the Speaker:

                                               Washington, DC,

                                                   April 13, 2000.
       I hereby appoint the Honorable Frank R. Wolf or, if not 
     available to perform this duty, the Honorable Constance A. 
     Morella to act as Speaker pro tempore to sign enrolled bills 
     and joint resolutions through May 2, 2000.
                                                J. Dennis Hastert,
                          Speaker of the House of Representatives.

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Without objection, the appointment is agreed 
to.
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
