# SENATE JOINT RESOLUTIONS REFERRED

**Measures:** S.J.Res. 40, S.J.Res. 41, S.J.Res. 42

```
                   SENATE JOINT RESOLUTIONS REFERRED

  Joint resolutions of the Senate of the following titles were taken 
from the Speaker's table and, under the rule, referred as follows:

       S.J. Res. 40. Joint resolution providing for the 
     appointment of Alan G. Spoon as a citizen regent of the Board 
     of Regents of the Smithsonian Institution; to the Committee 
     on House Administration.
       S.J. Res. 41. Joint resolution providing for the 
     appointment of Sheila E. Widnall as a citizen regent of the 
     Board of Regents of the Smithsonian Institution; to the 
     Committee on House Administration.
       S.J. Res. 42. Joint resolution providing for the 
     reappointment of Manuel L. Ibanez as a citizen regent of the 
     Board of Regents of the Smithsonian Institution; to the 
     Committee on House Administration.

                          ____________________
```
