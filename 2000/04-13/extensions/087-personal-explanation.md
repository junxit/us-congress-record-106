# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Johnson, Sam (J000174) [R-TX]

**Measures:** H.R. 4051

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. SAM JOHNSON

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, April 13, 2000

  Mr. SAM JOHNSON of Texas. Mr. Speaker, due to a scheduling conflict I 
was unavoidably detained and missed rollcall vote 115. Had I been 
present I would have voted ``aye'' on H.R. 4051, Project Exile: The 
Safe Streets and Neighborhoods Act of 2000. This bill would establish a 
grant program that provides incentives for States to enact mandatory 
minimum sentences for certain firearm offenses.
```
