# TRIBUTE TO WILLIAM AND CATHERINE UPCHURCH

**Speaking:** Hayworth, J. D. (H000413) [R-AZ]

```
               TRIBUTE TO WILLIAM AND CATHERINE UPCHURCH

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. J.D. HAYWORTH

                               of arizona

                    in the house of representatives

                          Monday, May 8, 2000

  Mr. HAYWORTH. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to pay tribute to a wonderful 
Arizona family on the very happy occasion of the 40th anniversary of 
William and Catherine Upchruch. From this marriage came two beautiful 
daughters who have always been a source of pride and joy to their 
parents. Susan Upchurch was born on May 1, 1962, and Sharon Upchurch 
was born on November 5, 1963,
  The marriage of Sharon Upchurch to Michael Maita has been blessed 
with two children. William and Catherine are the proud grandparents of 
Alyssa Morgan Maita, born on January 5, 1998, and Andrew Jordan Maita, 
born on October 1, 1999.
  I am pleased to help honor the Upchurches, their strong and enduring 
marriage, and the wonderful family they have raised. Mr. Speaker, I am 
sure the whole House will join me in wishing the Upchurches all the 
best in the years to come.

                          ____________________
```
