# NOTICE OF HEARING

```
[[Page 7044]]

                           NOTICE OF HEARING


               committee on energy and natural resources

  Mr. CRAIG. Mr. President, I would like to announce for the public 
that a hearing has been scheduled before the Subcommittee on Forests 
and Public Land Management of the Senate Committee on Energy and 
Natural Resources.
  The hearing will take place on Wednesday, June 7, 2000 at 2:30 p.m. 
in room SD-366 of the Dirksen Senate Office Building in Washington, 
D.C.
  The purpose of this hearing is to receive testimony on S. 2300, a 
bill to amend the Mineral Leasing Act to increase the maximum acreage 
of Federal leases for coal that may be held by an entity in any 1 
State; S. 2069, a bill to permit the conveyance of certain land in 
Powell, Wyoming; and S. 1331, a bill to give Lincoln County, Nevada, 
the right to purchase at fair market value certain public land in the 
county.
  Those who wish to submit written statements should write to the 
Committee on Energy and Natural Resources, U.S. Senate, Washington, 
D.C. 20510. For further information, please call Kathleen Elder or Mike 
Menge (202) 224-6170.

                          ____________________
```
