# MORNING BUSINESS

**Measures:** S. 2194

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. There will now be a period for the transaction 
of morning business not to extend beyond the hour of 12:30 p.m. with 
Senators permitted to speak therein for up to 5 minutes each.
  (The remarks of Ms. Collins pertaining to the introduction of S. 2194 
are located in today's Record under ``Statements on Introduced Bills 
and Joint Resolutions.'')
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Senator from Nevada is recognized.
```
