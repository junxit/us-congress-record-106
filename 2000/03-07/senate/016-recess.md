# RECESS

```
                                 RECESS

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, the hour of 12:30 
p.m. having arrived and passed, the Senate stands in recess until 2:15 
p.m.
  Thereupon, at 12:41 p.m., the Senate recessed until 2:14 p.m.; 
whereupon, the Senate reassembled when called to order by the Presiding 
Officer (Mr. Thomas).
```
