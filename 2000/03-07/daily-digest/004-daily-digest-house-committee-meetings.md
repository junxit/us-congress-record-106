# Daily Digest/House Committee Meetings

```
Committee Meetings
LABOR-HHS-EDUCATION APPROPRIATIONS
Committee on Appropriations: Subcommittee on Labor, Health and Human 
Services, and Education continued appropriation hearings. Testimony was 
heard from public witnesses.

Joint Meetings
VETERANS PROGRAMS
Joint Hearing: Senate Committee on Veterans' Affairs concluded joint 
hearings with the House Committee on Veterans' Affairs to review the 
legislative recommendations of certain veterans organizations, after 
receiving testimony from SMSgt Frederick P. Athans, USAF (Ret.), 
Retired Enlisted Association, Margaret M. Peterson, Gold Star Wives of 
America, Inc., Richard H. Esau, Jr., Military Order of the Purple 
Heart, CMSgt James D. Staton, USAF (Ret.), Air Force Sergeants 
Association, and Charles L. Calkins, Fleet Reserve Association, all of 
Washington, D.C.
```
