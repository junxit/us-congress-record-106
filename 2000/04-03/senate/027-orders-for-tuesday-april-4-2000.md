# ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, APRIL 4, 2000

**Speaking:** Craig, Larry E. (C000858) [R-ID]

**Measures:** S. 101

```
                   ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, APRIL 4, 2000

  Mr. CRAIG. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until the hour of 9:30 
a.m. on Tuesday, April 4. I further ask unanimous consent that on 
Tuesday immediately following the prayer, the Journal of proceedings be 
approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, the time for the 
two leaders be reserved for their use later in the day, and the Senate 
then begin consideration of the Senate Concurrent Resolution 101, the 
budget resolution. I further ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
stand in recess from the hours of 12:30 p.m. until 2:15 p.m. for the 
weekly policy conferences to meet.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
