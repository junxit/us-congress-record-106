# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Chenoweth-Hage, Helen (C000345) [R-ID]

**Measures:** H.R. 3908

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                       HON. HELEN CHENOWETH-HAGE

                                of idaho

                    in the house of representatives

                         Monday, April 3, 2000

  Mrs. CHENOWETH-HAGE. Mr. Speaker, on March 30, 2000, I missed several 
rollcall votes on the account that I had unavoidable obligations 
elsewhere. Had I been present, I would have voted ``yea'' on rollcall 
vote 89 (Mr. Kasich's amendment to H.R. 3908), ``nay'' on rollcall vote 
90 (Mr. Weldon's amendment to H.R. 3908), ``yea'' on rollcall vote 91 
(Mr. Stearns' amendment to H.R. 3908), ``yea'' on rollcall vote 92 (Mr. 
Paul's amendment to H.R. 3908), ``yea'' on rollcall vote 93 (Mr. 
Tancredo's amendment to H.R. 3908), ``nay'' on rollcall vote 94 (on 
motion to recommit with instructions), and ``nay'' on rollcall vote 95 
(on passage of H.R. 3908).

                          ____________________
```
