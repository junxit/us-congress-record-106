# ELECTION OF MEMBER TO COMMITTEE ON APPROPRIATIONS

**Speaking:** Watts, J. C., Jr. (W000210) [R-OK]

**Measures:** H.Res. 410

```
           ELECTION OF MEMBER TO COMMITTEE ON APPROPRIATIONS

  Mr. WATTS of Oklahoma. Mr. Speaker, I offer a resolution (H. Res. 
410) and I ask unanimous consent for its immediate consideration in the 
House.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The Clerk will report the resolution.
  The Clerk read as follows:

                              H. Res. 410

       Resolved, That the following named Member be, and he is 
     hereby, elected to the following standing committee of the 
     House of Representatives:
       Committee on Appropriations: Mr. Goode of Virginia.

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Oklahoma?
  There was no objection.
  The resolution was agreed to.
  A motion to reconsider was laid on the table.

                          ____________________
```
