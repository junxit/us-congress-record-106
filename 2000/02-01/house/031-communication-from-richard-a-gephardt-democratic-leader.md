# COMMUNICATION FROM RICHARD A. GEPHARDT, DEMOCRATIC LEADER

```
       COMMUNICATION FROM RICHARD A. GEPHARDT, DEMOCRATIC LEADER

  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. LaHood) laid before the House the 
following communication from Richard A. Gephardt, Democratic Leader:

                                         House of Representatives,


                              Office of the Democratic Leader,

                                 Washington, DC, February 1, 2000.
     Hon. J. Dennis Hastert,
     Speaker of the House, House of Representatives, Washington, 
         DC.
       Dear Mr. Speaker: Pursuant to Section 602(b) of the 
     Intelligence Authorization Act for Fiscal Year 2000 (Public 
     Law 106-120), I hereby appoint the following member to the 
     National Commission for the Review of the National 
     Reconnaissance Office:
       Mr. Dicks, WA.
           Yours very truly,
     Richard A. Gephardt.
```
