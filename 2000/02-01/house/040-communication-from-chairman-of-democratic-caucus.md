# COMMUNICATION FROM CHAIRMAN OF DEMOCRATIC CAUCUS

```
            COMMUNICATION FROM CHAIRMAN OF DEMOCRATIC CAUCUS

  The Speaker pro tempore (Mr. Hastings of Washington) laid before the 
House the following communication from the Hon. Martin Frost, Chairman 
of the Democratic Caucus:

         Democratic Caucus, House of Representatives, Longworth 
           House Office Building, Washington, DC,
                                                 January 27, 2000.
     Hon. Dennis Hastert,
     Speaker, House of Representatives, The Capitol, Washington, 
         DC.
       Dear Mr. Speaker: This is to notify you that the Honorable 
     Virgil Goode of Virginia has resigned as a Member of the 
     Democratic Caucus.
           Sincerely,
                                                     Martin Frost,
     Chairman, Democratic Caucus.

                          ____________________
```
