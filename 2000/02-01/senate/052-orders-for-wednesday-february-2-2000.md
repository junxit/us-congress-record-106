# ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, FEBRUARY 2, 2000

**Speaking:** Murkowski, Frank H. (M001085) [R-AK]

**Measures:** S. 625

```
                 ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, FEBRUARY 2, 2000

  Mr. MURKOWSKI. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until the hour of 9:30 
a.m. on Wednesday, February 2. I further ask unanimous consent that on 
Wednesday, immediately following the prayer, the Journal of the 
proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed to have 
expired, the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later 
in the day, and the Senate then resume debate on S. 625, the bankruptcy 
reform bill, and Senator Schumer be recognized to call up his two 
remaining amendments.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
