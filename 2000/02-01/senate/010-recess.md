# RECESS

```
                                 RECESS

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, the hour of 12:30 
having arrived and passed, the Senate is in recess until the hour of 
2:15 p.m.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 12:46 p.m., recessed; whereupon, at 2:15 
p.m., the Senate reassembled when called to order by the Presiding 
Officer (Mr. Inhofe).
```
