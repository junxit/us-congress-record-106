# INTRODUCTION OF BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

**Measures:** S. 2018, S. 2019, S. 2020, S. 2021

```
              INTRODUCTION OF BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

  The following bills and joint resolutions were introduced, read the 
first time and second time by unanimous consent, and referred as 
indicated:

           By Mrs. HUTCHISON (for herself, Mr. Abraham, and Ms. 
             Snowe):
       S. 2018. A bill to amend title XVIII of the Social Security 
     Act to revise the update factor used in making payments to 
     PPS hospitals under the medicare program; to the Committee on 
     Finance.
           By Mr. KYL:
       S. 2019. A bill for the relief of Malia Miller; to the 
     Committee on the Judiciary.
           By Mr. COCHRAN (for himself and Mr. Lott):
       S. 2020. A bill to adjust the boundary of the Natchez Trace 
     Parkway, Mississippi, and for other purposes; to the 
     Committee on Energy and Natural Resources.
           By Mr. BROWNBACK (for himself, Mr. Leahy, Mr. Cochran, 
             Mr. Jeffords, Mr. Helms, Mr. Durbin, Mr. Lugar, Mr. 
             Edwards, Mr. Voinovich, Mr. McCain, and Mrs. 
             Feinstein):
       S. 2021. A bill to prohibit high school and college sports 
     gambling in all States including States where such gambling 
     was permitted prior to 1991; to the Committee on the 
     Judiciary.

                          ____________________
```
