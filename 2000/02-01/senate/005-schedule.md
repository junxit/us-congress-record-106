# SCHEDULE

**Speaking:** Hatch, Orrin G. (H000338) [R-UT]

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. HATCH. Mr. President, today the Senate will immediately resume 
consideration of the bankruptcy bill under the previous order. Senator 
Wellstone will be in control of the first hour to debate his amendments 
regarding lifeline accounts and debt collection. There are other 
remaining amendments that will be debated and voted on throughout 
today's session with a vote on final passage expected to occur no later 
than tomorrow.
  As a reminder, a cloture motion was filed on the motion to proceed to 
the nuclear waste disposal legislation during Monday's session, and by 
previous consent that vote will occur following completion of the 
bankruptcy bill during Wednesday's session of the Senate.
  I thank my colleagues for their attention.

                          ____________________
```
