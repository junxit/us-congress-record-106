# ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, JULY 13, 2000

```
                   ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, JULY 13, 2000

  Mr. WARNER. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until the hour of 8:30 
a.m. on Thursday, July 13.
  I further ask unanimous consent that on Thursday, immediately 
following the prayer, the Journal of proceedings be approved to date, 
the morning hour be deemed expired, the time for the two leaders be 
reserved for their use later in the day, and the Senate then resume 
H.R. 8, the Death Tax Elimination Act.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
