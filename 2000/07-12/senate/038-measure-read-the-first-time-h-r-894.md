# MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--H.R. 894

**Speaking:** Warner, John (W000154) [R-VA]

**Measures:** H.R. 894

```
                 MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--H.R. 894

  Mr. WARNER. Mr. President, I understand that H.R. 894 is at the desk. 
I ask for its first reading.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report the bill by title.
  The assistant legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (H.R. 894) to encourage States to incarcerate 
     individuals convicted of murder, rape, or child molestation.

  Mr. WARNER. Mr. President, I ask for its second reading, and object 
to my own request.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The bill will receive its second reading on 
the next legislative day.

                          ____________________
```
