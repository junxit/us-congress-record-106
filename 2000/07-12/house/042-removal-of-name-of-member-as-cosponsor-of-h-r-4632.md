# REMOVAL OF NAME OF MEMBER AS COSPONSOR OF H.R. 4632

**Speaking:** Souder, Mark E. (S001143) [R-IN]

**Measures:** H.R. 4632

```
          REMOVAL OF NAME OF MEMBER AS COSPONSOR OF H.R. 4632

  Mr. SOUDER. Mr. Speaker, my name was mistakenly added as an original 
cosponsor of H.R. 4632. I ask unanimous consent to withdraw my name as 
an original cosponsor of this bill.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Blunt). Is there objection to the 
request of the gentleman from Indiana?
  There was no objection.
```
