# RECESS

```
                                 RECESS

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, the Senate will 
stand in recess until the hour of 2:15 p.m.
  Thereupon, at 12:31 p.m., the Senate recessed until 2:15 p.m.; 
whereupon, the Senate reassembled when called to order by the Presiding 
Officer (Mr. Brownback).
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. In my capacity as a Senator from the State of 
Kansas, I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The clerk will call the roll.
  The assistant legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. CRAIG. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the order for 
the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
