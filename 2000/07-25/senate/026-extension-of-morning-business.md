# EXTENSION OF MORNING BUSINESS

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
                     EXTENSION OF MORNING BUSINESS

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that morning 
business be extended to the hour of 4 p.m. with the time equally 
divided between the majority and minority.
```
