# Text of House Amendment 17

**Speaking:** Moran, James P. (M000933) [D-VA]

**Measures:** H.R. 4942

```
H.R. 4942

                   Offered By: Mr. Moran of Virginia

       Amendment No. 17: Amend section 168 to read as follows:
       Sec. 168. None of the funds contained in this Act may be 
     used to carry out the Health Insurance Coverage for 
     Contraceptives Act of 2000 (D.C. Bill 13-399) unless such Act 
     includes a religious exemption adopted by the Council of the 
     District of Columbia and approved by the Mayor of the 
     District of Columbia.
```
