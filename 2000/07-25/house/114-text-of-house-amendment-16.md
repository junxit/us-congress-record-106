# Text of House Amendment 16

**Speaking:** Moran, James P. (M000933) [D-VA]

**Measures:** H.R. 4942

```
H.R. 4942

                   Offered By: Mr. Moran of Virginia

       Amendment No. 16: In section 168, strike ``the Health 
     Insurance Coverage'' and all that follows and insert the 
     following: ``none of the Federal funds appropriated under 
     this Act may be used to implement or enforce the Health 
     Insurance Coverage for Contraceptives Act of 2000 (D.C. Bill 
     13-399).''.
```
