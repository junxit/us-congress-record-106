# THANKING THE PRESIDENT PRO TEMPORE AND THE CHAPLAIN

```
          THANKING THE PRESIDENT PRO TEMPORE AND THE CHAPLAIN

  Mr. LOTT. We thank you for the job you do as the President pro 
tempore and the fact that you keep us on time. ``In time and on time,'' 
that is the motto for Strom Thurmond. We thank the Chaplain for his 
beautiful prayer as always.

                          ____________________
```
