# ORDER FOR ADJOURNMENT

**Speaking:** Grassley, Chuck (G000386) [R-IA], Durbin, Richard J. (D000563) [D-IL]

```
                         ORDER FOR ADJOURNMENT

  Mr. GRASSLEY. If there is no further business to come before the 
Senate, I now ask that the Senate stand in adjournment under the 
previous order, following the remarks of Senator Durbin.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. GRASSLEY. I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will call the roll.
  The legislative clerk called the roll.
  Mr. DURBIN. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that the order 
for the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
