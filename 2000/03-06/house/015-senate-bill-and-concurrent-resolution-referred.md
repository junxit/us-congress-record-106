# SENATE BILL AND CONCURRENT RESOLUTION REFERRED

**Measures:** S.Con.Res. 91, S. 1794

```
[[Page H645]]
             SENATE BILL AND CONCURRENT RESOLUTION REFERRED

  A bill and a concurrent resolution of the Senate of the following 
titles were taken from the Speaker's table and, under the rule, 
referred as follows:

       S. 1794. An act to designate the Federal courthouse at 145 
     East Simpson Avenue in Jackson, Wyoming, as the ``Clifford P. 
     Hansen Federal Courthouse''; to the Committee on 
     Transportation and Infrastructure.
       S. Con. Res. 91. Concurrent resolution congratulating the 
     Republic of Lithuania on the tenth anniversary of the 
     reestablishment of its independence from the rule of the 
     former Soviet Union; to the Committee on International 
     Relations.
```
