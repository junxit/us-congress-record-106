# ADJOURNMENT TO TUESDAY, FEBRUARY 29, 2000

**Speaking:** Oxley, Michael G. (O000163) [R-OH]

**Measures:** S. 80

```
               ADJOURNMENT TO TUESDAY, FEBRUARY 29, 2000

  Mr. OXLEY. Mr. Speaker, pursuant to Senate Concurrent Resolution 80, 
I move that the House do now adjourn.
  The motion was agreed to.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Ney). Pursuant to the provisions of 
Senate Concurrent Resolution 80 of the 106th Congress, the House stands 
adjourned until 12:30 p.m. on Tuesday, February 29, 2000, for morning 
hour debates.
  Thereupon (at 5 o'clock and 19 minutes p.m.), pursuant to Senate 
Concurrent Resolution 80, the House adjourned until Tuesday, February 
29, 2000, at 12:30 p.m. for morning hour debates.

                          ____________________
```
