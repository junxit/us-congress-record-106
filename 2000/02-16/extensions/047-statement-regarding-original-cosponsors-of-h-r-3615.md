# STATEMENT REGARDING ORIGINAL COSPONSORS OF H.R. 3615

**Speaking:** Goodlatte, Bob (G000289) [R-VA]

**Measures:** H.R. 3615

```
          STATEMENT REGARDING ORIGINAL COSPONSORS OF H.R. 3615

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. BOB GOODLATTE

                              of virginia

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, February 16, 2000

  Mr. GOODLATTE. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to state that Congressman 
Jerry Moran of Kansas was meant to be listed as an original cosponsor 
of important legislation, H.R. 3615, The Rural Local Broadcast Signal 
Act, which I introduced on February 10. I have added him as a cosponsor 
today.
```
