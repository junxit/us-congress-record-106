     Bureau of Reclamation, to convey to the Loup Basin 
     Reclamation District, the Sargent River Irrigation District,

     comprising the assets of the Middle Loup Division of the 
     Missouri River Basin Project, Nebraska; with an amendment

     on the State of the Union.
       Mr. YOUNG of Alaska: Committee on Resources. H.R. 1460. A 
     bill to amend the Ysleta del Sur Pueblo and Alabama and

     Committee of the Whole House on the State of the Union.
       Mr. YOUNG of Alaska: Committee on Resources. H.R. 1751. A

       Mr. YOUNG of Alaska: Committee on Resources. H.R. 2674. A

       Mr. YOUNG of Alaska: Committee on Resources. H.R. 3388. A

       Mr. LEACH: Committee on Banking and Financial Services.
