# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Gorton, Slade (G000333) [R-WA]

**Measures:** H.R. 4444

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. GORTON. Mr. President, at 10 a.m., the Senate will resume debate 
on the China trade bill. Amendments are expected to be offered and 
debated throughout the day. As previously announced, there will be no 
votes during tomorrow's session of the Senate. Therefore, any votes 
ordered with respect to the China PNTR bill will be scheduled to occur 
on Monday or Tuesday of next week. If significant progress can be made 
during tomorrow's session, votes may be delayed until Tuesday morning, 
September 12. Therefore, those Senators who have amendments to H.R. 
4444 are encouraged to come to the floor during Friday's session.

                          ____________________
```
