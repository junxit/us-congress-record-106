# ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, SEPTEMBER 8, 2000

```
                  ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, SEPTEMBER 8, 2000

  Mr. GORTON. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until the hour of 10 
a.m. on Friday, September 8. I further ask that on Friday, immediately 
following the prayer, the Journal of proceedings be approved to date, 
the morning hour be deemed to have expired, the time for the two 
leaders be reserved for their use later in the day, and the Senate then 
resume debate on H.R. 4444, the China PNTR legislation.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
