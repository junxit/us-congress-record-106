# WHITE HOUSE THROWING MONEY AROUND AT CAMP DAVID

```
            WHITE HOUSE THROWING MONEY AROUND AT CAMP DAVID

  (Mr. TRAFICANT asked and was given permission to address the House 
for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. TRAFICANT. Mr. Speaker, yesterday it was $40 billion, today they 
are talking $150 billion at Camp David. Unbelievable.
  While the White House continues to oppose a tax cut for married 
couples in America, the White House is literally throwing money around 
at Camp David like confetti, like cotton candy at a summer festival.
  Beam me up, Mr. Speaker, I believe this is a flawed and dangerous 
policy. The bottom line, a true, lasting and enduring peace will never 
be built on a foundation of dollars in the first place. I yield back 
the auction at Camp David.

                          ____________________
```
