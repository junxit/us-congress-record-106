# ADJOURNMENT

**Speaking:** McInnis, Scott (M000477) [R-CO]

**Measures:** H.Res. 558

```
                              ADJOURNMENT

  Mr. McINNIS. Mr. Speaker, pursuant to House Resolution 558, I move 
that the House do now adjourn in memory of the late Hon. Paul 
Coverdell.
  The motion was agreed to; accordingly (at 10 o'clock and 31 minutes 
p.m.), pursuant to House Resolution 558, the House adjourned until 
tomorrow, Thursday, July 20, 2000, at 10 a.m., in memory of the late 
Hon. Paul Coverdell of Georgia.

                          ____________________
```
