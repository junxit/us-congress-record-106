# WHO IS WORKING?

```
                            WHO IS WORKING?

  (Mr. BURTON of Indiana asked and was given permission to address the 
House for 1 minute.)
  Mr. BURTON of Indiana. Let me see if I have got this straight. The 
Democrats are complaining because we have not gotten our work done, but 
we are supposed to work out a compromise with the President and the 
minority. We are here working; we are here in Washington working.
  That is right here. The minority leader, the gentleman from Missouri 
(Mr. Gephardt), is over here in Missouri; and the President is out in 
Beverly Hills, California, campaigning. Who do you think is trying to 
get their work done?

                          ____________________
```
