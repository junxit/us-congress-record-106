# RECESS UNTIL TUESDAY, NOVEMBER 14, 2000

**Speaking:** Murkowski, Frank H. (M001085) [R-AK]

**Measures:** S.Con.Res. 160

```
                RECESS UNTIL TUESDAY, NOVEMBER 14, 2000

  Mr. MURKOWSKI. Mr. President, seeing no other Members here and no one 
seeking recognition, if there is no further business to come before the 
Senate, I now ask unanimous consent that the Senate stand in recess 
under the provisions of S. Con. Res. 160.
  There being no objection, the Senate, at 8:37 p.m., recessed until 
Tuesday, November 14, 2000, at 12 noon.
```
