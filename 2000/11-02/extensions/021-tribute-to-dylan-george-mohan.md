# TRIBUTE TO DYLAN GEORGE MOHAN

**Speaking:** Talent, Jim (T000024) [R-MO]

```
                     TRIBUTE TO DYLAN GEORGE MOHAN

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JAMES M. TALENT

                              of missouri

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, November 2, 2000

  Mr. TALENT. Mr. Speaker, today I honor the May 16, 2000, birth of 
Dylan George Mohan. Dylan was born at Sibley Memorial Hospital in 
Washington, DC, at 8:56 p.m. He is the son of Kristin Young and Matthew 
Mohan. Dylan is the first grandson of his grandparent George and 
Phyllis Young and grandparents Jim and Mary Mohan.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask you to join me in congratulating this new family 
and to wish Dylan much joy and happiness in the years to come.

                          ____________________
```
