# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Thompson, Fred (T000457) [R-TN]

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. THOMPSON. For the information of all Senators, at 9:30 a.m. 
tomorrow the Senate will be in a period of morning business until 10 
a.m. Following morning business, there will be 60 minutes for closing 
remarks on two amendments: The Byrd amendment, regarding safeguards; 
and division 6 of the Smith amendment, No. 4129. Votes on those two 
amendments will be back to back at 11 a.m.
  Senators should be aware that there are amendments currently pending 
to the PNTR bill and further amendments are expected to be offered. 
Therefore, votes are expected throughout the remainder of the week.
```
