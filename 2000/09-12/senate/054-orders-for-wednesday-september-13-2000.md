# ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, SEPTEMBER 13, 2000

**Speaking:** Thompson, Fred (T000457) [R-TN]

```
                ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, SEPTEMBER 13, 2000

  Mr. THOMPSON. I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate completes 
its business today, it adjourn until the hour of 9:30 a.m. on 
Wednesday, September 13. I further ask consent that on Wednesday, 
immediately following the prayer, the Journal of proceedings be 
approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, the time for the 
two leaders be reserved for their use later in the day, and the Senate 
then begin a period of morning business until 10 a.m., with the time 
equally divided between Senator Thomas and Senator Durbin.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
