# ADJOURNMENT

```
                              ADJOURNMENT

  Mr. METCALF. Mr. Speaker, pursuant to House Resolution 573, I move 
that the House do now adjourn in the memory of the late Honorable 
Herbert H. Bateman.
  The motion was agreed to; accordingly (at 11 o'clock and 37 minutes 
p.m.) pursuant to House Resolution 573, the House adjourned until 
tomorrow, Wednesday, September 13, 2000, at 10 a.m. in memory of the 
late Honorable Herbert H. Bateman of Virginia.
```
