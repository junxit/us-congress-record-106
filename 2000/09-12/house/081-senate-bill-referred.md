# SENATE BILL REFERRED

**Measures:** S. 2386

```
                          SENATE BILL REFERRED

  A bill of the senate of the following title was taken from the 
Speaker's table and, under the rule, referred as follows:

       S. 2386. an act to authorize the United States Postal 
     Service to issue semipostals, and for other purposes; to the 
     Committee on Government Reform, in addition to the Committee 
     on Rules for a period to be subsequently determined by the 
     Speaker, in each case for consideration of such provisions as 
     fall within the jurisdiction of the committee concerned.
```
