# REPORT ON RESOLUTION WAIVING POINTS OF ORDER AGAINST CONFERENCE REPORT ON H.R. 1654, NATIONAL AERONAUTICS AND SPACE ADMINISTRATION AUTHORIZATION ACT OF 2000

```
REPORT ON RESOLUTION WAIVING POINTS OF ORDER AGAINST CONFERENCE REPORT 
      ON H.R. 1654, NATIONAL AERONAUTICS AND SPACE ADMINISTRATION 
                       AUTHORIZATION ACT OF 2000

  Mr. REYNOLDS (during debate on H. Con. Res. 368) from the Committee 
on Rules, submitted a privileged report (Rept. No. 106-844) on the 
resolution (H. Res. 574) waiving points of order against the conference 
report to accompany the bill (H.R. 1654) to authorize appropriations 
for the National Aeronautics and Space Administration for fiscal years 
2000, 2001, and 2002, and for other purposes, which was referred to the 
House Calendar and ordered to be printed.
```
