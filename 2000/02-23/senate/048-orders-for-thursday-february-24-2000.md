# ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, FEBRUARY 24, 2000

**Speaking:** Coverdell, Paul (C000813) [R-GA]

```
                 ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, FEBRUARY 24, 2000

  Mr. COVERDELL. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until the hour of 11:30 
a.m. on Thursday, February 24. I further ask unanimous consent that on 
Thursday, immediately following the prayer, the Journal of proceedings 
be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, the time for 
the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the day, and the 
Senate then proceed to a vote on the Iran nonproliferation bill as 
under the previous order.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
