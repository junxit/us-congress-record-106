# MORNING BUSINESS

**Speaking:** Dorgan, Byron L. (D000432) [D-ND]

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, there will now be a 
period for the transaction of morning business not to extend beyond the 
hour of 12:30 p.m. The time until 10:45 a.m. shall be under the control 
of the Senator from Illinois, Mr. Durbin, or his designee.
  The Senator from North Dakota is recognized.
  Mr. DORGAN. Mr. President, I yield myself such time of Senator 
Durbin's as I might use.

                          ____________________
```
