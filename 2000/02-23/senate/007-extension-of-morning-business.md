# EXTENSION OF MORNING BUSINESS

**Speaking:** Crapo, Mike (C000880) [R-ID]

```
                     EXTENSION OF MORNING BUSINESS

  Mr. CRAPO. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the period of 
morning business be extended until the hour of 12:30 p.m. and between 
11:30 and 12:30 Senators be limited to 10 minutes each.
  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
