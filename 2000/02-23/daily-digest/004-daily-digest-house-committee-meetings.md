Joint Meetings
CYBER-THREATS AND THE U.S. ECONOMY
Joint Economic Committee: Committee concluded hearings to examine the 
threat of cyber-terrorism and hackers to both the United States high-
tech infrastructure and private sector, after receiving testimony from 
Vinton G. Cerf, MCI WorldCom, Ashburn, Virginia, on behalf of the 
Information Technology Association of America; Roger C. Molander, RAND, 
and Daniel T. Kuehl, National Defense University School of Information 
Warfare and Strategy, both of Washington, D.C.; Stephen E. Cross, 
Carnegie Mellon University Software Engineering Institute, Pittsburgh, 
Pennsylvania; Fred Cohen, Sandia National Laboratories, Livermore, 
California; and Mark Graff, Sun Microsystems, Palo Alto, California.
