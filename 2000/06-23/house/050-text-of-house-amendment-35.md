# Text of House Amendment 35

**Speaking:** Aderholt, Robert B. (A000055) [R-AL]

**Measures:** H.R. 4461

```
H.R. 4461

                        Offered By: Mr. Aderholt

       Amendment No. 35: Page 91, line 11, strike ``or''.
       Page 91, line 25, strike the period and insert ``; or''.
       Page 91, after line 25, insert the following:
       (3) against a foreign country or foreign entity that--
       (A) refuses to allow nonprofit organizations to distribute 
     free food or medicine; or
       (B) refuses to allow members of such organizations to 
     travel to any destination within the country to oversee the 
     distribution of such food or medicine.
```
