# MERVYN MALCOLM DYMALLY POST OFFICE BUILDING

```
              MERVYN MALCOLM DYMALLY POST OFFICE BUILDING

  The bill (H.R. 642) to redesignate the Federal building located at 
701 South Santa Fe Avenue in Compton, California, and known as the 
Compton Main Post Office, as the ``Mervyn Malcolm Dymally Post Office 
Building'' was considered, read a third time, and passed.
```
