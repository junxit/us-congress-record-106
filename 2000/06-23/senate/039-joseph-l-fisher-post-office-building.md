# JOSEPH L. FISHER POST OFFICE BUILDING

```
                 JOSEPH L. FISHER POST OFFICE BUILDING

  The bill (H.R. 3701) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 3118 Washington Boulevard in Arlington, 
Virginia, as the ``Joseph L. Fisher Post Office Building'' was 
considered, read a third time, and passed.
```
