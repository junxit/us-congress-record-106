# THOMAS J. BROWN POST OFFICE BUILDING

**Measures:** H.R. 2307

```
                  THOMAS J. BROWN POST OFFICE BUILDING

  The bill (H.R. 2307) to designate the building of the United States 
Postal Service located at 5 Cedar Street in Hopkinton, Massachusetts, 
as the ``Thomas J. Brown Post Office Building'' was considered, read a 
third time, and passed.
```
