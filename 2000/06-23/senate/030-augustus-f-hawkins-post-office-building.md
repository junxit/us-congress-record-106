# AUGUSTUS F. HAWKINS POST OFFICE BUILDING

```
                AUGUSTUS F. HAWKINS POST OFFICE BUILDING

  The bill (H.R. 643) to redesignate the Federal building located at 
10301 South Compton Avenue, in Los Angeles, California, and known as 
the Watts Finance Office, as the ``Augustus F. Hawkins Post Office 
Building'' was considered, read a third time, and passed.
```
