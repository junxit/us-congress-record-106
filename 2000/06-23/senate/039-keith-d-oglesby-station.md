# KEITH D. OGLESBY STATION

**Measures:** H.R. 2952

```
                        KEITH D. OGLESBY STATION

  The bill (H.R. 2952) to redesignate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 100 Orchard Park Drive in Greenville, South 
Carolina, as the ``Keith D. Oglesby Station'' was considered, read a
```
