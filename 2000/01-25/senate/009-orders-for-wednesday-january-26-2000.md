# ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, JANUARY 26, 2000

```
                 ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, JANUARY 26, 2000

  Mr. LOTT. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate 
completes its business today, it adjourn until the hour of 11 a.m. on 
Wednesday, January 26. I further ask consent that on Wednesday, 
immediately following the prayer, the Journal of proceedings be 
approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, and the time for 
the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the day, and the 
Senate then resume debate on S. 625, the bankruptcy reform bill, as 
under the previous agreement.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Without objection, it is so 
ordered.

                          ____________________
```
