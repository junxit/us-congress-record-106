# EXPLANATION OF ABSENCE

```
                         EXPLANATION OF ABSENCE

  Mr. HUTCHINSON. Mr. President, I ask that the Record reflect the 
purpose of my absence during final passage of H.R. 8, the Death Tax 
Elimination Act. I departed Washington this morning to attend the 
wedding of my youngest son, Joshua. I would add that my absence would 
not have changed the outcome of this vote. If I had been present, 
however, I would have voted ``aye.''

                          ____________________
```
