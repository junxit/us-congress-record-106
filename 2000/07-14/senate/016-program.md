# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Roth Jr., William V. (R000460) [R-DE]

```
                                PROGRAM

Interior appropriations bill under the previous consent, with several 
amendments to be offered and debated throughout the day. However, any 
votes ordered with respect to the Interior bill will occur at 9:45 a.m. 
on Tuesday, July 18. As a reminder, there will be votes on the
```
