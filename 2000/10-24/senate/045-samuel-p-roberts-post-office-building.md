# SAMUEL P. ROBERTS POST OFFICE BUILDING

```
[[Page 23907]]

                 SAMUEL P. ROBERTS POST OFFICE BUILDING

  The bill (H.R. 4786) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 110 Postal Way in Carrollton, Georgia, as the 
``Samuel P. Roberts Post Office Building'', which had been discharged 
from the Committee on Governmental Affairs, was considered, ordered to 
a third reading, read the third time, and passed.
```
