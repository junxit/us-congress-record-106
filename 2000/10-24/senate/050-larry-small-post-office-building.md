# LARRY SMALL POST OFFICE BUILDING

**Measures:** H.R. 4315

```
                    LARRY SMALL POST OFFICE BUILDING

  The bill (H.R. 4315) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 3695 Green Road in Beachwood, Ohio, as the 
``Larry Small Post Office Building'', which had been discharged from 
the Committee on Governmental Affairs, was considered, ordered to a 
third reading, read the third time, and passed.
```
