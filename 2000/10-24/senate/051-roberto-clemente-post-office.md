# ROBERTO CLEMENTE POST OFFICE

**Measures:** H.R. 4831

```
                      ROBERTO CLEMENTE POST OFFICE

  The bill (H.R. 4831) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 2339 North California Avenue in Chicago, 
Illinois, as the ``Roberto Clemente Post Office'', which had been 
discharged from the Committee on Governmental Affairs, was considered, 
ordered to a third reading, read the third time, and passed.
```
