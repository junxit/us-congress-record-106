# FREDERICK L. DEWBERRY, JR. POST OFFICE BUILDING

**Measures:** H.R. 4451

```
            FREDERICK L. DEWBERRY, JR. POST OFFICE BUILDING

  The bill (H.R. 4451) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 1001 Frederick Road in Baltimore, Maryland, 
as the ``Frederick L. Dewberry, Jr. Post Office Building'', which had 
been discharged from the Committee on Governmental Affairs, was 
considered, ordered to a third reading, read the third time, and 
passed.
```
