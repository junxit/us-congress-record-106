# PROGRAM

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. SMITH of New Hampshire. Mr. President, for the information of all 
Senators, the Senate will be in a period of morning business on 
Wednesday until 12:30 p.m. Following morning business, the Senate will 
recess until 2:15 p.m. for the weekly party conferences.
  The House is expected to consider the foreign operations conference 
report during tomorrow morning's session, and it is hoped that the 
Senate can begin consideration of that conference report upon 
reconvening at 2:15 p.m.
  The Senate is also expected to have the final votes on S. 2508, the 
Colorado Ute Settlement Act Amendments of 2000, as well as a vote on 
the continuing resolution.
  Therefore, Senators can expect votes during tomorrow afternoon's 
session.

                          ____________________
```
