# RUTH HARRIS COLEMAN POST OFFICE BUILDING

```
                RUTH HARRIS COLEMAN POST OFFICE BUILDING

  The bill (H.R. 5229) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 219 South Church Street in Odum, Georgia, as 
the ``Ruth Harris Coleman Post Office Building'', which had been 
discharged from the Committee on Governmental Affairs, was considered, 
ordered to a third reading, read the third time, and passed.
```
