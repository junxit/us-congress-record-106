# ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

```
                ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to clause 8 of rule XX, the Chair 
announces that he will postpone further proceedings today on the 
remaining motions to suspend the rules on which a recorded vote or the 
yeas and nays are ordered, or on which the vote is objected to under 
clause 6 of rule XX.
```
