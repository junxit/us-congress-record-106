# REPORT ON RESOLUTION WAIVING POINTS OF ORDER AGAINST CONFERENCE REPORT ON S. 835, ESTUARIES AND CLEAN WATERS ACT OF 2000

```
REPORT ON RESOLUTION WAIVING POINTS OF ORDER AGAINST CONFERENCE REPORT 
           ON S. 835, ESTUARIES AND CLEAN WATERS ACT OF 2000

  Mr. LINDER (during the special order of Mr. Blumenauer), from the 
Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged report (Rept. No. 106-1000) 
on the resolution (H. Res. 648) waiving points of order against the 
conference report to accompany the Senate bill (S. 835) to encourage 
the restoration of estuary habitat through more efficient project 
financing and enhanced coordination of Federal and non-Federal 
restoration programs, and for other purposes,


which was referred to the House Calendar and ordered to be printed.
```
