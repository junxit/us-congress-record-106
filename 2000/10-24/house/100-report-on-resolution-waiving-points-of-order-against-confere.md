# REPORT ON RESOLUTION WAIVING POINTS OF ORDER AGAINST CONFERENCE REPORT ON H.R. 4811, FOREIGN OPERATIONS, EXPORT FINANCING, AND RELATED PROGRAMS APPROPRIATIONS ACT, 2001

```
REPORT ON RESOLUTION WAIVING POINTS OF ORDER AGAINST CONFERENCE REPORT 
    ON H.R. 4811, FOREIGN OPERATIONS, EXPORT FINANCING, AND RELATED 
                   PROGRAMS APPROPRIATIONS ACT, 2001

  Mr. LINDER (during the special order of Mr. Blumenauer), from the 
Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged report (Rept. No. 106-999) 
on the resolution (H. Res. 647) waiving points of order against the 
conference report to accompany the bill (H.R. 4811) making 
appropriations for foreign operations, export financing, and related 
programs for the fiscal year ending September 30, 2001, and for other 
purposes, which was referred to the House Calendar and ordered to be 
printed.
```
