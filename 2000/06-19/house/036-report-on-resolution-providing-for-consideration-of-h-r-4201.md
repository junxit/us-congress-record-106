# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 4201, NONCOMMERCIAL BROADCASTING FREEDOM OF EXPRESSION ACT OF 2000

**Measures:** H.Res. 527, H.R. 4201

```
    REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 4201, 
      NONCOMMERCIAL BROADCASTING FREEDOM OF EXPRESSION ACT OF 2000

  Mr. LINDER, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 106-681) on the resolution (H. Res. 527) providing 
for consideration of the bill (H.R. 4201) to amend the Communications 
Act of 1934 to clarify the service obligations of noncommercial 
educational broadcast stations, which was referred to the House 
Calendar and ordered to be printed.
```
