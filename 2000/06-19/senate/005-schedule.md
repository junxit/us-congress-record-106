# SCHEDULE

**Speaking:** Kyl, Jon (K000352) [R-AZ]

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. KYL. Mr. President, today the Senate will be in a period of 
morning business until 3 p.m. with Senators Durbin and Thomas in 
control of the time.
  Following morning business, the Senate will resume consideration of 
the Department of Defense authorization bill. By previous consent, at 3 
p.m. Senators Hatch and Kennedy will be recognized to offer their 
amendments regarding hate crimes. Those amendments will be debated 
simultaneously during today's session.
  When the Senate convenes on Tuesday, Senator Dodd will offer his 
amendment to the Defense authorization bill regarding a Cuba 
commission.
  Those votes, along with the vote on the Murray amendment regarding 
abortions, are scheduled to occur in a stacked series on Tuesday at 
3:15 p.m.
  I thank my colleagues for their attention.

                          ____________________
```
