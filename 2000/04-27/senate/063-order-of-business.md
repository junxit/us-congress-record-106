# ORDER OF BUSINESS

**Speaking:** Sessions, Jeff (S001141) [R-AL]

**Measures:** S. 1608

                           ORDER OF BUSINESS

announce that it will be the majority leader's intention to turn to S. 
1608, the Craig-Wyden timber bill, at 10:30 a.m. on Monday. It is the 
leader's hope that the bill can be concluded in a couple of hours on 
Monday. However, no votes will occur during Monday's session. Any votes
