**Speaking:** Sessions, Jeff (S001141) [R-AL], Feingold, Russell D. (F000061) [D-WI]

```
                         ORDER FOR ADJOURNMMENT

  Mr. SESSIONS. Mr. President, if there is no further business to come 
before the Senate, I ask that the Senate stand in adjournment under the 
previous order following the remarks of the following Members: Senators

will speak for 5 minutes, Senator Feinstein will have 15 minutes, and
```
