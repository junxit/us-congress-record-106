# Daily Digest/House of Representatives

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 215, H.Con.Res. 235

```
                        House of Representatives


Chamber Action
Speaker Pro Tempore: Read a letter from the Speaker wherein he 
designated Representative Upton to act as Speaker pro tempore for 
today.
  Page H1
Convening of the Second Session of the 106th Congress: The Chair 
announced that, pursuant to H. Con. Res. 215, 106th Congress, the House 
shall conduct no organizational or legislative business on this day. 
The House will follow the practice announced by the Speaker at the 
convening of the second Session of the 102nd Congress under a similar 
order. Bills and resolutions introduced today will be numbered today 
but will not be noted in the record or referred by the Speaker until 
January 27, 2000. Executive communications, petitions, and memorials 
will not be numbered or referred until January 27, 2000.
  Page H1
Adjournment: The House met at 12:00 noon and pursuant to H. Con. Res. 
235, 106th Congress, adjourned at 12:05 p.m. until noon on Thursday, 
January 27, 2000.
```
