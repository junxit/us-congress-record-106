# SENATE RESOLUTION 242--NOTIFYING THE PRESIDENT THAT THE SENATE IS READY TO PROCEED TO BUSINESS

```
SENATE RESOLUTION 242--NOTIFYING THE PRESIDENT THAT THE SENATE IS READY 
                         TO PROCEED TO BUSINESS

  Mr. LOTT submitted the following resolution; which was considered and 
agreed to:

                              S. Res. 242

       Resolved, That the Secretary of the Senate inform the 
     President of the United States that a quorum of the Senate is 
     assembled and that the Senate is ready to proceed to 
     business.

                          ____________________
```
