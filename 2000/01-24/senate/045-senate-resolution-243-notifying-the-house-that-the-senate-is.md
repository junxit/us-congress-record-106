# SENATE RESOLUTION 243--NOTIFYING THE HOUSE THAT THE SENATE IS READY TO PROCEED TO BUSINESS

```
SENATE RESOLUTION 243--NOTIFYING THE HOUSE THAT THE SENATE IS READY TO 
                          PROCEED TO BUSINESS

  Mr. LOTT submitted the following resolution; which was considered and 
agreed to:

                              S. Res. 243

       Resolved, That the Secretary inform the House of 
     Representatives that a quorum of the Senate is assembled and 
     that the Senate is ready to proceed to business.
```
