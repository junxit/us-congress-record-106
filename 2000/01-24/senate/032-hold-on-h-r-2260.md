# HOLD ON H.R. 2260

```
                           HOLD ON H.R. 2260

  Mr. WYDEN. Mr. President, today I have notified the minority leader 
that I have placed a hold on H.R. 2260, the Pain Relief Promotion Act 
of 1999. This legislation would negate Oregon's physician assisted 
suicide law which was debated and passed twice by the voters of Oregon.

                          ____________________
```
