# UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--S. 376

```
                  UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--S. 376

  Mr. GRAMS. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
withdraw its request of November 19, 1999, for a conference on S. 376, 
and agree to the conference, with the same conferees previously 
appointed by the Senate, requested by the House of Representatives on 
November 10, 1999, which message was transmitted to the Senate on 
January 24, 2000.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
