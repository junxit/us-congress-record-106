# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, there will now be a 
period for the transaction of morning business, not to extend beyond 
the hour of 10:15 a.m., with the time to be equally divided between the 
Senator from Delaware and the Senator from New York.
  Who yields time?
  Mr. REID. On behalf of the Senator from New York, I yield 10 minutes 
to the Senator from North Dakota.
```
