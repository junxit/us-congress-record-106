# Text of House Amendment 22

**Speaking:** Tancredo, Thomas G. (T000458) [R-CO]

**Measures:** H.R. 4811

```
H.R. 4811

                        Offered By: Mr. Tancredo

       Amendment No. 22: At the end of the bill, insert after the 
     last section (preceding the short title) the following new 
     title:
                    TITLE VII--LIMITATION PROVISIONS
       Sec. ____. None of the funds appropriated in this Act may 
     be be made available for the United Nations Man and the 
     Biosphere Program or the United Nations World Heritage Fund.
```
