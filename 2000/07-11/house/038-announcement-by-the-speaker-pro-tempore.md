# ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

```
                ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

on the remaining motions to suspend the rules on which a recorded vote

under clause 6 of rule XX.
```
