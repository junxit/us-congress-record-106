# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Taylor, Gene (T000074) [D-MS]

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. GENE TAYLOR

                             of mississippi

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, July 11, 2000

  Mr. TAYLOR of Mississippi. Mr. Speaker, on the evening of Monday, 
July 10th, I was unavoidably detained because of inclement weather in

have voted: ``yea'' on rollcall No. 373, ``yea'' on rollcall No. 374, 
``yea'' on rollcall No. 375, ``yea'' on rollcall No. 376, ``yea'' on 
rollcall No. 377, and ``nay'' on rollcall No. 378.
