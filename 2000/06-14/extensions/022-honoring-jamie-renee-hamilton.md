# HONORING JAMIE RENEE HAMILTON

**Speaking:** Wilson, Heather (W000789) [R-NM]

```
                     HONORING JAMIE RENEE HAMILTON

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. HEATHER WILSON

                             of new mexico

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, June 14, 2000

  Mrs. WILSON. Mr. Speaker, today I would like to bring to your 
attention Jamie Renee Hamilton, an eighth grader at Madison Middle 
School in Albuquerque, New Mexico. Jamie Renee designed a poster for 
our local Campus Crime Stoppers. She is helping to stop crime in our 
schools. I have the Campus Crime Stoppers poster hanging in my 
Albuquerque office.
  So often, the power of young people to change our world is 
overlooked. Jamie Renee stood up to make a change for the better in 
schools and our community.
  Mr. Speaker, the Congress is working hard on school safety. Jamie 
Renee Hamilton is working hard in my home of Albuquerque on this very 
issue also. Please join me in honoring the contributions by Jamie Renee 
Hamilton to safety in our schools and in our community.

                          ____________________
```
