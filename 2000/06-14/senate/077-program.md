# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Allard, Wayne (A000109) [R-CO]

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. ALLARD. Mr. President, for the information of all Senators, the 
Senate will convene at 9:45 a.m. tomorrow and will resume debate of the 
Transportation appropriations legislation. Under the order, Senator 
Voinovich will be recognized immediately to offer his amendment 
regarding passenger rail flexibility. A vote on the amendment is 
expected to occur tomorrow morning at a time to be determined. Further 
amendments will be offered and voted on during tomorrow morning's 
session with the hope of final passage early in the day. As usual, 
Senators will be notified as votes are scheduled.

                          ____________________
```
