# ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, JUNE 15, 2000

```
                   ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, JUNE 15, 2000

  Mr. ALLARD. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it stand in adjournment until 9:45 
a.m. on Thursday, June 15. I further ask unanimous consent that on 
Thursday, immediately following the prayer, the Journal of proceedings 
be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, the time for 
the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the day, and the 
Senate then resume consideration of H.R. 4475, the Department of 
Transportation appropriations bill.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
