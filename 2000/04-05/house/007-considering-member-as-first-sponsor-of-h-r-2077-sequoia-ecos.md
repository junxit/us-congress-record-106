# CONSIDERING MEMBER AS FIRST SPONSOR OF H.R. 2077, SEQUOIA ECOSYSTEM AND RECREATION PRESERVE ACT OF 1999

**Speaking:** Miller, George (M000725) [D-CA]

**Measures:** H.R. 2077

```
CONSIDERING MEMBER AS FIRST SPONSOR OF H.R. 2077, SEQUOIA ECOSYSTEM AND 
                    RECREATION PRESERVE ACT OF 1999

  Mr. GEORGE MILLER of California. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent 
that I may hereafter be considered as the first sponsor of H.R. 2077, a 
bill originally introduced by Representative Brown of California, for 
the purposes of adding cosponsors and requesting reprints pursuant to 
clause 7 of rule XII.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from California?
  There was no objection.
```
