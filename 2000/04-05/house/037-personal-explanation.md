# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

  Mr. WEINER. Mr. Speaker, on March 30 the President and I made a 
Social Security policy announcement with senior citizens in my 
district. As a result, I was unable to vote in favor of the Emergency 
Supplemental Appropriations bill for fiscal year 2000. Had I been 
present, I would have voted as follows:
  Rollcall 91, the Stearns amendment, no; on rollcall 92, the Paul 
amendment, no; on rollcall 93, the Tancredo amendment, no; on rollcall 
94, on the Motion to Recommit, yes; and on rollcall 95, final passage, 
yes.
```
