# ONLY IN AMERICA, ONLY IN AMERICA

```
                    ONLY IN AMERICA, ONLY IN AMERICA

  (Mr. TRAFICANT asked and was given permission to address the House 
for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. TRAFICANT. Mr. Speaker, today Congress will debate two bills. The 
first bill is partial birth abortions. The second bill is wildlife and 
sport fish restoration.
  Unbelievable. Kill the babies but save the trout and the tit mouse. 
Beam me up. In fact, beam me up, Scotty.
  See, I believe that Congress and America can and should save both the 
babies and the wildlife. Think about it.
  I yield back an old street saying: Only in America, Mr. Speaker.




                          ____________________
```
