# CONSIDERING MEMBER AS FIRST SPONSOR OF H.R. 2077, SEQUOIA ECOSYSTEM AND RECREATION PRESERVE ACT OF 1999

```
CONSIDERING MEMBER AS FIRST SPONSOR OF H.R. 2077, SEQUOIA ECOSYSTEM AND 
                    RECREATION PRESERVE ACT OF 1999
```
