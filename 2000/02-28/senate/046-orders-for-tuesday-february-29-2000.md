# ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, FEBRUARY 29, 2000

**Speaking:** Coverdell, Paul (C000813) [R-GA]

**Measures:** S. 1134

```
                 ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, FEBRUARY 29, 2000

  Mr. COVERDELL. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until the hour of 9:30 
a.m. on Tuesday, February 29. I further ask unanimous consent that on 
Tuesday, immediately following the prayer, the Journal of proceedings 
be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, the time for 
the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the day, and the 
Senate then resume debate on S. 1134, the education savings account 
bill.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. COVERDELL. I further ask unanimous consent that the Senate stand 
in recess from the hours of 12:30 p.m. to 2:15 p.m. for the weekly 
policy conferences to meet.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
