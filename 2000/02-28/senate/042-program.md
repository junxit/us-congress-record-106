# PROGRAM

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. COVERDELL. Madam President, for the information of all Senators, 
tomorrow the Senate will resume consideration of the education savings 
account legislation. It is expected that a special education amendment 
may be offered tomorrow morning. Other amendments are expected to be 
offered and debated during tomorrow's session, with votes occurring 
throughout the day. Due to the pending agreement, the cloture vote for 
tomorrow has been vitiated. It is hoped that the education savings 
account bill can be completed by midweek, and therefore Senators are 
encouraged to work with the bill managers to offer their amendments in 
a timely manner.
```
