# TRIBUTE TO CHARLES MEIER

**Speaking:** Shimkus, John (S000364) [R-IL]

```
                        TRIBUTE TO CHARLES MEIER

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. JOHN SHIMKUS

                              of illinois

                    in the house of representatives

                         Thursday, June 8, 2000

  Mr. SHIMKUS. Mr. Speaker, I rise before you today to recognize 
Charles Meier of Okawville, IL. Charles was recently inducted into the 
Illinois Jaycees Recruiters Hall of Fame.
  He was inducted for his successful efforts to recruit many new 
members into the club during his 21 years as a Jaycee. His recruitment 
efforts have brought in new members from an area that extends from 
Steeleville and Waterloo to Interstate 64.
  I want to congratulate Charles on receiving such a prestigious honor. 
I wish him the best as he continues to serve.

                          ____________________
```
