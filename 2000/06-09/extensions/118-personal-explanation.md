# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Istook, Ernest J., Jr. (I000047) [R-OK]

**Measures:** H.R. 8

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                       HON. ERNEST J. ISTOOK, JR.

                              of oklahoma

                    in the house of representatives

                          Friday, June 9, 2000

  Mr. ISTOOK. Mr. Speaker, due to a family medical emergency, I was 
unable to vote on H.R. 8, the Death Tax Elimination Act of 2000. Had I 
been in Washington, I would have voted yes. I regret that I was not 
able to vote on this very important bill to help reduce the enormous 
tax burden on the American public.
  I was also unable to vote on the amendment to remove the prohibition 
on the Occupational Safety and Health Administration's (OSHA) proposed 
ergonomics regulations. I would have voted to keep the prohibition.
```
