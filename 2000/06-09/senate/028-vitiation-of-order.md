# VITIATION OF ORDER

```
                           VITIATION OF ORDER

  Mr. STEVENS. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the previous 
order with respect to rule XVI regarding the Defense appropriations 
bill be vitiated.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. STEVENS. Mr. President, in addition, any votes regarding those 
amendments will be scheduled to occur on Tuesday morning, June 13. As a 
reminder, Senators should inform the bill managers, Senator Inouye and 
myself, if they have amendments to the Defense appropriations bill. It 
is my hope we will have an announcement on Monday that any amendments 
that are stacked on Tuesday will commence very early in the day.

                          ____________________
```
