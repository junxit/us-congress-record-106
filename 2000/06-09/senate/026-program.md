# PROGRAM

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. STEVENS. For the information of all Senators, the Senate will 
convene at 12 noon on Monday and be in a period of morning business 
until 2 p.m. Following morning business, the Senate will resume 
consideration of the Defense appropriations bill. Amendments will be 
offered, and it is expected the two managers will agree to exchange a 
list of amendments at 2 p.m. Monday.

                          ____________________
```
