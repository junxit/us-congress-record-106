# REAPPOINTMENT AS MEMBER TO FEDERAL JUDICIAL CENTER FOUNDATION

```
     REAPPOINTMENT AS MEMBER TO FEDERAL JUDICIAL CENTER FOUNDATION

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Without objection, pursuant to 28 U.S.C. 
629(b) and upon the recommendation of the Minority Leader, the Chair 
announces the Speaker's reappointment of the following member on the 
part of the House to the Federal Judicial Center Foundation for a 5-
year term:
  Mr. Benjamin Zelenko of Maryland.
  There was no objection.
```
