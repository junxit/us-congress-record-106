# WILLIAM S. BROOMFIELD POST OFFICE BUILDING

```
               WILLIAM S. BROOMFIELD POST OFFICE BUILDING

  A bill (H.R. 4884) to designate the building located at 200 West 2nd 
Street in Royal Oak, Michigan as the ``William S. Broomfield Post 
Office Building'' was considered, ordered to a third reading, read the 
third time, and passed.
```
