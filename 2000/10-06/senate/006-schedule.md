# SCHEDULE

**Speaking:** Grassley, Chuck (G000386) [R-IA]

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. GRASSLEY. Mr. President, I say on behalf of the leader, the 
Senate will be in a period for morning business until 10 a.m. Following 
morning business the Senate is expected to begin consideration of the 
conference report to accompany the Transportation appropriations bill 
or the sex trafficking conference report. The House is expected to 
consider the Transportation appropriations legislation this morning. 
Therefore, it is hoped that a vote can occur prior to noon today. 
Senators will be notified as soon as votes are scheduled. The leader 
thanks our colleagues for their attention.

                          ____________________
```
