                         REPORTS OF COMMITTEES

  The following reports of committees were submitted:

        S. 1688: A bill to amend chapter 89 of title 5, United 
     States Code, relating to the Federal Employees Health 
     Benefits Program, to enable the Federal Government to enroll

     purposes (Rept. No. 106-492).

     responsibilities of court-appointed receivers who administer 
     departments, offices, and agencies of the District of
