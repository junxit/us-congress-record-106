# DR. FLOSSIE McCLAIN DEDMOND POST OFFICE BUILDING

```
            DR. FLOSSIE McCLAIN DEDMOND POST OFFICE BUILDING

Ellamont Street in Baltimore, Maryland as the ``Dr. Flossie McClain 
Dedmond Post Office Building'' was considered, ordered to a third
```
