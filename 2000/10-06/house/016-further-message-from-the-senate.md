# FURTHER MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

```
                    FURTHER MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

  A message from the Senate by Mr. Lundregon, one of its clerks, 
announced that the Senate agrees to the report of the committee of 
conference on the disagreeing votes of the two Houses on the amendment 
of the Senate to the bill (H.R. 4475) ``An Act making appropriations 
for the Department of Transportation and related agencies for the 
fiscal year ending September 30, 2001, and for other purposes.''.
  The message also announced that the Senate has passed a bill of the 
following title in which the concurrence of the House is requested:

       S. 2547. An act to provide for the establishment of the 
     Great Sand Dunes National Park and Preserve and the Baca 
     National Wildlife Refuge in the State of Colorado, and for 
     other purposes.

                          ____________________
```
