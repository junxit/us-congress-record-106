# CAT ISLAND NATIONAL WILDLIFE REFUGE

```
                  CAT ISLAND NATIONAL WILDLIFE REFUGE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The unfinished business is the question of 
suspending the rules and concurring in the Senate amendments to the 
bill, H.R. 3292.
  The Clerk read the title of the bill.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The question is on the motion of the 
gentleman from Utah (Mr. Hansen) that the House suspend the rules and 
concur in the Senate amendments to the bill, H.R. 3292.
  The question was taken; and (two-thirds having voted in favor 
thereof) the rules were suspended and the Senate amendments were 
concurred in.
  A motion to reconsider was laid on the table.
```
