                                 ______
                                 

                             HON. DAVID WU

                      Wednesday, October 11, 2000

distributions to accounts in the Thrift Savings Plan, to eliminate 
certain waiting-period requirements for participating in the Thrift 
Savings Plan, and for other purposes.

Amendments.
