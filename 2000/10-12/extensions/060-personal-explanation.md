                             HON. JIM KOLBE

                               of arizona

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 12, 2000

  Mr. KOLBE. Mr. Speaker, on October 10, 2000, I was on an airplane 
returning from my district and missed the following votes: H.R. 208, to 
amend Title 5, U.S. Code, to allow for the contribution of certain 
rollover distributions to accounts in the Thrift Savings Plan; H.R. 
762, the Lupus Research and Care Amendments; and S. 2438, the Pipeline 
Safety Improvement Act.

votes (#519, #520 and #521).
