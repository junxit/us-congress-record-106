# UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--H.J. RES. 111

```
               UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--H.J. RES. 111

  Mr. WARNER. I ask consent that immediately following the vote on 
passage of the Defense authorization conference report, the Senate 
proceed to the consideration of H.J. Res. 111, the continuing 
resolution, the resolution be read the third time, and the Senate then 
proceed immediately to a vote on passage of the resolution with no 
intervening action or debate.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
