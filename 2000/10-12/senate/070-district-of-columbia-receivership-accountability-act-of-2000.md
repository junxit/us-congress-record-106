# DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA RECEIVERSHIP ACCOUNTABILITY ACT OF 2000

```
      DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA RECEIVERSHIP ACCOUNTABILITY ACT OF 2000

  Mr. MURKOWSKI. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent the Senate now 
proceed to the consideration of Calendar No. 943, H.R. 3995.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report the bill by title.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (H.R. 3995) to establish procedures governing 
     responsibilities of court-appointed receivers who administer 
     departments, offices, and agencies of the District of 
     Columbia government.

  There being no objection, the Senate proceeded to consider the bill.
  Mr. MURKOWSKI. I ask unanimous consent the bill be read a third time 
and passed, the motion to reconsider be laid upon the table, and any 
statements relating to the bill be printed in the Record.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The bill (H.R. 3995) was read the third time and passed.
```
