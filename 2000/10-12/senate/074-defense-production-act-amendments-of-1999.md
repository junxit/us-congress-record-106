# DEFENSE PRODUCTION ACT AMENDMENTS OF 1999

**Speaking:** Murkowski, Frank H. (M001085) [R-AK]

**Measures:** H.R. 1715

```
               DEFENSE PRODUCTION ACT AMENDMENTS OF 1999

  Mr. MURKOWSKI. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
now proceed to consideration of H.R. 1715, which is at the desk.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report the resolution by title.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (H.R. 1715) to extend and reauthorize the Defense 
     Production Act of 1950, and for other purposes.

  There being no objection, the Senate proceeded to consider the bill.
  Mr. MURKOWSKI. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent the bill be 
considered read the third time and passed

[[Page S10489]]

and the motion to reconsider be laid upon the table, and any statements 
relating to the bill be printed in the Record.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The bill (H.R. 1715) was read the third time and passed.
```
