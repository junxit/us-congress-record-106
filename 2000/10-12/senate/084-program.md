# PROGRAM

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. MURKOWSKI. Mr. President, the leader has asked me to announce 
that, for the information of all Senators, the Senate will begin 
consideration of the conference report to accompany the Agriculture 
appropriations bill at 10 a.m. tomorrow. Debate on the conference 
report will take place all day tomorrow and all day on Tuesday, with a 
vote scheduled to occur on Wednesday at 11:30 a.m. Those Senators who 
intend to make statements on the conference report are encouraged to 
come to the floor as soon as possible due to the lack of time prior to 
the vote on Wednesday.
```
