# ENROLLED BILLS PRESENTED

**Measures:** S. 1236, S. 1849

```
                        ENROLLED BILLS PRESENTED

  The Secretary of the Senate reported that on today, October 12, 2000, 
he had presented to the President of the United States the following 
enrolled bills:

       S. 1236. An act to extend the deadline under the Federal 
     Power Act for commencement of the construction of the 
     Arrowrock Dam Hydroelectric Project in the State of Idaho.
       S. 1849. An act to designate segments and tributaries of 
     White Clay Creek, Delaware

[[Page S10410]]

     and Pennsylvania, as a component of the National Wild and 
     Scenic Rivers System.

                          ____________________
```
