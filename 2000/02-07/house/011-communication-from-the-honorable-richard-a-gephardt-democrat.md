# COMMUNICATION FROM THE HONORABLE RICHARD A. GEPHARDT, DEMOCRATIC LEADER

```
COMMUNICATION FROM THE HONORABLE RICHARD A. GEPHARDT, DEMOCRATIC LEADER

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following 
communication from the Honorable Richard A. Gephardt, Democratic 
Leader:

                                         House of Representatives,


                              Office of the Democratic Leader,

                                 Washington, DC, February 4, 2000.
     Hon. J. Dennis Hastert,
     Speaker of the House, House of Representatives, Washington, 
         DC.
       Dear Mr. Speaker: Pursuant to Section 702(b) of the 
     Intelligence Authorization Act for Fiscal Year 2000 (Public 
     Law 106-120), I hereby appoint the following member to the 
     National Commission for the Review of the National 
     Reconnaissance Office:
       Mr. Tony Beilenson, Chevy Chase, MD.
           Yours Very Truly,
     Richard A. Gephardt.

                          ____________________
```
