# INTRODUCTION OF BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

```
              INTRODUCTION OF BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

  The following bills and joint resolutions were introduced, read the 
first and second times by unanimous consent, and referred as indicated:

           By Mr. SPECTER (for himself, Mr. Santorum, Mr. 
             Lautenberg, Mr. Moynihan, Mr. Schumer, Mrs. Murray, 
             Mr. Dodd, Mr. Wellstone, Mr. Torricelli, Mr. 
             Sarbanes, Mr. Inhofe, Mr. Robb, Ms. Mikulski, and Mr. 
             Gramm):
       S. 2035. A bill to amend title 49, United States Code, to 
     clarify the application of the Act popularly known as the 
     ``Death on the High Seas Act'' to aviation incidents; to the 
     Committee on Commerce, Science, and Transportation.
           By Mr. SMITH of New Hampshire:
       S. 2036. A bill to make permanent the moratorium on the 
     imposition of taxes on the Internet; read the first time.
           By Ms. SNOWE (for herself and Mr. Helms):
       S. 2037. A bill to amend title XVIII of the Social Security 
     Act to extend the option to use rebased target amounts to all 
     sole community hospitals; to the Committee on Finance.
```
