# Daily Digest/COMMITTEE MEETINGS FOR 2000-02-08

                    COMMITTEE MEETINGS FOR TUESDAY,

                            FEBRUARY 8, 2000

        (Committee meetings are open unless otherwise indicated)


                                 Senate

  Special Committee on Aging: to hold hearings on certain provisions 
of S. 1895, to amend the Social Security Act to preserve and improve 
the medicare program, focusing on its overall restructuring plan,

  Committee on Agriculture, Nutrition, and Forestry: to hold 
hearings to examine the American dairy policy, 9 a.m., SH-216.

Committee, and the Congressional Budget Office, 9:30 a.m., SD-116.
