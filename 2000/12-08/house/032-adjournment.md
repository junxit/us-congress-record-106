# ADJOURNMENT

**Speaking:** Stark, Fortney Pete (S000810) [D-CA]

**Measures:** H.Res. 671

```
                              ADJOURNMENT

  Mr. STARK. Mr. Speaker, pursuant to House Resolution 671, I move that 
the House do now adjourn in memory of the late Honorable Julian C. 
Dixon.
  The motion was agreed to; accordingly (at 12 o'clock and 10 minutes 
p.m.), under its previous order, the House adjourned until Monday, 
December 11, 2000, at 5 p.m., in memory of the late Honorable Julian C. 
Dixon of California.

                          ____________________
```
