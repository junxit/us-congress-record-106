# PETITIONS, ETC.

```
                            PETITIONS, ETC.

  Under clause 3 of rule XII,

       123. The SPEAKER presented a petition of the Legislature of 
     Rockland County, New York, relative to Resolution No. 604 of 
     2000 petitioning the United States Congress to condemn the 
     murder of the two Israeli soldiers by a mob while in the 
     custody of the Palestinian Authority at Ramallah, and urges 
     President William Jefferson Clinton to strongly condemn this 
     atrocity and the violence which engendered it and to use all 
     the resources of the United States government to restore a 
     situation of peace and security in the Middle East; which was 
     referred to the Committee on the Judiciary.
```
