# MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

```
                        MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

  A message from the Senate by Mr. Lundregan, one of its clerks, 
announced that the Senate has passed without amendment a bill of the 
House of the following title:

       H.R. 5461. An act to amend the Magnuson-Stevens Fishery 
     Conservation and Management Act to eliminate the wasteful and 
     unsportsmanlike practice of shark finning.

  The message also announced that the Senate agrees to the amendments 
of the House to the bill (S. 439) ``An Act to amend the National Forest 
and Public Lands of Nevada Enhancement Act of 1988 to adjust the 
boundary of the Toiyabe National Forest, Nevada.''
  The message also announced that the Senate agrees to the amendments 
of the House to the bill (S. 1694) ``An Act to direct the Secretary of 
the Interior to conduct a study on the reclamation and reuse of water 
and wastewater in the State of Hawaii.''

                          ____________________
```
