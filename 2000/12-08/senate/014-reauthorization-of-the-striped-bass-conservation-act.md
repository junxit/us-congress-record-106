# REAUTHORIZATION OF THE STRIPED BASS CONSERVATION ACT

```
          REAUTHORIZATION OF THE STRIPED BASS CONSERVATION ACT

  Mr. BURNS. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate now 
proceed to consideration of H.R. 2903, which is at the desk.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report the bill.
  The assistant legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (H.R. 2903) to reauthorize the striped bass 
     conservation act and for other purposes.

  There being no objection, the Senate proceeded to consider the bill.
  Mr. BURNS. I ask unanimous consent that the bill be read the third 
time and passed, the motion to reconsider be laid upon the table, and 
any statements relating to the bill be printed in the Record.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The bill (H.R. 2903) was read the third time and passed.

                          ____________________
```
