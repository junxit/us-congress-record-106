# MAKING FURTHER CONTINUING APPROPRIATIONS FOR FISCAL YEAR 2001

**Speaking:** Gorton, Slade (G000333) [R-WA]

**Measures:** H.J.Res. 128

```
     MAKING FURTHER CONTINUING APPROPRIATIONS FOR FISCAL YEAR 2001

  Mr. GORTON. Given the presence of the assistant Democratic leader, I 
ask unanimous consent the Senate now turn to the consideration of H.J. 
Res. 128.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report the joint resolution by 
title.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       A joint resolution (H.J. Res. 128) making further 
     continuing appropriations for the fiscal year 2001, and for 
     other purposes.

  There being no objection, the Senate proceeded to consider the joint 
resolution.
  Mr. GORTON. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the 
resolution be considered read the third time and passed and the motion 
to reconsider be laid upon the table.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The joint resolution (H.J. Res. 128) was considered read the third 
time and passed.

                          ____________________
```
