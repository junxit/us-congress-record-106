# Text of House Amendment 7

**Speaking:** Kelly, Sue W. (K000078) [R-NY]

**Measures:** H.R. 4733

```
H.R. 4733

                         Offered By: Mrs. Kelly

       Amendment No. 7: Page 39, insert after line 21 the 
     following:
       Sec. 606. None of the funds in this Act may be available 
     for the restart of operations at Indian Point 2 nuclear power 
     facility in Buchanan, New York, prior to the replacement of 
     the plant's steam generators.
```
