# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, the Senate will now 
be in a period of morning business until the hour of 3 p.m., with 
Senators permitted to speak therein for up to 10 minutes each.
  Under the previous order, the Senator from Illinois, Mr. Durbin, or 
his designee, shall be in control of the time until 2 p.m.
  The Senator is recognized.

                          ____________________
```
