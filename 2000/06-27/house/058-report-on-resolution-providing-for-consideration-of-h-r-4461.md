# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 4461, AGRICULTURE, RURAL DEVELOPMENT, FOOD AND DRUG ADMINISTRATION, AND RELATED AGENCIES APPROPRIATIONS ACT, 2001

**Measures:** H.Res. 538, H.R. 4461

```
    REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 4461, 
   AGRICULTURE, RURAL DEVELOPMENT, FOOD AND DRUG ADMINISTRATION, AND 
               RELATED AGENCIES APPROPRIATIONS ACT, 2001

  Mr. SESSIONS, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 106-704) on the resolution (H. Res. 538) providing 
for consideration of the bill (H.R. 4461) making appropriations for 
Agriculture, Rural Development, Food and Drug Administration, and 
Related Agencies programs for the fiscal year ending September 30, 
2001, and for other purposes, which was referred to the House Calendar 
and ordered to be printed.
```
