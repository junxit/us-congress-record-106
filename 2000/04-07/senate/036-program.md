# PROGRAM

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. MURKOWSKI. For the information of all Senators, the Senate will 
convene at 12 noon on Monday and will be in a period of morning 
business throughout the day, with some debate on the gas tax repeal 
legislation. Cloture was filed on the gas tax legislation today, and 
that vote has been scheduled to occur on Tuesday at 2:25 p.m. That 
cloture vote will be the first vote of next week. Also, during next 
week's session, we expect to begin consideration of the marriage tax 
penalty legislation.

                          ____________________
```
