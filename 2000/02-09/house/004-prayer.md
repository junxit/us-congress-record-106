# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend James David Ford, D.D., offered the 
following prayer:
  O God, our hope for all the years, our faith by You is bold, You help 
us face unwanted tears, our hands with You do hold.
  You promise life without an end. You pledge the gift of love. Your 
peace and grace forever send, all gifts from heaven above. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
