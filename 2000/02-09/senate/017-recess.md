# RECESS

**Speaking:** Murkowski, Frank H. (M001085) [R-AK]

```
                                 RECESS

  Mr. MURKOWSKI. Mr. President, for the benefit of Senators, subject to 
the approval of the majority and minority leaders, it is our intention 
to break for lunch until 2:15.
  I ask unanimous consent that we recess for lunch, that the time be 
counted on the bill, and we resume debate again at 2:15.
  There being no objection, at 12:09 p.m., the Senate recessed until 
2:15 p.m.; whereupon, the Senate reassembled when called to order by 
the Presiding Officer (Mr. Gregg).
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Chair, in his capacity as a Senator from 
New Hampshire, suggests the absence of a quorum. The clerk will call 
the roll.
  The legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. MURKOWSKI. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the order 
for the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
