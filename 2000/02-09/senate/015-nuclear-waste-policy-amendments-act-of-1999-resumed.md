**Measures:** S. 1287

```
          NUCLEAR WASTE POLICY AMENDMENTS ACT OF 1999--RESUMED

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, the Senate will now 
resume consideration of S. 1287, which the clerk will report by title.

       A bill (S. 1287) to provide for the storage of spent

     repository, and for other purposes.

     substitute.

  Mr. BAUCUS. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the order for

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
