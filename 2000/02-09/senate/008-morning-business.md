# MORNING BUSINESS

**Speaking:** Durbin, Richard J. (D000563) [D-IL]

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, there will now be a 
period for the transaction of morning business not to extend beyond the 
hour of 11:30 a.m., with Senators permitted to speak therein for up to 
5 minutes each.
  Under the previous order, the time until 11 a.m. shall be under the 
control of the Senator from Illinois or his designee.
  The Senator from Illinois.
  Mr. DURBIN. I thank the Chair. I rise to speak in morning business.
```
