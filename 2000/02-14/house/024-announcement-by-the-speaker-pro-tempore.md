# ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 76, H.Con.Res. 247

```
                ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

  Mrs. BIGGERT. Pursuant to clause 8 of rule XX, the Chair will now put 
the question on each motion to suspend the rules on which further

  House Concurrent Resolution 247, by the yeas and nays; and

after the first such vote in this series.
```
