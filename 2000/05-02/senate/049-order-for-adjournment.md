# ORDER FOR ADJOURNMENT

```
                         ORDER FOR ADJOURNMENT

  Mr. GORTON. If there is no further business to come before the 
Senate, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate stand in adjournment 
under the previous order, following the remarks of Senator Schumer.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. GORTON. I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will call the roll.
  Mr. SCHUMER. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the order 
for the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER (Mr. Allard). Without objection, it is so 
ordered.
```
