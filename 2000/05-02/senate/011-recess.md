# RECESS

```
                                 RECESS

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, the hour of 12:30 
p.m. having arrived, the Senate will be in recess until the hour of 
2:15 p.m.
  Thereupon, at 12:33 p.m., the Senate recessed until 2:16 p.m.; 
whereupon, the Senate reassembled when called to order by the Presiding 
Officer (Mr. Inhofe).
```
