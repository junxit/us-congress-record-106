# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Gorton, Slade (G000333) [R-WA]

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. GORTON. For the information of all Senators, on Wednesday there 
will be a period of morning business until 11 a.m. Following morning 
business, the Senate will resume consideration of the Elementary and 
Secondary Education Act. Under the previous order, there will be four 
amendments debated during tomorrow's session, and therefore Senators 
can expect votes throughout the day. As previously announced, the 
Senate will not meet on Friday in order to accommodate the Democratic 
retreat.
```
