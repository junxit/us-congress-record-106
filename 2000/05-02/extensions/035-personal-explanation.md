# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Bereuter, Doug (B000403) [R-NE]

**Measures:** H.R. 4199

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. DOUG BEREUTER

                              of nebraska

                    in the house of representatives

                          Tuesday, May 2, 2000

  Mr. BEREUTER. Mr. Speaker, on April 13, 2000, this Member 
inadvertently voted ``aye'' on rollcall vote 127 on final passage of 
H.R. 4199, the Date Certain Tax Code Replacement Act. This Member is 
opposed to the bill and intended to vote ``no'' on final passage as his 
statement at that time on H.R. 4199 reflected his opposition to the 
bill.
```
