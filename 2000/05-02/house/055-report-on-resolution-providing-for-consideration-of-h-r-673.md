# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 673, FLORIDA KEYS WATER QUALITY IMPROVEMENTS ACT OF 2000

**Measures:** H.Res. 483, H.R. 673

```
 REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 673, FLORIDA 
              KEYS WATER QUALITY IMPROVEMENTS ACT OF 2000

  Mr. DIAZ-BALART, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 106-601) on the resolution (H. Res. 483) providing 
for consideration of the bill (H.R. 673) to authorize the Administrator 
of the Environmental Protection Agency to make grants to the Florida 
Keys Aqueduct Authority and other appropriate agencies for the purpose 
of improving water quality throughout the marine ecosystem of the 
Florida Keys, which was referred to the House Calendar and ordered to 
be printed.
```
