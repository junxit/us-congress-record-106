# ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, OCTOBER 17, 2000

**Speaking:** Lott, Trent (L000447) [R-MS]

**Measures:** H.R. 4461

```
                  ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, OCTOBER 17, 2000

  Mr. LOTT. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate 
completes its business today, it recess until the hour of 9:30 a.m. on 
Tuesday, October 17. I further ask unanimous consent that on Tuesday, 
immediately following the prayer, the Journal of proceedings be 
approved to date, the time for the two leaders be reserved for their 
use later in the day, and the Senate then proceed to the conference 
report to accompany H.R. 4461, the Agriculture appropriations bill, as 
under the previous order.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
