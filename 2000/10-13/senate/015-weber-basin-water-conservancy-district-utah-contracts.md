# WEBER BASIN WATER CONSERVANCY DISTRICT, UTAH CONTRACTS

**Measures:** H.R. 3236

```
         WEBER BASIN WATER CONSERVANCY DISTRICT, UTAH CONTRACTS

  The bill (H.R. 3236) to authorize the Secretary of the Interior to 
enter into contracts with the Weber Basin water Conservancy District, 
Utah, to use Weber Basin Project facilities for impounding, storage, 
and carriage of nonproject water for domestic, municipal, industrial, 
and other beneficial purposes, was considered, ordered to a third 
reading, read the third time, and passed.
```
