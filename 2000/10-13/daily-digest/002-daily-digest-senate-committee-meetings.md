# Daily Digest/Senate Committee Meetings

```
Committee Meetings
(Committees not listed did not meet)
U.S. SENTENCING COMMISSION
Committee on the Judiciary: Subcommittee on Criminal Justice Oversight 
concluded oversight hearings to examine the recent actions and agenda 
of the United States Sentencing Commission, and the implementation of 
the Federal Sentencing Guidelines within the federal criminal system, 
after receiving testimony from Diana E. Murphy, Chair, and John R. 
Steer, Vice-Chair, both of the United States Sentencing Commission; 
Laird Kirkpatrick, Counsel to the Assistant Attorney General, Criminal 
Division, Department of Justice; Carmen D. Hernandez, National 
Association of Criminal Defense Lawyers, Washington, D.C.; and William 
G. Otis, Falls Church, Virginia, former Assistant U.S. Attorney for the 
Eastern District of Virginia.
```
