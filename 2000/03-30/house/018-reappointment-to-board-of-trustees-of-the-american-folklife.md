# REAPPOINTMENT TO BOARD OF TRUSTEES OF THE AMERICAN FOLKLIFE CENTER IN THE LIBRARY OF CONGRESS

```
 REAPPOINTMENT TO BOARD OF TRUSTEES OF THE AMERICAN FOLKLIFE CENTER IN 
                        THE LIBRARY OF CONGRESS

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Without objection, pursuant to section 4(b) 
of Public Law 94-201 (20 U.S.C. 2103(b)), and upon the recommendation 
of the minority leader, the Chair announces the Speaker's reappointment 
of the following individual from private life to the Board of Trustees 
of the American Folklife Center in the Library of Congress on the part 
of the House:
  Mr. William L. Kinney of South Carolina.
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
