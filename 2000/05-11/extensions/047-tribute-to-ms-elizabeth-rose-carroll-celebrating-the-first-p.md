# TRIBUTE TO MS. ELIZABETH ROSE CARROLL--CELEBRATING THE FIRST PLACE WINNER OF THE 18TH CONGRESSIONAL DISTRICT HIGH SCHOOL ART COMPETITION, AN ARTISTIC DISCOVERY

```
[[Page 7780]]

  TRIBUTE TO MS. ELIZABETH ROSE CARROLL--CELEBRATING THE FIRST PLACE 
WINNER OF THE 18TH CONGRESSIONAL DISTRICT HIGH SCHOOL ART COMPETITION, 
                         AN ARTISTIC DISCOVERY

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. MICHAEL F. DOYLE

                            of pennsylvania

                    in the house of representatives

                         Thursday, May 11, 2000

  Mr. DOYLE. Mr. Speaker, I honor a very talented young lady from my 
congressional district, Elizabeth Rose Carroll of Springdale High 
School. Elizabeth is the top winner of the 2000 18th Congressional 
District High School Art Competition, An Artistic Discovery.
  Elizabeth's pen and ink entitled ``Petals in the Past'' depicting a 
veiled woman of a bygone era holding a bouquet, was selected from a 
number of outstanding entries to this year's competition. I know that, 
with her obvious talent, many successes await Elizabeth.
  I look forward to seeing ``Petals in the Past'' displayed along with 
the artwork of the other competition winners from across the country, 
and I am pleased to be associated with Elizabeth's artistic talents.
  Congratulations, Elizabeth. I wish you the very best of luck in the 
future.

                          ____________________
```
