# PERMISSION TO FILE DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE AUTHORIZATION BILL AND REPORT

**Speaking:** Smith, Gordon H. (S001142) [R-OR]

```
 PERMISSION TO FILE DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE AUTHORIZATION BILL AND REPORT

  Mr. SMITH of Oregon. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that 
notwithstanding the recess of the Senate, the Armed Services Committee 
be permitted to file the Department of Defense authorization bill and
```
