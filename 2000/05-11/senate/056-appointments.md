# APPOINTMENTS

```
                              APPOINTMENTS

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Chair, on behalf of the Vice President, 
pursuant to 22 U.S.C. 276d-276g, as amended, appoints the following 
Senators as members of the Senate Delegation to the Canada-U.S. 
Interparliamentary Group during the Second Session of the 106th 
Congress, to be held in Mississippi and Louisiana, May 19-22, 2000: the 
Senator from Iowa (Mr. Grassley); the Senator from Ohio (Mr. DeWine); 
the Senator from Minnesota (Mr. Grams); the Senator from Maine (Ms. 
Collins); the Senator from Ohio (Mr. Voinovich); the Senator from 
Vermont (Mr. Leahy); the Senator from Louisiana (Mr. Breaux); and the 
Senator from Hawaii (Mr. Akaka).
```
