# ENROLLED BILL SIGNED

```
                          ENROLLED BILL SIGNED

  Mr. THOMAS, from the Committee on House Administration, reported that

House of the following title, which was thereupon signed by the 
Speaker:

       H.R. 2412. An act to designate the Federal building and 
     United States courthouse at 1300 South Harrison Street in 
     Fort Wayne, Indiana, as the ``E. Ross Adair Federal Building 
     and United States Courthouse.''
```
