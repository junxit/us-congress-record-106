# PERMISSION FOR COMMITTEE ON APPROPRIATIONS TO HAVE UNTIL MIDNIGHT, FRIDAY, MAY 12, 2000, TO FILE PRIVILEGED REPORT ON LEGISLATIVE BRANCH APPROPRIATIONS ACT, 2001

```
  PERMISSION FOR COMMITTEE ON APPROPRIATIONS TO HAVE UNTIL MIDNIGHT, 
 FRIDAY, MAY 12, 2000, TO FILE PRIVILEGED REPORT ON LEGISLATIVE BRANCH 
                        APPROPRIATIONS ACT, 2001

  Mr. TAYLOR of North Carolina. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent 
that the Committee on Appropriations may have until midnight, Friday, 
May 12, 2000, to file a privileged report on a bill making 
appropriations for the legislative branch for the fiscal year ending 
September 30, 2001, and for other purposes.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from North Carolina?
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
