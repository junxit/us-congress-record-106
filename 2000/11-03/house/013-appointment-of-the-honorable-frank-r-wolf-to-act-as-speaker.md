# APPOINTMENT OF THE HONORABLE FRANK R. WOLF TO ACT AS SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE TO SIGN ENROLLED BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS THROUGH MONDAY, NOVEMBER 13, 2000

```
[[Page H11833]]
   APPOINTMENT OF THE HONORABLE FRANK R. WOLF TO ACT AS SPEAKER PRO 
 TEMPORE TO SIGN ENROLLED BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS THROUGH MONDAY, 
                           NOVEMBER 13, 2000

  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Pease) laid before the House the 
following communication from the Speaker:


                                               Washington, DC,

                                                 November 3, 2000.
       I hereby appoint the Honorable Frank R. Wolf to act as 
     Speaker pro tempore to sign enrolled bills and joint 
     resolutions through November 13, 2000.
                                                J. Dennis Hastert,
                          Speaker of the House of Representatives.

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Without objection, the appointment is agreed 
to.
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
