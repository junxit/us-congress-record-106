# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H. CON. RES. 290, CONCURRENT RESOLUTION ON THE BUDGET--FISCAL YEAR 2001

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 290, H.Res. 446

```
 REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H. CON. RES. 290, 
         CONCURRENT RESOLUTION ON THE BUDGET--FISCAL YEAR 2001

  Mr. GOSS, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged report 
(Rept. No. 106-535) on the resolution (H. Res. 446) providing for 
consideration of the concurrent resolution (H. Con. Res. 290) 
establishing the congressional budget for the United States Government 
for fiscal year 2001, revising the congressional budget for the United 
States government for fiscal year 2000, and setting forth appropriate 
budgetary levels for each of the fiscal years 2002 through 2005, which 
was referred to the House Calendar and ordered to be printed.

                          ____________________
```
