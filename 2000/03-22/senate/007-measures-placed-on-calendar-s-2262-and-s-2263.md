# MEASURES PLACED ON CALENDAR--S. 2262 AND S. 2263

**Speaking:** Roth Jr., William V. (R000460) [R-DE]

**Measures:** S. 2262, S. 2263

```
            MEASURES PLACED ON CALENDAR--S. 2262 AND S. 2263

  Mr. ROTH. Mr. President, I understand there are two bills at the desk 
due for their second reading.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report the bills by title.
  The bill clerk read as follows:

       A bill (S. 2262) to amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 
     to institute a Federal fuels tax holiday.
       A bill (S. 2263) to amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 
     to institute a Federal fuels tax holiday.

  Mr. ROTH. Mr. President, on behalf of the majority leader, I object 
to further proceedings on these bills at this time.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the rule, the bills will be placed on 
the calendar.
```
