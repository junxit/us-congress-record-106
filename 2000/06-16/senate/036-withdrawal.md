# WITHDRAWAL

```
                               WITHDRAWAL

  Executive message transmitted by the President to the Senate on June 
16, 2000, withdrawing from further Senate consideration the following 
nomination:


                     FEDERAL HOUSING FINANCE BOARD

       BRUCE A. MORRISON, OF CONNECTICUT, TO BE A DIRECTOR OF THE 
     FEDERAL HOUSING FINANCE BOARD FOR A TERM EXPIRING FEBRUARY 
     27, 2007 (REAPPOINTMENT), WHICH WAS SENT TO THE SENATE ON 
     OCTOBER 29, 1999.
```
