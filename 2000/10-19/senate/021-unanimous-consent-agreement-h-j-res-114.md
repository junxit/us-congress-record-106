# UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--H.J. RES. 114

```
               UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--H.J. RES. 114

  Mr. LOTT. I ask unanimous consent when the Senate receives from the 
House the continuing resolution, the resolution be immediately 
considered, advanced to third reading and passed, and the motion to 
reconsider be laid upon the table, all without intervening action, 
motion, or debate.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
