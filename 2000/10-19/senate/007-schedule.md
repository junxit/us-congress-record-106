# SCHEDULE

**Speaking:** Ashcroft, John (A000356) [R-MO]

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. ASHCROFT. Mr. President, for the information of all Senators, the 
Senate will be in a period of morning business until 12:30 p.m. today. 
At 12:30, the Senate will recess for a party caucus meeting until 2:15 
p.m. It is hoped that the Senate will receive the HUD-VA appropriations 
conference report and/or the continuing resolution from the House by 
early afternoon. The Senate may also have a procedural vote with 
respect to the bankruptcy reform bill during today's session. 
Therefore, Senators can expect up to three votes this afternoon. As 
usual, Senators will be notified as votes are scheduled.
  I thank my colleagues for their attention.

                          ____________________
```
