# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Sessions, Jeff (S001141) [R-AL]

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. SESSIONS. Mr. President, for the information of all Senators, the 
majority leader would advise them that the Senate will convene for a 
brief session on Monday afternoon for scheduled announcements and 
possible procedural action on the bankruptcy conference report.
  On Tuesday, the Senate is expected to begin consideration of any 
available conference reports. Leadership will notify the Senators on 
Monday if votes will be necessary during Tuesday's session of the 
Senate. It is hoped the Senate can complete its business prior to the 
expiration of the current continuing resolution. Therefore, votes are 
possible on Tuesday and will occur throughout the day on Wednesday.

                          ____________________
```
