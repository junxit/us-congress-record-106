# Daily Digest/House Committee Meetings

**Measures:** H.Res. 550, H.R. 4810

```
Committee Meetings
MARRIAGE TAX PENALTY RELIEF RECONCILIATION ACT--RESOLVE DIFFERENCES 
WITH SENATE
Committee on Rules: Granted, by voice vote, a rule providing for 
consideration of a motion to go to conference on H.R. 4810, Marriage 
Penalty Tax Elimination Reconciliation Act. The rule provides that the 
motion shall be debatable for one hour equally divided and controlled 
by the chairman and ranking minority member of the Committee on Ways 
and Means. Finally, the rule provides that H. Res. 550 is laid on the 
table.
MISCELLANEOUS TRADE AND TECHNICAL CORRECTIONS ACT
Committee on Ways and Means: Subcommittee on Trade approved for full 
Committee action the Miscellaneous Trade and Technical Corrections Act 
of 2000.
CORRECTIONS ACT
Committee on Ways and Means: Subcommittee on Trade approved for full 
Committee action the Miscellaneous Trade and Technical Corrections Act 
of 2000.
```
