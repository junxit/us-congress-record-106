# PERMISSION TO FILE CONFERENCE REPORT ON H.R. 4576, DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE APPROPRIATIONS ACT, 2001

```
   PERMISSION TO FILE CONFERENCE REPORT ON H.R. 4576, DEPARTMENT OF 
                    DEFENSE APPROPRIATIONS ACT, 2001

  Mr. LEWIS of California. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that 
the managers on the part of the House may have until midnight tonight, 
July 17, 2000, to file a conference report on the bill (H.R. 4576) 
making appropriations for the Department of Defense for the fiscal year 
ending September 30, 2001, and for other purposes.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Shimkus). Is there objection to the 
request of the gentleman from California?
  There was no objection.
```
