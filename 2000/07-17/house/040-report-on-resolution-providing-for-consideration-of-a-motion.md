# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF A MOTION TO GO TO CONFERENCE ON H.R. 4810, MARRIAGE TAX PENALTY ELIMINATION RECONCILIATION ACT OF 2000

**Measures:** H.Res. 553, H.R. 4810

```
 REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF A MOTION TO GO TO 
       CONFERENCE ON H.R. 4810, MARRIAGE TAX PENALTY ELIMINATION 
                       RECONCILIATION ACT OF 2000

  Ms. PRYCE of Ohio, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a 
privileged report (Rept. No. 106-752) on the resolution (H. Res. 553) 
providing for consideration of a motion to go to conference on any 
Senate amendments to the bill (H.R. 4810) to provide for reconciliation 
pursuant to section 103(a)(1) of the concurrent resolution on the 
budget for fiscal year 2001, which was referred to the House Calendar 
and ordered to be printed.
```
