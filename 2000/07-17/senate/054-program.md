# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Roth Jr., William V. (R000460) [R-DE]

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. ROTH. Mr. President, upon convening at 9:15 a.m., the Senate will 
immediately resume debate on the Interior appropriations bill, with 
Senators Feingold and Bingaman in control of 15 minutes each to offer 
and debate their amendments. Following that debate, at approximately 
9:45, the Senate will proceed to rollcall votes on the remaining 
amendments to the Interior appropriations bill, as well as on final 
passage. Following the disposition of the Interior appropriations bill, 
the Senate will begin the final four votes on the reconciliation bill. 
Therefore, Senators should be prepared to stay in the Chamber for up to 
12 votes, with all votes after the first vote limited to 10 minutes 
each.
  For the remainder of the day, it is expected that the Senate will 
begin consideration of the Agriculture appropriations bill.

                          ____________________
```
