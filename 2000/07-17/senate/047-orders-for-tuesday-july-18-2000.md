# ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, JULY 18, 2000

```
                   ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, JULY 18, 2000

  Mr. ROTH. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate 
completes its business today, it adjourn until the hour of 9:15 a.m. on 
Tuesday, July 18.
  I further ask unanimous consent that on Tuesday, immediately 
following the prayer, the Journal of the proceedings be approved to 
date, the morning hour be deemed to have expired, the time for the two 
leaders be reserved for their use later in the day, and the Senate then 
resume consideration of the Interior appropriations bill under the 
previous order.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. ROTH. Mr. President, further, I ask unanimous consent that the 
Senate stand in recess from 12:30 p.m. until 2:15 p.m. for the weekly 
party conferences to meet.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
