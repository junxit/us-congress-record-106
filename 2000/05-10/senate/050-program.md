# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Brownback, Sam (B000953) [R-KS]

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. BROWNBACK. Mr. President, for the information of all Senators, 
tomorrow from 9:30 a.m. until 10 a.m., the Senate will debate the 
conference report to accompany the African trade/Caribbean trade 
initiative. At 10 a.m., the Senate will proceed to a cloture vote on 
that legislation. If cloture is invoked, it is hoped a short time 
agreement can be made so a final passage vote can take place at a 
reasonable time. On Thursday, the Senate is also expected to begin 
consideration of the military construction appropriations bill. 
Therefore, additional votes will occur during tomorrow's session of the 
Senate.

                          ____________________
```
