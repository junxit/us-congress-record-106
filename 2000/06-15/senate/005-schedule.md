# SCHEDULE

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. ALLARD. Mr. President, today the Senate will resume debate on the 
Transportation appropriations legislation. Under the order, Senator 
Voinovich will be recognized to offer his amendment regarding passenger 
rail flexibility. A vote on the Voinovich amendment is expected to 
occur this morning at a time to be determined. Further amendments will 
be offered and voted on with the hope of final passage early in the 
day. As usual, Senators will be notified as votes are scheduled.
  Following the disposition of the Transportation legislation, the 
Senate may resume consideration of the Department of Defense 
authorization bill or any appropriations bills available for action.
  I thank my colleagues for their attention.

                          ____________________
```
