# MEASURE READ FOR THE FIRST TIME--S. 2742

**Speaking:** Abraham, Spencer (A000355) [R-MI]

**Measures:** S. 2742

```
                MEASURE READ FOR THE FIRST TIME--S. 2742

  Mr. ABRAHAM. Mr. President, I understand that 2742 is at the desk, 
and I ask for its first reading.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will read the bill for the first 
time.
  The assistant legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (S. 2742) to amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 
     to increase disclosure for certain political organizations 
     exempt from tax under section 527 and section 501(c), and for 
     other purposes.

  Mr. ABRAHAM. Mr. President, I now ask for its second reading, and I 
object to my own request.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The objection is heard.
  The bill will be read the second time on the next legislative day.

                          ____________________
```
