# SENATE BILL REFERRED

**Measures:** S. 935

```
                          SENATE BILL REFERRED

  A bill of the Senate of the following title was taken from the 
Speaker's table and, under the rule, referred as follows:

       S. 935. An act to authorize research to promote the 
     conversion of biomass into biobased industrial products, and 
     for other purposes; to the Committee on Agriculture; in 
     addition to the Committee on Science for a period to be 
     subsequently determined by the Speaker, in each case for 
     consideration of such provisions as fall within the 
     jurisdiction of the committee concerned.
```
