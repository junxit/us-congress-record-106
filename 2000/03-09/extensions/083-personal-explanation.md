# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Miller, Gary G. (M001139) [R-CA]

**Measures:** H.R. 1827, H.R. 2952, H.R. 3018

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. GARY G. MILLER

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, March 9, 2000

  Mr. GARY MILLER of California. Mr. Speaker, on Wednesday, March 8, 
2000 votes were held while I was in route to the Capitol, as were other 
members, therefore, I missed roll call votes 29, 30, and 31.
  Had I been present, I would have voted ``yes'' on the passage of H.R. 
1827, the ``Government Waste Corrections Act.''
  Had I been present, I would have voted ``yes'' to suspend the rules 
and pass the H.R. 2952 redesignate the facility of the U.S. Postal 
Service in Greenville, South Carolina as the Keith D. Oglesby Station.
  Had I been present, I would have voted ``yes'' to suspend the rules 
and pass, as amended H.R. 3018 to designate the U.S. Postal Office in 
Charleston, South Carolina as the Marybelle H. Howe Post Office.

                          ____________________
```
