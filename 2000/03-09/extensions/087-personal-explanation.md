# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Davis, Danny K. (D000096) [D-IL]

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. DANNY K. DAVIS

                              of illinois

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, March 9, 2000

  Mr. DAVIS of Illinois. Mr. Speaker, on March 8, 2000 I had to delay 
my return to the Capitol in order to attend to personal business in my 
district. During my absence, I missed rollcall vote 29, 30, 31 and 32.
  Had I been present, I would have voted yes on the motion to suspend 
the rules and pass H.R. 2952, the Keith D. Oglesby Post Office, H.R. 
3018, the South Carolina Post Office Designation and S. Con. Res. 91 
recognizing the forcible incorporation of the Baltic states of Estonia, 
Latvia, and Lithuania into the former Soviet Union.
  I would have also voted ``yes'' on final passage of H.R. 1827 the 
Government Waste Corrections Act on March 8, 2000.
```
