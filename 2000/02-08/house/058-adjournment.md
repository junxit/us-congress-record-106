# ADJOURNMENT

**Speaking:** Ganske, Greg (G000041) [R-IA]

**Measures:** H.Res. 418

```
                              ADJOURNMENT

  Mr. GANSKE. Mr. Speaker, pursuant to House Resolution 418, I move 
that the House do now adjourn in memory of the late Hon. Carl B. 
Albert.
  The motion was agreed to; accordingly (at 8 o'clock and 57 minutes 
p.m.), pursuant to House Resolution 418, the House adjourned until 
tomorrow, Wednesday, February 9, 2000, at 10 a.m., in memory of the 
late Hon. Carl B. Albert.

                          ____________________
```
