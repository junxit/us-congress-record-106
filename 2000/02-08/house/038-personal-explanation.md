# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Cubin, Barbara (C000962) [R-WY]

**Measures:** H.Res. 418, S. 632, H.R. 1451

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

  Mrs. CUBIN. Mr. Speaker, on February 8, 2000, I was unavoidably 
detained and missed rollcall vote numbers 8, 9, and 10. Had I been 
present, I would have voted ``yes'' on H.R. 1451, the Abraham Lincoln 
Bicentennial Commission Act; ``yes'' on S. 632, the Poison Control 
Center Enhancement and Awareness Act; and ''yes'' on H. Res. 418, 
honoring former Speaker Carl Albert.
```
