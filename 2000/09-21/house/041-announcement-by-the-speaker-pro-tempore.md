# ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

```
[[Page 18823]]

                ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The Chair must remind all Members that 
although remarks in debate may level criticism against the policies of 
the President and the Vice President or against the nominated 
candidates for those offices, still, remarks in debate must avoid 
personality and, therefore, may not include personal accusations or 
characterizations.
```
