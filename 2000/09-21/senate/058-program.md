# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Smith, Bob (S000606) [R-NH]

**Measures:** S. 2045

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. SMITH of New Hampshire. Mr. President, when the Senate convenes 
at 10 a.m. tomorrow, the Senate will be in a period for morning 
business throughout the day. The Senate may also resume debate on the 
motion to proceed to S. 2045, the H-1B visa bill, as well as any other 
items available for action. As previously announced, no votes will 
occur during tomorrow's session. The next vote will occur at 4:50 p.m. 
on Monday, September 25, on final passage of the Water Resources 
Development Act.
```
