# UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--H.R. 5633

```
                 UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--H.R. 5633

  Mr. WARNER. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate receives from the House H.R. 5633, the appropriations bill to 
fund the District of Columbia, if the text is identical to the text I 
now send to the desk, then the bill be considered passed and the motion 
to reconsider laid upon the table.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. WARNER. I now send the text of the bill to the desk.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The bill will be received.

                          ____________________
```
