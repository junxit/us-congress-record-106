# INTRODUCTION OF BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

```
              INTRODUCTION OF BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

  The following bills and joint resolutions were introduced, read the 
first and second times by unanimous consent, and referred as indicated:

           By Mr. SPECTER (for himself and Mr. Harkin):
       S. 3269. A bill to establish a Commission for the 
     comprehensive study of voting procedures in Federal, State, 
     and local elections, and for other purposes; to the Committee 
     on Rules and Administration.
           By Mr. HATCH (for himself and Mr. Campbell):
       S. 3270. A bill to amend title XVIII of the Social Security 
     Act to provide for a modification of medicare billing 
     requirements for certain Indian providers; to the Committee 
     on Finance.
           By Mr. TORRICELLI:
       S. 3271. A bill to require increased waste prevention and 
     recycling measures to be incorporated in the daily operations 
     of Federal agencies, and for other purposes; to the Committee 
     on Governmental Affairs.
```
