# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  Mr. LOTT. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that there be a 
period for the transaction of morning business until 12:30 p.m., with 
the time equally divided between the two leaders and each Member be 
limited to 5 minutes.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
