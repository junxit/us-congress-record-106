# SCHEDULE

**Speaking:** Roberts, Pat (R000307) [R-KS]

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. ROBERTS. Mr. President, today the Senate will resume debate on 
the China PNTR legislation. Under the order, Senator Byrd will debate 
his amendment in regard to subsidies for 1 hour. Following the debate 
on the Byrd amendment, Senator Thompson will be recognized to offer his 
China nonproliferation amendment. Further amendments may be offered 
during today's session, however, any votes during today's session 
ordered with respect to those amendments will be scheduled to occur at 
9:30 in the morning on Tuesday. It is hoped that the Senate can 
complete action on this important trade bill as early as possible so 
that the Senate may begin consideration of those appropriations bills 
still available for action.
  I thank my colleagues for their attention.

                          ____________________
```
