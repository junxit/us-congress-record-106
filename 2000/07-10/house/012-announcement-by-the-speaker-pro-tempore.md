# ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

```
                ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to clause 4 of rule I, the Speaker 
signed the following enrolled bill on Friday, June 30, 2000:
  H.R. 4425, making appropriations for military construction, family 
housing, and base realignment and closure for the Department of Defense 
for the fiscal year ending September 30, 2001, and for other purposes.
  And the Speaker pro tempore signed the following enrolled bill on 
Tuesday, July 4, 2000:
  S. 148, to require the Secretary of the Interior to establish a 
program to provide assistance in the conservation of neotropical 
migratory birds.
```
