# Text of House Amendment 79

**Speaking:** Waters, Maxine (W000187) [D-CA]

**Measures:** H.R. 4461

```
H.R. 4461

                         Offered By: Ms. Waters

       Amendment No. 79: Page 96, after line 4, insert the 
     following new section:
       Sec.   . There is hereby established the position of 
     Assistant Secretary of Agriculture for Civil Rights, and all 
     funds that would otherwise be expended for or provided to, 
     and all duties and authorities of, the Special Assistant to 
     the Secretary for Civil Rights shall be expended for or 
     provided to, or transferred to, the Assistant Secretary of 
     Agriculture for Civil Rights.
```
