# ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, JULY 11, 2000

```
                   ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, JULY 11, 2000

  Mr. WARNER. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until the hour of 9:30 
a.m. on Tuesday, July 11. I further ask consent that on Tuesday, 
immediately following the prayer, the Journal of proceedings be 
approved to date, the morning hour be deemed to have expired, the time 
for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the day, and the 
Senate then begin a period of morning business until 10:15 a.m., with 
the time equally divided between Senators Roth and Moynihan.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
