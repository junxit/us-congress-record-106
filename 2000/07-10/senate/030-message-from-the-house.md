# MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

```
                         MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE


                          ENROLLED BILL SIGNED

  At 1:09 p.m., a message from the House of Representatives, delivered 
by Ms. Niland, one of its reading clerks, announced that the Speaker 
pro tempore has signed the following enrolled bill:

       S. 148. An act to require the Secretary of the Interior to 
     establish a program to provide assistance in the conservation 
     of neotropical migratory birds.

  The bill was signed subsequently by the President pro tempore (Mr. 
Thurmond).
```
