  Mr. HELMS. Mr. President, at the close of business yesterday, Monday, 
September 25, 2000, the Federal debt stood at $5,646,252,666,475.97,

dollars and ninety-seven cents.

$3,213,942,000,000, three trillion, two hundred thirteen billion, nine 
hundred forty-two million.
