# UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--H.J. RES. 109

```
               UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--H.J. RES. 109

  Mr. LOTT. Mr. President, I understand that Senator Reid is here. I 
ask unanimous consent that notwithstanding rule XXII, at 9:30 a.m. on 
Thursday, September 28, the Senate proceed to the continuing 
resolution, H.J. Res. 109; that the joint resolution be immediately 
advanced to third reading and no amendments or motions be in order; 
that there be up to 7 hours for final debate to be divided as follows: 
6 hours under the control of Senator Byrd, and 1 hour under the control 
of Senator Stevens.
  Finally, I ask unanimous consent that the resolution be placed on the 
calendar when received from the House.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there objection?
  Without objection, it is so ordered.




                          ____________________
```
