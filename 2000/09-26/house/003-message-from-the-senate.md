# MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

**Measures:** S. 2796, H.R. 4864

```
                        MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

  A message from the Senate by Mr. Lundregan, one of its clerks, 
announced that the Senate has passed with an amendment in which the 
concurrence of the House is requested, a bill of the House of the 
following title:

       H.R. 4864. An act to amend title 38, United States Code, to 
     reaffirm and clarify the duty of the Secretary of Veterans 
     Affairs to assist claimants for benefits under laws 
     administered by the Secretary, and for other purposes.

  The message also announced that the Senate has passed a bill of the 
following title in which the concurrence of the House is requested:

       S. 2796. An act to provide for the conservation and 
     development of water and related resources, to authorize the 
     Secretary of the Army to construct various projects for 
     improvements to rivers and harbors of the United States, and 
     for other purposes.

                          ____________________
```
