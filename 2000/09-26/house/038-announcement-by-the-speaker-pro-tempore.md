# ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

**Measures:** H.J.Res. 100, H.R. 1248, H.R. 2572, H.R. 5117

```
                ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to clause 8 of rule XX, the Chair 
will now put the question on each motion to suspend the rules on which 
further proceedings were postponed earlier today and on the motion to 
suspend the rules on which further proceedings were postponed 
yesterday.
  Votes will be taken in the following order:
  H.R. 5117, by the yeas and nays;
  H.R. 2572, by the yeas and nays;
  H.R. 1248, by the yeas and nays;
  House Joint Resolution 100, by the yeas and nays.
  The Chair will reduce to 5 minutes the time for any electronic vote 
after the first such vote in this series.
```
