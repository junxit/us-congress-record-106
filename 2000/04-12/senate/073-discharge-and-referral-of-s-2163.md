# DISCHARGE AND REFERRAL OF S. 2163

**Speaking:** Smith, Bob (S000606) [R-NH]

**Measures:** S. 2163

```
                   DISCHARGE AND REFERRAL OF S. 2163

  Mr. SMITH of New Hampshire. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent 
that the Committee on Indian Affairs be discharged from further 
consideration of S. 2163, a bill to provide for a study of the 
engineering feasibility of a water exchange in lieu of electrification 
of the Chandler Pumping Plant at Prosser Diversion Dam, Washington, and 
that the measure be referred to the Committee on Energy and Natural 
Resources.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
