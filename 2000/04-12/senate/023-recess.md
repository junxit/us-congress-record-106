# RECESS

```
                                 RECESS

  Mr. LOTT. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent the Senate recess 
for 2 minutes for the purpose of the Senate welcoming and receiving to 
the U.S. Senate, the President of Colombia, President Andres Pastrana.
  There being no objection, the Senate, at 5:23 p.m., recessed until 
5:28 p.m.; whereupon, the Senate reassembled when called to order by 
the Presiding Officer (Mr. Voinovich).
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Senator from Kansas.
  Mr. BROWNBACK. Mr. President, I seek to be recognized to speak as in 
morning business for up to 10 minutes.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Senator from Kansas.
```
