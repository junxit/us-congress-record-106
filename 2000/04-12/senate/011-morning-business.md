# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  Mr. CRAIG. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate now 
proceed to a period of morning business, with Members permitted to 
speak up to 10 minutes each, until the hour of 1:30 p.m. today, with 
time to be equally divided between the two leaders.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there objection?
  Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
