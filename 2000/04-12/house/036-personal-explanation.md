                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

Program--``yes''; rollcall 121, to pass H.R. 3039, Chesapeake Bay water 
restoration--``yes''; rollcall 122, to pass H.R. 2884, to extend the 
Strategic Petroleum Reserve program--``yes.''
