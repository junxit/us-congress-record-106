# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 3615, RURAL LOCAL BROADCAST SIGNAL ACT

**Measures:** H.Res. 475, H.R. 3615

```
 REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 3615, RURAL 
                       LOCAL BROADCAST SIGNAL ACT

  Mr. GOSS, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged report 
(Rept. No. 106-579) on the resolution (H. Res. 475) providing for 
consideration of the bill (H.R. 3615) to amend the Rural 
Electrification Act of 1936 to ensure improved access to the signals of 
local television stations by multichannel video providers to all 
households which desire such service in unserved and underserved rural 
areas by December 31, 2006, which was referred to the House Calendar 
and ordered to be printed.
```
