```
                        PRIVILEGES OF THE FLOOR

  Mr. ROBERTS. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that Major Scott 
Kindsvater from Dodge City, KS, a major in the United States Air Force,

Lederer, a congressional fellow serving in Senator Conrad's office, be 
granted floor privileges during the consideration of the crop insurance 
conference report.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
