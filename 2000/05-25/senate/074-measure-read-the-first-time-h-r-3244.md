# MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--H.R. 3244

```
                 MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--H.R. 3244

  Mr. LOTT. Mr. President, I understand H.R. 3244 is at the desk. I ask 
for its first reading.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report the bill by title.
  The assistant legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (H.R. 3244) to combat trafficking of persons, 
     especially into the sex trade, slavery, and slavery-like 
     conditions in the United States.

  Mr. LOTT. I now ask for its second reading and object to my own 
request.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Objection is heard.

                          ____________________
```
