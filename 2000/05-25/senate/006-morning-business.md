# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the 
Senate will now begin a period for the transaction of morning business 
until 10:30 a.m., with Senators permitted to speak therein for up to 5 
minutes each. Under the previous order, the time until 10 a.m. shall be 
under the control of the Senator from Delaware, Mr. Biden, or his 
designee.
  The Senator from Delaware.

                          ____________________
```
