# PERMISSION FOR COMMITTEE ON APPROPRIATIONS TO HAVE UNTIL MIDNIGHT, JUNE 1, 2000, TO FILE PRIVILEGED REPORT ON DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE APPROPRIATIONS BILL, 2001

```
PERMISSION FOR COMMITTEE ON APPROPRIATIONS TO HAVE UNTIL MIDNIGHT, JUNE 
      1, 2000, TO FILE PRIVILEGED REPORT ON DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE 
                       APPROPRIATIONS BILL, 2001

  Mr. LEWIS of California. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that 
the Committee on Appropriations may have until midnight, June 1, 2000, 
to file a privileged report on a bill making appropriations for the 
Department of Defense for the fiscal year ending September 30, 2001, 
and for other purposes.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. LaTourette). Is there objection to the 
request of the gentleman from California?
  There was no objection.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to clause 1 of rule XXI, all points 
of order are reserved on the bill.

                          ____________________
```
