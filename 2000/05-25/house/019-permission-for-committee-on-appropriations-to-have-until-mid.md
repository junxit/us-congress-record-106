# PERMISSION FOR COMMITTEE ON APPROPRIATIONS TO HAVE UNTIL MIDNIGHT, JUNE 1, 2000, TO FILE PRIVILEGED REPORT ON DEPARTMENT OF THE INTERIOR AND RELATED AGENCIES APPROPRIATIONS BILL, 2001

```
PERMISSION FOR COMMITTEE ON APPROPRIATIONS TO HAVE UNTIL MIDNIGHT, JUNE 
 1, 2000, TO FILE PRIVILEGED REPORT ON DEPARTMENT OF THE INTERIOR AND 
               RELATED AGENCIES APPROPRIATIONS BILL, 2001

  Mr. REGULA. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that the Committee 
on Appropriations may have until midnight, June 1, 2000, to file a 
privileged report on a bill making appropriations for the Department of 
the Interior and related agencies for the fiscal year ending September 
30, 2001, and for other purposes.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Ohio?
  There was no objection.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to clause 1 of rule XXI, all points 
of order are reserved on the bill.

                          ____________________
```
