# ADJOURNMENT

**Speaking:** Tauzin, W. J. (Billy) (T000058) [R-LA]

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 336

```
                              ADJOURNMENT

  Mr. TAUZIN. Mr. Speaker, pursuant to House Concurrent Resolution 336, 
106th Congress, I move that the House do now adjourn.
  The motion was agreed to.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Shimkus). Pursuant to House Concurrent 
Resolution 336, 106th Congress, the House stands adjourned until 10:30 
a.m. on Tuesday, June 6, 2000, for morning hour debates.
  Thereupon (at 5 o'clock and 14 minutes p.m.), pursuant to House 
Concurrent Resolution 336, the House adjourned until Tuesday, June 6, 
2000, at 10:30 a.m. for morning hour debates.

                          ____________________
```
