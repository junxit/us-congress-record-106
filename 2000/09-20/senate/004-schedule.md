# SCHEDULE

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. LOTT. Mr. President, today the Senate will be in a period of 
Morning Business until 11:30 a.m. Following Morning Business, the 
Senate will resume the final debate on the conference report to 
accompany H.R. 4516, the Legislative Branch Appropriations Bill. A vote 
on final passage of the Conference Report is expected to occur at 
approximately 3:30 p.m. After the vote, it is hoped that the Senate can 
begin consideration of the Water Resources Development Act under a time 
agreement. Therefore, Senators can expect votes throughout this 
afternoon's session.
  I thank my colleagues for their attention.

                          ____________________
```
