# PROGRAM

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. ENZI. Mr. President, when the Senate convenes at 9:30 a.m., the 
Senate will be in a period of morning business until 11:30 a.m. 
Following morning business, the Senate will resume postcloture debate 
on the motion to proceed to S. 2045, the H-1B visa bill. An agreement 
is being negotiated regarding the Water Resources Development Act, and 
it is hoped that the Senate can begin consideration of the bill


this week. Therefore, Senators should be prepared to vote during 
tomorrow's session of the Senate.

                          ____________________
```
