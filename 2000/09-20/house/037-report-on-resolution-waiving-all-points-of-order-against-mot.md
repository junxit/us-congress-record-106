# REPORT ON RESOLUTION WAIVING ALL POINTS OF ORDER AGAINST MOTION TO CONCUR IN SENATE AMENDMENTS TO H.R. 940, LACKAWANNA VALLEY NATIONAL HERITAGE ACT OF 1999

```
  REPORT ON RESOLUTION WAIVING ALL POINTS OF ORDER AGAINST MOTION TO 
  CONCUR IN SENATE AMENDMENTS TO H.R. 940, LACKAWANNA VALLEY NATIONAL 
                          HERITAGE ACT OF 1999

  Mr. DIAZ-BALART (during special order of Mr. Blumenauer) from the 
Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged report (Rept. No. 106-873) 
on the resolution (H. Res. 583) providing for consideration of the 
Senate amendments to the bill (H.R. 940) to designate the Lackawanna 
Valley National Heritage Area, and for other purposes, which was 
referred to the House Calendar and ordered to be printed.
```
