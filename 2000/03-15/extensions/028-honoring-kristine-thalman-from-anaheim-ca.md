# HONORING KRISTINE THALMAN FROM ANAHEIM, CA

**Speaking:** Cox, Christopher (C000830) [R-CA]

```
               HONORING KRISTINE THALMAN FROM ANAHEIM, CA

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. CHRISTOPHER COX

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                       Wednesday, March 15, 2000

  Mr. COX. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Kristine Thalman, a 
loyal staff member at the city of Anaheim, CA. Kris will be retiring 
from the city after a long and distinguished career.
  In her career, especially for the last 13 years, Kris has served as 
the Governmental Relations Director for the city. She has ensured very 
smooth relations between the city

[[Page E324]]

of California and many of us in Congress that we represent.
  Mr. Speaker, it is with great pleasure that I ask my colleagues to 
join with me in honoring Kristine Thalman. It is fitting that all of us 
join with the family, friends, and the community of Anaheim, CA, in 
recognizing her service and dedication to the city and wish her well in 
her future endeavors.

                          ____________________
```
