# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Gorton, Slade (G000333) [R-WA]

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. GORTON. For the information of all Senators, when the Senate 
reconvenes, Senator Moynihan will be recognized to read Washington's 
Farewell Address in honor of the impending holiday. Following this 
annual Senate tradition, the Senate will be in a period of morning 
business until the Senate recesses at 12:30 p.m. for the weekly policy 
luncheons. When the Senate reconvenes at 2:15 p.m., it will begin 
consideration of any executive or legislative items cleared for action. 
However, the leader has announced there will be no votes prior to 2:15 
p.m.

                          ____________________
```
