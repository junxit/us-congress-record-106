# ANNOUNCEMENT REGARDING AMENDMENT PROCESS FOR H.R. 2366, THE SMALL BUSINESS LIABILITY REFORM ACT OF 2000

```
   ANNOUNCEMENT REGARDING AMENDMENT PROCESS FOR H.R. 2366, THE SMALL 
                 BUSINESS LIABILITY REFORM ACT OF 2000

  Ms. PRYCE of Ohio. Mr. Speaker, this afternoon a ``Dear Colleague'' 
letter will be sent to all Members informing them that the Committee on 
Rules is planning to meet the week of February 14 to grant a rule which 
may limit the amendment process for H.R. 2366, the Small Business 
Liability Reform Act of 2000.
  Any Member who wishes to offer an amendment should submit 55 copies 
and a brief explanation of the amendment by noon on Tuesday, February 
15, to the Committee on Rules in room H-312 in the Capitol. Amendments 
should be drafted to the text of the bill as reported by the Committee 
on the Judiciary.
  Members should use the Office of Legislative Counsel to ensure that 
their amendments are properly drafted and should check with the office 
of the parliamentarian to be certain their amendments comply with the 
Rules of the House.

                          ____________________
```
