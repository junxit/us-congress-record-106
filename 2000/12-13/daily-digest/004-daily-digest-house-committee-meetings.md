# Daily Digest/House Committee Meetings

```
Committee Meetings
THREAT POSED BY CONVERGENCE OF ORGANIZED CRIME, DRUG TRAFFICKING, AND 
TERRORISM
Committee on the Judiciary: Subcommittee on Crime held an oversight 
hearing on ``The Threat Posed by the Convergence of Organized Crime, 
Drug Trafficking, and Terrorism.'' Testimony was heard from the 
following officials of the Department of Justice:

[[Page D1215]]

Donnie R. Marshall, Administrator, DEA; and Steven C. McCraw, 
Inspector-General Assistant Director, Information, Analysis, and 
Assessments Branch, Investigative Division, FBI; Michael A. Sheehan, 
Ambassador at Large and Coordinator for Counterterrorism, Department of 
State; Raphael Perl, Specialist in International Affairs, Congressional 
Research Service, Library of Congress; Ralf Mutschke, Assistant 
Director, Sub-Directorate for Crimes Against Persons and Property, 
INTERPOL General Secretariat; and a public witness.
HEADWATERS FOREST
Committee on Resources: On December 12, the Task Force on Headwaters 
Forest and Related Issues held a hearing on matters related to 
Headwaters Forest. Testimony was heard from Donna Tanoue, Chairman, 
FDIC; Ellen Seidman, Director, Office of Thrift Supervision, Department 
of the Treasury; and William M. Isaac, former Chairman, FDIC.
```
