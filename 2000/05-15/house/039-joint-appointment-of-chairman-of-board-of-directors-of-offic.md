# JOINT APPOINTMENT OF CHAIRMAN OF BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF OFFICE OF COMPLIANCE

```
   JOINT APPOINTMENT OF CHAIRMAN OF BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF OFFICE OF 
                               COMPLIANCE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Without objection, and pursuant to section 
301 of Public Law 104-1, the Chair announces on behalf of the Speaker 
and minority leader of the House of Representatives and the majority 
and minority leaders of the United States Senate their joint 
appointment of Ms. Susan S. Robfogel, New York, Chairman of the Board 
of Directors of the Office of Compliance, to fill the existing vacancy 
thereon.
  There was no objection.
```
