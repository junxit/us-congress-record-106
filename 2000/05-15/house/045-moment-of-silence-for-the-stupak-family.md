# MOMENT OF SILENCE FOR THE STUPAK FAMILY

**Speaking:** Armey, Richard K. (A000217) [R-TX]

```
                MOMENT OF SILENCE FOR THE STUPAK FAMILY

  (Mr. ARMEY asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute.)
  Mr. ARMEY. Madam Speaker, all of us are so hurt and heartbroken over 
the loss that Bart and Laurie Stupak had in their family.
  I wanted to advise the Members that we will be working with those 
Members wanting to travel on Wednesday, with the floor schedule, to be 
sure we accommodate them. I wanted to recommend, Madam Speaker, that 
the House have a moment of silence on behalf of Bart and Laurie and 
family.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mrs. Biggert). The Members will rise for a 
moment of silence.
  (Members observed a moment of silence.)

                          ____________________
```
