# BILL PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

**Measures:** H.R. 2412

```
                    BILL PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

  Mr. THOMAS, from the Committee on House Administration, reported that 
that committee did on the following date present to the President, for 
his approval, a bill of the House of the following title:

           On Thursday, May 11, 2000:
       H.R. 2412. To designate the Federal building and United 
     States courthouse located at 1300 South Harrison Street in 
     Fort Wayne, Indiana, as the ``E. Ross Adair Federal Building
```
