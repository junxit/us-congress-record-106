# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Grassley, Chuck (G000386) [R-IA]

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. GRASSLEY. Mr. President, I announce that tomorrow morning the 
Senate will begin consideration of the nuclear waste bill and 
overriding the President's veto. Under the previous order, there will 
be 90 minutes under the control of Senator Murkowski and 90 minutes 
under the control of the Senators from Nevada.
  At 2:15 p.m., following the weekly party conferences, the Senate will 
resume consideration of the veto override for 1 hour, with a vote 
scheduled to occur at 3:15 p.m. Following the vote, the Senate is 
expected to resume consideration of the Elementary and Secondary 
Education Reauthorization Act. Further votes could occur throughout 
tomorrow's session of the Senate.

                          ____________________
```
