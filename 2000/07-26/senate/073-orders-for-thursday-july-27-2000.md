# ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, JULY 27, 2000

```
                   ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, JULY 27, 2000

  Mr. DeWINE. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until the hour of 9:30 
a.m. on Thursday, July 27. I further ask consent that on Thursday, 
immediately following the prayer, the Journal of proceedings be 
approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, the time for the 
two leaders be reserved for their use later in the day, and the Senate 
then begin a period of morning business for Coverdell tributes only 
until 11 a.m., with Senators permitted to speak for up to 10 minutes 
each.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
