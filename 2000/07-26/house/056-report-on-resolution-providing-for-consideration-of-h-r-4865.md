# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 4865, SOCIAL SECURITY BENEFITS TAX RELIEF ACT OF 2000

**Measures:** H.Res. 564, H.R. 4865

```
 REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 4865, SOCIAL 
                SECURITY BENEFITS TAX RELIEF ACT OF 2000

  Mr. DREIER (during the Special Order of Mr. Ganske), from the 
Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged report (Rept. No. 106-795) 
on the resolution (H. Res. 564) providing for consideration of the bill 
(H.R. 4865) to amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to repeal the 
1993 income tax increase on Social Security benefits, which was 
referred to the House Calendar and ordered to be printed.
```
