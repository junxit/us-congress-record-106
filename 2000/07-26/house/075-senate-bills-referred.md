# SENATE BILLS REFERRED

**Measures:** S. 1586, S. 2516

```
                         SENATE BILLS REFERRED

  Bills of the Senate of the following titles were taken from the 
Speaker's table and, under the rule, referred as follows:

       S. 1586. An act to reduce the fractionated ownership of 
     Indian lands, and for other purposes; to the Committee on 
     Resources.
       S. 2516. An act to fund task forces to locate and apprehend 
     fugitives in Federal, State, and local felony criminal cases 
     and give administrative subpoena authority to the United 
     States Marshals Service; to the Committee on the Judiciary.

                          ____________________
```
