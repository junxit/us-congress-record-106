# PROGRAM

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. LOTT. Mr. President, for the information of all Senators, the 
Senate will be in a period for morning business until 11:30 tomorrow 
morning. Following morning business, it is hoped the Senate can begin 
consideration of S. 2536, the Agriculture appropriations bill. It is my 
intention to complete action on this important spending bill and the 
legislative appropriations bill, if it is available from the House. 
Senators can expect votes throughout the week.
```
