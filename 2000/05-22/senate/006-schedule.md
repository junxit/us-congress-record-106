# SCHEDULE

**Speaking:** Kyl, Jon (K000352) [R-AZ], Dorgan, Byron L. (D000432) [D-ND]

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. KYL. Mr. President, today the Senate will be in a period of 
morning business with Senators Durbin and Thomas in control of the 
first 2 hours. For the information of all Senators, it is the intention 
of the majority leader to begin consideration of the agricultural 
appropriations bill during Tuesday's session. The leader has announced 
that the Senate will remain in session notwithstanding the Memorial Day 
recess in order to complete this important spending bill. Therefore, 
Senators can expect votes throughout the week and into the weekend if 
necessary.
  Mr. President, I observe the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. The clerk will call the roll.
  The legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. DORGAN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the order for 
the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER (Mr. Kyl). Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
