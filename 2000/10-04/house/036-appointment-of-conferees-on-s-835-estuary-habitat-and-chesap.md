# APPOINTMENT OF CONFEREES ON S. 835, ESTUARY HABITAT AND CHESAPEAKE BAY RESTORATION ACT OF 2000

**Measures:** S. 835

```
APPOINTMENT OF CONFEREES ON S. 835, ESTUARY HABITAT AND CHESAPEAKE BAY 
                        RESTORATION ACT OF 2000

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Without objection, the Chair appoints the 
following conferees on the Senate bill (S. 835) to encourage the 
restoration of estuary habitat through more efficient project financing 
and enhanced coordination of Federal and non-Federal restoration 
programs, and for other purposes:
  Messrs. Shuster, Young of Alaska, Boehlert, and Gilchrest, Mrs. 
Fowler, and Messrs. Sherwood, Sweeney, Kuykendall, Vitter, Oberstar, 
Borski, Barcia, Filner, Taylor of Mississippi, Blumenauer, and 
Baldacci.
  There was no objection.
```
