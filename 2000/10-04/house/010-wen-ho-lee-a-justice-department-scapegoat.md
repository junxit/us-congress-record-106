# WEN HO LEE, A JUSTICE DEPARTMENT SCAPEGOAT

**Speaking:** Traficant, James A., Jr. (T000350) [D-OH]

```
               WEN HO LEE, A JUSTICE DEPARTMENT SCAPEGOAT

  (Mr. TRAFICANT asked and was given permission to address the House 
for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. TRAFICANT. Mr. Speaker, I do not know if Wen Ho Lee is a spy, but 
one thing for sure, Wen Ho Lee is a scapegoat. Wen Ho Lee was a 
diversion used by Janet Reno to avoid the appointment of an independent 
counsel to investigate illegal Chinese campaign contributions to the 
Democrat National Committee.
  Who is kidding whom? Even Barney Fife can see through this ploy. Wake 
up, Congress. A Chinese Red Army general, a Red Army general was one of 
the Chinese who funneled money to the Democrat National Committee, and 
there has been no investigation. Beam me up.
  I yield back the treason of Janet Reno and the secrets still to be 
stolen by the Chinese.

                          ____________________
```
