# ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, OCTOBER 5, 2000

```
                  ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, OCTOBER 5, 2000

  Mr. FITZGERALD. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it recess until the hour of 9:30 
a.m., on Thursday, October 5. I further ask consent that on Thursday, 
immediately following the prayer, the Journal of proceedings be 
approved to date, the time for the two leaders be reserved for their 
use later in the day, and the Senate then resume consideration of H.J. 
Res. 110, the continuing resolution, under the previous order.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
