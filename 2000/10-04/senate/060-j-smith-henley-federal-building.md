# J. SMITH HENLEY FEDERAL BUILDING

```
                    J. SMITH HENLEY FEDERAL BUILDING

  Mr. FITZGERALD. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent the Senate now 
proceed to the consideration of Calendar No. 847, H.R. 1605.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report the bill by title.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (H.R. 1605) to designate the Federal Building and 
     United States Courthouse located at 402 North Walnut Street 
     in Harrison, Arkansas, as the ``J. Smith Henley Federal 
     Building and United States Courthouse.''


[[Page 20817]]


  There being no objection, the Senate proceeded to consider the bill.
  Mr. FITZGERALD. I ask unanimous consent the bill be read the third 
time and passed, the motion to reconsider be laid upon the table, and 
any statements relating to the bill be printed in the Record.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The bill (H.R. 1605) was read the third time and passed.
```
