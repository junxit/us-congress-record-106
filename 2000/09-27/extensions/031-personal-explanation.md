# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Bereuter, Doug (B000403) [R-NE]

**Measures:** H.R. 4292

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. DOUG BEREUTER

                              of nebraska

                    in the house of representatives

                     Wednesday, September 27, 2000

  Mr. BEREUTER. Mr. Speaker, on September 26, 2000, this Member 
inadvertently missed rollcall No. 495 on final passage of H.R. 4292, 
the Born-Alive Infants Protection Act, while he was in a room where the 
bells did not ring announcing the vote. Had this Member been present, 
he would have voted ``aye.''
```
