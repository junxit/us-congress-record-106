# DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA APPROPRIATIONS ACT, 2001

**Measures:** S. 3041, H.R. 4942

```
             DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA APPROPRIATIONS ACT, 2001

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, the clerk will 
report the bill by title.
  The assistant legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (S. 3041) making appropriations for the government 
     of the District of Columbia and other activities chargeable 
     in whole or in part against the revenues of said District for 
     the fiscal year ending September 30, 2001, and for other 
     purposes.

  The Senate proceeded to consider the bill.
  The amendment (No. 4271) was agreed to.
  (The text of the amendment is printed in today's Record under 
``Amendments Submitted.'')
  The bill (S. 3041), as amended, was read the third time.
  The bill (H.R. 4942), as amended, was read the third time and passed.
  (The bill will be printed in a future edition of the Record.)

                          ____________________
```
