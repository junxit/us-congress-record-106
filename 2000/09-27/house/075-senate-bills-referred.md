# SENATE BILLS REFERRED

**Measures:** S. 1658, S. 1865, S. 1929, S. 2272

```
                         SENATE BILLS REFERRED

  Bills of the Senate of the following titles were taken from the 
Speaker's table and, under the rule, referred as follows:

       S. 1658. An act to authorize the construction of a 
     Reconciliation Place in Fort Pierre, South Dakota, and for 
     other purposes; to the Committee on Resources.
       S. 1865. An act to provide grants to establish 
     demonstration mental health courts; to the Committee on the 
     Judiciary.
       S. 1929. An act to amend the Native Hawaiian Health Care 
     Improvement Act to revise and extend such Act; to the 
     Committee on Commerce.
       S. 2272. An act to improve the administrative efficiency 
     and effectiveness of the Nation's abuse and neglect courts 
     and for other purposes consistent with the Adoption and Safe 
     Families Act of 1997; to the Committee on the Judiciary.

                          ____________________
```
