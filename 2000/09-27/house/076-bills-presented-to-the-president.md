# BILLS PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

**Measures:** H.R. 940, H.R. 2909, H.R. 4919

```
                    BILLS PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

  Mr. THOMAS, from the Committee on House Administration, reported that 
that committee did on the following date present to the President, for 
his approval, bills of the House of the following titles:

           On Wednesday, September 26, 2000:
       H.R. 2909. To provide for implementation by the United 
     States of the Hague Convention on Protection of Children and 
     Co-operation in Respect of Intercountry Adoption, and for 
     other purposes.
       H.R. 4919. To amend the Foreign Assistance Act of 1961 and 
     the Arms Export Control Act to make improvements to certain 
     defense and security assistance provisions under those Acts, 
     to authorize the transfer of naval vessels to certain foreign 
     countries, and for other purposes.
       H.R. 940. To designate the Lackawanna Valley and the 
     Schuylkill River National Heritage Areas, and for other 
     purposes.

                          ____________________
```
