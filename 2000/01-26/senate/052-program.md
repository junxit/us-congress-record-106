# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Hatch, Orrin G. (H000338) [R-UT]

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. HATCH. Mr. President, for the information of all Senators, the 
Senate will be in session at 8:30 p.m. tomorrow in order to proceed as 
a body to the House of Representatives to hear the President's address. 
Following his remarks, the Senate will adjourn until Monday at 12 noon. 
At 2 p.m., the Senate will resume the bankruptcy bill. As announced 
previously, no rollcall votes will occur on Monday. Any Senator who 
still intends to debate bankruptcy amendments should be available to 
debate those amendments on Monday. Any votes ordered on those 
amendments will be postponed to occur on Tuesday, February 1.

                          ____________________
```
