# ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, JANUARY 27, 2000

**Speaking:** Hatch, Orrin G. (H000338) [R-UT]

```
                 ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, JANUARY 27, 2000

  Mr. HATCH. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it stand in adjournment until the 
hour of 8:30 p.m. on Thursday, January 27. I further ask consent that 
on Thursday, immediately following the prayer, the Journal of the 
proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed to have 
expired, the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later 
in the day, and the Senate then begin a brief period for morning 
business to consider a few housekeeping matters prior to the Senate 
proceeding as a body to the Hall of the House of Representatives to 
hear the President's address.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
