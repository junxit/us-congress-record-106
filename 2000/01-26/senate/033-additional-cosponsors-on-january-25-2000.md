# ADDITIONAL COSPONSORS ON JANUARY 25, 2000

**Measures:** S. 1197

```
               ADDITIONAL COSPONSORS ON JANUARY 25, 2000


                                S. 1197

  At the request of Mr. Roth, the name of the Senator from 
Massachusetts (Mr. Kerry) was added as a cosponsor of S. 1197, a bill 
to prohibit the importation of products made with dog of cat fur, to 
prohibit the sale, manufacture, offer for sale, transportation, and 
distribution of products made with dog or cat fur in the United States, 
and for other purposes.
```
