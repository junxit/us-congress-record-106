# NATIONAL HEART FAILURE AWARENESS WEEK

**Speaking:** Santorum, Rick (S000059) [R-PA]

**Measures:** S.Res. 256

```
                 NATIONAL HEART FAILURE AWARENESS WEEK

● Mr. SANTORUM. Mr. President, on February 10th, I phoned in a 
request to become a cosponsor of S. Res. 256, a resolution designating 
the week of February 14 through 18, 2000, as ``National Heart Failure 
Awareness Week''. Unfortunately, my name was inadvertently not included 
in the list of cosponsors at that time. Hence, I ask unanimous consent 
that the Record reflect my support and cosponsorship of the resolution.
  Mr. President, I cosponsored this important resolution because it 
will help to promote research related to all aspects of heart failure 
and enhance the quality and duration of life for those

[[Page S696]]

with heart failure. With that in mind, I was pleased that S. Res. 256 
passed the Senate by unanimous consent.●

                          ____________________
```
