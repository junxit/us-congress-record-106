# MORNING BUSINESS

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, it is my understanding that we are in a 
period of morning business and that the time used by the Senator from 
Alabama and the Senator from Nevada will be deducted from the 2 hours 
that are evenly divided for morning business, that the Democrats have 
the first half and the Republicans have the second half. Is that 
accurate?
```
