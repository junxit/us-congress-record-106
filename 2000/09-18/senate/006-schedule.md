# SCHEDULE

**Speaking:** Roberts, Pat (R000307) [R-KS]

**Measures:** S. 2045, H.R. 4444

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. ROBERTS. Today, the Senate will be in a period of morning 
business until 2 p.m., with Senators Graham and Thomas in control of 
the time. Following morning business, the Senate will resume 
consideration of H.R. 4444, the China PNTR legislation. Under the 
order, there are 6 hours of final debate on the China trade bill with a 
vote scheduled to occur at 2:15 on Tuesday.
  As a reminder, cloture was filed on the motion to proceed to S. 2045, 
the
H-1B visa bill on Friday. That cloture vote has been scheduled to occur 
immediately following the vote on final passage of the China PNTR 
legislation. Therefore, the first votes of this week will be two back-
to-back votes on Tuesday, at 2:15 p.m.
  I thank my colleagues for their attention.

                          ____________________
```
