# ORDER FOR ADJOURNMENT

**Speaking:** Specter, Arlen (S000709) [R-PA], Robb, Charles S. (R000295) [D-VA]

**Measures:** S.Res. 358

```
                         ORDER FOR ADJOURNMENT

  Mr. SPECTER. Madam President, if there is no further business to come 
before the Senate--and I note there are no other Senators on the 
floor--I ask unanimous consent that the Senate stand in adjournment 
under the provisions of S. Res. 358, following the remarks of Senator 
Robb.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. SPECTER. Madam President, I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will call the roll.
  The legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. ROBB. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that the order for 
the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The Senator from Virginia.

                          ____________________
```
