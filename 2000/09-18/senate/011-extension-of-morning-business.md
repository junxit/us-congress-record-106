# EXTENSION OF MORNING BUSINESS

```
                     EXTENSION OF MORNING BUSINESS

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Senator from New Mexico is recognized.
  Mr. DOMENICI. Parliamentary inquiry: Is there time now remaining to 
the Republicans to speak?
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Time has expired for morning business.
  Mr. DOMENICI. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent to be permitted 
to speak for an additional 10 minutes.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
