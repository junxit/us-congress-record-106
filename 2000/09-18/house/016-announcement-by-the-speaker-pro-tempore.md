# ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

```
                ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to clause 8 of rule XX, the Chair 
announces that he will postpone further proceedings today on each 
motion to suspend the rules on which a recorded vote or the yeas or 
nays are ordered, or on which the vote is objected to under clause 6 of 
rule XX.
  Such record votes on postponed questions will be taken after debate 
is concluded on all motions to suspend the rules but not before 6 p.m. 
today.
```
