# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF S. 1287, NUCLEAR WASTE POLICY AMENDMENTS ACT OF 2000

**Measures:** H.Res. 444, S. 1287

```
 REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF S. 1287, NUCLEAR 
                  WASTE POLICY AMENDMENTS ACT OF 2000

  Mr. DIAZ-BALART, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 106-532) on the resolution (H. Res. 444) providing 
for consideration of the Senate bill (S. 1287) to provide for the 
storage of spent nuclear fuel pending completion of the nuclear waste 
repository, and for other purposes, which was referred to the House 
Calendar and ordered to be printed.
```
