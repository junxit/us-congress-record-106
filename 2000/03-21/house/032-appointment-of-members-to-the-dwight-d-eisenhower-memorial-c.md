# APPOINTMENT OF MEMBERS TO THE DWIGHT D. EISENHOWER MEMORIAL COMMISSION

```
 APPOINTMENT OF MEMBERS TO THE DWIGHT D. EISENHOWER MEMORIAL COMMISSION

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Without objection, and pursuant to section 
8162(c)(3) of Public Law 106-79, the Chair announces the Speaker's 
appointment of the following Members of the House to the Dwight D. 
Eisenhower Memorial Commission:
  Mr. Thornberry of Texas,
  Mr. Moran of Kansas,
  Mr. Moore of Kansas, and
  Mr. Boswell of Iowa.
  There was no objection.
```
