# ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, MARCH 22, 2000

**Speaking:** Brownback, Sam (B000953) [R-KS]

**Measures:** H.R. 5

```
                  ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, MARCH 22, 2000

  Mr. BROWNBACK. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until the hour of 9:45 
a.m. on Wednesday, March 22. I further ask unanimous consent that on 
Wednesday, immediately following the prayer, the Journal of proceedings 
be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, the time for 
the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the day, and the 
Senate immediately begin the final debate on H.R. 5, the Social 
Security earnings legislation.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
