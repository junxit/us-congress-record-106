# READY TO PROCEED

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
                            READY TO PROCEED

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I know the Senator from Missouri wants to 
speak in morning business.
  We are ready to proceed on the issues that have been outlined. We are 
anxious to get to the Social Security earnings limit withdrawal. Also, 
we are anxious and look forward to the budget debate which will take 
place, we hope, next week. We must keep our eyes on the prize, and that 
is to do something about the $5 trillion debt that has accumulated, 
recognizing that is necessary for a tax cut for everybody in America.
```
