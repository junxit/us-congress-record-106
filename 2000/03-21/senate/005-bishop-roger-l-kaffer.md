# BISHOP ROGER L. KAFFER

**Speaking:** Fitzgerald, Peter (F000442) [R-IL]

```
                         BISHOP ROGER L. KAFFER

  Mr. FITZGERALD. Mr. President, I wish to speak for a couple of 
moments about our guest Chaplain, Bishop Roger Kaffer from Joliet, IL, 
who just gave the opening prayer.
  Bishop Kaffer is an old friend of my family. In fact, he went to 
grade school and to high school with my mother back in Joliet, IL--St. 
Raymond's grade school and Joliet Township high school. He is now the 
Auxiliary Bishop in the Joliet diocese outside of Chicago, IL.
  I thank him for his prayer and welcome him to the Senate. We very 
much appreciate it.
  I thank the Chair.

                          ____________________
```
