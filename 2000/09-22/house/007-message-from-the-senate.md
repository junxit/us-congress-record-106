# MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

**Measures:** S. 522, H.R. 999, S. 1810, S. 2046

```
                        MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

  A message from the Senate by Mr. Lundregan, one of its clerks, 
announced that the Senate has passed with an amendment in which the 
concurrence of the House is requested, a bill of the House of the 
following title:

       H.R. 999. An act to amend the Federal Water Pollution 
     Control Act to improve the quality of coastal recreation 
     waters, and for other purposes.

  The message also announced that the Senate has passed bills of the 
following titles in which the concurrence of the House is requested:

       S. 522. An act to amend the Federal Water Pollution Control 
     Act to improve the quality of beaches and coastal recreation 
     water, and for other purposes.
       S. 1810. An act to amend title 38, United States Code, to 
     expand and improve compensation and pension, education, 
     housing loan, insurance, and other benefits for veterans, and 
     for other purposes.
       S. 2046. An act to reauthorize the Next Generation Internet 
     Act, and for other purposes.

                          ____________________
```
