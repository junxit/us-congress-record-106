National Energy Security Act: Senate began consideration of the motion 
to proceed to consideration of S. 2557, to protect the energy security 
of the United States and decrease America's dependency on foreign oil 
sources to 50 percent by the Year 2010 by enhancing the use of 
renewable energy resources, conserving energy resources, improving 
energy efficiencies, and increasing domestic energy supplies, 
mitigating the effect of increases in energy prices on the American 
consumer, including the poor and the elderly.
  Page S9029

consideration of the bill on Monday, September 25, 2000.
