# Daily Digest/House Committee Meetings

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 293

```
Committee Meetings
OVERSIGHT--2000 CENSUS
Committee on Government Reform: Subcommittee on the Census held an 
oversight hearing on the 2000 Census: Accuracy and Coverage Evaluation-
Still More Questions than Answers. Testimony was heard from Kenneth 
Prewitt, Director, Bureau of the Census, Department of Commerce.
INTERNATIONAL CHILD ABDUCTION--HAGUE CONVENTION COMPLIANCE
Committee on International Relations: Favorably considered the 
following resolution and adopted a motion urging the Chairman to 
request that it be considered on the Suspension Calendar: H. Con. Res. 
293, urging compliance with the Hague Convention on the Civil Aspects 
of International Child Abduction.
```
