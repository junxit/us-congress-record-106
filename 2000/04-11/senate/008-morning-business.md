# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. There will now be a period for transaction of 
morning business not to extend beyond the hour of 12:30 p.m., with 
Senators permitted to speak for up to 5 minutes each.
  Under the previous order, the Democratic leader, or his designee, is 
recognized to speak for up to 75 minutes.
  The Senator from Illinois is recognized.
```
