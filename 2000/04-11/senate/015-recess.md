# RECESS

```
                                 RECESS

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. All time having been yielded back, under the 
previous order, the Senate is in recess until the hour of 2:15 p.m.
  Thereupon, at 12:41 p.m., the Senate recessed until 2:15 p.m.; 
whereupon, the Senate reassembled when called to order by the Presiding 
Officer (Mr. Inhofe).
```
