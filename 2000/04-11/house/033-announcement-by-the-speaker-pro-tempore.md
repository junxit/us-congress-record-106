# ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

**Measures:** H.Res. 465, H.R. 4051

```
                ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Shimkus). Pursuant to clause 8 of rule 
XX, the Chair announces that he will postpone further proceedings today 
on motions to suspend the rules on which a recorded vote or the yeas 
and nays are ordered, or on which the vote is objected to under clause 
6 of rule XX.
  Any record votes on House Resolution 465 and H.R. 4051 will be taken 
after debate has concluded on those motions.
  Record votes on remaining motions to suspend the rules will be taken 
later today.

                          ____________________
```
