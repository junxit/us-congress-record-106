# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.J. RES. 94, TAX LIMITATION CONSTITUTIONAL AMENDMENT

```
 REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.J. RES. 94, TAX 
                  LIMITATION CONSTITUTIONAL AMENDMENT

report (Rept. No. 106-574) on the resolution (H. Res. 471) providing
```
