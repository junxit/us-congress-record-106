# APPOINTMENT AS MEMBER TO NATIONAL SKILL STANDARDS BOARD

```
        APPOINTMENT AS MEMBER TO NATIONAL SKILL STANDARDS BOARD

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Without objection, pursuant to Section 
503(b)(3)

[[Page H2094]]
```
