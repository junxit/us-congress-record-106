# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 3039, CHESAPEAKE BAY RESTORATION ACT OF 1999

**Measures:** H.Res. 470, H.R. 3039

```
    REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 3039, 
                 CHESAPEAKE BAY RESTORATION ACT OF 1999

  Mr. REYNOLDS, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 106-573) on the resolution (H. Res. 470) providing 
for consideration of the bill (H.R. 3039) to amend the Federal Water 
Pollution Control Act to assist in the restoration of the Chesapeake 
Bay, and for other purposes, which was referred to the House Calendar 
and ordered to be printed.
```
