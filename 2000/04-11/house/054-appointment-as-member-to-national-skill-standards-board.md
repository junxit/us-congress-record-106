# APPOINTMENT AS MEMBER TO NATIONAL SKILL STANDARDS BOARD

```
        APPOINTMENT AS MEMBER TO NATIONAL SKILL STANDARDS BOARD

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Without objection, pursuant to Section 
503(b)(3) of the National Skill Standards Act of 1994 (20 U.S.C. 5933), 
and upon the recommendation of the majority leader, the Chair announces 
the Speaker's appointment of the following member on the part of the 
House to the National Skill Standards Board for a 4-year term to fill 
the existing vacancy thereon:
  Mr. William L. Lepley, Hershey, Pennsylvania.
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
