# ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, OCTOBER 18, 2000

```
                 ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, OCTOBER 18, 2000

  Mr. COCHRAN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it recess until the hour of 10 
a.m. on Wednesday, October 18. I further ask unanimous consent that on 
Wednesday, immediately following the prayer, the Journal of proceedings 
be approved to date, the time for the two leaders be reserved for their 
use later in the day, and the Senate then proceed to the conference 
report to accompany H.R. 4461, the Agriculture appropriations bill, as 
under the previous order.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
