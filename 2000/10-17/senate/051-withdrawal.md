# WITHDRAWAL

```
                               WITHDRAWAL

  Executive message transmitted by the President to the Senate on 
October 17, 2000, withdrawing from further Senate consideration the 
following nomination:


                         DEPARTMENT OF JUSTICE

       SARAH MC CRACKEN FOX, OF NEW YORK, TO BE A MEMBER OF THE 
     NATIONAL LABOR RELATIONS BOARD FOR A TERM OF FIVE YEARS 
     EXPIRING DECEMBER 16, 2004, TO WHICH POSITION SHE WAS 
     APPOINTED DURING THE RECESS OF THE SENATE FROM NOVEMBER 19, 
     1999, TO JANUARY 24, 2000, WHICH WAS SENT TO THE SENATE ON 
     MARCH 2, 2000.



             CONGRESSIONAL RECORD 

                United States
                 of America


October 17, 2000
```
