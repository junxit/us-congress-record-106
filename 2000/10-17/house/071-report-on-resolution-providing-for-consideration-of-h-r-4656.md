# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 4656, LAKE TAHOE BASIN LAND CONVEYANCE

**Measures:** H.Res. 634, H.R. 4656

```
  REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 4656, LAKE 
                      TAHOE BASIN LAND CONVEYANCE

  Mr. SESSIONS, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 106-985) on the resolution (H. Res. 634) providing 
for consideration of the bill (H.R. 4656) to authorize the Forest 
Service to convey certain lands in the Lake Tahoe Basin to the Washoe 
County School District for use as an elementary school site, which was 
referred to the House Calendar and ordered to be printed.

                          ____________________
```
