# PROGRAM

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. GRAMS. Mr. President, for the information of all Senators, the 
Senate will convene at noon tomorrow. Following approximately an hour 
of morning business, the Senate will begin consideration of the final 
appropriations bill if it has been received from the House. A vote is 
expected on the bill shortly after the morning hour, with the sine die 
adjournment to occur shortly after that.
```
