# ORDER OF PROCEDURE

**Speaking:** Domenici, Pete V. (D000407) [R-NM]

```
                           ORDER OF PROCEDURE

  Mr. DOMENICI. Mr. President, I note the presence on the floor of the 
distinguished Senator from Connecticut who just came off a major 
campaign. We welcome him back to the Senate. He has never stopped being 
a Senator, but he has been very busy doing other things.
  I yield so he can speak. I will speak following his remarks. So I ask 
unanimous consent that Senator Lieberman be permitted to speak for up 
to 15 minutes, and then I ask consent Senator Domenici be permitted to 
speak for 15 minutes thereafter.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
