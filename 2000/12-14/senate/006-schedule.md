# SCHEDULE

**Speaking:** Craig, Larry E. (C000858) [R-ID]

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. CRAIG. Mr. President, for the information of all Senators, the 
Senate will be in a period of morning business for a short time today.
  The Senate was expected to consider the final appropriations bill 
during today's session. However, because of changes in the House 
schedule, the Senate will not begin consideration of the final package 
until tomorrow morning pending its receipt from the House. It is hoped 
that the House can complete action shortly after noon tomorrow. 
Senators will be updated on the vote time throughout the day today.
  I think that probably covers the concerns of my colleagues, and I 
thank my colleagues for their attention.

                          ____________________
```
