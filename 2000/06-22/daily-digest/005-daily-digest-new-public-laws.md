# Daily Digest/NEW PUBLIC LAWS

**Measures:** H.R. 2559, H.R. 3642

```
                            NEW PUBLIC LAWS

      (For last listing of Public Laws, see Daily Digest, p. D640)
  H.R. 2559, to amend the Federal Crop Insurance Act to strengthen the 
safety net for agricultural producers by providing greater access to 
more affordable risk management tools and improved protection from 
production and income loss, to improve the efficiency and integrity of 
the Federal crop insurance program. Signed June 20, 2000. (P.L. 106-
224)
  H.R. 3642, to authorize the President to award posthumously a gold 
medal on behalf of the Congress to Charles M. Schulz in recognition of 
his lasting artistic contributions to the Nation and the world. Signed 
June 20, 2000. (P.L. 106-225)
```
