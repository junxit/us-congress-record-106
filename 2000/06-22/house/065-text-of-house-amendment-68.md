# Text of House Amendment 68

**Speaking:** Knollenberg, Joe (K000288) [R-MI]

**Measures:** H.R. 4690

```
H.R. 4690

                      Offered By: Mr. Knollenberg

       Amendment No. 68: At the end of Section 623 insert the 
     following: ``: Provided further, That any limitation imposed 
     under this act on funds made available by this act shall not 
     apply to any activities related to the Kyoto Protocol which 
     are otherwise authorized by law.''
```
