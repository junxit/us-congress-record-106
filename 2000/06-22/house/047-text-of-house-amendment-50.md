# Text of House Amendment 50

**Speaking:** Bilbray, Brian P. (B000461) [R-CA]

**Measures:** H.R. 4690

```
H.R. 4690

                        Offered By: Mr. Bilbray

       Amendment No. 50: Page 79, after line 22, insert the 
     following:

     In addition, for a feasibility study for the construction of 
     a sewage diversionary structure in the flood control channel 
     of the Tijuana River as it enters the United States, to be 
     derived by transfer from the amount provided in this title 
     for ``Diplomatic and Consular Programs'', $500,000.
```
