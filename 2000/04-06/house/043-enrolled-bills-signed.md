# ENROLLED BILLS SIGNED

```
                         ENROLLED BILLS SIGNED

  Mr. THOMAS, from the Committee on House Administration, reported that 
that committee had examined and found truly enrolled bills of the House 
of the following titles, which were thereupon signed by the Speaker:

       H.R. 1374. An act to designate the United States Post 
     Office building located at 680 U.S. Highway 130 in Hamilton, 
     New Jersey, as the ``John K. Rafferty Hamilton Post Office 
     Building''.
       H.R. 3189. An act to designate the United States Post 
     Office building located at 14071 Peyton Drive in Chino Hills, 
     California, as the ``Joseph Ileto Post Office''.
```
