# PROGRAM

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. SESSIONS. For the information of all Senators, the Senate will 
begin the vote-arama at 9 a.m. tomorrow morning. To make this process 
as smooth as possible, on behalf of the leader, I ask all Senators to 
remain in the Chamber between votes. As a reminder, there will be 2 
minutes, equally divided, between each vote for explanation of the 
amendments. The majority leader asks all Senators for their 
cooperation.
```
