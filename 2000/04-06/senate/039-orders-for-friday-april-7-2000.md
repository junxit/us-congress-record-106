# ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, APRIL 7, 2000

```
[[Page 4941]]

                    ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, APRIL 7, 2000

  Mr. SESSIONS. Mr. President, if there are no Senators seeking to 
speak in morning business, I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate 
completes its business today it adjourn until the hour of 9 a.m. on 
Friday, April 7. I further ask consent that on Friday, immediately 
following the prayer, the Journal of proceedings be approved to date, 
the morning hour be deemed expired, the time for the two leaders be 
reserved for their use later in the day, and the Senate then resume 
consideration of S. Con. Res. 101, the budget resolution.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
