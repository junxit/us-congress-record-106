**Speaking:** Spence, Floyd (S000718) [R-SC]

**Measures:** H.R. 4205

```
 ORDER OF CONSIDERATION OF AMENDMENTS DURING FURTHER CONSIDERATION OF 
   H.R. 4205, FLOYD D. SPENCE NATIONAL DEFENSE AUTHORIZATION ACT FOR 
                            FISCAL YEAR 2001

28 through 35, No. 10, and No. 27.
```
