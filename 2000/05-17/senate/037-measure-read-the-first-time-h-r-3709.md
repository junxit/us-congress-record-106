# MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--H.R. 3709

```
                 MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--H.R. 3709

  Mr. SESSIONS. Mr. President, I understand that H.R. 3709 is at the 
desk. I ask for its first reading.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report the bill by title.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (H.R. 3709) to extend for 5 years the moratorium 
     enacted by the Internet Tax Freedom Act, and for other 
     purposes.

  Mr. SESSIONS. I ask for its second reading and object to my own 
request.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Objection is heard.

                          ____________________
```
