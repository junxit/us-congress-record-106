# NOTICE OF HEARING

**Speaking:** Murkowski, Frank H. (M001085) [R-AK]

```
                           NOTICE OF HEARING


               Committee on Energy and Natural Resources

  Mr. MURKOWSKI. Mr. President, I would like to announce for the 
information of the Senate and the public that a hearing has been 
scheduled before the Senate Committee on Energy and Natural Resources 
to consider the outlook for America's natural gas demand.
  The hearing will take place on Thursday, May 25, 2000, beginning at 
9:30 a.m. in room SD-366 of the Dirksen Senate Office Building in 
Washington, DC.
  Presentation of oral testimony is by Committee invitation only. 
However, those who wish to submit written testimony for the hearing 
record should send two copies of their testimony to the Committee on 
Energy and Natural Resources, United States Senate, 364 Dirksen Senate 
Office Building, Washington, DC 20510-6150.
  For further information regarding the hearing, please contact Dan 
Kish at (202) 224-8276.

                          ____________________
```
