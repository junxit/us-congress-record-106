# ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, JULY 21, 2000

**Speaking:** Sessions, Jeff (S001141) [R-AL]

**Measures:** H.R. 4810

```
                    ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, JULY 21, 2000

  Mr. SESSIONS. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until the hour of 9 
a.m. on Friday, July 21. I further ask consent that on Friday, 
immediately following the prayer, the Journal of proceedings be 
approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, the time for the 
two leaders be reserved for their use later in the day, and the Senate 
then resume consideration of a conference report to accompany H.R. 
4810, the reconciliation bill.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
