# DISASTER WAITING TO HAPPEN ON U.S. BORDERS

```
               DISASTER WAITING TO HAPPEN ON U.S. BORDERS

  (Mr. TRAFICANT asked and was given permission to address the House 
for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. TRAFICANT. Mr. Speaker, a study finally admits, and I quote, 
``America's borders are so wide open, terrorists could easily smuggle a 
nuclear bomb across both our borders.'' Think about it, 3 million 
illegal immigrants, heroin and cocaine by the tons, and now a report 
that further says it is so bad in some areas orange cones are used like 
scarecrows with no border patrol presence at all.
  Unbelievable. We have soldiers vaccinating dogs in Haiti, while 
terrorists can bring nukes across our border. Beam me up here. Who 
master-minded this policy? The Proctologist Association of North 
America?
  Mr. Speaker, I yield back a disaster waiting to happen on the borders 
of the United States of America with a Congress sleeping at the switch.

                          ____________________
```
