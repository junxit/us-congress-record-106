```
REPORT ON RESOLUTION WAIVING POINTS OF ORDER AGAINST CONFERENCE REPORT 
 ON H.R. 4205, FLOYD D. SPENCE NATIONAL DEFENSE AUTHORIZATION ACT FOR 
                            FISCAL YEAR 2001

  Mr. REYNOLDS, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 106-952) on the resolution (H. Res. 616) waiving
```
