# REMOVAL OF NAME OF MEMBER AS COSPONSOR OF HOUSE RESOLUTION 184, H.R. 745, H.R. 1640, AND H.R. 3634

**Speaking:** Brady, Kevin (B000755) [R-TX]

**Measures:** H.Res. 184, H.R. 745, H.R. 1640, H.R. 3634

```
 REMOVAL OF NAME OF MEMBER AS COSPONSOR OF HOUSE RESOLUTION 184, H.R. 
                     745, H.R. 1640, AND H.R. 3634

  Mr. BRADY of Texas. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that my name 
be removed as a cosponsor from the following bills: House Resolution
```
