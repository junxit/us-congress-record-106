# ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

**Measures:** H.R. 4444

```
                ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to clause 4 of rule I, the Speaker 
signed the following enrolled bill earlier today:
  H.R. 4444, an act to authorize extension of nondiscriminatory 
treatment (normal trade relations treatment) to the People's Republic 
of China, and to establish a framework for relations between the United 
States and the People's Republic of China.
```
