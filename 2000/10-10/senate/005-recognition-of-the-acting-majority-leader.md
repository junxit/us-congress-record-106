# RECOGNITION OF THE ACTING MAJORITY LEADER

**Speaking:** Voinovich, George V. (V000126) [R-OH]

```
               RECOGNITION OF THE ACTING MAJORITY LEADER

  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. The acting majority leader is recognized.

                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. VOINOVICH. Mr. President, today the Senate will be in a period of 
morning business until 4 p.m. with Senators Durbin and Thomas in 
control of the time. Following morning business, the Senate is expected 
to consider the VA-HUD appropriations bill. It is hoped that 
legislation can be completed in short order and without a rollcall 
vote. However, if a rollcall vote is requested, the vote will occur 
tomorrow at a time to be determined.
  On Wednesday, there will be up to 7 hours of debate on the conference 
report to accompany the sex trafficking victims bill. Senator Thompson 
will make a point of order against the report, and a vote is expected 
relative to appealing the ruling of the Chair. Senators can also expect 
a vote on the adoption of the sex trafficking conference report. The 
Senate may also begin consideration of the Agriculture appropriations 
conference report during tomorrow's session.
  I thank my colleagues for their attention.

                          ____________________
```
