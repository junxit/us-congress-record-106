# ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, SEPTEMBER 14, 2000

```
                ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, SEPTEMBER 14, 2000

  Mr. CRAIG. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until the hour of 11 
a.m. on Thursday, September 14. I further ask unanimous consent that on 
Thursday, immediately following the prayer, the Journal of proceedings 
be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, the time for 
the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the day, and the 
Senate then resume consideration of H.R. 4444, the PNTR China 
legislation as under the previous order.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. CRAIG. I further ask unanimous consent the two leaders have an 
extra 10 minutes each for purposes of morning business during 
tomorrow's session.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
