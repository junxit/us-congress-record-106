# SCHEDULE

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. SANTORUM. Mr. President, today the Senate will be in a period of 
morning business until 10 a.m. Following morning business, there will 
be 60 minutes for closing remarks on two amendments: the Byrd amendment 
regarding safeguards and division 6 of the Smith amendment regarding 
organ harvesting.
  After all time is used or yielded back, there will be two back-to-
back votes at 11 a.m. Senators should be aware that there are 
amendments currently pending to the PNTR bill and further amendments 
are expected to be offered during today's session. Therefore, votes are 
expected throughout the day.
  I thank my colleagues for their attention.
  Mr. REID. Mr. President, at this time I ask the Chair to call regular 
order.
```
