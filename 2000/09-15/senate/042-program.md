# PROGRAM

**Measures:** S. 2045, H.R. 4444

```
                                PROGRAM

  MR. CRAIG. For the information of all Senators, the Senate will 
convene on Monday at 12 noon and be in a period of morning business 
until 2 p.m., with Senators Graham and Thomas in control of the time. 
Following morning business, the Senate will resume the final debate on 
H.R. 4444, the China PNTR legislation. Those Members who have closing 
remarks are encouraged to come to the floor during Monday's session.
  As a reminder, the first votes of next week will be two back-to-back 
votes on Tuesday, at 2:15 p.m. The first vote will be on final passage 
of the PNTR bill, and the second vote will be on cloture on the motion 
to proceed to S. 2045, the H-1B visa bill. The cloture motion was filed 
during today's session.

                          ____________________
```
