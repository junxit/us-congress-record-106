# COMMUNICATION FROM THE CLERK OF THE HOUSE

```
               COMMUNICATION FROM THE CLERK OF THE HOUSE

  The SPEAKER laid before the House the following communication from 
the Clerk of the House of Representatives:

                                     House of Representatives,

                                 Washington, DC, October 18, 2000.
     Hon. J. Dennis Hastert,
     The Speaker, House of Representatives,
     Washington, DC.
       Dear Mr. Speaker: Pursuant to the permission granted to 
     Clause 2(h) of Rule II of the Rules of the House of 
     Representatives, the Clerk received the following message 
     from the Secretary of the Senate on October 18, 2000, at 9:27 
     a.m.
       That the Senate passed without amendment H.R. 2296.
       That the Senate passed without amendment H.R. 5212.
       That the Senate passed without amendment H. Con. Res. 428.
       With best wishes, I am
           Sincerely,
                                                    Jeff Trandahl,
     Clerk of the House.

                          ____________________
```
