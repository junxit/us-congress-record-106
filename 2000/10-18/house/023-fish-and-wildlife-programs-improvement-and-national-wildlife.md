# FISH AND WILDLIFE PROGRAMS IMPROVEMENT AND NATIONAL WILDLIFE REFUGE SYSTEM CENTENNIAL ACT OF 2000

```
  FISH AND WILDLIFE PROGRAMS IMPROVEMENT AND NATIONAL WILDLIFE REFUGE 
                     SYSTEM CENTENNIAL ACT OF 2000

  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Pease). The unfinished business is the 
question of suspending the rules and concurring in the Senate 
amendments to the bill, H.R. 3671.
  The Clerk read the title of the bill.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The question is on the motion offered by the 
gentleman from Alaska (Mr. Young) that the House suspend the rules and 
concur in the Senate amendments to the bill, H.R. 3671.
  The question was taken; and (two-thirds having voted in favor 
thereof) the rules were suspended and the Senate amendments were 
concurred in.
  A motion to reconsider was laid on the table.
```
