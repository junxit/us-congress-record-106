# ORDER FOR ADJOURNMENT

```
                         ORDER FOR ADJOURNMENT

  Mr. CRAPO. Mr. President, if there is no further business to come 
before the Senate, I now ask that the Senate stand in adjournment under 
the previous order following the remarks of Senator Reed of Rhode 
Island.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. CRAPO. Mr. President, I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will call the roll.
  The assistant legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. REED. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the order for 
the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER (Mr. Brownback). Without objection, it is so 
ordered.
  Mr. REED. I ask to speak pursuant to the unanimous consent request.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Senator is recognized.

                          ____________________
```
