# ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, MARCH 1, 2000

```
                  ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, MARCH 1, 2000

  Mr. CRAPO. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until the hour of 9:30 
a.m., Wednesday, March 1. I further ask consent that on Wednesday, 
immediately following the prayer, the Journal of the proceedings be 
approved to date, the morning hour be deemed to have expired, the time 
for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the day, and the 
Senate resume debate on the pending Robb amendment to S. 1134, the 
education savings account bill.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
