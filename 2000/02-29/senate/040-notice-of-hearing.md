# NOTICE OF HEARING

**Speaking:** Murkowski, Frank H. (M001085) [R-AK]

```
                           NOTICE OF HEARING


               committee on energy and natural resources

  Mr. MURKOWSKI. Mr. President, I would like to announce for the 
information of the Senate and the public that a hearing has been 
scheduled before the Senate Committee on Energy and Natural Resources 
to consider the President's proposed FY 2001 budget for the U.S. Forest 
Service. The hearing will be held on Tuesday, February 29, 2000, 
beginning at 2:30 p.m., in room SD-366 of the Dirksen Senate Office 
Building in Washington, D.C.
  Those who wish to submit written statements, should write to the 
Committee on Energy and Natural Resources, U.S. Senate, Washington, DC. 
20510. For further information, please call Mark Rey, Professional 
Staff Member, at (202) 224-2878.

                          ____________________
```
