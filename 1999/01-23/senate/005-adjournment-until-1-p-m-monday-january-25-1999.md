# ADJOURNMENT UNTIL 1 P.M. MONDAY, JANUARY 25, 1999

**Speaking:** Lott, Trent (L000447) [R-MS], Harkin, Tom (H000206) [D-IA]

```
           ADJOURNMENT UNTIL 1 P.M. MONDAY, JANUARY 25, 1999

  Mr. LOTT. Mr. Chief Justice, I ask that the Senate stand in 
adjournment under the previous order.
  Mr. HARKIN. I object.
  Mr. LOTT. Mr. Chief Justice, I move that the Senate stand in 
adjournment under the previous order.
  Mr. HARKIN. Mr. Chief Justice, I seek recognition.
  The CHIEF JUSTICE. The question is on the motion to adjourn.
  The motion was agreed to.
  Thereupon, at 3:55 p.m., the Senate, sitting as a Court of 
Impeachment, adjourned until Monday, January 25, 1999, at 1 p.m.
```
