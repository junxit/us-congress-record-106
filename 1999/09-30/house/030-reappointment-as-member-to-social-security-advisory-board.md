# REAPPOINTMENT AS MEMBER TO SOCIAL SECURITY ADVISORY BOARD

```
[[Page 23350]]

       REAPPOINTMENT AS MEMBER TO SOCIAL SECURITY ADVISORY BOARD

  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Barrett of Nebraska). Without objection, 
and pursuant to section 703 of the Social Security Act (42 U.S.C. 903) 
as amended by section 103 of Public Law 103-296, and upon the 
recommendation of the Minority Leader, the Chair announces the 
Speaker's reappointment


of the following Member on the part of the House to the Social Security 
Advisory Board for a 6-year term:

  Ms. Martha Keys of Virginia.

  There was no objection.
```
