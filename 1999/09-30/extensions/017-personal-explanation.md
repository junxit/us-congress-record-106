                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. XAVIER BECERRA

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, September 30, 1999

  Mr. BECERRA. Mr. Speaker, on September 30, 1999, I was unavoidably 
detained during three rollcall votes: number 460, H. Res. 312 on 
Agreeing to the Resolution Providing for Consideration of H.R. 2910, 
National Transportation Safety Board Authorization; number 461 on 
Approving the Journal; and number 462 on Passage of H.R. 2910, the 
National Transportation Safety Board Authorization. Had I been present 
for the votes, I would have voted ``aye'' on rollcall votes 460, 461, 
and 462.
