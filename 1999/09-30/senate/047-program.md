# PROGRAM

```
[[Page 23344]]

                                PROGRAM

  Mr. SESSIONS. For the information of all Senators, the Senate will 
convene at 9 a.m. tomorrow and immediately begin 30 minutes of debate 
on the Collins amendment regarding diabetes. At the expiration of that 
debate, the Senate will proceed to a vote on the amendment. Therefore, 
Senators may expect the first vote at approximately 9:30 a.m. Further 
consideration of the Labor-HHS bill is expected during tomorrow's 
session of the Senate, to be followed by a period of morning business.
```
