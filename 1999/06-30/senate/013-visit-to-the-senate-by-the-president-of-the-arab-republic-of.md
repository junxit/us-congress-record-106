# VISIT TO THE SENATE BY THE PRESIDENT OF THE ARAB REPUBLIC OF EGYPT, MOHAMMED HOSNI MUBARAK

```
  VISIT TO THE SENATE BY THE PRESIDENT OF THE ARAB REPUBLIC OF EGYPT, 
                         MOHAMMED HOSNI MUBARAK

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The distinguished chairman of the Foreign 
Relations Committee, Senator Helms, is recognized.
  Mr. HELMS. I thank the Chair.
  Mr. President, I have the honor and privilege of presenting to 
Members of the Senate and to the Pages the distinguished and very 
popular President of the Republic of Egypt, Mohammed Hosni Mubarak.
```
