# MISS MISSOURI 1999

```
                           MISS MISSOURI 1999

● Mr. BOND. Mr. President, I rise today to recognize the winner 
of the 1999 Miss Missouri Pageant, Miss Patryce CoRae King. On June 
12th, in my home town of Mexico, MO, Miss King won the State pageant 
and will go on to represent Missouri in the Miss America Pageant. Miss 
King is an accomplished pianist and won the talent competition of the 
pageant with a rendition of Gershwin's ``Rhapsody in Blue.'' I wish 
Miss King the best of luck and know that she will represent Missouri 
well at the Miss America Pageant in September.●

                          ____________________
```
