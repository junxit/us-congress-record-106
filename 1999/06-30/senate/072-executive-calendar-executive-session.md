                           EXECUTIVE CALENDAR

  Mr. DeWINE. Mr. President, I would like to now, on behalf of the 
leadership, the majority leader, Senator Lott, ask unanimous consent 
that the Senate immediately proceed to executive session to consider 
the following nominations on the Executive Calendar:
  Nos. 109 through 130, and all nominations on the Secretary's desk in 
the Air Force, the Army, the Coast Guard, the Marine Corps, and the 
Navy.
  I further ask unanimous consent that the nominations be confirmed, 
the motions to reconsider be laid upon the table, and that any 
statements relating to the nominations be printed in the Record, that 
the President be immediately notified of the Senate's action, and the 
Senate then return to legislative session.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
