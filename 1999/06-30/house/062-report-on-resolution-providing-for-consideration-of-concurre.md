# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF CONCURRENT RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR ADJOURNMENT OF HOUSE AND SENATE FOR INDEPENDENCE DAY WORK PERIOD

**Measures:** H.Res. 236

```
    REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF CONCURRENT 
     RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR ADJOURNMENT OF HOUSE AND SENATE FOR 
                      INDEPENDENCE DAY WORK PERIOD

  Mr. SESSIONS, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 106-215) on the resolution (H. Res. 236) providing 
for consideration of a concurrent resolution providing for adjournment 
of the House and Senate for the Independence Day district work period, 
which was referred to the House Calendar and ordered to be printed.
```
