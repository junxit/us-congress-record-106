# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Smith, Adam (S000510) [D-WA]

**Measures:** H.R. 1714

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. ADAM SMITH

                             of washington

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, November 5, 1999

  Mr. SMITH of Washington. Mr. Speaker, on the afternoon of November 1, 
I was attending to family business in my district and was unable to 
vote on H.R. 1714, legislation to provide for digital signatures.
  Had I been present, I would have voted ``yes.'' I strongly support 
this legislation to ensure that our high-technology economy continues 
to grow and provides consumers more opportunities to conduct business 
on-line.
```
