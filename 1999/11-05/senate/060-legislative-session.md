# LEGISLATIVE SESSION

```
                          LEGISLATIVE SESSION

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, the Senate will now 
return to legislative session.
  Mr. DOMENICI. I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will call the roll.
  The legislative assistant proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. DOMENICI. I ask unanimous consent that the order for the quorum 
call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. DOMENICI. Mr. President, I am prepared to recite the closing 
script, but I understand the distinguished Senator from Alabama wants 
to be recognized.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Does the Senator want to go through with that 
and just accept whatever statement the Senator from Alabama wishes to 
make?
  Mr. DOMENICI. All right.

                          ____________________
```
