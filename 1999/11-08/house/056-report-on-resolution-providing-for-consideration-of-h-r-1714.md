# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 1714, ELECTRONIC SIGNATURES IN GLOBAL AND NATIONAL COMMERCE ACT

```
    REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 1714, 
       ELECTRONIC SIGNATURES IN GLOBAL AND NATIONAL COMMERCE ACT

  Ms. PRYCE of Ohio, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a 
privileged report (Rept. No. 106-462) on the resolution (H. Res. 366)
```
