# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 3073, FATHERS COUNT ACT OF 1999

**Measures:** H.Res. 367, H.R. 3073

```
REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 3073, FATHERS 
                           COUNT ACT OF 1999

  Ms. PRYCE of Ohio, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a 
privileged report (Rept. No. 106-463) on the resolution (H. Res. 367) 
providing for consideration of the bill (H.R. 3073) to amend part A of 
title IV of the Social Security Act to provide for grants for projects 
designed to promote responsible fatherhood, and for other purposes, 
which was referred to the House Calendar and ordered to be printed.
```
