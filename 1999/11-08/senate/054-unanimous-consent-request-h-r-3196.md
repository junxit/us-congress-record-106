# UNANIMOUS CONSENT REQUEST--H.R. 3196

**Speaking:** Grassley, Chuck (G000386) [R-IA], Landrieu, Mary L. (L000550) [D-LA]

**Measures:** H.R. 3196

```
                  UNANIMOUS CONSENT REQUEST--H.R. 3196

  Mr. GRASSLEY. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
now proceed to the consideration of H.R. 3196, the foreign operations 
appropriations bill. I further ask consent that a substitute amendment, 
which is at the desk, be agreed to, the bill be read a third time and 
passed, the motion to reconsider be laid upon the table, and any 
statement relating to the bill be printed in the Record. I further ask 
consent that the Senate insist on its amendment and request a 
conference with the House.
  Ms. LANDRIEU. I object.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Objection is heard.

                          ____________________
```
