# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Grassley, Chuck (G000386) [R-IA]

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. GRASSLEY. Mr. President, for the information of all Senators, the 
Senate will resume consideration of the bankruptcy bill at 9:30 on 
Tuesday. There will be 1 hour of debate on the pending minimum wage and 
business cost amendments, with votes scheduled to occur at 10:30 a.m. 
Further amendments are expected to be offered and debated and therefore 
votes are expected throughout tomorrow's session of the Senate. 
Senators can also anticipate votes regarding the appropriations process 
prior to the Veterans Day recess.
```
