Impeachment of President Clinton: Senate, sitting as a Court of Impeachment, 
continued consideration of the articles of impeachment against William 
Jefferson Clinton, President of the United States, taking the following 
action:
  Pages S1385-88
  By 59 yeas to 41 nays (Vote No. 15), two-thirds of those Senators voting, 
a quorum being present, not having voted in the affirmative, Senate rejected 
the Lott motion to suspend Senate rules.
Pages S1385-86
  By voice vote, Senate agreed to the Lott/Daschle motion to provide that 
any Senator may insert their final deliberations on the Articles of 
Impeachment during proceedings held in closed session in the Congressional 
Record at the conclusion of the trial.
Pages S1386-87
  By 53 yeas to 47 nays (Vote No. 16), Senate agreed to the Lott motion to 
close the doors of the Senate Chamber.
Pages S1387-88
  Senate will continue to sit as a Court of Impeachment on Wednesday, 
February 10, 1999.
