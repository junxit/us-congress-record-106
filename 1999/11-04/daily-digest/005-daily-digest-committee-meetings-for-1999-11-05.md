# Daily Digest/COMMITTEE MEETINGS FOR 1999-11-05

```
                     COMMITTEE MEETINGS FOR FRIDAY,

                            NOVEMBER 5, 1999

        (Committee meetings are open unless otherwise indicated)


                                 Senate

  Committee on Banking, Housing, and Urban Affairs: to hold hearings 
on the nomination of Gregory A. Baer, of Virginia, to be an 
Assistant Secretary of the Treasury; and the nomination of Susan M. 
Wachter, of Pennsylvania, to be an Assistant Secretary of Housing 
and Urban Development, 10 a.m., SD-538.
  Committee on Foreign Relations: to hold hearings to examine issues 
relating to the International Monetary Fund, focusing on lessons 
learned from the Asian financial crisis, 11 a.m., SD-419.
  Full Committee, to hold hearings on the nomination of Carol 
Moseley-Braun, of Illinois, to be Ambassador to New Zealand, 1 p.m., 
SD-419.


                                 House

  Committee on Resources. Subcommittee on Fisheries Conservation, 
Wildlife and Oceans, oversight hearing to examine the affects on 
living marine resources from dredged material disposal or placement 
in the New York Bight, 10 a.m., 1324 Longworth.
```
