# MAKING FURTHER CONTINUING APPROPRIATIONS FOR THE FISCAL YEAR 2000

```
   MAKING FURTHER CONTINUING APPROPRIATIONS FOR THE FISCAL YEAR 2000

  Mr. LOTT. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate now 
proceed to the consideration of H.J. Res. 75, the continuing resolution 
received from the House. I further ask unanimous consent that the joint 
resolution be read a third time, passed, and the motion to reconsider 
be laid upon the table.
  This has been cleared with the Democratic leader.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The joint resolution (H.J. Res. 75) was read the third time and 
passed.
```
