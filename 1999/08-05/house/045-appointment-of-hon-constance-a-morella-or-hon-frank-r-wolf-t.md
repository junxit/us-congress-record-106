# APPOINTMENT OF HON. CONSTANCE A. MORELLA OR HON. FRANK R. WOLF TO ACT AS SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE TO SIGN ENROLLED BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS THROUGH SEPTEMBER 8, 1999

```
       APPOINTMENT OF HON. CONSTANCE A. MORELLA OR HON. FRANK R. 
     WOLF TO ACT AS SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE TO SIGN ENROLLED BILLS AND 
              JOINT RESOLUTIONS THROUGH SEPTEMBER 8, 1999

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following 
communication from the Speaker:

                                               Washington, DC,

                                                   August 5, 1999.
       I hereby appoint the Honorable Constance A. Morella or, if 
     not available to perform this duty, the Honorable Frank R. 
     Wolf to act as Speaker pro tempore to sign enrolled bills and 
     joint resolutions through September 8, 1999.
                                                J. Dennis Hastert,
                          Speaker of the House of Representatives.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Without objection, the appointment is agreed 
to.
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
