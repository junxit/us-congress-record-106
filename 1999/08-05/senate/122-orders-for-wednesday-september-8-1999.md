# ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, SEPTEMBER 8, 1999

```
                ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, SEPTEMBER 8, 1999

  Mr. BROWNBACK. Mr. President, we have been through a lot. I now ask 
unanimous consent that when the Senate completes its business today, it 
adjourn until the hour of 12 noon on Wednesday, September 8. I further 
ask consent that on Wednesday, immediately following the prayer, the 
Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed 
expired, the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later 
in the day, and the Senate then begin 1 hour of morning business.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
