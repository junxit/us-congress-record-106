# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Brownback, Sam (B000953) [R-KS]

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. BROWNBACK. For the information of all Senators, the Senate will 
convene on Wednesday, September 8, at 12 noon, with morning business 
until 1 p.m. Following morning business, the Senate will resume 
consideration of the pending Interior bill. Any votes ordered on that 
bill will be stacked to occur at 5:30 p.m. on Wednesday, September 8. 
As a reminder, a cloture motion on the Transportation appropriations 
bill was filed today, and by previous order that vote will occur at 
9:30 a.m. on Thursday, September 9.
  Further, the Senate may also begin consideration of the bankruptcy 
bill following completion of the Interior appropriations bill.

                          ____________________
```
