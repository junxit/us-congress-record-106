# NATIONAL OILHEAT RESEARCH ALLIANCE ACT OF 1999

**Measures:** S. 348

```
             NATIONAL OILHEAT RESEARCH ALLIANCE ACT OF 1999

                                 ______
                                 

                      MURKOWSKI AMENDMENT NO. 1570

  (Ordered to lie on the table.)
  Mr. MURKOWSKI submitted an amendment intended to be proposed by him 
to the bill (S. 348) to authorize and facilitate a program to enhance 
training, research and development, energy conservation and efficiency, 
and consumer education in the oilheat industry for the benefit of 
oilheat consumers and the public, and for other purposes; as follows:

       On page 6, after line 18, insert the following:
       ``(15) State.--The term ``State'' means the several states, 
     except the State of Alaska.''.
```
