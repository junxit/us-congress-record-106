# PROGRAM

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. SMITH of New Hampshire. Mr. President, for the information of all 
Senators, the Senate will begin consideration of the Nuclear Test Ban 
Treaty at 9:30 a.m. on Friday. By previous consent, debate time is 
limited to 14 hours equally divided between the two leaders. Debate on 
the treaty is expected to take place throughout the day tomorrow and 
will resume at 9:30 a.m. on Tuesday.
  As a reminder, cloture was filed on the conference report to 
accompany the Agriculture appropriations bill today.
  By a previous consent, the Senate will proceed to the cloture vote 
Tuesday, October 12, at 5:30 p.m. It is hoped that the vote regarding 
the Nuclear Test Ban Treaty can be stacked to follow that 5:30 vote. 
Therefore, the next rollcall vote will occur at 5:30 p.m. on Tuesday, 
October 12.

                          ____________________
```
