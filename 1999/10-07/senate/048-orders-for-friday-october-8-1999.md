# ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, OCTOBER 8, 1999

```
[[Page 24558]]

                   ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, OCTOBER 8, 1999

  Mr. SMITH of New Hampshire. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent 
that when the Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until the 
hour of 9:30 a.m. on Friday, October 8.


I further ask unanimous consent that on Friday, immediately following 
the prayer, the Journal of the proceedings be approved to date, the 
morning hour be deemed to have expired, the time for the two leaders be 
reserved for their use later in the day, and the Senate then proceed to 
executive session for consideration of the Comprehensive Nuclear Test 
Ban Treaty.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
