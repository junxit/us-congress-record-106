# HEALTH CARE REFORM

**Speaking:** Hayes, Robin (H001029) [R-NC]

```
                           HEALTH CARE REFORM

  (Mr. HAYES asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. HAYES. Madam Speaker, this morning I rise to simply say that the 
people in the 8th District of North Carolina care about access, they 
care about quality, they care about affordability. That is what we on 
our side of the aisle care about this morning. We want to provide that.
  The language that some of my liberal friends use may be good 
politics, but it is bad medicine for the people in the 8th District. 
Support the bill that gives access, that gives affordability, and give 
quality to the people of America. Support Boehner. Support Shadegg/
Coburn.

                          ____________________
```
