# IT IS NOT MANAGED CARE ANY MORE, IT IS MANAGED COSTS

**Speaking:** Traficant, James A., Jr. (T000350) [D-OH]

```
          IT IS NOT MANAGED CARE ANY MORE, IT IS MANAGED COSTS

  (Mr. TRAFICANT asked and was given permission to address the House 
for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. TRAFICANT. Madam Speaker, America's the land of the free, but one 
cannot choose one's doctor. Freedom of speech; but doctors are gagged. 
Judicial review; HMOs are judge and jury.
  Madam Speaker, health care in America has gone from the Constitution 
to HMOs. Beam me up. It is not managed care any more; it is managed 
costs. It is time for Congress to vote on behalf of the American people 
and pass the Patients' Bill of Rights, stone cold simple remedy today.
  I yield back more medicine than ever in America and less health care.

                          ____________________
```
