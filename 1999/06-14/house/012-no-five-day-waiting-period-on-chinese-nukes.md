# NO FIVE-DAY WAITING PERIOD ON CHINESE NUKES

**Speaking:** Traficant, James A., Jr. (T000350) [D-OH]

```
              NO FIVE-DAY WAITING PERIOD ON CHINESE NUKES

  (Mr. TRAFICANT asked and was given permission to address the House 
for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. TRAFICANT. Mr. Speaker, China spies and buys our secrets. Then 
China points their missiles at American cities. Now if that is not 
enough to put trigger locks on Chinese missiles, a White House 
spokesman said, and I quote, ``We will grant China swift admission to 
the World Trade Organization.'' Swift admission no less. Beam me up 
here. I am firmly convinced those experts at the White House are 
smoking dope.
  I yield back the fact that there is no 5-day waiting period on 
Chinese nukes. Think about that.

                          ____________________
```
