# FLAG DAY

**Speaking:** Lott, Trent (L000447) [R-MS]

```
                                FLAG DAY

  Mr. LOTT. Mr. President, I thank the Chaplain, as always, for his 
beautiful prayer and for recognizing this is Flag Day, June 14. It is a 
day in which we should all take a moment to be proud and thankful for 
the country that we live in because the flag is the symbol of our 
country, and it is appropriate that we honor it on this day, June 14.
  (Mrs. HUTCHISON assumed the Chair.)
```
