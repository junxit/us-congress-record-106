# WELCOME TO THE NEW SENATE PAGES

```
                    WELCOME TO THE NEW SENATE PAGES

  Mr. LOTT. Madam President, I take note that we have a new group of 
pages that are joining us today. We look forward to having their 
presence and their assistance as we carry out our duties on behalf of 
the American people. They will be playing an important role in how the 
Senate conducts itself. We are delighted to have them here and we 
welcome them aboard.
  Madam President, I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will call the roll.
  The assistant legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.

                          ____________________
```
