# CONGRATULATIONS TO THE KARBIN FAMILY

**Speaking:** Gutierrez, Luis V. (G000535) [D-IL]

```
                  CONGRATULATIONS TO THE KARBIN FAMILY

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. LUIS V. GUTIERREZ

                              of illinois

                    in the house of representatives

                       Wednesday, August 4, 1999

  Mr. GUTIERREZ. Mr. Speaker, I am very pleased today to honor two of 
my neighbors and constituents on a very happy and joyous occasion.

[[Page E1744]]

  On February 21st, my friends Carolyn and Martin Karbin welcomed to 
the world a beautiful baby girl. Laura Marie Karbin was born at 8:37 
a.m., she was 21.5 inches long and weighed eight pounds, three ounces.
  I want to share my good wishes and warmest congratulations with the 
Karbin family. I know that Laura will receive the best of guidance, 
support and love from her parents and I wish her a life filled with 
peace, happiness and good health.

                          ____________________
```
