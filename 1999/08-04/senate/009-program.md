# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Cochran, Thad (C000567) [R-MS]

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. COCHRAN. Mr. President, when the Senate receives the Tax 
Reconciliation conference report from the House of Representatives, it 
will begin consideration of that legislation. Therefore, Senators 
should expect votes into the evening during Thursday's session of the 
Senate.

[[Page S10200]]

    

                  ADJOURNMENT UNTIL 9:30 A.M. TOMORROW

  Mr. COCHRAN. If there is no further business to come before the 
Senate, I now ask unanimous consent the Senate stand in adjournment 
under the previous order.
  There being no objection, the Senate, at 9:48 p.m., adjourned until 
Thursday, August 5, 1999, at 9:30 a.m.
```
