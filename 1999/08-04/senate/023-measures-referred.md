# MEASURES REFERRED

**Measures:** H.R. 987, H.R. 2031

```
                           MEASURES REFERRED

  The following bills were read the first and second times by unanimous 
consent and referred as indicated:

       H.R. 987. An act to require the Secretary of Labor to wait 
     for completion of a National Academy of Sciences study before 
     promulgating a standard or guideline on ergonomics; to the 
     Committee on Health, Education, Labor, and Pensions.
       H.R. 2031. An act to provide for injunctive relief in 
     Federal district court to enforce State laws relating to the 
     interstate transportation of intoxicating liquor; to the 
     Committee on the Judiciary.
```
