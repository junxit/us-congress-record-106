# EXTENSION OF MORNING BUSINESS

**Speaking:** Gregg, Judd (G000445) [R-NH]

```
                     EXTENSION OF MORNING BUSINESS

  Mr. GREGG. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the period for 
morning business be extended until 3 p.m. and that the time be equally 
divided between the minority and majority.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
