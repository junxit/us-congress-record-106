# ADJOURNMENT UNTIL 9:30 A.M., TUESDAY, MARCH 2, 1999

```
          ADJOURNMENT UNTIL 9:30 A.M., TUESDAY, MARCH 2, 1999

  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the Senate 
stands adjourned until 9:30 a.m. tomorrow, Tuesday, March 2, 1999.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 10 o'clock and 41 seconds a.m., adjourned 
until Tuesday, March 2, 1999, at 9:30 a.m.
```
