# MORNING BUSINESS

**Speaking:** Hagel, Chuck (H001028) [R-NE]

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, there will now be a 
period for the transaction of morning business not to extend beyond the 
hour of 10:30 a.m. Senators are permitted to speak therein for up to 5 
minutes each.
  Under the previous order, the Senator from Nebraska or his designee 
is recognized to speak for up to 30 minutes.
  Mr. HAGEL. Mr. President, I yield such time as I may require.
```
