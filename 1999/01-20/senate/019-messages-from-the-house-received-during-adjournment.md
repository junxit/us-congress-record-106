# MESSAGES FROM THE HOUSE RECEIVED DURING ADJOURNMENT

```
          MESSAGES FROM THE HOUSE RECEIVED DURING ADJOURNMENT

  Under the authority of the order of the Senate of January 6, 1999, 
the Secretary of the Senate, on January 20, during the adjournment of 
the Senate, received a message from the House of Representatives 
announcing that the House has agreed to the following concurrent 
resolution, in which it requests the concurrence of the Senate:

       H. Con. Res. 11. Concurrent resolution providing for an 
     adjournment of the House.
```
