# PROVIDING FOR THE INTRODUCTION OF LEGISLATION AND SUBMISSION OF STATEMENTS

```
    PROVIDING FOR THE INTRODUCTION OF LEGISLATION AND SUBMISSION OF 
                               STATEMENTS

  Mr. LOTT. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that on Thursday and 
Friday it be in order for Senators to introduce legislation and to 
submit statements at the desk during the Senate's consideration of the 
articles of impeachment.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
