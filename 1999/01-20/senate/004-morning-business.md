# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, there will now 
be a period for the transaction of morning business until the hour of 1 
p.m., with 60 minutes under the control of the Democratic leader and 60 
minutes under the control of the Senator from Georgia, Mr. Coverdell, 
or his designee.
```
