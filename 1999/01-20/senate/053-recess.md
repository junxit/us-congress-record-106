# RECESS

**Speaking:** Coverdell, Paul (C000813) [R-GA]

```
[[Page S810]]
                                 RECESS

  Mr. COVERDELL. Mr. President, if there is no further business to come 
before the Senate, I now ask unanimous consent that the Senate stand in 
recess under the previous order.
  There being no objection, at 12:55 p.m., the Senate, in legislative 
session, recessed until 1:05 p.m.; whereupon, the Senate, sitting as a 
Court of Impeachment, reassembled when called to order by the Chief 
Justice.
```
