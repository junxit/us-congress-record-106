# THE AMERICAN PEOPLE DESERVE SOME ANSWERS

**Speaking:** Duncan, John J., Jr. (D000533) [R-TN]

```
                THE AMERICAN PEOPLE DESERVE SOME ANSWERS

  (Mr. DUNCAN asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute.)
  Mr. DUNCAN. Mr. Speaker, the American people need to ask many 
questions about our relationship with China. Why did the President 
approve the sale of missile technology to the Chinese against the 
objection of his own Defense Department, his own State Department and 
his own Justice Department? Was it because of the millions of dollars 
of campaign contributions from the Chinese military and top executives 
of the Hughes Electronics Corporation? Why over the last 5 years have 
there been 3,567 requests for wiretaps and search warrants under the 
Foreign Intelligence Surveillance Act and only one turned down, and 
that one involving Mr. Lee and the spying at Los Alamos?
  There are many other questions exactly like these. The American 
people deserve some answers.
  The Cox report says the Chinese espionage goes on even to this day. 
Things are going on today that have never happened before in the 
history of this Nation, Mr. Speaker, and the American people deserve to 
know why.

                          ____________________
```
