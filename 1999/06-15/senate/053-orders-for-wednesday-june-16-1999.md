# ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, JUNE 16, 1999

```
                  ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, JUNE 16, 1999

  Mr. JEFFORDS. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it stand in adjournment until 10 
a.m. on Wednesday, June 16. I further ask that on Wednesday, 
immediately following the prayer, the Journal of proceedings be 
approved to date, the morning hour be deemed to have expired, and the 
time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the day.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
