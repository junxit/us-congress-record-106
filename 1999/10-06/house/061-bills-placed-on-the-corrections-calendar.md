# BILLS PLACED ON THE CORRECTIONS CALENDAR

**Measures:** H.R. 576

```
                BILLS PLACED ON THE CORRECTIONS CALENDAR

  Under clause 4 of rule XIII, the Speaker filed with the Clerk a 
notice requesting that the following bill be placed upon the 
Corrections Calendar:

       H.R. 576. A bill to amend title 4, United States Code, to 
     add the Martin Luther King, Jr. holiday to the list of days 
     on which the flag should especially be displayed.
```
