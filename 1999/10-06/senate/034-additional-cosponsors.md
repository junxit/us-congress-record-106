# ADDITIONAL COSPONSORS

```
                         ADDITIONAL COSPONSORS


                                 S. 185

  At the request of Mr. Ashcroft, the names of the Senator from South 
Dakota (Mr. Johnson), the Senator from Iowa (Mr. Harkin), and the

185, a bill to establish a Chief Agricultural Negotiator in the Office 
of the United States Trade Representative.

authorize the extension of nondiscriminatory treatment (normal trade 
relations treatment) to the products of Kyrgyzstan.


                                 S. 446

  At the request of Mrs. Boxer, the name of the Senator from New York 
(Mr. Moynihan) was added as a cosponsor of S. 446, a bill to provide 
for the permanent protection of the resources of the United States in 
the year 2000 and beyond.

(Mr.

timely development of a more cost effective United States commercial

                                 S. 631

  At the request of Mr. DeWine, the name of the Senator from Delaware 
(Mr. Biden) was added as a cosponsor of S. 631, a bill to amend the 
Social Security Act to eliminate the time limitation on benefits for

requirements.

incentives for the purchase of alternative fuel and electric vehicle, 
and for other purposes.


                                S. 1085

  At the request of Mrs. Murray, the name of the Senator from Oregon 
(Mr. Smith) was added as a cosponsor of S. 1085, a bill to amend the

  At the request of Mr. Grams, the name of the Senator from 
Pennsylvania (Mr. Santorum) was added as a cosponsor of S. 1102, a bill

  At the request of Mr. Grams, the name of the Senator from Alabama

Poultry Products Inspection Act to cover birds of the order Ratitae

                                S. 1155

  At the request of Mr. Roberts, the name of the Senator from Georgia

                                S. 1187

  At the request of Mr. Dorgan, the name of the Senator from Idaho (Mr.

Secretary of the Treasury to mint coins in commemoration of the 
bicentennial of the Lewis and Clark Expedition, and for other purposes.


                                S. 1272

  At the request of Mr. Nickles, the name of the Senator from Idaho 
(Mr. Crapo) was added as a cosponsor of S. 1272, a bill to amend the

                                S. 1277

  At the request of Mr. Baucus, the name of the Senator from New Mexico

  At the request of Mr. Bingaman, the name of the Senator from Arizona

leasing of oil and gas rights on certain lands held in trust for the 
Navajo Nation or allotted to a member of the Navajo Nation, in any case

                                S. 1384

                                S. 1445

                                S. 1452

                                S. 1488

  At the request of Mr. Gorton, the names of the Senator from Indiana

protections from civil liability arising from the emergency use of the 
devices.
```
