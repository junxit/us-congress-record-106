# ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, OCTOBER 7, 1999

**Speaking:** Warner, John (W000154) [R-VA]

**Measures:** S. 1650

```
                  ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, OCTOBER 7, 1999

  Mr. WARNER. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until the hour of 9:30 
a.m. on Thursday, October 7. I further ask consent that on Thursday, 
immediately following the prayer, the Journal of proceedings be 
approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, the time for the 
two leaders be reserved for their use later in the day, and the Senate 
then resume debate on the pending Abraham amendment to S. 1650, the 
Labor-HHS Appropriations bill.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
