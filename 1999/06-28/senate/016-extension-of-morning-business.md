# EXTENSION OF MORNING BUSINESS

**Speaking:** Cochran, Thad (C000567) [R-MS]

```

told that it has been cleared on both sides of the aisle to continue 
morning business until 3:45 under the same terms with equal division of 
time between both sides.

p.m.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there objection?
  Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
