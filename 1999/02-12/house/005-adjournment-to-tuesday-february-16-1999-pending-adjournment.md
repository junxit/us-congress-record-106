# ADJOURNMENT TO TUESDAY, FEBRUARY 16, 1999, PENDING ADJOURNMENT MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

```
ADJOURNMENT TO TUESDAY, FEBRUARY 16, 1999, PENDING ADJOURNMENT MESSAGE 
                            FROM THE SENATE

  Mr. GIBBONS. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that when the House 
adjourns on the legislative day of February 12, 1999, it stand 
adjourned until 2 p.m. on Tuesday, February 16, 1999, unless the House 
sooner receives a message from the Senate transmitting its concurrence 
in House Concurrent Resolution 27, in which case the House shall stand 
adjourned pursuant to that concurrent resolution.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Nevada?
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
