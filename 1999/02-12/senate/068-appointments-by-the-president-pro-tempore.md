# APPOINTMENTS BY THE PRESIDENT PRO TEMPORE

```
               APPOINTMENTS BY THE PRESIDENT PRO TEMPORE

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Chair, on behalf of the President pro 
tempore, pursuant to Public Law 96-388, as amended by Public Law 97-84, 
appoints the following Senators to the United States Holocaust Memorial 
Council: The Senator from California (Mrs. Boxer), and the Senator from 
New Jersey (Mr. Lautenberg).
  The Chair, on behalf of the President pro tempore, pursuant to Public 
Law 99-498, appoints Donald R. Vickers, of Vermont, to the Advisory 
Committee on Student Financial Assistance for term ending September 30, 
2001.
```
