# APPOINTMENT BY THE DEMOCRATIC LEADER OF THE SENATE AND THE MINORITY LEADER OF THE HOUSE

```
  APPOINTMENT BY THE DEMOCRATIC LEADER OF THE SENATE AND THE MINORITY 
                          LEADER OF THE HOUSE

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Chair, on behalf of the Democratic Leader 
of the Senate and the Minority Leader of the House, pursuant to Public 
Law 105-277, announces the appointment of the following individuals to 
serve as members of the International Financial Institution Advisory 
Commission:
  Richard L. Huber of Connecticut,
  Jerome L. Levinson of Maryland,
  Jeffrey D. Sachs of Massachusetts,
  Esteban E. Torres of California, and
  Paul A. Volcker of New York.

                          ____________________
```
