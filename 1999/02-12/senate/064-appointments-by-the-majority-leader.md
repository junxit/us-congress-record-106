# APPOINTMENTS BY THE MAJORITY LEADER

```
                  APPOINTMENTS BY THE MAJORITY LEADER

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Chair announces, on behalf of the Majority 
Leader, pursuant to Public Law 101-509, his reappointment of C. John 
Sobotka, of Mississippi, to the Advisory Committee on the Records of 
Congress.

Democratic Leader, pursuant to Public Law 105-389, announces the 
appointment of the following citizens to serve as members of the First 
Flight Centennial Federal Advisory Board: Peggy Baty of Ohio, Lauch 
Faircloth of North Carolina, and Wilkinson Wright of Ohio.
```
