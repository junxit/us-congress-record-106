# RECESS SUBJECT TO CALL OF THE CHAIR

```
                  RECESS SUBJECT TO CALL OF THE CHAIR

  Mr. LOTT. Mr. President, I move the Senate recess subject to the call 
of the Chair.
  The motion was agreed to, and at 1:08 p.m., the Senate took a recess 
subject to the call of the Chair.
  The Senate reassembled at 2:43 p.m., when called to order by the 
Presiding Officer (Mr. Inhofe).
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The acting majority leader is recognized.
  Mr. THOMAS. Mr. President, I would like to go through a number of 
closing activities here.
```
