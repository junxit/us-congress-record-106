# ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

**Measures:** H.R. 435, H.R. 1915

```
                ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to clause 8 of rule XX, the Chair 
will now put the question on approval of the Journal and then on each 
motion to suspend the rules on which further proceedings were postponed 
earlier today in the order in which that motion was entertained.
  Votes will be taken in the following order:
  Approval of the Journal, de novo;
  H.R. 435, concurring in Senate amendment, by the yeas and nays;
  H.R. 1915, by the yeas and nays.
  The Chair will reduce to 5 minutes the time for any electronic vote 
after the second such vote in this series.

                          ____________________
```
