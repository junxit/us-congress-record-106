# SLEEPWALKING MURDERER NEEDS TO CATCH A FEW Z'S IN ELECTRIC CHAIR

**Speaking:** Traficant, James A., Jr. (T000350) [D-OH]

```
    SLEEPWALKING MURDERER NEEDS TO CATCH A FEW Z'S IN ELECTRIC CHAIR

  (Mr. TRAFICANT asked and was given permission to address the House 
for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. TRAFICANT. Mr. Speaker, Scott Falater does not deny it. He admits 
that he stabbed his wife 44 times. He then held her underwater while 
she bled to death, and then he hid the evidence. But, after all that, 
Falater says he is not guilty because he was sleepwalking.
  Unbelievable, Mr. Speaker. Are we to believe that Falater was just 
dreaming through his wife's screams? Are we to believe he was just 
walking in the park when he stabbed her 44 times?
  Beam me up. I say it is time for Scott Falater to sleepwalk down 
murderer's row and catch a few Z's right in the electric chair. Sleep 
on that, Falater.

                          ____________________
```
