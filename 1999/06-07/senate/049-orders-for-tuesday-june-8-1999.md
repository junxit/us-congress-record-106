# ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, JUNE 8, 1999

```
                    ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, JUNE 8, 1999

  Mr. COCHRAN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it stand in recess until the hour 
of 9:30 a.m. on Tuesday, June 8. I further ask consent that on Tuesday, 
immediately following the prayer, the Journal of proceedings be 
approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, the time for the 
two leaders be reserved for their use later in the day, and the Senate 
then resume debate on S. 1122, the defense appropriations bill.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. COCHRAN. Mr. President, I further ask consent that the Senate 
stand in recess from the hours of 12:30 p.m. to 2:15 p.m. for the 
weekly policy conferences to meet.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
