                         REPORTS OF COMMITTEES

  The following reports of committees were submitted:
       By Mr. MURKOWSKI, from the Committee on Energy and Natural 
     Resources, with amendments:
       S. 109. A bill to improve protection and management of the 
     Chattahoochee River National Recreation Area in the State of 
     Georgia (Rept. No. 106-62).

     designate the route of the War of 1812 British invasion of 
     Maryland and Washington, District of Columbia, and the route

     the national trails system (Rept. No. 106-63).

     Resources, with an amendment in the nature of a substitute:
       S. 548. A bill to establish the Fallen Timbers Battlefield 
     and Fort Miamis National Historical Site in the State of Ohio 
     (Rept. No. 106-64).
       By Mr. MURKOWSKI, from the Committee on Energy and Natural 
     Resources, with amendments:
       S. 700. A bill to amend the National Trails System Act to 
     designate the Ala Kahakai Trail as a National Historic Trail 
     (Rept. No. 106-65).
       S. 776. A bill to authorize the National Park Service to 
     conduct a feasibility study for the preservation of the Loess 
     Hills in western Iowa (Rept. No. 106-66).
       By Mr. MURKOWSKI, from the Committee on Energy and Natural 
     Resources, with an amendment in the nature of a substitute 
     and an amendment to the title:

     the making of motion pictures, television productions, and 
     sound tracks in
