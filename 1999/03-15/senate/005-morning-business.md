# MORNING BUSINESS

**Speaking:** Inhofe, James M. (I000024) [R-OK]

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, there will now be a 
period for the transaction of morning business, not to extend beyond 
the hour of 3 p.m.
  Under the previous order, the Senator from Oklahoma is recognized to 
speak for up to 30 minutes.
  Mr. INHOFE. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that I be 
recognized for up to 35 minutes in morning business.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there objection? Hearing none, without 
objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. INHOFE. I ask unanimous consent that at the conclusion of my 
remarks Senator Orrin Hatch be recognized for 10 minutes.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The Senator is recognized.

                          ____________________
```
