# SCHEDULE

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. INHOFE. Mr. President, following morning business, the Senate 
will resume consideration of S. 257, the missile defense bill. The 
majority leader has announced there will be no rollcall votes during 
today's session. However, Members are encouraged to come to the floor 
and offer amendments in relation to the missile defense bill. Any 
rollcall votes ordered today on amendments will be postponed to occur 
on Tuesday at a time to be determined by the two leaders.
  I thank my colleagues for their attention.

                          ____________________
```
