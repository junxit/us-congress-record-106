# APPOINTMENT AS MEMBERS TO COMMISSION ON ONLINE CHILD PROTECTION ACT

```
  APPOINTMENT AS MEMBERS TO COMMISSION ON ONLINE CHILD PROTECTION ACT

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Without objection, and pursuant to Section 
1405(b) of the Child Online Protection Act (47 U.S.C. 231) and upon the 
recommendation of the minority leader, the Chair announces the 
Speaker's appointment of the following members on the part of the House 
to the Commission on Online Child Protection:
  Mr. James Schmidt, California, engaged in the business of making 
content available over the Internet;
  Mr. George Vrandenburg, Virginia, engaged in the business of 
providing domain name registration services;
  Mr. Larry Shapiro, California, engaged in the business of providing 
Internet portal or search services.
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
