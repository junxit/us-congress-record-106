# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Specter, Arlen (S000709) [R-PA]

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. SPECTER. Mr. President, for the information of all Senators, the 
Senate will resume consideration of the motion to proceed to the 
partial-birth abortion bill tomorrow morning. By previous order, a vote 
on the motion will occur after 20 minutes of debate. Therefore, 
Senators can expect the first vote at 9:50 a.m. If the motion is 
adopted, it is anticipated the Senate will continue debate on the bill 
throughout the day. It is the hope of the majority leader an agreement 
can be reached with regard to amendments so that the bill can be 
completed prior to the close of business on Thursday. The Senate may 
also consider any appropriations conference reports available for 
action.
```
