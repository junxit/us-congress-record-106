# SCHEDULE

**Speaking:** Thomas, Craig (T000162) [R-WY]

**Measures:** S.J.Res. 33

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. THOMAS. Mr. President, today the Senate will be in a period of 
morning business until 2 p.m. Following morning business, the Senate 
will resume consideration of the Interior appropriations bill. As a 
reminder, cloture motions were filed on Friday on S.J. Res. 33 
denouncing the offer of clemency to Puerto Rican terrorists and on the 
Hutchison amendment regarding oil royalties. These cloture votes have 
been scheduled for 5 p.m. today and may be followed by additional votes 
on judicial nominations. It is hoped that action on the Interior 
appropriations bill can be completed by tomorrow and that the Senate 
can begin consideration of the bankruptcy reform bill.
  I thank colleagues for their attention.

                          ____________________
```
