# ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, SEPTEMBER 14, 1999

**Speaking:** Hutchison, Kay Bailey (H001016) [R-TX]

**Measures:** H.R. 2466

```
                 ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, SEPTEMBER 14, 1999

  Mrs. HUTCHISON. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until the hour of 9:30 
a.m. on Tuesday, September 14. I further ask unanimous consent that on 
Tuesday, immediately following the prayer, the Journal of proceedings 
be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, the time for 
the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the day, and the 
Senate then resume debate on the Bryan second-degree amendment No. 1623 
to H.R. 2466, the Interior appropriations bill.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mrs. HUTCHISON. I further ask unanimous consent that the Senate stand 
in recess from the hours of 12:30 p.m. to 2:15 p.m. for the weekly 
policy conferences to meet.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
