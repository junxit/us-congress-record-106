# ADJOURNMENT UNTIL WEDNESDAY, FEBRUARY 3, 1999

```
             ADJOURNMENT UNTIL WEDNESDAY, FEBRUARY 3, 1999

  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the Senate 
stands adjourned until 12 noon, Wednesday, February 3, 1999.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 10 o'clock and 12 seconds a.m., adjourned 
until Wednesday, February 3, 1999, at 12 noon.
```
