# APPOINTMENT OF MEMBER TO BOARD OF TRUSTEES OF THE JOHN F. KENNEDY CENTER FOR THE PERFORMING ARTS

```
   APPOINTMENT OF MEMBER TO BOARD OF TRUSTEES OF THE JOHN F. KENNEDY 
                     CENTER FOR THE PERFORMING ARTS

  The SPEAKER. Pursuant to the provisions of section 2(a) of the 
National Cultural Center Act (20 U.S.C. 76h(a)) and the order of the 
House of Tuesday, January 19, 1999, the Speaker on Tuesday, January 26, 
1999 appointed the following Member of the House to the Board of 
Trustees of the John F. Kennedy Center for the Performing Arts:
  Mr. Hastert of Illinois.

                          ____________________
```
