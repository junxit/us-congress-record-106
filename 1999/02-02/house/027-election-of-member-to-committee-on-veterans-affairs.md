# ELECTION OF MEMBER TO COMMITTEE ON VETERANS' AFFAIRS

```
          ELECTION OF MEMBER TO COMMITTEE ON VETERANS' AFFAIRS

  Mr. FROST. Mr. Speaker, by direction of the Democratic Caucus, I 
offer a privileged resolution (H. Res. 29) and ask for its immediate 
consideration.
  The Clerk read the resolution, as follows:

                               H. Res. 29

       Resolved, That the following named Member is, and is 
     hereby, elected to serve on the standing committee as 
     follows:
       Committee on Veterans' Affairs: Ms. Berkley, Nevada.

  The resolution was agreed to.
  A motion to reconsider was laid on the table.

                          ____________________
```
