# ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

```
                ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Burr of North Carolina). Pursuant to the 
provisions of clause 9 of rule XX, the Chair announces that he will 
reduce to a minimum of 5 minutes the period of time within which a vote 
by electronic device may be taken on each additional motion to suspend 
the rules on which the Chair has postponed further proceedings.
```
