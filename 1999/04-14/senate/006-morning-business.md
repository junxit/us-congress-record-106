# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, there will now be a 
period of morning business not to extend beyond 1 p.m., with Senators 
permitted to speak up to 10 minutes each, with the following 
exceptions: Senator Brownback, 20 minutes; Senator Bayh, 10 minutes; 
Senators Domenici and Wellstone, 15 minutes total; Senator Leahy, 15 
minutes; and Senator Cleland, 15 minutes.
  The Senator from Vermont is recognized.
```
