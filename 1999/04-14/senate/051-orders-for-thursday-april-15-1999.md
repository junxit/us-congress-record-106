# ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, APRIL 15, 1999

```
                  ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, APRIL 15, 1999

  Mr. NICKLES. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today it stand in adjournment until 9:30 
a.m. on Thursday, April 15. I further ask that on Thursday, immediately 
following the prayer, the Journal of proceedings be approved to date, 
the morning hour be deemed to have expired, the time for the two 
leaders be reserved, and the Senate then resume debate on the budget 
resolution conference report.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
