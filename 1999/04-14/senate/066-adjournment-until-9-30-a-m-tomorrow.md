# ADJOURNMENT UNTIL 9:30 A.M. TOMORROW

**Speaking:** Nickles, Don (N000102) [R-OK]

```
                  ADJOURNMENT UNTIL 9:30 A.M. TOMORROW

  Mr. NICKLES. Mr. President, if there is no further business to come

adjournment until 9:30 a.m. tomorrow.

Thursday, April 15, 1999, at 9:30 a.m.
```
