# NOTICE OF INTENT TO SUSPEND THE RULES

```
                 NOTICE OF INTENT TO SUSPEND THE RULES

● Mrs. FEINSTEIN. In accordance with rule V, on behalf of myself 
and Senator Daschle, I hereby give notice in writing that it is my 
intention to move to suspend the following:
  Rule VII, paragraph 2 the phrase ``upon the calendar''; and

[[Page 2042]]

  Rule VIII, paragraph 2 the phrase ``during the first two hours of a 
new legislative day.''
  In order to permit a motion to proceed to a censure resolution, to be 
introduced on the day of the motion to proceed, notwithstanding the 
fact that it is not on the calendar of business.●

                          ____________________
```
