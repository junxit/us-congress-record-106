# MEASURE PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--H.R. 99

```
                MEASURE PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--H.R. 99

  Mr. LOTT. Mr. President, there is a bill at the desk due for its 
second reading.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will read.
  The bill clerk read as follows:

       A bill (H.R. 99) to amend title 49, United States Code, to 
     extend Federal Aviation Administration programs through 
     September 30, 1999, and for other purposes.

  Mr. LOTT. Mr. President, I object to further proceedings on this 
matter at this time.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Objection is heard. It will be placed on the 
calendar.

                          ____________________
```
