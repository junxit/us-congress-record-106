# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Lott, Trent (L000447) [R-MS]

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. LOTT. Under the order just granted, the Senate will meet again as 
the Court of Impeachment on Saturday. On Saturday, the Senate will hear 
presentations from the House managers and the White House counsel for 
not to exceed 6 hours. After those presentations, the Senate will 
resume its business on Monday for 6 hours, beginning at 1 p.m.
```
