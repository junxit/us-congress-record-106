# ADJOURNMENT UNTIL 1 P.M., THURSDAY, JANUARY 14, 1999

```
          ADJOURNMENT UNTIL 1 P.M., THURSDAY, JANUARY 14, 1999

  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the Senate will 
stand adjourned until 1 p.m., Thursday, January 14, 1999.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 12 o'clock and 21 seconds p.m., adjourned 
until Thursday, January 14, 1999, at 1 p.m.
```
