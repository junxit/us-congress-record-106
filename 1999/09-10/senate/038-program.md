# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Jeffords, James M. (J000072) [R-VT]

**Measures:** S.J.Res. 33

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. JEFFORDS. Mr. President, for the information of all Senators, the 
Senate will convene at 12 noon on Monday and be in a period for morning 
business until 2 p.m. Following morning business, the Senate will 
resume consideration of the Interior appropriations bill.
  As a reminder, cloture motions were filed today on S.J. Res. 33 
denouncing the offer of clemency to Puerto Rican terrorists and on the 
Hutchison amendment regarding oil royalties. These cloture votes have 
been scheduled for 5 p.m. on Monday.
  For the remainder of the next week, the Senate is expected to 
complete action on the Interior appropriations bill and to begin 
consideration of the bankruptcy reform bill. The Senate may also begin 
consideration of any appropriations bills available for action.

                          ____________________
```
