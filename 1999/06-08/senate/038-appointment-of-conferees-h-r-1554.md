# APPOINTMENT OF CONFEREES--H.R. 1554

**Speaking:** Stevens, Ted (S000888) [R-AK]

**Measures:** H.R. 1554

```
                  APPOINTMENT OF CONFEREES--H.R. 1554

  Mr. STEVENS. I move that with respect to H.R. 1554, the Senate insist 
on its amendment, request a conference with the House, and further, the 
Chair be authorized to appoint conferees on the part of the Senate.
  The motion was agreed to and the Presiding Officer appointed from the 
Judiciary Committee: Mr. Hatch, Mr. Thurmond, Mr. DeWine, Mr. Leahy, 
and Mr. Kohl; from the Commerce, Science and Transportation Committee: 
Mr. McCain, Mr. Stevens, and Mr. Hollings conferees on the part of the 
Senate.

                          ____________________
```
