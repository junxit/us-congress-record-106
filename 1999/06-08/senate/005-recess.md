# RECESS

```
                                 RECESS

  Mr. STEVENS. Mr. President, we are working on the managers' package, 
and to do this, we have to be off the floor. Therefore, I ask unanimous 
consent that the Senate stand in recess until 11:30 a.m. We hope 
Members will come and talk to us about this managers' package in the 
event they want amendments in it.
  There being no objection, at 10:42 a.m., the Senate recessed until 
11:32 a.m.; whereupon, the Senate reassembled when called to order by 
the Presiding Officer (Mr. Enzi).
```
