                            HON. TODD TIAHRT

                               of kansas

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 9, 1999

Amend the Ethics in Government Act of 1978 to Reauthorize Funding for 
the Office of Government Ethics, and ``yes'' on H. Res. 344, 
Recognizing and Honoring Payne Stewart and Expressing the Condolences 
of the House of Representatives to His Family on His Death.
