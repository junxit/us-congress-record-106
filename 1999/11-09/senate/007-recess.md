# RECESS

```
                                 RECESS

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, the Senate will now 
stand in recess until the hour of 2:15 p.m.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 12:35 p.m., recessed until 2:15 p.m.; 
whereupon, the Senate reassembled when called to order by the Presiding 
Officer [Mr. Inhofe].
  Mrs. LINCOLN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent to proceed in 
morning business for 7 minutes.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
