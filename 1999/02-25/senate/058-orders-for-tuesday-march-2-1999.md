# ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, MARCH 2, 1999

```
                   ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, MARCH 2, 1999

  Mr. GRAMS. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate reconvenes on Tuesday, March 2, immediately following the 
prayer, the Journal of the proceedings be approved to date, the morning 
hour be deemed to have expired, and the time for the two leaders be 
reserved.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
