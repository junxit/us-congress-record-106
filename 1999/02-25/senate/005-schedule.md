# SCHEDULE

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. HUTCHINSON. Mr. President, this morning the Senate will begin 
consideration of Senate Resolution 45, regarding human rights 
violations in China. There will be 1 hour for debate on the resolution 
equally divided between myself and Senator Wellstone. No amendments are 
in order. At the conclusion of debate time, the Senate will proceed to 
vote on adoption of the resolution. That vote will occur at 
approximately 12 noon. Following that vote, the Senate will begin a 
period of morning business to allow Members to make statements and to 
introduce legislation.
  I thank my colleagues for their attention.
```
