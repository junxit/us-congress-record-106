# PROGRAM

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. GORTON. For the information of all Senators, the Senate will 
convene tomorrow at 1:15 p.m., and at 2:15 p.m. two cloture votes will 
occur with respect to amendments to the campaign finance bill. 
Following the vote or votes, the Senate may resume consideration of the 
campaign finance bill. However, debate on this legislation is coming to 
a close, and Senators should anticipate the consideration of the 
partial-birth abortion bill, the continuing resolution, and available 
appropriations conference reports during the remainder of this week's 
session of the Senate.
```
