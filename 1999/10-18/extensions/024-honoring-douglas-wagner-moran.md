# HONORING DOUGLAS WAGNER MORAN

**Speaking:** Radanovich, George (R000004) [R-CA]

```
                     HONORING DOUGLAS WAGNER MORAN

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. GEORGE RADANOVICH

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, October 18, 1999

  Mr. RADANOVICH. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to celebrate the birth of 
Douglas Wagner Moran. Douglas, the first child of Mary and Michael 
Moran of San Francisco, California, arrived on Friday, October 15th, 
1999, at 7:45 a.m., weighing in at a healthy 7 pounds three ounces and 
an impressive 21\1/2\ inches. Mr. Speaker I request my colleagues in 
joining me in offering our heartiest congratulations to the Moran 
family and share their happiness in being new parents.

                          ____________________
```
