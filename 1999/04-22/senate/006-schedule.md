# SCHEDULE

**Speaking:** Enzi, Michael B. (E000285) [R-WY]

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. ENZI. Mr. President, this morning the Senate will immediately 
resume debate on the Social Security lockbox legislation with a vote on 
cloture at 11:30 a.m. Pursuant to rule XXII, Senators have until 10:30 
a.m. to file second-degree amendments to the Lott amendment. Following 
the vote, if cloture is not invoked, it is the intention of the leader 
to proceed to the important Y2K legislation. The Senate may also 
consider any other legislative or executive items cleared for action. 
As a reminder, the Senate will not be in session on Friday due to the 
NATO summit taking place in Washington throughout the weekend.
  I thank my colleagues for their attention and, Mr. President, I yield 
myself such time as might be necessary.

                          ____________________
```
