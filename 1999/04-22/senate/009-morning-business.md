# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  Mr. VOINOVICH. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
now proceed to a period of morning business with Senators permitted to 
speak for up to 15 minutes each.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. VOINOVICH. I further ask unanimous consent that Senator Bingaman 
be recognized to speak following my remarks, but that before I speak, 
Senator Stevens be recognized for a couple of minutes.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The Senator from Alaska.
```
