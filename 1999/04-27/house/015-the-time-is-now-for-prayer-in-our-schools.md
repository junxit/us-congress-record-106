# THE TIME IS NOW FOR PRAYER IN OUR SCHOOLS

**Speaking:** Traficant, James A., Jr. (T000350) [D-OH]

```
               THE TIME IS NOW FOR PRAYER IN OUR SCHOOLS

  (Mr. TRAFICANT asked and was given permission to address the House 
for 1 minute.)
  Mr. TRAFICANT. Mr. Speaker, another school tragedy, another 
scapegoat. This time it is guns. Littleton is not just about guns, 
parents or discipline. Littleton is much to do with Congress.
  That is right. A Congress that allows God to be banned from our 
schools while our schools can teach about

[[Page H2311]]

cults, Hitler, and even devil worship is wrong, out of touch, and needs 
some common sense.
  It is time for Congress to look in the mirror, and it is time for 
Congress to allow local school boards to make those decisions.

                          ____________________
```
