# PROGRAM

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. McCAIN. For the information of all Senators, the Senate will 
convene at 10:30 a.m. and be in a period of morning business until 12 
noon. Following morning business, the Senate will immediately resume 
debate on the Y2K legislation. I encourage my colleagues to come to the 
floor to debate this important issue. Further, the Senate may consider 
any other legislative or executive items cleared for action during 
today's session of the Senate.
```
