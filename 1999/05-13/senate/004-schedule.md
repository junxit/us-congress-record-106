# SCHEDULE

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. HATCH. This morning the Senate will resume consideration of the 
juvenile justice legislation. Pending is the Hatch-Leahy amendment with 
a vote to take place at approximately 9:40 a.m. Following the 
disposition of the Hatch-Leahy amendment, Senator Hollings will resume 
debate of his television violence amendment with 2 hours of debate 
remaining on the amendment, with the time for a vote to be determined. 
It is hoped that significant progress can continue to be made on this 
important legislation. Therefore, Senators can expect votes throughout 
today's session of the Senate.
  I thank my colleagues for their attention.

                          ____________________
```
