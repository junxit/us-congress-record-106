# SENATE RESOLUTION 102--APPOINTING SENATE LEGAL COUNSEL

```
         SENATE RESOLUTION 102--APPOINTING SENATE LEGAL COUNSEL

  Mr. LOTT submitted the following resolution; which was considered and 
agreed to:

                              S. Res. 102

       Resolved, That the appointment of Patricia Mack Bryan, of 
     Virginia, to be Senate Legal Counsel, made by the President 
     pro tempore of the Senate on May 13, 1999, shall become 
     effective as of June 1, 1999, and the term of service of the 
     appointee shall expire at the end of the 107th Congress.

                          ____________________
```
