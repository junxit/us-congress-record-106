# NOTICE OF HEARING

```
                           NOTICE OF HEARING


               committee on energy and natural resources

  Mr. MURKOWSKI. Mr. President, I would like to announce that on 
Wednesday, October 13, 1999, the Committee on Energy and Natural 
Resources and the Committee on Governmental Affairs will hold a joint 
oversight hearing on the Department of Energy's implementation of 
provisions of the Department of Defense Authorization Act which create 
the National Nuclear Security Administration. The hearing will begin at 
10 a.m. in room 366 of the Dirksen Senate Office Building in 
Washington, DC.

                          ____________________
```
