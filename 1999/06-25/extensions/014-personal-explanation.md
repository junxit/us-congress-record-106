# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Rogers, Harold (R000395) [R-KY]

**Measures:** H.Res. 218

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. HAROLD ROGERS

                              of kentucky

                    in the house of representatives

                         Friday, June 25, 1999

  Mr. ROGERS. Mr. Speaker, on Wednesday, June 23rd, I was speaking to a 
large group of Kentucky high school students on school violence, and 
was unable to arrive for rollcall vote No. 247. The vote was on passage 
of H. Res. 218, providing consideration for the FY 2000 Transportation 
and Related Agencies Appropriations Act. If I had been present, I would 
have voted ``aye.''
```
