# RESIGNATION AS MEMBER OF COMMITTEE ON THE JUDICIARY

```
          RESIGNATION AS MEMBER OF COMMITTEE ON THE JUDICIARY

resignation as a member of the Committee on the Judiciary:

                                               Washington, DC,

                                                    June 24, 1999.
     Hon. J. Dennis Hastert,
     The Capitol.
       Dear Mr. Speaker: Effective immediately, I hereby resign 
     from the House Judiciary Committee.
           Yours truly,
     Ed Bryant.
```
