# ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, MARCH 5, 1999

**Speaking:** Jeffords, James M. (J000072) [R-VT]

**Measures:** S. 280

```
                    ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, MARCH 5, 1999

  Mr. JEFFORDS. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it stand in adjournment until 9:30 
a.m. on Friday, March 5. I further ask unanimous consent that on 
Friday, immediately following the prayer, the Journal of proceedings be 
approved to date, the morning hour be deemed to have expired, the time 
for the two leaders be reserved and the Senate then resume 
consideration of S. 280, the Education Flexibility Partnership Act.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
