# STOP ILLEGAL TRADE

```
                           STOP ILLEGAL TRADE

  (Mr. TRAFICANT asked and was given permission to address the House 
for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. TRAFICANT. Mr. Speaker, take the steel crisis, please. America is 
being violated every day, every hour, every minute by illegal trade, 
and the White House has done nothing. To make matters even worse, 
Congress has done nothing. This is wrong, this is stupid, this is 
unAmerican. Illegal trade must be stopped. Congress must grow a 
backbone.
  I yield back 10,000 jobs, 10,000 American jobs already lost in the 
steel industry.

                          ____________________
```
