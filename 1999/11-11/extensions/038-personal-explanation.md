# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Shuster, Bud (S000394) [R-PA]

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 223, H.Res. 1554

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. BUD SHUSTER

                            of pennsylvania

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, November 10, 1999

  Mr. SHUSTER. Mr. Speaker, on the evening of Tuesday, November 9, 
1999, I was unavoidably detained by matters relating to the Aviation 
Conference and because of this missed rollcall votes 580 and 581. On 
rollcall vote No. 580, House Concurrent Resolution 223, Expressing the 
Sense of the Congress on the Fall of the Berlin Wall, I would have 
voted ``yea.'' In addition, on rollcall vote No. 581, House Resolution 
1554--the Satellite Copyright, Competition and Consumer Protection Act, 
had I been present, I would have voted ``yea.''
```
