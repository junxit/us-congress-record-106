# SINE DIE ADJOURNMENT

```
                          SINE DIE ADJOURNMENT

  Mr. WOLF. Mr. Speaker, pursuant to House Concurrent Resolution 235, 
106th Congress, and as the designee of the majority leader, I move that 
the House do now adjourn.
  The motion was agreed to.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to the provisions of House 
Concurrent Resolution 235, 106th Congress, the Chair declares the first 
session of the 106th Congress adjourned sine die.
  Thereupon (at 12 o'clock and 3 minutes p.m.) pursuant to House 
Concurrent Resolution 235, the House adjourned.

                          ____________________
```
