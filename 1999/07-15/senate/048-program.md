# PROGRAM

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. JEFFORDS. For the information of all Senators, the Senate will 
convene at 9:30 a.m. Under the previous order, the Senate will debate 
the Social Security lockbox legislation for 1 hour with a vote to occur 
at approximately 10:30 a.m. For the information of all Senators, that 
vote will be the only rollcall vote during Friday's session of the 
Senate. Following the vote, Senator Coverdell will be recognized to 
begin a period of morning business.
```
