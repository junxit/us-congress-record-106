# Daily Digest/House Committee Meetings

**Measures:** H.R. 1141

```
Committee Meetings
CONFERENCE REPORT--EMERGENCY SUPPLEMENTAL APPROPRIATIONS
Committee on Rules: Granted, by voice vote, a rule waiving all points 
of order against the conference report to accompany H.R. 1141, making 
emergency supplemental appropriations for the fiscal year ending 
September 30, 1999, and against its consideration. The rule provides 
that the conference report shall be considered as read. Testimony was 
heard from Chairman Young.
```
