# AUTHORIZING CLERK TO MAKE CHANGES IN THE ENGROSSMENT OF H.R. 2506, HEALTH RESEARCH AND QUALITY ACT OF 1999

**Speaking:** Bilirakis, Michael (B000463) [R-FL]

**Measures:** H.R. 2506

```
  AUTHORIZING CLERK TO MAKE CHANGES IN THE ENGROSSMENT OF H.R. 2506, 
                HEALTH RESEARCH AND QUALITY ACT OF 1999

  Mr. BILIRAKIS. Madam Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that, in the 
engrossment of the bill, H.R. 2506, the Clerk be authorized to correct 
section numbers, punctuation, and cross references and to make such 
other technical and conforming changes as may be necessary to reflect 
the actions of the House.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Florida?
  There was no objection.
```
