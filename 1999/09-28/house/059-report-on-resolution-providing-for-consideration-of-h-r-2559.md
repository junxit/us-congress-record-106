# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 2559, AGRICULTURE RISK PROTECTION ACT

**Measures:** H.Res. 308, H.R. 2559

```
    REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 2559, 
                    AGRICULTURE RISK PROTECTION ACT

  Mr. SESSIONS, from the Committee on Rules (during the special order 
of Mr. Pallone), submitted a privileged report (Rept. No. 106-346) on 
the resolution (H. Res. 308) providing for consideration of the bill 
(H.R. 2559) to amend the Federal Crop Insurance Act to strengthen the 
safety net for agricultural producers by providing greater access to 
more affordable risk management tools and improved protection from 
production and income loss, to improve the efficiency and integrity of 
the Federal crop insurance program, and for other purposes, which was 
referred to the House Calendar and ordered to be printed.
```
