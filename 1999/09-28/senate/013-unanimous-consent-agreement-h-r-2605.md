# UNANIMOUS-CONSENT AGREEMENT--H.R. 2605

**Speaking:** Roberts, Pat (R000307) [R-KS]

**Measures:** H.R. 2605

```
                 UNANIMOUS-CONSENT AGREEMENT--H.R. 2605

  Mr. ROBERTS. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that following 
Senator Bryan's remarks, the Senate then proceed to consideration of 
the conference report to accompany H.R. 2605, the energy and water 
appropriations bill. I further ask consent that reading of the report 
be waived and there then be 1 hour of debate equally divided between 
the chairman and ranking member.
  I finally ask consent that at 2:15 today the Senate proceed to a vote 
on the adoption of the conference report, with no intervening action or 
debate.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there objection?
  Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
