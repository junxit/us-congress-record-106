# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 1487, NATIONAL MONUMENT NEPA COMPLIANCE ACT

```
REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 1487, NATIONAL 
                      MONUMENT NEPA COMPLIANCE ACT

  Mr. HASTINGS of Washington (during the special order of Mr. Ganske), 
from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged report (Rept. No. 
106-327) on the resolution (H. Res. 296) providing for consideration of 
the bill (H.R. 1487) to provide for public participation in the 
declaration of national monuments under the Act popularly known as the 
Antiquities Act of 1906, which was referred to the House Calendar and 
ordered to be printed.
```
