# ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

**Measures:** H.R. 468, H.R. 1431, H.R. 2116

```
                ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Pease). Pursuant to the provisions of 
clause 8, rule XX, the Chair will now put the question on each motion 
to suspend the rules on which further proceedings were postponed in the 
order in which that motion was entertained.
  The votes will be taken in the following order:
  H.R. 2116, by the yeas and nays;
  H.R. 1431, by the yeas and nays; and
  H.R. 468, by the yeas and nays.
  The Chair will reduce to 5 minutes the time for any electronic vote 
after the first such vote in this series.
```
