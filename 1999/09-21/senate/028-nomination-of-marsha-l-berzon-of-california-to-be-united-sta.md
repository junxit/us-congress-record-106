         CIRCUIT JUDGE FOR THE NINTH CIRCUIT--MOTION TO PROCEED

calendar No. 159, Marsha L. Berzon, of California, to be United States 
Circuit Judge for the Ninth Circuit, and I ask for the yeas and nays.

roll.

necessarily absent.
