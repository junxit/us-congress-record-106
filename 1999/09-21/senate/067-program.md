# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Warner, John (W000154) [R-VA]

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. WARNER. For the information of all Senators, the Senate will 
convene at 9:30 a.m. and immediately begin 15 minutes of debate on the 
Department of Defense authorization conference report, with a vote 
immediately following. Therefore, Senators can expect the first vote at 
approximately 9:45 a.m. tomorrow. Following the vote, the Senate will 
begin consideration of the VA/HUD appropriations bill. Amendments are 
expected to be offered, and therefore Senators can anticipate votes 
throughout the day.
```
