# 1999 ANNUAL REPORT OF THE RAILROAD RETIREMENT BOARD--MESSAGE FROM THE PRESIDENT--PM 76

```
 1999 ANNUAL REPORT OF THE RAILROAD RETIREMENT BOARD--MESSAGE FROM THE 
                            PRESIDENT--PM 76

  The PRESIDING OFFICER laid before the Senate the following message 
from the President of the United States, together with an accompanying 
report; which was referred to the Committee on Health, Education, 
Labor, and Pensions.

To the Congress of the United States:
  I transmit herewith the Annual Report of the Railroad Retirement 
Board for Fiscal Year 1998, pursuant to the provisions of section 
7(b)(6) of the Railroad Retirement Act and section 12(1) of the 
Railroad Unemployment Insurance Act.
                                                  William J. Clinton.  
  The White House, November 16, 1999.

                          ____________________
```
