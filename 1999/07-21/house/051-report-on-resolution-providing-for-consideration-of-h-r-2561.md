# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 2561, DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE APPROPRIATIONS ACT, 2000

**Measures:** H.Res. 257, H.R. 2561

```
    REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 2561, 
             DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE APPROPRIATIONS ACT, 2000

(Rept. No. 106-247) on the resolution (H. Res. 257) providing for 
consideration of the bill (H.R. 2561) making appropriations for the

and for other purposes, which was referred to the House Calendar and
```
