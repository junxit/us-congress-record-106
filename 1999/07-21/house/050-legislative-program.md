  (Mr. RANGEL asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute.)

to what will be the remainder of the schedule for today, specifically 
as it relates to tax legislation.

  Mr. GOSS. Mr. Speaker, I do not know how I found myself in the

then plan to reassemble. I would say check in about early evening.
  Mr. RANGEL. Mr. Speaker, so that the Members will have an opportunity 
to plan the rest of the evening, is it possible to have some 
guesstimate as to what time the majority will be prepared to return to 
the floor?
