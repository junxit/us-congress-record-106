# APPOINTMENTS

**Measures:** S.Res. 75, S.Res. 105, S.Res. 149

```
                              APPOINTMENTS

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Chair announces, on behalf of the Majority 
Leader, pursuant to the provisions of S. Res. 105 (adopted April 13, 
1989), as amended by S. Res. 149 (adopted October 5, 1993), as amended 
by Public Law 105-275, and further amended by S. Res. 75 (adopted March 
25, 1999), the appointment of the following Senators to serve as 
members of the Senate National Security Working Group:
  The Senator from Mississippi (Mr. Cochran) (Majority Administrative 
Cochairman);
  The Senator from Alaska (Mr. Stevens) (Majority Cochairman);
  The Senator from Arizona (Mr. Kyl) (Majority Cochairman);
  The Senator from North Carolina (Mr. Helms);
  The Senator from Indiana (Mr. Lugar);
  The Senator from Virginia (Mr. Warner);
  The Senator from Oklahoma (Mr. Inhofe); and
  The Senator from Wyoming (Mr. Enzi).
```
