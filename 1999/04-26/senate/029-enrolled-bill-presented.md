# ENROLLED BILL PRESENTED

```
                        ENROLLED BILL PRESENTED

  The Secretary of the Senate reported that on Thursday, April 26, 
1999, he had presented to the President of the United States, the 
following enrolled bill:

       S. 531. An act to authorize the President to award a gold 
     medal on behalf of the Congress to Rosa Parks in recognition 
     of her contribution to the Nation.
```
