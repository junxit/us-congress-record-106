# Daily Digest/House Committee Meetings

```
Committee Meetings
NATURAL RESOURCES CONSERVATION--MAJOR CONTRIBUTIONS MADE BY BUSINESSES
  Committee on Small Business: On April 23, the Subcommittee on 
Government Programs and Oversight held a hearing on the continuing 
need to conserve natural resources because of their limited nature, 
focusing on the major contributions made by businesses both large 
and small to conservation and a healthy environment through science 
and technology. Testimony was heard from public witnesses.
```
