# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Roth Jr., William V. (R000460) [R-DE]

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. ROTH. For the information of all Senators, the Senate will 
reconvene tomorrow morning at 9:30. By previous order, the Senate will 
immediately begin a stacked series of votes on the Abraham Social 
Security lockbox amendment, the Baucus motion to recommit, and the 
Graham amendment regarding effective dates of the provisions in the 
Taxpayer Refund Act of 1999. Following the votes, Senator Gramm of 
Texas will be recognized to offer an amendment regarding across-the-
board tax cuts, estate taxes, and capital gains taxes. By previous 
consent, there will be 10 hours of debate

[[Page S9650]]

time remaining on the bill tomorrow. Therefore, it is hoped that the 
Senate can continue to make significant progress on the bill and that 
the Senate action can be completed no later than Friday.

                          ____________________
```
