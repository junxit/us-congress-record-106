# ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, JULY 29, 1999

**Speaking:** Roth Jr., William V. (R000460) [R-DE]

```
                   ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, JULY 29, 1999

  Mr. ROTH. Mr. President, I can unanimous consent that when the Senate 
completes its business today, it adjourn until the hour of 9:30 a.m. on 
Thursday, July 29. I further ask consent that on Thursday, immediately 
following the prayer, the Journal of proceedings be approved to date, 
the morning hour be deemed to have expired, and the time for the two 
leaders be reserved for their use later in the day.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
