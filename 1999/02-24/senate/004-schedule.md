# SCHEDULE

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. WARNER. Mr. President, this morning the Senate will resume 
consideration of S. 4, the Soldiers', Sailors', Airmen's and Marines' 
Bill of Rights Act of 1999. There will be a short period of debate 
until 9:45 a.m., at which time the Senate will proceed to two back-to-
back rollcall votes. The first vote will be on or in relation to a 
Sarbanes-Warner amendment regarding civilian pay, followed immediately 
by a vote on or in relation to a Cleland amendment regarding thrift 
savings.
  Following those two votes, the Senate will continue consideration of 
S. 4, with the intention of completing action on the bill by, I would 
hope--there is even the possibility, and I would like to have the views 
of my distinguished ranking member--maybe the middle of the day. We are 
getting excellent cooperation from all Senators on this matter. We are 
quickly going through the amendments and I will momentarily address the 
amendments. I believe it could be done by sometime this afternoon. 
Therefore, Members should expect rollcall votes throughout the 
deliberation on this bill.
  Again, the first vote is to begin at 9:45.

                          ____________________
```
