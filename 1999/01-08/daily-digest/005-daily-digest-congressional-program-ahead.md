# Daily Digest/CONGRESSIONAL PROGRAM AHEAD

```
                      CONGRESSIONAL PROGRAM AHEAD

              Week of January 11 through January 16, 1999

                             Senate Chamber

  On Monday, Senate is not in session.
  On Tuesday, Senate will meet in a pro forma session.
  On Wednesday, Senate will continue to sit as a Court of Impeachment 
to consider the articles of impeachment against President Clinton.
  During the remainder of the week, it is expected that the Senate will 
continue to consider the articles of impeachment against President 
Clinton.


                           Senate Committees

        (Committee meetings are open unless otherwise indicated)
  Committee on Appropriations: January 12, Subcommittee on Labor, 
Health and Human Services, and Education, to hold hearings to 
examine the patenting and health implications of stem cell research, 
9:30 a.m., SD-192.


                             House Chamber

  The House is not in session.


                            House Committees

  No Committee meetings are scheduled.
```
