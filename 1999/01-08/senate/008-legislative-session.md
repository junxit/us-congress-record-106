# LEGISLATIVE SESSION

**Speaking:** Lott, Trent (L000447) [R-MS]

```
                          LEGISLATIVE SESSION

  Mr. LOTT addressed the Chair.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER (Mr. Gregg). The majority leader is recognized.
  Mr. LOTT. Mr. President, for the information of all Senators, 
momentarily we will do the closeout for the day, and we will, in that 
period, after consultation with the Democratic leader, notify the 
Senators about what the schedule will be next week and when the next 
anticipated time will occur for us to be here and expect votes. That 
will probably be Wednesday afternoon.
  With that, I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will call the roll.
  The legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. LOTT. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the order for 
the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
