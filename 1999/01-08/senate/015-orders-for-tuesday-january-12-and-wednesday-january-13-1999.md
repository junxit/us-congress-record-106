# ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, JANUARY 12, AND WEDNESDAY, JANUARY 13, 1999

```
    ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, JANUARY 12, AND WEDNESDAY, JANUARY 13, 1999

  Mr. LOTT. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate 
completes its business today, it stand in adjournment until the hour of 
12 noon on Tuesday, January 12, for a pro forma session only. I further 
ask that the Senate then stand adjourned to reconvene at 1 p.m. on 
Wednesday, January 13, to consider the articles of impeachment.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
