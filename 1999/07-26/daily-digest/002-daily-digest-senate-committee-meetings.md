# Daily Digest/Senate Committee Meetings

```
Committee Meetings
(Committees not listed did not meet)
NOMINATIONS
Committee on Foreign Relations: On Friday, July 23, Committee concluded 
hearings on the nomination of Michael A. Sheehan, of New Jersey, to be 
Coordinator for Counterterrorism, Department of State, with the rank 
and status of Ambassador at Large, after the nominee testified and 
answered questions in his own behalf.
NOMINATION
Committee on Foreign Relations: Committee concluded hearings on the 
nomination of Evelyn Simonowitz Lieberman, of New York, to be Under 
Secretary of State for Public Diplomacy, after the nominee, who was 
introduced by Senator Biden, testified and answered questions in her 
own behalf.
```
