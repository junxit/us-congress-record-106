# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 2587, DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA APPROPRIATIONS ACT, 2000

**Measures:** H.Res. 260, H.R. 2587

```
REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 2587, DISTRICT 
                  OF COLUMBIA APPROPRIATIONS ACT, 2000

  Mr. LINDER, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 106-263) on the resolution (H. Res. 260) providing 
for consideration of the bill (H.R. 2587) making appropriations for the 
government of the District of Columbia and other activities chargeable 
in whole or in part against revenues of said District for the fiscal 
year ending September 30, 2000, and for other purposes, which was 
referred to the House Calendar and ordered to be printed.
```
