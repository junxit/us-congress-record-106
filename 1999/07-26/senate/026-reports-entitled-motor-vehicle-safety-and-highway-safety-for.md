# REPORTS ENTITLED ``MOTOR VEHICLE SAFETY'' AND ``HIGHWAY SAFETY'' FOR CALENDAR YEARS 1996--MESSAGE FROM THE PRESIDENT--PM 52

```
 REPORTS ENTITLED ``MOTOR VEHICLE SAFETY'' AND ``HIGHWAY SAFETY'' FOR 
         CALENDAR YEARS 1996--MESSAGE FROM THE PRESIDENT--PM 52

  The PRESIDING OFFICER laid before the Senate the following message 
from the President of the United States, together with an accompanying 
report; which was referred to the Committee on Commerce, Science, and 
Transportation.

To the Congress of the United States:
  I transmit herewith the 1996 calendar year reports as prepared by the 
Department of Transportation on activities under the National Traffic 
and Motor Vehicle Safety Act of 1966, the Highway Safety Act, and the 
Motor Vehicle Information and Cost Savings Act of 1972, as amended.
                                                  William J. Clinton.  
  The White House, July 26, 1999.

                          ____________________
```
