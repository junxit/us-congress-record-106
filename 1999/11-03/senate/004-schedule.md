# SCHEDULE

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. ALLARD. Mr. President, today the Senate will resume consideration 
of the CBI/African trade bill. Amendments to the bill are expected to 
be offered during the postcloture debate, and therefore Senators can 
expect votes throughout the day. The Senate may also begin 
consideration of the conference report to accompany the financial 
services modernization bill during today's session of the Senate. It is 
hoped the Senate can complete action on the African trade bill and the 
financial services conference report by tomorrow's session. It is also 
still possible an agreement can be reached regarding the bankruptcy 
reform bill so the Senate can consider that legislation prior to the 
impending adjournment.
  I thank my colleagues for their attention.

                          ____________________
```
