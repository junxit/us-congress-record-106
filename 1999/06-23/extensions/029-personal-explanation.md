# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Houghton, Amo (H000814) [R-NY]

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. AMO HOUGHTON

                              of new york

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, June 23, 1999

  Mr. HOUGHTON. Mr. Speaker, I rise to say that I missed votes numbered 
204-238 from June 14 to 18, 1999, as I was attending the inauguration 
ceremony of South African President Thabo Mbeki.
  Under the authorization of Chairman Ben Gilman of the House 
International Relations Committee, I was the sole representative of the 
U.S. Congress at the inauguration.
```
