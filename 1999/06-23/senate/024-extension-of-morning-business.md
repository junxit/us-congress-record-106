# EXTENSION OF MORNING BUSINESS

**Speaking:** Craig, Larry E. (C000858) [R-ID], Wellstone, Paul D. (W000288) [D-MN]

```
                     EXTENSION OF MORNING BUSINESS

  Mr. CRAIG. Mr. President, our leaders are still in negotiation as to 
terms and conditions under which the Senate will deal with the 
Patients' Bill of Rights. With that understanding, I ask unanimous 
consent that morning business be extended until 4:30 p.m. under the 
conditions of the previous extension.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. CRAIG. I thank the Chair. I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will call the roll.
  The assistant legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. WELLSTONE. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the order 
for the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
