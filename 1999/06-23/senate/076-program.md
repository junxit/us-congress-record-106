# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Grassley, Chuck (G000386) [R-IA]

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. GRASSLEY. For the information of all Senators, tomorrow the 
Senate will convene at 9:30 a.m. and immediately resume consideration 
of the agriculture appropriations bill. It is hoped that an agreement 
can be reached to consider agriculture-related amendments during 
Thursday's session of the Senate. All Senators can expect rollcall 
votes throughout the session tomorrow as the Senate works to make 
progress on the agriculture appropriations bill.
```
