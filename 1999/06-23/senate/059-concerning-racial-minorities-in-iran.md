# CONCERNING RACIAL MINORITIES IN IRAN

**Measures:** S.Con.Res. 39

```
                  CONCERNING RACIAL MINORITIES IN IRAN

                                 ______
                                 

                       SCHUMER AMENDMENT NO. 734

  Mr. SCHUMER proposed an amendment to the concurrent resolution (S. 
Con. Res. 39) expressing the sense of the Congress regarding the 
treatment of religious minorities in the Islamic Republic of Iran, and 
particularly the recent arrests of members of that country's Jewish 
community; as follows:

       On page 3, line 3, strike ``Clinton Administration'' and 
     insert ``United States''.
       On page 3, strike line 4 to line 5 before ``continue''.
       On page 3, beginning with line 7, strike the word 
     ``recommendation'' and insert ``the recommendation of 
     resolution 1999/13.''
       On page 3, line 9, insert after ``(2)'' ``continue to''.

                          ____________________
```
