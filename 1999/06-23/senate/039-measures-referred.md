# MEASURES REFERRED

**Measures:** H.R. 659, H.R. 1175

```
                           MEASURES REFERRED

  The following bills were read the first and second times by unanimous 
consent and referred as indicated:

       H.R. 659. An act to authorize appropriations for the 
     protection of Paoli and Brandywine Battlefields in

     Natural Resources.

     Zachary Baumel, a United States citizen, and other Israeli 
     soldiers missing in action; to the Committee on Foreign
```
