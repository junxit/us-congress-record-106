# RETURN OF OFFICIAL PAPERS--S. 331

```
                   RETURN OF OFFICIAL PAPERS--S. 331

  Mr. GRASSLEY. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
proceed to the immediate consideration of S. Res. 127, submitted 
earlier by Senator Lott, and I further ask unanimous consent that the 
resolution be agreed to and the motion to reconsider be laid upon the 
table.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The resolution (S. Res. 127) was agreed to, as follows:

                              S. Res. 127

       Resolved, That the Secretary of the Senate is directed to 
     request the House of Representatives to return the official 
     papers on S. 331.
```
