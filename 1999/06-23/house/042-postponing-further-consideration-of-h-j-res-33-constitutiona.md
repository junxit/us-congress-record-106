# POSTPONING FURTHER CONSIDERATION OF H.J. RES. 33, CONSTITUTIONAL AMENDMENT AUTHORIZING CONGRESS TO PROHIBIT PHYSICAL DESECRATION OF THE FLAG OF THE UNITED STATES, AFTER GENERAL DEBATE TODAY TO A TIME DESIGNATED BY THE SPEAKER

```
   POSTPONING FURTHER CONSIDERATION OF H.J. RES. 33, CONSTITUTIONAL 
AMENDMENT AUTHORIZING CONGRESS TO PROHIBIT PHYSICAL DESECRATION OF THE 
    FLAG OF THE UNITED STATES, AFTER GENERAL DEBATE TODAY TO A TIME 
                       DESIGNATED BY THE SPEAKER

  Mr. CANADY of Florida. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that 
after debate on H.J. Res. 33, notwithstanding the operation of the 
previous question, it may be in order at that point for the Chair to 
postpone further consideration of the bill until the following 
legislative day on which consideration may resume at a time designated 
by the Speaker.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Foley). Is there objection to the 
request of the gentleman from Florida?
  There was no objection.
```
