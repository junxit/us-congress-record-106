# A NATION THAT BANS GOD IS A NATION THAT OPENS THE DOOR TO THE DEVIL

```
  A NATION THAT BANS GOD IS A NATION THAT OPENS THE DOOR TO THE DEVIL

  (Mr. TRAFICANT asked and was given permission to address the House 
for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. TRAFICANT. Mr. Speaker, another school shooting; this time in 
Georgia. Everyone is desperately searching for answers. I say the 
search should stop right here. Congress must look in the mirror, 
because in America today our students can study cults, devil worship, 
Hitler, but God is banned, banned from our schools. I say a nation that 
bans God is a nation that opens the door to the devil and to the 
problems that we are facing as a nation.
  Congress, it is time to allow God back into our schools, and I 
further recommend after all the technicalities we allow God back into 
our Nation.

                          ____________________
```
