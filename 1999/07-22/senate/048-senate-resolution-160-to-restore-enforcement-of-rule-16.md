# SENATE RESOLUTION 160--TO RESTORE ENFORCEMENT OF RULE 16

**Measures:** S.Res. 160

```
        SENATE RESOLUTION 160--TO RESTORE ENFORCEMENT OF RULE 16

  Mr. LOTT submitted the following resolution; which was ordered placed 
on the calendar:

                              S. Res. 160

       Resolved, That the presiding officer of the Senate should 
     apply all precedents of the Senate under Rule 16, in effect 
     at the conclusion of the 103d Congress.
```
