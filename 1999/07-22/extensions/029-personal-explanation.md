# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Crowley, Joseph (C001038) [D-NY]

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JOSEPH CROWLEY

                              of new york

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, July 22, 1999

  Mr. CROWLEY. Mr. Speaker, on July 19, 1999, I was unavoidably 
detained at LaGuardia Airport in New York due to poor weather 
conditions. The weather delays caused me to miss rollcall votes 308, 
309, and 310. I would like the Record to reflect that had I been 
present, I would have voted in the affirmative on all three rollcall 
votes, numbers 308, 309, and 310.
```
