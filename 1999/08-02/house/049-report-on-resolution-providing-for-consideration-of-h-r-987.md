# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 987, WORKPLACE PRESERVATION ACT

```
[[Page 18942]]

REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 987, WORKPLACE 
                            PRESERVATION ACT

  Mr. REYNOLDS, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 106-280) on the resolution (H. Res. 271) providing 
for consideration of the bill (H.R. 987) to require the Secretary of 
Labor to wait for completion of a National Academy of Sciences study 
before promulgating a standard or guideline on ergonomics, which was 
referred to the House Calendar and ordered to be printed.
```
