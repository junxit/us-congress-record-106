# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 2031, TWENTY- FIRST AMENDMENT ENFORCEMENT ACT

```
 REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 2031, TWENTY-
                    FIRST AMENDMENT ENFORCEMENT ACT

  Mr. REYNOLDS, from the Committee on rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 106-281) on the resolution (H. Res. 272) providing 
for consideration of the bill (H.R. 2031) to provide for injunctive 
relief in Federal district court to enforce State laws relating to the 
interstate transportation of intoxicating liquor, which was referred to 
the House Calendar and ordered to be printed.
```
