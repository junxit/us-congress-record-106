# REPORT ON REVISED DEFERRAL OF BUDGET AUTHORITY--MESSAGE FROM THE PRESIDENT OF THE UNITED STATES (H. DOC. NO. 106-109)

```
   REPORT ON REVISED DEFERRAL OF BUDGET AUTHORITY--MESSAGE FROM THE 
          PRESIDENT OF THE UNITED STATES (H. DOC. NO. 106-109)

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following message 
from the President of the United States; which was read and, together 
with the accompanying papers, without objection, referred to the 
Committee on Appropriations and ordered to be printed:

To the Congress of the United States:
  In accordance with the Congressional Budget and Impoundment Control 
Act of 1974, I herewith report one revised deferral of budget 
authority, now totaling $173 million.
  The deferral affects programs of the Department of State.
                                                  William J. Clinton.  
  The White House, August 2, 1999.

                          ____________________
```
