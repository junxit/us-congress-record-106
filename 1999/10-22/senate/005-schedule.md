# SCHEDULE

**Speaking:** Roth Jr., William V. (R000460) [R-DE]

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. ROTH. Mr. President, today the Senate will resume consideration 
of the motion to proceed to the sub-Saharan Africa free trade bill. Any 
Senator desiring to debate the motion to proceed is encouraged to come 
to the floor to make their statement. As announced last night, there 
will be no rollcall votes today or during Monday's session of the 
Senate. The next vote will be on the morning of Tuesday, October 26. 
The Senate may also consider appropriations conference reports or any 
other legislative or executive matters that can be cleared.
  I thank my colleagues for their attention.

                          ____________________
```
