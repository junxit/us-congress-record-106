# ADJOURNMENT

**Speaking:** Capps, Lois (C001036) [D-CA]

**Measures:** H.Res. 252

```
                              ADJOURNMENT

  Mrs. CAPPS. Mr. Speaker, pursuant to House Resolution 252, I move 
that the House do now adjourn in memory of the late Hon. George E. 
Brown, Jr.
  The motion was agreed to; accordingly (at 2 o'clock and 34 minutes 
p.m.), under its previous order, and pursuant to House Resolution 252, 
the House adjourned in memory of the late Hon. George E. Brown, Jr. 
until Monday, July 19, 1999, at 12:30 p.m., for morning hour debates.




                          ____________________
```
