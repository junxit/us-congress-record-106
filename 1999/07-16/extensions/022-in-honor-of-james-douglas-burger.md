# IN HONOR OF JAMES DOUGLAS BURGER

**Speaking:** Kucinich, Dennis J. (K000336) [D-OH]

```
[[Page E1575]]
                    IN HONOR OF JAMES DOUGLAS BURGER

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. DENNIS J. KUCINICH

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                         Friday, July 16, 1999

  Mr. KUCINICH. Mr. Speaker, today I rise to honor Mr. James Douglas 
Burger, of Fairview Park, Ohio, who graduated from the Federal Bureau 
of Investigation National Academy on June 25th, 1999.
  Mr. Burger was selected to be among the special few to attend the 
prestigious Academy to become a member of its 197th graduating class. 
The Academy's facilities and faculty are acknowledged as world leaders 
in law enforcement training. Its rigorous eleven week course provides 
one of the premier training programs and prepares its graduates for 
bright futures in law enforcement.
  The graduates of the FBI National Academy set the standard for 
integrity, commitment, and expertise throughout the law enforcement 
community. I am proud to have a member of my district join these elite 
few in protecting the borders of civilized society.
  My fellow colleagues, join me in recognizing Mr. Burger's achievement 
and we wish him much success in the future.

                          ____________________
```
