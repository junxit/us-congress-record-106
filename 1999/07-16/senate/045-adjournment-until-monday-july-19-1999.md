# ADJOURNMENT UNTIL MONDAY, JULY 19, 1999

```
                ADJOURNMENT UNTIL MONDAY, JULY 19, 1999

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. If there is no further business to come before 
the Senate, under the previous order, the Senate stands adjourned until 
the hour of 12 noon, on Monday, July 19, 1999.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 2:14 p.m., adjourned until Monday, July 19, 
1999, at 12 noon.
```
