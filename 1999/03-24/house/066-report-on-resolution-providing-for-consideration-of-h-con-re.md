# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H. CON. RES. 68, CONCURRENT RESOLUTION ON BUDGET FOR FISCAL YEAR 2000

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 68, H.Res. 131

```
 REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H. CON. RES. 68, 
          CONCURRENT RESOLUTION ON BUDGET FOR FISCAL YEAR 2000

  Mr. LINDER (during the special order of Mr. Smith of Washington), 
from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged report (Rept. No. 
106-77) on the resolution (H. Res. 131) providing for consideration of 
the concurrent resolution (H. Con. Res. 68) establishing the 
congressional budget for the United States Government for fiscal year 
2000 and setting forth appropriate budgetary levels for each of the 
fiscal years 2001 through 2009, which was referred to the House 
Calendar and ordered to be printed.
```
