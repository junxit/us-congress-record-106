# APPOINTMENT OF MEMBERS TO PRESIDENT'S EXPORT COUNCIL

```
          APPOINTMENT OF MEMBERS TO PRESIDENT'S EXPORT COUNCIL

  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. LaTourette). Without objection, and 
pursuant to the provisions of Executive Order Number 12131, the Chair 
announces the Speaker's appointment of the following Members of the 
House to the President's Export Council:

  Mr. English of Pennsylvania, and
  Mr. Pickering of Mississippi.
  There was no objection.
```
