# ADJOURNMENT UNTIL 2:15 P.M. TUESDAY, SEPTEMBER 21, 1999

```
        ADJOURNMENT UNTIL 2:15 P.M. TUESDAY, SEPTEMBER 21, 1999

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the 
Senate will stand adjourned until 2:15 p.m., Tuesday, September 21, 
1999.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 10 o'clock and 26 seconds a.m., adjourned 
until Tuesday, September 21, 1999, at 2:15 p.m.
```
