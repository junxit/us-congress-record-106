# Text of House Amendment 6

**Speaking:** Istook, Ernest J., Jr. (I000047) [R-OK]

**Measures:** H.R. 1664

```
H.R. 1664

                         Offered By: Mr. Istook

       Amendment No. 6: At the appropriate place in the bill 
     insert the following new section:

[[Page H2813]]
```
