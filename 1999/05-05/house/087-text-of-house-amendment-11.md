# Text of House Amendment 11

**Speaking:** Souder, Mark E. (S001143) [R-IN]

**Measures:** H.R. 1664

```
H.R. 1664

                         Offered By: Mr. Souder

       Amendment No. 11: At the end of the bill, insert after the 
     last section (preceding the short title) the following new 
     section:
       Sec. 503. None of the funds appropriated in this or any 
     other Act may be used for military operations in the Federal 
     Republic of Yugoslavia, except operations specifically 
     limited to rescuing United States military personnel or 
     United States citizens.
```
