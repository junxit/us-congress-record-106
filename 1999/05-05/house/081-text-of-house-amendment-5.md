# Text of House Amendment 5

**Speaking:** Istook, Ernest J., Jr. (I000047) [R-OK]

**Measures:** H.R. 1664

```
H.R. 1664

                         Offered By: Mr. Istook

       Amendment No. 5: At the appropriate place in the bill 
     insert the following new section:
       None of the funds appropriated by this Act may be used to 
     initiate or conduct military operations by the United States 
     Armed Forces except in accordance with the war powers clause 
     of the Constitution (article 1, section 8).
```
