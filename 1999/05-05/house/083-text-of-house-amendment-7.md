# Text of House Amendment 7

**Speaking:** Istook, Ernest J., Jr. (I000047) [R-OK]

**Measures:** H.R. 1664

```
H.R. 1664

                         Offered By: Mr. Istook

       Amendment No. 7: At the appropriate place in the bill 
     insert the following new section:
       None of the funds in this act may be used to invade 
     Yugoslavia with ground forces in contravention of the War 
     Powers Resolution (Title 50 U.S.C. Chapter 33).
```
