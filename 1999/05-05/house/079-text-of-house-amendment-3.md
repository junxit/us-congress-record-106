# Text of House Amendment 3

**Speaking:** Istook, Ernest J., Jr. (I000047) [R-OK]

**Measures:** H.R. 1664

```
H.R. 1664

                         Offered By: Mr. Istook

       Amendment No. 3: At the end of the bill, insert after the 
     last section (preceding the short title) the following new 
     section:
       Sec. 503. None of the funds appropriated in this Act may be 
     used for the deployment of ground elements of the United 
     States Armed Forces in the Federal Republic of Yugoslavia.
```
