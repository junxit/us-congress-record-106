# AUTHORIZING THE CLERK TO MAKE CORRECTIONS IN THE ENGROSSMENT OF H.R. 833, BANKRUPTCY REFORM ACT OF 1999

```
 AUTHORIZING THE CLERK TO MAKE CORRECTIONS IN THE ENGROSSMENT OF H.R. 
                   833, BANKRUPTCY REFORM ACT OF 1999

  Mr. GEKAS. Madam Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that in the 
engrossment of the bill, H.R. 833, the Clerk be authorized to correct 
section numbers, cross-references, and punctuation, and to make such 
stylistic, clerical, technical, conforming, and other changes as may be 
necessary to reflect the actions of the House in amending the bill.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mrs. Northup). Is there objection to the 
request of the gentleman from Pennsylvania?
  There was no objection.
```
