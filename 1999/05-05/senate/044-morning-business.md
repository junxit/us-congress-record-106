# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  Mr. GRAMM. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate now 
proceed to a period of morning business, with Senators permitted to 
speak therein for up to 10 minutes each.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. BURNS. Mr. President, I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will call the roll.
  The legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. GRAMM. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the order for 
the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. GRAMM. Mr. President, I have several unanimous consent requests. 
All of them are agreed to on both sides of the aisle. Let me just go 
through them.

                          ____________________
```
