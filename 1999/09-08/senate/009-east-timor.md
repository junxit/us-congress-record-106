# EAST TIMOR

```
                               EAST TIMOR

  Mrs. BOXER. Mr. President, I add my voice in praising Senator 
Feingold for his leadership in the Foreign Relations Committee, on 
which I serve, on this whole issue of East Timor.
  There are some things we can do very quickly in the Senate to send a 
message to Indonesia that we will not stand by and see this violation 
of human rights occur. We have some leverage. We have some agreements. 
We can make a difference.

                          ____________________
```
