# THANKING THE CHAPLAIN

```
                         THANKING THE CHAPLAIN

  Mrs. BOXER. Mr. President, I thank the Chaplain today for his very 
inclusive prayer, calling to our attention the things we take for 
granted, the good people around here who work so hard and always do it 
in a way that makes us feel as though we are not asking them to work 
very hard, and we are asking them to work very hard. They are always 
pleasant. That includes the staff on both sides. I thank the Chaplain 
for that.

                          ____________________
```
