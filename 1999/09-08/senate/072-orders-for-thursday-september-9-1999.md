# ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, SEPTEMBER 9, 1999

```
                 ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, SEPTEMBER 9, 1999

  Mr. BROWNBACK. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it stand adjourned until the hour 
of 9:30 a.m. on Thursday, September 9. I further ask consent that on 
Thursday, immediately following the prayer, the Journal of proceedings 
be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, the time for 
the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the day, and the 
Senate immediately begin three consecutive votes as previously ordered.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
