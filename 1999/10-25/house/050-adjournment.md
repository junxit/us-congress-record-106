# ADJOURNMENT

```
                              ADJOURNMENT

  Mr. HAYWORTH. Mr. Speaker, pursuant to House Resolution 341, I move 
that the House do now adjourn in memory of the late Honorable John H. 
Chafee.
  The motion was agreed to; accordingly (at 9 o'clock and 58 minutes 
p.m.), under its previous order and pursuant to House Resolution 341, 
the House adjourned in memory of the late Honorable John H. Chafee 
until tomorrow, Tuesday, October 26, 1999, at 9 a.m., for morning hour 
debates.
```
