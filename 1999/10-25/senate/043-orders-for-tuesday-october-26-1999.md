# ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, OCTOBER 26, 1999

**Speaking:** Helms, Jesse (H000463) [R-NC]

**Measures:** H.R. 434

```
                  ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, OCTOBER 26, 1999

  Mr. HELMS. Mr. President, on behalf of the majority leader, I ask 
unanimous consent that when the Senate completes its business today, it 
adjourn until the hour of 9:30 a.m. on Tuesday, October 26. I further 
ask unanimous consent that on Tuesday, immediately following the 
prayer, the Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the morning 
hour be deemed expired, the time for the two leaders be reserved for 
their use later in the day, and the Senate then begin 30 minutes of 
debate on the motion to proceed to H.R. 434, the African trade bill, to 
be equally divided in the usual form.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. HELMS. I thank the Chair.
  I further ask unanimous consent that the cloture vote regarding the 
motion to proceed to the trade bill occur at 10 a.m. on Tuesday.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. HELMS. I further ask unanimous consent that the Senate stand in 
recess from the hour of 12:30 p.m. until 2:15 p.m. on Tuesday so that 
the weekly party conferences can meet.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
