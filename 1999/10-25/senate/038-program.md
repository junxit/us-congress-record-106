# PROGRAM

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. HELMS. Mr. President, for the information of all Senators, the 
Senate will immediately resume debate on the motion to proceed to the 
African trade bill at 9:30 a.m. on Tuesday. In accordance with rule 
XXII, the Senate will proceed to a cloture vote on the motion to 
proceed at 10 a.m. It is hoped that cloture will be invoked and that a 
time agreement can be reached so that the Senate may begin debate on 
the bill and that Senators may begin to offer their amendments. The 
Senate may also consider any legislative or Executive Calendar items 
cleared for action, as well as any appropriations conference reports 
that may become available.

                          ____________________
```
