# SCHEDULE

**Speaking:** Hagel, Chuck (H001028) [R-NE]

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. HAGEL. Mr. President, this morning the Senate will be in a period 
of morning business until 2 p.m., with Senators permitted to speak up 
to 10 minutes each. Following morning business, the Senate may consider 
any legislative or executive items cleared for action. The leader has 
announced that there will be no rollcall votes during today's session. 
So any votes ordered today will be postponed to occur on Tuesday at a 
time to be determined by the two leaders. It is hoped that the 
conferees on the budget resolution will be able to complete their work 
early this week so the Senate may begin consideration of the budget 
conference report and have a final vote by Thursday.

                          ____________________
```
