# MORNING HOUR DEBATES

```
                          MORNING HOUR DEBATES

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to the order of the House of 
January 19, 1999, the Chair will now recognize Members from lists 
submitted by the majority and minority leaders for morning hour debate. 
The Chair will alternate recognition between the parties, with each 
party limited to 30 minutes, and each Member, except the majority 
leader, the minority leader, or the minority whip, limited to 5 
minutes.
  The Chair recognizes the gentleman from Florida (Mr. Stearns) for 5 
minutes.

                          ____________________
```
