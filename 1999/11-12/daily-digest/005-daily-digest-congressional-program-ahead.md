# Daily Digest/CONGRESSIONAL PROGRAM AHEAD

**Measures:** S. 625

```
                      CONGRESSIONAL PROGRAM AHEAD

             Week of November 15 through November 20, 1999

                             Senate Chamber

  On Monday, Senate will not be in session.
  On Tuesday and Wednesday, Senate expects to resume consideration of 
S. 625, Bankruptcy Reform Act, with a vote in relation to Wellstone 
Amendment No. 2752 to occur on Wednesday. Also, Senate expects to 
consider appropriations bills and conference reports, when available, 
and any other cleared legislative and executive business.
  Balance of the week is uncertain as the Senate attempts to complete 
the First Session of the 106th Congress.


                           Senate Committees

  No committee meetings are scheduled.


                             House Chamber

  To be announced.


                            House Committees

  Committee on Veterans' Affairs: November 16, Subcommittee on 
Health and the Subcommittee on Oversight and Investigations, joint 
hearing on the possible health effects of the drug pyridostigmine 
bromide (PB) on veterans who served in the Persian Gulf War, 2:30 
p.m., 334 Cannon.
```
