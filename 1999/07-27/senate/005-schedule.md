# SCHEDULE

**Speaking:** Gorton, Slade (G000333) [R-WA]

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. GORTON. Mr. President, today the Senate will be in a period of 
morning business until 10 o'clock. Following morning business, the 
Senate will begin consideration of any available appropriations bills. 
Amendments are expected to be offered, and therefore Senators can 
expect votes throughout the day's session.
  For the information of all Senators, the Senate is expected to begin 
consideration of the reconciliation bill during Wednesday's session of 
the Senate. That legislation is limited to 20 hours of debate, and 
therefore it is hoped the Senate can complete action on that bill 
Thursday.
  I thank my colleagues for their attention.

                          ____________________
```
