# ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, JULY 28, 1999

```
[[Page 17971]]

                  ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, JULY 28, 1999

  Mr. SESSIONS. Mr. President, on behalf of the majority leader, I ask 
unanimous consent that when the Senate completes its business today, it 
adjourn until the hour of 9:30 a.m. on Wednesday, July 28. I further 
ask consent that on Wednesday, immediately following the prayer, the 
Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed 
expired, and the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use 
later in the day.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
