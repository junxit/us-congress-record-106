# AMERICA MUST NOT TOLERATE MURDERERS

**Speaking:** Traficant, James A., Jr. (T000350) [D-OH]

```
                  AMERICA MUST NOT TOLERATE MURDERERS

  (Mr. TRAFICANT asked and was given permission to address the House 
for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. TRAFICANT. Mr. Speaker, the Yosemite murderer confessed to four

[[Page H6431]]

brutal killings. Cary Stayner said he beheaded one victim. Cary Stayner 
then said he had killed the mother and her 15-year-old daughter. Cary 
Stayner then said he killed their 16-year-old friend as well. Then Cary 
Stayner apologized. My colleagues, Cary Stayner said, ``I'm sorry.''
  Beam me up, Mr. Speaker. I say it is time for a jury to tell Cary 
Stayner, Goodnight, sweet Prince. It is time to meet the devil.
  An America that tolerates murderers like Cary Stayner is an America 
that will have more murderers like Cary Stayner.
  I yield back the record number of victims laid to rest in cemeteries 
all over America.

                          ____________________
```
