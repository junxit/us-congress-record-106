# ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, SEPTEMBER 30, 1999

```
                ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, SEPTEMBER 30, 1999

  Mr. SPECTER. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until the hour of 9:30 
a.m. on Thursday, September 30. I further ask consent that immediately 
following the prayer, the Journal of proceedings be approved to date, 
the morning hour be deemed to have expired, the time for the two 
leaders be reserved for their use later in the day, and the Senate then 
resume consideration of the pending Gregg amendment to the Labor-HHS 
appropriations bill.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
