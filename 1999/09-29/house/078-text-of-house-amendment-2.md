# Text of House Amendment 2

**Speaking:** Andrews, Robert E. (A000210) [D-NJ]

**Measures:** H.R. 2910

```
H.R. 2910

                        Offered by: Mr. Andrews

       Amendment No. 2: At the end of the bill, add the following:

     SEC. 11. TRANSPORTATION OF INCINERATED SOLID WASTE.

       (a) Study.--The National Transportation Safety Board shall 
     conduct a study on risks to public safety related to the 
     transportation of incinerated solid waste through populated 
     areas.
       (b) Report.--Not later than 2 years after the date of 
     enactment of this Act, the Board shall transmit to Congress a 
     report on the results of the study conducted under subsection 
     (a).
```
