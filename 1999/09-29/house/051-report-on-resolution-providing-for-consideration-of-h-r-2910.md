# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 2910, NATIONAL TRANSPORTATION SAFETY BOARD AMENDMENTS ACT OF 1999

```
REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 2910, NATIONAL 
           TRANSPORTATION SAFETY BOARD AMENDMENTS ACT OF 1999

  Mrs. MYRICK, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 106-342) on the resolution (H. Res. 312) providing 
for consideration of the bill (H.R. 2910) to amend title 49, United 
States Code, to authorize appropriations for the National 
Transportation Safety Board for fiscal years 2000, 2001, and 2002, and 
for other purposes, which was referred to the House Calendar and 
ordered to be printed.
```
