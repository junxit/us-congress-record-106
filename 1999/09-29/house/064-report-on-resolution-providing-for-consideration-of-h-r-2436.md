# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 2436, UNBORN VICTIMS OF VIOLENCE ACT OF 1999

**Measures:** H.Res. 313, H.R. 2436

```
 REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 2436, UNBORN 
                    VICTIMS OF VIOLENCE ACT OF 1999

  Mrs. MYRICK, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 106-348) on the resolution (H. Res. 313) providing 
for consideration of the bill (H.R. 2436) to amend title 18, United 
States Code, and the Uniform Code of Military Justice to protect unborn 
children from assault and murder, and for other purposes, which was 
referred to the House Calendar and ordered to be printed.
```
