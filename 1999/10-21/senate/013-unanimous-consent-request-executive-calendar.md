# UNANIMOUS CONSENT REQUEST--EXECUTIVE CALENDAR

```
             UNANIMOUS CONSENT REQUEST--EXECUTIVE CALENDAR

  Mr. LOTT. Madam President, as in executive session, I ask unanimous 
consent that on Monday, October 25, it be in order for the majority 
leader, after consultation with the Democratic leader, to proceed to 
executive session in order to consider the following nominations on the 
Executive Calendar: Nos. 253, 254, 255, 257, 278, and 279.
  Mr. DASCHLE. Reserving the right to object, I ask unanimous consent 
that Calendar No. 159, Marsha Berzon, and Calendar No. 208, Richard 
Paez, be added.
  Mr. LOTT. Madam President, I object to the addition of those nominees 
at this time, although we are working to see if at some point one or 
both of these nominees could be considered.
  Mr. DASCHLE. Madam President, on behalf of a number of colleagues on 
this side, I will be compelled to object at this time.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The objection is heard.

                          ____________________
```
