# ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, OCTOBER 22, 1999

**Speaking:** Brownback, Sam (B000953) [R-KS]

**Measures:** H.R. 434

```
                  ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, OCTOBER 22, 1999

  Mr. BROWNBACK. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until the hour of 9:30 
a.m. on Friday, October 22. I further ask unanimous consent that on 
Friday immediately following the prayer, the Journal of proceedings be 
approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, the time for the 
two leaders be reserved for their use later in the day, and 
notwithstanding the adjournment of the Senate, the Senate then resume 
debate on the motion to proceed to H.R. 434, the sub-Saharan Africa 
free trade bill.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
