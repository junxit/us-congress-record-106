# SCHEDULE

**Speaking:** Mack, Connie, III (M000019) [R-FL]

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. MACK. Mr. President, today the Senate will resume consideration 
of the pending Harkin amendment to the partial-birth abortion ban bill. 
By previous consent, there are 2 hours of debate on the amendment. 
Therefore, Senators can anticipate a vote at approximately 11:30 a.m., 
unless the time is yielded back on the amendment. Senators should be 
aware future rollcall votes are expected in an attempt to complete 
action on the bill prior to adjournment today.
  Following the completion of the partial-birth abortion ban bill, the 
Senate may begin consideration of any legislative items on the calendar 
or any conference reports available for action.
  I thank my colleagues for their attention.

                          ____________________
```
