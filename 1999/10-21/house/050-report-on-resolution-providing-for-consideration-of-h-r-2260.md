# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 2260, PAIN RELIEF PROMOTION ACT OF 1999

**Measures:** H.Res. 339, H.R. 2260

```
  REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 2260, PAIN 
                      RELIEF PROMOTION ACT OF 1999

  Ms. PRYCE of Ohio, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a 
privileged report (Rept. No. 106-409) on the resolution (H. Res. 339) 
providing for consideration of the bill (H.R. 2260) to amend the 
Controlled Substances Act to promote pain management and palliative 
care without permitting assisted suicide and euthanasia, and for other 
purposes, which was referred to the House Calendar and ordered to be 
printed.

                          ____________________
```
