# APPOINTMENT OF MEMBER OF THE HOUSE TO LIBRARY OF CONGRESS TRUST FUND BOARD

```
 APPOINTMENT OF MEMBER OF THE HOUSE TO LIBRARY OF CONGRESS TRUST FUND 
                                 BOARD

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Without objection, and pursuant to the 
provisions of section 1 of the act to create a Library of Congress 
Trust Fund Board, (2 U.S.C. 154), amended by section 1 of Public Law 
102-246, the Chair announces the Speaker's appointment of the following 
Member on the part of the House to the Library of Congress Trust Fund 
Board for a 5-year term to fill the existing vacancy thereon:
  Mr. John Henry of Florida.
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
