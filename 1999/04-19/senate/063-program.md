# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Craig, Larry E. (C000858) [R-ID]

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. CRAIG. For the information of all Senators, the Senate will 
reconvene on Tuesday at 10:30 a.m. and be in a period of morning 
business until 11:30 a.m. At 2:15, the Senate will begin consideration 
of the budget reform legislation, with votes possible throughout the 
day on this bill or any other legislation or executive items cleared 
for action. Later this week, a vote on adoption of the education 
flexibility conference report is expected.
```
