# MESSAGES FROM THE HOUSE

```
                        MESSAGES FROM THE HOUSE

  At 12:17 p.m., a message from the House of Representatives, delivered 
by Mr. Hays, one of its reading clerks, announced that the House has 
agreed to the following concurrent resolutions, in which it requests 
the concurrence of the Senate:

       H. Con. Res. 81. Concurrent resolution permitting the use 
     of the Rotunda of the Capitol for a Ceremony in honor of the 
     Fiftieth Anniversary of the North Atlantic Treaty 
     Organization (NATO) and welcoming the three newest members of 
     NATO, the Republic of Poland, the Republic of Hungary, and 
     the Czech Republic, into NATO.
       H. Con. Res. 83. Concurrent resolution expressing the sense 
     of the Congress that the Government of the Federal Republic 
     of Yugoslavia and its President Slobodan Milosevic release 
     the three detained United States servicemen and abide by the 
     Geneva Conventions regarding the treatment of both prisoners 
     of war and civilians.
```
