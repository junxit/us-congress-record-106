# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 472, LOCAL CENSUS QUALITY CHECK ACT

```
  REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 472, LOCAL 
                        CENSUS QUALITY CHECK ACT

  Mr. SESSIONS, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 106-93) on the resolution (H. Res. 138) providing for 
consideration of the bill (H.R. 472) to amend title 13, United States 
Code, to require the use of postcensus local review as part of each 
decennial census, which was referred to the House Calendar and ordered 
to be printed.
```
