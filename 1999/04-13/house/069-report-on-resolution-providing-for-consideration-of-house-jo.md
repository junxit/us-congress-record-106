# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF HOUSE JOINT RESOLUTION 37, TAX LIMITATION CONSTITUTIONAL AMENDMENT

```
    REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF HOUSE JOINT 
         RESOLUTION 37, TAX LIMITATION CONSTITUTIONAL AMENDMENT

  Mr. SESSIONS, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 106-94) on the resolution (H. Res. 139) providing for 
consideration of the joint resolution (H.J. Res. 37) proposing an 
amendment to the Constitution of the United States with respect to tax 
limitations, which was referred to the House Calendar and ordered to be 
printed.
```
