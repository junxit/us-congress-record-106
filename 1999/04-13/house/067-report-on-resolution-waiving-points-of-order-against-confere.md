# REPORT ON RESOLUTION WAIVING POINTS OF ORDER AGAINST CONFERENCE REPORT ON HOUSE CONCURRENT RESOLUTION 68, CONCURRENT RESOLUTION ON THE BUDGET FOR FISCAL YEAR 2000

```
REPORT ON RESOLUTION WAIVING POINTS OF ORDER AGAINST CONFERENCE REPORT 
ON HOUSE CONCURRENT RESOLUTION 68, CONCURRENT RESOLUTION ON THE BUDGET 
                          FOR FISCAL YEAR 2000

  Mr. SESSIONS, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 106-92) on the resolution (H. Res. 137) waiving 
points of order against the conference report to accompany the 
concurrent resolution (H. Con. Res. 68) establishing the congressional 
budget for the United States Government for fiscal year 2000 and 
setting forth appropriate budgetary levels for each of fiscal years 
2001 through 2009, which was referred to the House Calendar and ordered 
to be printed.
```
