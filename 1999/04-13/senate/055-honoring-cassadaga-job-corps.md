# HONORING CASSADAGA JOB CORPS

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

```
                      HONORING CASSADAGA JOB CORPS

● Mr. SCHUMER. Mr. President, I rise today to honor the 
Cassadaga Job Corps in Cassadaga, New York, which was recently rated 
the nation's top job center. The center's director, Andrew Carpenter, 
and his staff have earned top billing for overall training and job 
placement performance. In addition to winning national and county 
recognition, the Cassadaga Job Corps' achievements have also set state 
records.
  Over the past four years, time and money has been invested in 
upgrading Cassadaga Job Corps facilities, including construction of an 
academic and training center which opened in 1997. Upcoming projects 
include dormitory renovations and construction, development of a waste 
water plant, and remodeling of the nursing education complex.
  I would like to express my congratulations to the Cassadaga Job 
Corps' 120 staffers and 255 students who have earned the privilege of 
being named the best job training center in the nation, and my thanks 
to them for their hard work and dedication.●
```
