# SCHEDULE

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. SESSIONS. This morning, the Senate will be in a period of morning 
business until 12:30 p.m. Following morning business, the Senate will 
recess until 2:15 p.m. to allow the weekly party caucuses to meet, and 
upon reconvening at 2:15 p.m. the Senate will begin immediate 
consideration of the appointment of conferees with respect to the 
budget resolution. Therefore, Members should expect rollcall votes 
during today's session of the Senate.
  The leader has also expressed his intent to consider the budget 
conference report this week, with the hope of a final vote on that 
important legislation by Thursday.
```
