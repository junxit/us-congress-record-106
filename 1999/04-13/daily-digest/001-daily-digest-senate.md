Congressional Budget House Message: Senate received message on House

2009, taking the following action:
  Pages S3615-28
Adopted:
  By a unanimous vote of 98 yeas (Vote No. 82), Lautenberg motion to 
instruct conferees to include in the conference report provisions that 
would reserve all Social Security surpluses only for Social Security, 
and not for other programs (including other retirement programs) or tax 
cuts.
Pages S3615-18, S3626-27
  By 57 yeas to 42 nays (Vote No. 83), Domenici motion to instruct 
conferees to include in the conference report (1) Roth/Breaux Modified 
Amendment No. 176 regarding Medicare reform (adopted March 25, 1999), 
and (2) section 209 of the Senate passed resolution regarding the use 
of non-budget surpluses for a prescription drug benefit. 
                                                     Pages S3621, S3627
  By 66 yeas to 33 nays (Vote No. 85), Dodd motion to instruct 
conferees to include in the conference report the Dodd/Jeffords 
Modified Amendment No. 160 to provide for an increase in the mandatory 
spending in the Child Care and Development Block Grant (adopted on 
March 25, 1999).
Pages S3621-24, S3628
  Dorgan motion to instruct conferees to include in the conference 
report provisions that would provide additional funding for income 
assistance for family farmers above the level provided in the Senate 
passed resolution.
Pages S3624-26
Rejected:
  Kennedy motion to instruct conferees to include in the conference 
report provisions that would (1) allow targeted tax relief for low-and 
middle-income working families, and (2) reserve a sufficient portion of 
projected non-Social Security surpluses to extend significantly the 
solvency of the Medicare Hospital Insurance Trust fund and modernize 
and strengthen the program. (By 54 yeas to 45 nays (Vote No. 84), 
Senate tabled the motion.)
Pages S3618-21, S3627-28
Withdrawn:
  Domenici Amendment No. 252 (to Kennedy motion to instruct conferees-
listed above), to instruct conferees to include in the conference 
report (1) Roth/Breaux Modified Amendment No. 176 regarding Medicare 
reform (adopted March 25, 1999), and (2) section 209 of the Senate 
passed resolution regarding the use of non-budget surpluses for a 
prescription drug benefit.
