# WITHDRAWAL

```
                               WITHDRAWAL

  Executive message transmitted by the President to the Senate on June 
17, 1999, withdrawing from further Senate consideration the following 
nomination:


                       Department of the Treasury

       James W. Wetzler, of New York, to be a Member of the 
     Internal Revenue Oversight Board for a term of three years 
     (New Position), which was sent to the Senate on May 27, 1999.
```
