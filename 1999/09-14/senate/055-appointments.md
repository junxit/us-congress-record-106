# APPOINTMENTS

                              APPOINTMENTS

Public Law 102-586, announces the appointment of Charles Sims, of 
Mississippi, to serve as a member of the Coordinating Council on 
Juvenile Justice and Delinquency Prevention, vice William Keith Oubre.
