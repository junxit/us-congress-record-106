# REPORT ON RESOLUTION WAIVING POINTS OF ORDER AGAINST CONFERENCE REPORT ON H.R. 2490, TREASURY AND GENERAL GOVERNMENT APPROPRIATIONS ACT, 2000

```
REPORT ON RESOLUTION WAIVING POINTS OF ORDER AGAINST CONFERENCE REPORT 
 ON H.R. 2490, TREASURY AND GENERAL GOVERNMENT APPROPRIATIONS ACT, 2000

  Mr. SESSIONS, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 106-322) on the resolution (H. Res. 291) waiving 
points of order against the conference report to accompany the bill 
(H.R. 2490) making appropriations for the Treasury Department, the 
United States Postal Service, the Executive Office of the President, 
and certain Independent Agencies, for the fiscal year ending September 
30, 2000, and for other purposes, which was referred to the House 
Calendar and ordered to be printed.
```
