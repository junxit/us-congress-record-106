# CONSIDERING MEMBER AS PRIMARY SPONSOR OF H.R. 88

**Speaking:** Holt, Rush (H001032) [D-NJ]

**Measures:** H.R. 88

```
            CONSIDERING MEMBER AS PRIMARY SPONSOR OF H.R. 88

  Mr. HOLT. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that I may hereafter 
be considered as the primary sponsor of H.R. 88, a bill originally 
introduced by our esteemed former colleague, Representative Brown of 
California, for the purposes of adding cosponsors and requesting 
reprintings pursuant to clause 7 of rule XII.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. LaHood). Is there objection to the 
request of the gentleman from New Jersey?
  There was no objection.
```
