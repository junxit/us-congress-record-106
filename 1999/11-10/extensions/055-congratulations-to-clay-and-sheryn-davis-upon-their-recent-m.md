# CONGRATULATIONS TO CLAY AND SHERYN DAVIS UPON THEIR RECENT MARRIAGE

```
[[Page 29650]]

  CONGRATULATIONS TO CLAY AND SHERYN DAVIS UPON THEIR RECENT MARRIAGE

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. HAROLD ROGERS

                              of kentucky

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, November 10, 1999

  Mr. ROGERS. Mr. Speaker, I rise to give heartfelt congratulations to 
Mr. Clay Davis and Ms. Sheryn Shearer who were united in marriage on 
Saturday, October 30th, 1999 in Somerset, Kentucky. Clay has been a 
dear friend of mine for many, many years and it was a personal honor 
for me to serve as his best man that day. Clay and Sheryn declared 
their love before God, family, and friends, and I can not think of two 
people who more deserve the everlasting love and happiness they have 
prayed for.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in congratulating Clay 
and Sheryn on their recent nuptials. May their love only continue to 
grow with each passing year.

                          ____________________
```
