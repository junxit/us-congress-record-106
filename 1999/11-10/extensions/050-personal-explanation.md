# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Reyes, Silvestre (R000170) [D-TX]

**Measures:** H.R. 3075, H.R. 3196

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. SILVESTRE REYES

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 9, 1999

  Mr. REYES. Mr. Speaker, on Friday, November 5, 1999, I was away on 
official business and missed rollcall votes 571, 572, and 573. Had I 
been present, I would have voted ``yes'' on the following: Rollcall 
vote No. 571, the Young Amendment to H.R. 3196; rollcall vote No. 572, 
final passage of H.R. 3196 (the Foreign Operations Appropriations bill 
for Fiscal Year 2000); and rollcall vote No. 573, H.R. 3075 (the 
Medicare, Medicaid, and SCHIP Balanced Budget Refinement Act).
```
