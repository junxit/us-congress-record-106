# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 2415, AMERICAN EMBASSY SECURITY ACT OF 1999

**Measures:** H.Res. 247, H.R. 2415

```
REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 2415, AMERICAN 
                      EMBASSY SECURITY ACT OF 1999

  Mr. DIAZ-BALART, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 106-235) on the resolution (H. Res. 247) providing 
for consideration of the bill (H.R. 2415) to enhance security of United 
States missions and personnel overseas, to authorize appropriations for 
the Department of State for fiscal year 2000, and for other purposes, 
which was referred to the House Calendar and ordered to be printed.
```
