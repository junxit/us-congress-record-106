# THE RADOM POST OFFICE

**Speaking:** Shimkus, John (S000364) [R-IL]

```
                         THE RADOM POST OFFICE

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. JOHN SHIMKUS

                              of illinois

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, July 14, 1999

  Mr. SHIMKUS. Mr. Speaker, I would like to take this time to call 
attention to the 125th anniversary of the Radom Post Office. The 
celebration was held on June 16th. Refreshments were served and a 
raffle was held at the end of the day. The post office has been a 
pillar of the community since it was built in 1874. Jane Restoff, the 
current postmaster in Radom, organized the event.

[[Page E1535]]

  In small towns like Radom, the Post Office serves not only as a place 
to send letters, it is a place where the community comes together to 
interact. It is an important part of our heritage and must not be 
forgotten.

                          ____________________
```
