# AMENDING PARAGRAPH 1(m)(1) OF RULE XXV

```
                 AMENDING PARAGRAPH 1(m)(1) OF RULE XXV

  Mr. LOTT. I ask unanimous consent that the Senate proceed to the 
immediate consideration of S. Res. 28 which would change the words 
``Handicapped individuals'' to ``Individuals with disabilities'' in 
Rule XXV.
  I further ask consent the resolution be agreed to and the motion to 
reconsider be laid upon the table.
  The CHIEF JUSTICE. Is there objection?
  Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The resolution (S. Res. 28) was agreed to as follows:

                               S. Res. 28

       Resolved, That paragraph 1(m)(1) of Rule XXV is amended as 
     follows:
       Strike ``Committee on Labor and Human Resources'' and 
     insert in lieu thereof ``Committee on Health, Education, 
     Labor, and Pensions''.
       Strike ``Handicapped individuals'' and insert in lieu 
     thereof ``Individuals with disabilities''.

  Mr. LOTT. That concludes our regular business.

                          ____________________
```
