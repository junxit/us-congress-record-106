# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Brownback, Sam (B000953) [R-KS]

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. BROWNBACK. Mr. President, for the information of all Senators, 
the Senate will be in a period of morning business from 9:30 to 10:30 
a.m. By a previous consent agreement, debate on the African trade bill 
will begin at 10:30 a.m. Amendments to the bill are expected, and it is 
hoped that time agreements can be reached on those amendments so that 
the Senate can complete action on the bill in a timely manner. The 
Senate may also consider legislative or executive calendar items 
cleared for action during tomorrow's session of the Senate.
```
