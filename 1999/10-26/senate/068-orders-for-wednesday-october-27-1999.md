# ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, OCTOBER 27, 1999

```
[[Page 26808]]

                 ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, OCTOBER 27, 1999

  Mr. BROWNBACK. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until 9:30 a.m. on 
Wednesday, October 27. I further ask consent that on Wednesday, 
immediately following the prayer, the Journal of the proceedings be 
approved to date, the morning hour be deemed to have expired, the time 
for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the day, and the 
Senate then proceed to a period of morning business, with Senators 
permitted to speak therein for up to 5 minutes each, with the following 
exceptions: Senator Durbin or designee, from 9:30 to 10 a.m.; Senator 
Thomas or designee, from 10 to 10:30 a.m.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
