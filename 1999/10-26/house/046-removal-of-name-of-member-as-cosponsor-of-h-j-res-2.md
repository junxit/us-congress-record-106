# REMOVAL OF NAME OF MEMBER AS COSPONSOR OF H.J. RES. 2

**Speaking:** Frank, Barney (F000339) [D-MA]

**Measures:** H.J.Res. 2

```
         REMOVAL OF NAME OF MEMBER AS COSPONSOR OF H.J. RES. 2

  Mr. FRANK of Massachusetts. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that 
my name be removed as a cosponsor of House Joint Resolution 2, of which 
I am not particularly fond, and to which my name was added without my 
knowledge in error.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Massachusetts?
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
