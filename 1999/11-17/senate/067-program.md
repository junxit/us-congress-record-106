# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Sessions, Jeff (S001141) [R-AL]

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. SESSIONS. For the information of all Senators, at 11 a.m. on 
Thursday, the Senate will begin a period of morning business until 12 
noon. Following morning business, it is expected that the Senate will 
begin work on measures regarding the appropriations process. Final 
agreements are being made, and it is hoped final action on the 
appropriations measures can begin as soon as possible.
  I thank my colleagues for their patience and cooperation during these 
final days prior to adjournment.

                          ____________________
```
