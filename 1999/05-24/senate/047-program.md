# PROGRAM

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. WARNER. For the information of all Senators, the Senate will 
resume consideration of the Defense Authorization bill tomorrow. Under 
the order, the Senate will debate several amendments, with the votes on 
those amendments occurring in a stacked sequence beginning at 2:15 p.m. 
Tuesday afternoon. All Senators should, therefore, expect at least 
three votes occurring at 2:15. It is the intention of the majority 
leader to complete action on this bill as early as possible this week.
```
