# EXTENSION OF MORNING BUSINESS

```
                     EXTENSION OF MORNING BUSINESS

  Mr. DORGAN. Mr. President, I have cleared this request. I ask 
unanimous consent that morning business be extended until the hour of 
1:30, and that at 1 I be recognized for 20 minutes in morning business.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there objection? Without objection, it is 
so ordered.
  The distinguished Senator from Nebraska is recognized.

                          ____________________
```
