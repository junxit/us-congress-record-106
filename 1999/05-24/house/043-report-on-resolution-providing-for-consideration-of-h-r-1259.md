# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 1259, SOCIAL SECURITY AND MEDICARE SAFE DEPOSIT BOX ACT OF 1999

**Measures:** H.Res. 186, H.R. 1259

```
 REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 1259, SOCIAL 
           SECURITY AND MEDICARE SAFE DEPOSIT BOX ACT OF 1999

  Mr. LINDER, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 106-160) on the resolution (H. Res. 186) providing 
for consideration of the bill (H.R. 1259) to amend the Congressional

strengthened budgetary enforcement mechanisms, which was referred to
```
