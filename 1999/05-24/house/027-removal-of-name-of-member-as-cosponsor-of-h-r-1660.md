# REMOVAL OF NAME OF MEMBER AS COSPONSOR OF H.R. 1660

**Speaking:** Davis, Tom (D000136) [R-VA]

**Measures:** H.R. 1660

```
          REMOVAL OF NAME OF MEMBER AS COSPONSOR OF H.R. 1660

  Mr. DAVIS of Virginia. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that my 
name be removed as a cosponsor of H.R. 1660. I believe it was an honest 
mistake. I was confused with another Davis in the House on that 
legislation. I do not support the legislation.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Virginia?
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
