```
    REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 1906, 
   AGRICULTURE, RURAL DEVELOPMENT, FOOD AND DRUG ADMINISTRATION, AND 
               RELATED AGENCIES APPROPRIATIONS BILL, 2000
```
