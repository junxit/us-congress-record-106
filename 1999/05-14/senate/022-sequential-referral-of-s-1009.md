# SEQUENTIAL REFERRAL OF S. 1009

```
                     SEQUENTIAL REFERRAL OF S. 1009

  Mr. WARNER. Mr. President, pursuant to section 3(b) of S. Res. 400 of 
the 94th Congress, I request that S. 1009, the Intelligence 
Authorization Act for Fiscal Year 2000, which was reported out on May 
11 by the Select Committee on Intelligence, be sequentially referred to 
the Committee on Armed Services for a period not to exceed 30 days.

                          ____________________
```
