# RECESS UNTIL MONDAY, MAY 17, 1999

```
                   RECESS UNTIL MONDAY, MAY 17, 1999

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, the Senate now 
stands in recess until Monday, May 17, 1999.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 3:29 p.m., recessed until Monday, May 17, 
1999, at 12 noon.
```
