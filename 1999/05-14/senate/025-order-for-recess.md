# ORDER FOR RECESS

```
                            ORDER FOR RECESS

  Mr. VOINOVICH. Mr. President, if there is no further business to come 
before the Senate, I now ask that the Senate stand in recess under the 
previous order, following the remarks of Senator Baucus and Senator 
Wyden.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. VOINOVICH. Mr. President, I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will call the roll.
  The legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. BAUCUS. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the order for 
the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
