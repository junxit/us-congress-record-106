# PROGRAM

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. ALLARD. Mr. President, for the information of all Senators, the 
Senate will reconvene tomorrow morning at 10:30 a.m. and begin a period 
of morning business until 11 a.m. Following morning business, the 
Senate will resume consideration of S. 4. It is expected that Senators 
will begin to offer amendments to S. 4 once the bill is reported, and, 
therefore, rollcall votes are possible prior to the policy luncheon 
recess at 12 noon.
```
