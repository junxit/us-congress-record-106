# EXPLANATION OF VOTE

```
                          EXPLANATION OF VOTE

● Mr. SPECTER. Mr. President, on rollcall votes No. 17 and No. 
18, I am recorded as voting ``not guilty.'' I ask that the Record 
reflect that, in fact, when the roll was called, I stood and voted, 
``not proven, therefore not guilty'' on both votes.●
```
