# MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--H.R. 1883

```
[[Page 27844]]

                 MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--H.R. 1883

  Mr. HAGEL. Mr. President, on behalf of the leader, I understand that 
H.R. 1883 is at the desk. I now ask for its first reading.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report the bill by title.
  The legislative assistant read as follows:

       A bill (H.R. 1883) to provide for application of measures 
     to foreign persons who transfer to Iran certain goods, 
     services, or technology, and for other purposes.

  Mr. HAGEL. Mr. President, I now ask for its second reading, and I 
object to my own request.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Objection is heard. The bill will remain at 
the desk.
```
