                         REPORTS OF COMMITTEES

     Resources, with an amendment in the nature of a substitute:
       S. 1296. A bill to designate portions of the lower Delaware

     National Wild and Scenic Rivers System (Rept. No. 106-207).
       By Mr. MURKOWSKI, from the Committee on Energy and Natural 
     Resources, with amendments.

     System (Rept. No. 106-208).
       S. 1569. A bill to amend the Wild and Scenic Rivers Act to

       H.R. 20. A bill to authorize the Secretary of the Interior

       By Mr. MURKOWSKI, from the Committee on Energy and Natural 
     Resources, without amendment.
       H.R. 592. A bill to redesignate Great Kills Park in the

       H.R. 1619. A bill to amend Quinebaug and Shetucket Rivers

       By Mr. BOND, from the Committee on Small Business, with an

       S. 1515. A bill to amend the Radiation Exposure
