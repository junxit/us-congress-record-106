# COSPONSORSHIP OF AMENDMENT

```
[[Page 27800]]

                       COSPONSORSHIP OF AMENDMENT

  Mr. ROBB. Mr. President, on October 20, 1999, during debate over S. 
1692, the Partial Birth Abortion Ban Act, I had asked to be added as a 
cosponsor of Senate amendment 2319, offered by Senator Durbin. 
Unfortunately, my cosponsorship of this amendment was never reflected 
in the Record. Therefore, I ask unanimous consent that my name be added 
as a cosponsor of Senator Durbin's amendment, and that the Record 
reflect that I was a cosponsor of this amendment when it was offered on 
October 20, 1999.

                          ____________________
```
