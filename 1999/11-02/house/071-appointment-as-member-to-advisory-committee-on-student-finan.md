# APPOINTMENT AS MEMBER TO ADVISORY COMMITTEE ON STUDENT FINANCIAL ASSISTANCE

```
   APPOINTMENT AS MEMBER TO ADVISORY COMMITTEE ON STUDENT FINANCIAL 
                               ASSISTANCE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Without objection, and pursuant to section 
491 of the Higher Education Act (20 U.S.C. 1098(c)), and upon the 
recommendation of the majority leader, the Chair announces the 
Speaker's appointment of the following member on the part of the House 
to the Advisory Committee on Student Financial Assistance for a 3-year 
term to fill the existing vacancy thereon.
  Ms. Judith Flink, Illinois.
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
