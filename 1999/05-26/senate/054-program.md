# PROGRAM

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. GRASSLEY. For the information of all Senators, the Senate will 
resume consideration of the Department of Defense authorization bill at 
9:30 a.m. By a previous order, the Senate will immediately begin debate 
on the Allard amendment regarding the Civil Air Patrol. Further, a vote 
will occur in relation to the Allard amendment at 10 a.m. It is the 
intention of the bill managers to complete action on this bill early in 
the day tomorrow, and therefore cooperation of all Senators is 
appreciated.
```
