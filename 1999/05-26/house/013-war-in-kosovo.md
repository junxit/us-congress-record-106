# WAR IN KOSOVO

```
                             WAR IN KOSOVO

  (Mr. TRAFICANT asked and was given permission to address the House 
for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. TRAFICANT. Mr. Speaker, the headlines read, crisis in Kosovo. 
Conflict in Kosovo. Spare me, Mr. Speaker. This is war in Kosovo, 
stone-cold war. And it is time, it is time to support independence for 
Kosovo. There will be no long-lasting peace without it. It is time to 
arm the KLA and send Milosevic looking over his shoulder, and it is 
time to arrest Milosevic for war crimes.
  One last point. After it is over, Europe should clean up Kosovo and 
Europe should pay for the concrete and steel to rebuild Kosovo, not the 
American people.

                          ____________________
```
