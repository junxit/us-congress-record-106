# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. DAN MILLER

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, September 9, 1999

  Mr. MILLER of Florida. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to insert in the 
Record that I inadvertently voted ``yes'' on rollcall 392 on September 
8, 1999. I intended to vote ``no'' on this Roemer amendment to H.R. 
2684 to stop funding for the international space station.
  I believe this is an important NASA project. I have supported the 
space station in the past and have voted against Mr. Roemer's previous 
amendments to kill the space station.
```
