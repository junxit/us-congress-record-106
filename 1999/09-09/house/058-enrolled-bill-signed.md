# ENROLLED BILL SIGNED

```
                          ENROLLED BILL SIGNED

House of the following title, which was thereupon signed by the 
Speaker:

       H.R. 457. An act to amend title 5, United States Code, to

     employee in any year in connection with serving as an organ
```
