# PROGRAM

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. SESSIONS. For the information of all Senators, the Senate will 
convene at 9:30 a.m. and will be in a period of morning business 
throughout the day. As for next week, it is the intention of the 
majority leader to complete action on the Interior appropriations bill 
early next week and to begin consideration of the bankruptcy reform 
bill as well as any available appropriations bills. As previously 
announced by the leader, the next series of rollcall votes will occur 
on Monday, September 13, at 5 p.m.

                          ____________________
```
