# ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, SEPTEMBER 10, 1999

```
                 ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, SEPTEMBER 10, 1999

  Mr. SESSIONS. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it stand adjourned until the hour 
of 9:30 a.m. on Friday, September 10. I further ask unanimous consent 
that on Friday, immediately following the prayer, the Journal of 
proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed to have 
expired, the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later 
in the day, and the Senate then begin morning business time with 
Senators speaking for up to 10 minutes each with the following 
exceptions: Senator Durbin, or his designee, 9:30 to 10:30; Senator 
Coverdell, 10:30 to 11:30.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
