# Text of House Amendment 12

**Speaking:** Hyde, Henry J. (H001022) [R-IL]

**Measures:** H.R. 1658

```
H.R. 1658

                          Offered By: Mr. Hyde

       Amendment No. 12: Page 8, line 10, redesignate paragraph 
     (8) as paragraph (9), and insert after line 9 the following:
       ``(8) When a State or local law enforcement agency 
     participated directly in the seizure or forfeiture of 
     property forfeited under any civil forfeiture statute, that 
     part of the property to be transferred to any State and local 
     entities shall be distributed according to the rules set 
     forth in that State's law or Constitution as to property 
     forfeited under the State forfeiture law.
```
