# COMPANIES MOVING OVERSEAS AND AMERICA IS LOSING JOBS

**Speaking:** Traficant, James A., Jr. (T000350) [D-OH]

```
          COMPANIES MOVING OVERSEAS AND AMERICA IS LOSING JOBS

  (Mr. TRAFICANT asked and was given permission to address the House 
for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. TRAFICANT. Mr. Speaker, the Hanover Shoe Company of Franklin, 
West Virginia is moving overseas. Another 350 jobs going overseas. But 
the workers have been told, and I quote, ``Do not worry. You will find 
a job.''
  Beam me up, Mr. Speaker. Every day, good paying manufacturing jobs 
going overseas, being replaced by minimum wage service-sector jobs. 
Enough is enough. A superpower does not act like a colony.
  The sad truth is ``made in America'' is now street talk for teen 
pregnancy.
  I yield back all the minimum wage part-time jobs without benefits in 
these United States of America.

                          ____________________
```
