# CORRECTION OF NAMES OF COMMITTEES IN HOUSE RESOLUTION 7 AND VACATION OF ELECTION OF MEMBER TO PERMANENT SELECT COMMITTEE ON INTELLIGENCE

**Speaking:** Hoyer, Steny H. (H000874) [D-MD]

**Measures:** H.Res. 7

```
CORRECTION OF NAMES OF COMMITTEES IN HOUSE RESOLUTION 7 AND VACATION OF 
    ELECTION OF MEMBER TO PERMANENT SELECT COMMITTEE ON INTELLIGENCE

  Mr. HOYER. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that any references 
to the Committee on Government Reform and Oversight and the Committee 
on National Security in House Resolution 7 adopted on January 6, 1999, 
be changed to the Committee on Government Reform and the Committee on 
Armed Services, respectively, and that the election of Mr. Dixon of 
California to the Permanent Select Committee on Intelligence by the 
adoption of House Resolution 7 be vacated.
  The SPEAKER. Is there objection to the request of the gentleman from 
Maryland?
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
