# HOUR OF MEETING ON TOMORROW PENDING MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

```
      HOUR OF MEETING ON TOMORROW PENDING MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

  Mr. ARMEY. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that when the House 
adjourns today it stand adjourned until 2 p.m. tomorrow, unless the 
House sooner receives a message from the Senate transmitting its 
concurrence in House Concurrent Resolution 11, in which case the House 
shall stand adjourned pursuant to that concurrent resolution.
  The SPEAKER. Is there objection to the request of the gentleman from 
Texas?
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
