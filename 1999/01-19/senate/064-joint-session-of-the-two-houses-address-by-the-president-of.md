# JOINT SESSION OF THE TWO HOUSES--ADDRESS BY THE PRESIDENT OF THE UNITED STATES

```
JOINT SESSION OF THE TWO HOUSES--ADDRESS BY THE PRESIDENT OF THE UNITED 
                                 STATES

  Mrs. HUTCHISON. I ask unanimous consent that the President of the 
Senate be authorized to appoint a committee on the part of the Senate 
to join with a like committee on the part of the House of 
Representatives to escort the President of the United States into the 
House Chamber for the joint session to be held at 9 p.m. this evening, 
Tuesday, January 19, 1999.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
