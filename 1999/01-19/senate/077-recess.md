# RECESS

```
                                 RECESS

  Mr. LOTT. Mr. Chief Justice, in a moment the Senate will recess until 
8:35 this evening, at which time the Senate will proceed as a body over 
to the House of Representatives as a joint session to receive a message 
from the President. Following the joint session, the Senate will 
adjourn until 11 o'clock tomorrow morning.
  The Senators' lecture series is scheduled for tomorrow evening at 6 
o'clock in the old Senate Chamber with former President George Bush as 
guest speaker.
  I now ask that the Senate stand in recess under the previous order.
  There being no objection, the Senate, at 3:33 p.m., recessed until 
8:35 p.m.; whereupon, the Senate reassembled when called to order by 
the Presiding Officer (Mr. Crapo).

                          ____________________
```
