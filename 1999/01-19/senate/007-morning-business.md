# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, there 
will now be a period for the transaction of morning business until the 
hour of 11:30, with 60 minutes under the control of the majority leader 
and with 60 minutes under the control of the Democratic leader, or 
their designees.
  The Senator from Illinois.
  Mr. DURBIN. Mr. President, as I understand it, there are 2 hours 
equally divided. I ask unanimous consent to designate myself as the 
Senator in charge of the 1 hour designated to the Democrats.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. DURBIN. Thank you, Mr. President.

                          ____________________
```
