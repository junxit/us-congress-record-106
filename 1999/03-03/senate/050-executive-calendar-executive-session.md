                           EXECUTIVE CALENDAR

  Mr. MACK. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
immediately proceed to executive session to consider the following 
nominations on the Executive Calendar: Nos. 10 through 13, and all 
nominations on the Secretary's desk in the Air Force, Army, Marine 
Corps, and Navy.
  I further ask unanimous consent that the nominations be confirmed, 
the motion to reconsider be laid upon the table, any statements 
relating to the nominations appear at the appropriate place in the 
Record, the President be immediately notified of the Senate's action, 
and the Senate then return to legislative session.
