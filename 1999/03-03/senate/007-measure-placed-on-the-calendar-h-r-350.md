# MEASURE PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--H.R. 350

**Speaking:** Jeffords, James M. (J000072) [R-VT]

**Measures:** H.R. 350

```
                MEASURE PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--H.R. 350

  Mr. JEFFORDS. Mr. President, a bill is at the desk due for its second 
reading. I ask it be read.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will read.
  The bill clerk read as follows:

       A bill (H.R. 350) to improve congressional deliberation on 
     proposed Federal private sector mandates, and for other 
     purposes.

  Mr. JEFFORDS. Mr. President, I object to further consideration of 
this measure at this time.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The measure will be placed on the calendar.
```
