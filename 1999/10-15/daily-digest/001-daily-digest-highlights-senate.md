commissions, corporations, and offices for the fiscal year ending

Quality Care for the Uninsured Act--Conferees: Senate authorized the 
Chair to appoint the following conferees on the part of the Senate to 
H.R. 2990, to amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to allow 
individuals greater access to health insurance through a health care 
tax deduction, a long-term care deduction, and other health-related tax 
incentives, to amend the Employee Retirement Income Security Act of 
1974 to provide access to and choice in health care through association 
health plans, to amend the Public Health Service Act to create new 
pooling opportunities for small employers to obtain greater access to 
health coverage through HealthMarts: Senators Jeffords, Gregg, Frist, 
Hutchinson, Nickles, Gramm, Enzi, Kennedy, Dodd, Harkin, Mikulski, and 
Rockefeller.
  Page S12659
  By unanimous consent, Senate vitiated the July 15, 1999 passage of S.

October 14, 1999, after the Senate struck all after the enacting clause 
and inserted the text of S. 1344, as passed. Subsequently, S. 1344 was 
indefinitely postponed.
