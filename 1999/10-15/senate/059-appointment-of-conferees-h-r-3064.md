# APPOINTMENT OF CONFEREES--H.R. 3064

**Speaking:** Daschle, Thomas A. (D000064) [D-SD]

**Measures:** H.R. 3064

```
                  APPOINTMENT OF CONFEREES--H.R. 3064

  Mr. DASCHLE. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that with respect 
to H.R. 3064, the Senate insist on its amendment, request a conference 
with the House, and the Chair be authorized to appoint conferees on the 
part of the Senate.

  The PRESIDING OFFICER (Mr. Brownback) appointed Mrs. Hutchison, Mr. 
Stevens, Mr. Kyl, Mr. Durbin, and Mr. Inouye conferees on the part of 
the Senate.
```
