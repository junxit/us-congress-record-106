# RECESS

```
                                 RECESS

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. All time having expired, under the previous 
order, the Senate will stand in recess until the hour of 2:15 p.m.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 12:38 p.m., recessed until 2:15 p.m.; 
whereupon, the Senate reassembled when called to order by the Presiding 
Officer (Mr. Inhofe).
```
