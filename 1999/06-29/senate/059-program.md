# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Nickles, Don (N000102) [R-OK]

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. NICKLES. For the information of all Senators, Wednesday the 
Senate will convene at 9:30 and will begin consideration of the foreign 
operations appropriations bill. Amendments to that bill are expected, 
and therefore votes are to be expected throughout the day.
  Due to the agreement reached regarding health care reform, it is 
hoped the Senate can complete action on a number of appropriations 
bills prior to the Fourth of July recess.
```
