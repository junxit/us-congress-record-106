# EXPLANATION OF MISSED VOTE

```
                       EXPLANATION OF MISSED VOTE

  Mr. DODD. Mr. President, on Monday June 28, 1999, I was not present 
during Senate action on rollcall vote No. 184, a motion to invoke 
cloture on S. 1233, the Agriculture, Rural Development, Food and Drug 
Administration, and Related Agencies Appropriations Act, 2000, because 
my flight was delayed by inclement weather.
  Had I been present for the vote, I would have voted ``no.''

                          ____________________
```
