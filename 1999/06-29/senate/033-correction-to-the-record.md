# CORRECTION TO THE RECORD

**Speaking:** Bryan, Richard H. (B000993) [D-NV]

**Measures:** S. 1280

```
                        CORRECTION TO THE RECORD

  In the Record of June 24, 1999, on page S7590, the introduction of S. 
1280, a bill to terminate the exemption of certain contractors, and 
other entities from civil penalties for violations of nuclear safety 
requirements under Atomic Energy Act of 1954, and for other purposes, 
was incorrectly attributed to Mrs. Boxer. The permanent Record will be 
corrected to reflect the following:
      By Mr. BRYAN:
  S. 1280. A bill to terminate the exemption of certain contractors and 
other entities from civil penalties for violations of nuclear safety 
requirements under the Atomic Energy Act of 1954; to the Committee on 
Environment and Public Works.

                          ____________________
```
