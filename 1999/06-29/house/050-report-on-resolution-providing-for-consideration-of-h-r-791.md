# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 791, STAR- SPANGLED BANNER NATIONAL HISTORIC TRAIL STUDY ACT OF 1999

```
  REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 791, STAR-
       SPANGLED BANNER NATIONAL HISTORIC TRAIL STUDY ACT OF 1999

  Mrs. MYRICK, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 106-210) on the resolution (H. Res. 232) providing 
for consideration of the bill (H.R. 791) to amend the National Trail 
Systems Act to designate the route of the War of 1812 British invasion 
of Maryland and Washington, District of Columbia, and the route of the 
American defense, for study for potential addition to the national 
trail systems, which was referred to the House Calendar and ordered to 
be printed.
```
