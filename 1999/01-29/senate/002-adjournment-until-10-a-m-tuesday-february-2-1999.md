# ADJOURNMENT UNTIL 10 A.M., TUESDAY, FEBRUARY 2, 1999

```
          ADJOURNMENT UNTIL 10 A.M., TUESDAY, FEBRUARY 2, 1999

  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the Senate 
stands adjourned until 10 a.m. on Tuesday, February 2, 1999.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 10 o'clock and 22 seconds a.m., adjourned 
until Tuesday, February 2, 1999, at 10 a.m.
```
