# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Fitzgerald, Peter (F000442) [R-IL]

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. FITZGERALD. For the information of all Senators, at 9:30 a.m. on 
Tuesday, the Senate will immediately begin 30 minutes of debate on the 
conference report to accompany the D.C./Labor-HHS appropriations bill. 
Following the debate, the Senate will proceed to a vote on the 
conference report which will be followed by possibly two cloture votes 
in relation to the African trade bill. Therefore, Senators can 
anticipate up to three stacked votes at approximately 10 a.m. It is 
expected cloture will be invoked and the Senate will begin the 30-hours 
of postcloture debate on the CBI/African trade bill.
  The leader has indicated he hopes to complete action on the trade 
bill this week.

                          ____________________
```
