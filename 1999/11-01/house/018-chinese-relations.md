# CHINESE RELATIONS

```
                           CHINESE RELATIONS

  (Mr. TRAFICANT asked and was given permission to address the House 
for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. TRAFICANT. Mr. Speaker, reports say the White House will support 
China over Taiwan, thus communism over democracy. Unbelievable. The 
reports say the White House will put tough conditions on it. Like what, 
Mr. Speaker? A waiting period on Chinese missile launches? A promise 
that China will not sell any of their stolen technology at missile 
shows? How about trigger locks on all those Chinese missiles?
  Beam me up, Mr. Speaker. These cerebral constipators have already 
given away the farm. Now they are starting to play with our freedom.
  I yield back the fact that we built the Panama Canal and China now 
runs it.

                          ____________________
```
