# ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

```
                ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Barton of Texas). Pursuant to clause 8 
of rule XX, the Chair announces that he will reduce to a minimum of 5 
minutes the period of time within which a vote by electronic device may 
be taken on each additional motion to suspend the rules on which the 
Chair has postponed further proceedings.
```
