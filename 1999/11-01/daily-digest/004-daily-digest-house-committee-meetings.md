# Daily Digest/House Committee Meetings

**Measures:** H.R. 2990

```
Committee Meetings
QUALITY CARE FOR THE UNINSURED ACT--MOTION TO GO TO CONFERENCE
Committee on Rules; Granted, by voice vote, a rule providing that the 
House disagrees to the Senate amendment to H.R. 2990, Quality Care for 
the Uninsured Act of 1999. The rule provides that the House agrees to 
the conference requested by the Senate thereon.
```
