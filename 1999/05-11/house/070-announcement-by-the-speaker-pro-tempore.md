# ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

```
                ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Kuykendall). The Chair is concerned 
about a couple of remarks made by previous speakers earlier this 
evening and will remind all Members that the rules of decorum in the 
debate prohibit the attribution of unworthy motives to the President. 
That standard applies both to debate and to extraneous material read 
into the Record.
```
