# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** DeWine, Mike (D000294) [R-OH]

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. DeWINE. For the information of all Senators, the Senate will 
convene on Wednesday, May 12 at 9:30 a.m. and immediately resume 
consideration of the Leahy amendment, with a vote to take place at 
approximately 10:30 a.m. Following the disposition of the Leahy 
amendment, Senator Brownback will be recognized to offer an amendment. 
Senators can expect votes throughout Wednesday's session of the Senate, 
with the possibility of votes into the evening. I appreciate the 
cooperation of my colleagues.
```
