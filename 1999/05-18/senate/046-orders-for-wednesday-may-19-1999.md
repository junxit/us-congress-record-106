# ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, MAY 19, 1999

```
                   ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, MAY 19, 1999

  Mr. HATCH. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it stand in adjournment until 10 
a.m. on Wednesday, May 19. I further ask that on Wednesday, immediately 
following the prayer, the Journal of proceedings be approved to date, 
the morning hour be deemed to have expired, and the time for the two 
leaders be reserved for their use later in the day.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
