# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF CONCURRENT RESOLUTION WAIVING SECTION 132 OF THE LEGISLATIVE REORGANIZATION ACT OF 1946

```
    REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF CONCURRENT 
RESOLUTION WAIVING SECTION 132 OF THE LEGISLATIVE REORGANIZATION ACT OF 
                                  1946

  Mr. DREIER, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 106-274) on the resolution (H.Res. 266) providing for 
consideration of a concurrent resolution waiving the requirement in 
section 32 of the Legislative Reorganization Act of 1946 that the 
Congress adjourn sine die not later than July 31, 1999, which was 
referred to the House Calendar and ordered to be printed.

                          ____________________
```
