# DEPARTMENT OF THE INTERIOR AND RELATED AGENCIES APPROPRIATIONS ACT, 2000

```
  DEPARTMENT OF THE INTERIOR AND RELATED AGENCIES APPROPRIATIONS ACT, 
                                  2000

                                 ______
                                 

                  BURNS (AND CRAIG) AMENDMENT NO. 1408

  (Ordered to lie on the table.)
  Mr. BURNS (for himself and Mr. Craig) submitted an amendment intended 
to be proposed by them to the bill (H.R. 2466) making appropriations 
for the Department of the Interior and related agencies for the fiscal 
year ending September 30, 2000, and for other purposes; as follows:

       Insert in general provisions the following:
       None of the funds made available by this Act may be used 
     for the physical relocation of grizzly bears into the Selway-
     Bitterroot Wilderness of Idaho and Montana.

                          ____________________
```
