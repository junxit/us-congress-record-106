# PROGRAM

```
                               PROGRAM

  Mr. ROTH. For the information of all Senators, when the Senate 
reconvenes on Friday, there will be 30 minutes for closing remarks with 
respect to the Bingaman amendment and the Hutchison amendment. Two 
back-to-back votes will then occur at 9 a.m. Following those two votes, 
any additional amendments will be limited to 2 minutes of debate. 
Therefore, numerous votes will occur in a stacked sequence. 
Consequently, Senators are asked not to leave the Chamber in order to 
conclude the voting process as early as possible.
```
