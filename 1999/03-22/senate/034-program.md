# PROGRAM

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. STEVENS. For the information of all Senators, the Senate will 
reconvene tomorrow at 10 a.m. and resume consideration of the Lott 
amendment to the supplemental appropriations bill. Under the previous 
order, the time until 12:30 will be equally divided for debate on the 
amendment.
  The Senate will then recess until 2:15 p.m. for the policy lunches 
and upon reconvening will proceed to vote on the motion to invoke 
cloture on the Lott amendment. Following that vote, it is hoped that 
the Senate will begin consideration of the fiscal year 2000 budget 
resolution. Therefore, Members should expect rollcall votes throughout 
Tuesday's session, with the first vote to occur on cloture at 2:15.
```
