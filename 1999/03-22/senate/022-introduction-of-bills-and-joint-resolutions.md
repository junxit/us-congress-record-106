              INTRODUCTION OF BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

  The following bills and joint resolutions were introduced, read the 
first and second time by unanimous consent, and referred as indicated:

           By Mr. CAMPBELL (for himself, Mr. McCain, Mr. Smith of 
             New Hampshire, Mr. Kerry, Mr. Lugar, Mr. Coverdell, 
             Mr. Lieberman, Mr. Lautenberg, Mr. Ashcroft, Mr. 
             Torricelli, Mr. Kennedy, Mr. Schumer, Mr. Allard, and 
             Mr. Santorum):
       S. 676. A bill to locate and secure the return of Zachary 
     Baumel, a citizen of the United States, and other Israeli 
     soldiers missing in action; to the Committee on Foreign 
     Relations.

       S. 677. A bill to amend the Immigration and Nationality Act 
     to provide a limited waiver of a requirement for 
     reimbursement of local educational agencies for the costs of 
     foreign students' education in certain cases; to the 
     Committee on the Judiciary.
