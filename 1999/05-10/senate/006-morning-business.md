# MORNING BUSINESS

**Speaking:** Collins, Susan M. (C001035) [R-ME]

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, there will now be a 
period for the transaction of morning business not to extend beyond the 
hour of 2 p.m. with the time equally divided between the majority 
leader or his designee and the minority leader or his designee, with 
Senator Collins permitted to speak therein for not to exceed 15 minutes 
utilizing the majority time.
  The distinguished Senator is recognized.
  Ms. COLLINS. I thank the Chair.

                          ____________________
```
