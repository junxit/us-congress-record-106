# PROGRAM

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. ROBERTS. For the information of all Senators, the Senate will 
begin debate on the juvenile justice bill at 9:30. Amendments are 
expected to that legislation, and therefore rollcall votes can be 
expected during tomorrow afternoon's session of the Senate. As always, 
Members will be notified accordingly as any votes are ordered with 
respect to this legislation.
  Members who intend to offer amendments to the juvenile justice bill 
are encouraged to work with the chairman and the ranking member to 
schedule a time to come to the floor to debate those amendments.
```
