# SCHEDULE

**Speaking:** Collins, Susan M. (C001035) [R-ME]

**Measures:** S. 254

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Ms. COLLINS. Today the Senate will be in a period of morning business 
until 2 p.m. There will be no rollcall votes during today's session of 
the Senate. Under the previous order, the Senate will begin 
consideration of S. 254, the juvenile justice bill, at 9:30 a.m. 
tomorrow. It is hoped significant progress will be made on that bill, 
and therefore Senators should expect votes after 2:15 p.m. during 
Tuesday's session of the Senate. I thank my colleagues for their 
attention.

                          ____________________
```
