# CONGRATULATIONS TO AMERICA'S TEAM, THE ATLANTA BRAVES

**Speaking:** Lewis, John (L000287) [D-GA]

```
         CONGRATULATIONS TO AMERICA'S TEAM, THE ATLANTA BRAVES

  (Mr. LEWIS of Georgia asked and was given permission to address the 
House for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. LEWIS of Georgia. Mr. Speaker, I rise this morning to 
congratulate the Atlanta Braves, America's team. I wish Bobby Cox and 
all the members of the Braves family the very best in their great 
nonviolent struggle against the New York Yankees.
  I say this morning: Braves, go Braves. Go and win. You must win. When 
you win, America wins. Go Braves. Go Braves.

                          ____________________
```
