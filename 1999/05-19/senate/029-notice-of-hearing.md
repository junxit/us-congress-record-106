# NOTICE OF HEARING

**Speaking:** Murkowski, Frank H. (M001085) [R-AK]

```
                           NOTICE OF HEARING


               Committee on Energy and Natural Resources

  Mr. MURKOWSKI. Mr. President, I would like to announce for the 
information of the Senate and the public that a full committee hearing 
has been scheduled before the Committee on Energy and Natural 
Resources.
  The hearing will take place on Thursday, May 27, 1999 at 9:30 a.m. in 
room SD-366 of the Dirksen Senate Office Building in Washington, DC.
  The purpose of this hearing is to consider the nomination of David L. 
Goldwyn to be an Assistant Secretary for International Affairs at the 
Department of Energy.
  For further information, please contact David Dye of the Committee 
staff at (202) 224-0624.

                          ____________________
```
