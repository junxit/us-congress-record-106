# LEGISLATIVE PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Sensenbrenner, F. James, Jr. (S000244) [R-WI]

```
                          LEGISLATIVE PROGRAM

  (Mr. SENSENBRENNER asked and was given permission to address the 
House for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. SENSENBRENNER. Mr. Speaker, I have discussed with the gentleman 
from Illinois (Mr. Costello), and unless there is an amendment that we 
do not know about, we will probably not have votes on the next bill 
that is coming up. I cannot give a complete assurance that there will 
be no rollcall votes, but my guess is that all of the amendments and 
the bill will be disposed of by voice vote and the Members can take 
that into account when making their plans.

                          ____________________
```
