  Pages H1036-41

expressing the sense of Congress that the United States should introduce and 
make all efforts necessary to pass a resolution criticizing the People's 
Republic of China for its human rights abuses in China and Tibet at the 
annual meeting of the United Nations Commission on Human Rights.

H. Con. Res. 45, providing for the use of the catafalque situated in the 
crypt beneath the rotunda of the Capitol in connection with memorial 
services to be conducted in the Supreme Court Building for the late 
honorable Harry A. Blackmun, former Associate Justice of the Supreme Court 
of the United States.
