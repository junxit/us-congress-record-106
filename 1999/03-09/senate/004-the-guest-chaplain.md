# THE GUEST CHAPLAIN

**Speaking:** Santorum, Rick (S000059) [R-PA], Kennedy, Edward M. (K000105) [D-MA]

```
                           THE GUEST CHAPLAIN

  Mr. SANTORUM. Mr. President, I, too, want to welcome Reverend Kelsey 
today and thank him for his inspirational prayer. He is one of the 
truly distinguished leaders of the church community here in Washington, 
DC.
  Welcome back to Washington. You have been away for a while. It is 
good to have you back here, and it is terrific to have you here in the 
U.S. Senate.
  Thank you for being here today.
  Mr. KENNEDY. Mr. President, I join my friend, the Senator from 
Pennsylvania, in welcoming our distinguished guest Chaplain. I thank 
him very much for his presence and for his inspiring message to all of 
us. We are very grateful to him for joining us here today.
  We thank him very much for all the good work that he does and 
continues to do for his parishioners.

                          ____________________
```
