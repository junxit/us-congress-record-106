# PROGRAM

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. BENNETT. Mr. President, for the information of all Senators, when 
the Senate reconvenes on Monday, there will be an hour for morning 
business, to be followed by 2 hours for debate on the sweepstakes bill. 
At 3 p.m. on Monday, the Senate will resume the Agriculture 
Appropriations bill, and the next rollcall vote will occur at 5:30 p.m. 
Monday, August 2, on passage of S. 335. Additional votes could occur 
relative to the Agriculture Appropriations bill.
```
