# APPOINTMENT OF MEMBERS TO BOARD OF TRUSTEES OF HARRY S. TRUMAN SCHOLARSHIP FOUNDATION

```
    APPOINTMENT OF MEMBERS TO BOARD OF TRUSTEES OF HARRY S. TRUMAN 
                         SCHOLARSHIP FOUNDATION

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Without objection, and pursuant to section 
5(b) of Public Law 93-642 (20 U.S.C. 2004(b)), the Chair announces the 
Speaker's appointment of the following Members of the House as members 
of the Board of Trustees of the Harry S. Truman Scholarship Foundation:
  Mrs. Emerson, Missouri and
  Mr. Skelton, Missouri.
  There was no objection.
```
