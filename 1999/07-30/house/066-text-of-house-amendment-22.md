# Text of House Amendment 22

**Speaking:** Kucinich, Dennis J. (K000336) [D-OH]

**Measures:** H.R. 2606

```
H.R. 2606

                        Offered By: Mr. Kucinich

       Amendment No. 22:
       Sec. __. None of the funds made available in this Act may 
     be used by the Overseas Private Investment Corporation to 
     provide any administrative support, credit program support, 
     loan, loan guaranty, insurance, or other assistance for any 
     environmentally sensitive Investment Fund project.
```
