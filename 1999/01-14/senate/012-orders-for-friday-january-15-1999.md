# ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, JANUARY 15, 1999

**Speaking:** Lott, Trent (L000447) [R-MS]

```
                  ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, JANUARY 15, 1999

  Mr. LOTT. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate 
completes its business today, it stand in adjournment until the hour of 
1 p.m. on Friday, January 15. I further ask unanimous consent that on 
Friday, immediately following the prayer, the Senate resume 
consideration of the articles of impeachment.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
