# ADJOURNMENT UNTIL 1 P.M. TOMORROW

**Speaking:** Lott, Trent (L000447) [R-MS]

```
                   ADJOURNMENT UNTIL 1 P.M. TOMORROW

  Mr. LOTT. Mr. President, if there is no further business to come 
before the Senate, I now ask unanimous consent that the Senate stand in 
adjournment under the previous order.

Friday, January 15, 1999, at 1 p.m.

       David C. Williams, of Maryland, to be Inspector General for 
     Tax Administration, Department of the Treasury. (New 
     Position)


              Federal Mediation and Conciliation Director

     and Conciliation Director, vice John Calhoun Wells, resigned.


                        Department of Education

       Lorraine Pratte Lewis, of the District of Columbia, to be 
     Inspector General, Department of Education, vice Thomas R. 
     Bloom.
```
