# ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, OCTOBER 6, 1999

```
                 ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, OCTOBER 6, 1999

  Mr. LOTT. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate 
completes its business today, it adjourn until 9:30 a.m., Wednesday, 
October 6.
  I further ask unanimous consent that, on Wednesday, immediately 
following the prayer, the journal of the proceedings be approved to 
date, the morning hour be deemed expired, the time for the two leaders 
be reserved for their use later in the day, and the Senate then resume 
debate on S. 1650, the Labor-HHS Appropriations bill.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
