# SCHEDULE

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. McCAIN. Mr. President, today the Senate will resume consideration 
of the pending amendments to the FAA bill. Senators should be aware 
that rollcall votes are possible today prior to the 12:30 recess in an 
attempt to complete action on the bill by the end of the day. As a 
reminder, first-degree amendments to the bill must be filed by 10 a.m. 
today. As a further reminder, debate on three judicial nominations took 
place last night and by previous consent there will be three stacked 
votes on those nominations at 2:15 p.m. today. Following the completion 
of the FAA bill, the Senate will resume consideration of the Labor-HHS 
appropriations bill.
  I thank my colleagues for their attention.

                          ____________________
```
