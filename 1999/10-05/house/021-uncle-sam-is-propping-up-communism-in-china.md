# UNCLE SAM IS PROPPING UP COMMUNISM IN CHINA

**Speaking:** Traficant, James A., Jr. (T000350) [D-OH]

```
              UNCLE SAM IS PROPPING UP COMMUNISM IN CHINA

  (Mr. TRAFICANT asked and was given permission to address the House 
for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. TRAFICANT. Mr. Speaker, last week China celebrated 50 years of 
communist rule. They had parades with tanks, missiles, communism on 
display after all our efforts to defeat communism.
  What is troubling, Mr. Speaker, is they were partying in China on our 
cash, a $70-billion trade surplus. Unbelievable. The truth is, 
communism in China would be belly up today if it were not for our trade 
policy.
  Beam me up. Uncle Sam is now propping up communism. I yield back 
Taiwan, Johnny Huang, Charlie Trie, and all the Chinese spies running 
around our nuclear labs.

                          ____________________
```
