# UNANIMOUS-CONSENT AGREEMENTS EN BLOC--STANDING ORDERS

**Speaking:** Lott, Trent (L000447) [R-MS]

```
         UNANIMOUS-CONSENT AGREEMENTS EN BLOC--STANDING ORDERS

  Mr. LOTT. Mr. President, the following unanimous consent requests are 
those of the standing orders--for example, setting the leader's time 
each day--which are obtained at the beginning of each Congress, which 
govern the day-to-day activity. As in the past, these consents have 
been cleared by the Democratic leader. Therefore, I send to the desk 11 
unanimous consent

[[Page S6]]

requests and ask for their immediate consideration en bloc, that the 
requests be agreed to en bloc, and that the various consents be shown 
separately in the Record.
  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
