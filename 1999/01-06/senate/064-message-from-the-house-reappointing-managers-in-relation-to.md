# MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE--REAPPOINTING MANAGERS IN RELATION TO THE IMPEACHMENT OF WILLIAM JEFFERSON CLINTON, PRESIDENT OF THE UNITED STATES

**Measures:** H.Res. 10

```
   MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE--REAPPOINTING MANAGERS IN RELATION TO THE 
   IMPEACHMENT OF WILLIAM JEFFERSON CLINTON, PRESIDENT OF THE UNITED 
                                 STATES

  A message from the House of Representatives by Mr. Hays, one of its 
reading clerks, announced that the House of Representatives had passed 
a resolution (H. Res. 10) reappointing managers in relation to the 
impeachment of William Jefferson Clinton, President of the United 
States.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The message will be received and the Senate 
takes notice of the action by the House.
```
