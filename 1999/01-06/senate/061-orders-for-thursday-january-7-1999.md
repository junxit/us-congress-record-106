# ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, JANUARY 7, 1999

```
                  ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, JANUARY 7, 1999

  Mr. LOTT. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate

[[Page 38]]

completes its business today it stand in adjournment until 9:45 a.m., 
on Thursday, January 7. I further ask that when the Senate reconvenes 
on Thursday, immediately following the prayer, the Journal of 
proceedings be approved, the morning hour be deemed to have expired, 
and the majority leader then be immediately recognized.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
