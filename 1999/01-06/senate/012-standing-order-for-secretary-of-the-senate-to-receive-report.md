# STANDING ORDER FOR SECRETARY OF THE SENATE TO RECEIVE REPORTS

**Speaking:** Lott, Trent (L000447) [R-MS]

```
     STANDING ORDER FOR SECRETARY OF THE SENATE TO RECEIVE REPORTS

  Mr. LOTT. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that for the 
duration of the Congress, it be in order for the Secretary of the 
Senate to receive reports at the desk when presented by a Senator at 
any time during the day of the session of the Senate.
  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
