# RECESS

**Speaking:** Burns, Conrad R. (B001126) [R-MT], Lott, Trent (L000447) [R-MS]

```
                                 RECESS

  Mr. BURNS. Mr. President, seeing no Senator seeking the floor, I ask 
unanimous consent that the Senate stand in recess until 6:30 p.m. today 
and that the majority leader be recognized at that time.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Thereupon, at 6 p.m., the Senate recessed until 6:31 p.m.; whereupon, 
the Senate reassembled when called to order by the Presiding Officer 
(Mr. Brownback).
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. In my capacity as a Senator from the State of 
Kansas, I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The clerk will call the roll.
  The legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. LOTT. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the order for 
the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
