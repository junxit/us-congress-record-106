# SENATE RESOLUTION 1--INFORMING THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES THAT A QUORUM OF THE SENATE IS ASSEMBLED

**Measures:** S.Res. 1

```
  SENATE RESOLUTION 1--INFORMING THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES THAT A 
                   QUORUM OF THE SENATE IS ASSEMBLED

  Mr. LOTT submitted the following resolution; which was considered and 
agreed to:

                               S. Res. 1

       Resolved, That the Secretary inform the House of 
     Representatives that a quorum of the Senate is assembled and 
     that the Senate is ready to proceed to business.
```
