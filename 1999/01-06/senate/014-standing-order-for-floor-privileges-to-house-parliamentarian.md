# STANDING ORDER FOR FLOOR PRIVILEGES TO HOUSE PARLIAMENTARIAN

**Speaking:** Lott, Trent (L000447) [R-MS]

```
      STANDING ORDER FOR FLOOR PRIVILEGES TO HOUSE PARLIAMENTARIAN

  Mr. LOTT. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the 
Parliamentarian of the House of Representatives and his four assistants 
be given the privilege of the floor during the 106th Congress.
  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
