# RECESS

```
                                 RECESS

  Mr. COVERDELL. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
stand in recess until 6 p.m. today, with the majority leader recognized 
at that time.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 4:58 p.m., recessed until 6 p.m.; 
whereupon, the Senate reassembled when called to order by the Presiding 
Officer (Mr. Grams).

                          ____________________
```
