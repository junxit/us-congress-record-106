# APPOINTMENT AS MEMBERS OF HOUSE OFFICE BUILDING COMMISSION

```
       APPOINTMENT AS MEMBERS OF HOUSE OFFICE BUILDING COMMISSION

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Without objection and pursuant to the 
provisions of 40 United States Code, 175 and 176, the Chair announces 
the Speaker's appointment of the gentleman from Texas, (Mr. Armey) and 
the gentleman from Missouri (Mr. Gephardt) as members of the House 
Office Building Commission to serve with himself.
  There was no objection.
```
