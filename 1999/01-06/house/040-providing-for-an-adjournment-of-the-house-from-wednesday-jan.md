# PROVIDING FOR AN ADJOURNMENT OF THE HOUSE FROM WEDNESDAY, JANUARY 6, 1999, TO TUESDAY, JANUARY 19, 1999

**Speaking:** Armey, Richard K. (A000217) [R-TX]

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 2

```
 PROVIDING FOR AN ADJOURNMENT OF THE HOUSE FROM WEDNESDAY, JANUARY 6, 
                   1999, TO TUESDAY, JANUARY 19, 1999

  Mr. ARMEY. Mr. Speaker, I offer a privileged concurrent resolution 
(H. Con. Res. 2) and ask for its immediate consideration.

[[Page H221]]

  The Clerk read the concurrent resolution, as follows:

                             H. Con. Res. 2

       Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate 
     concurring), That when the House adjourns on the legislative 
     day of Wednesday, January 6, 1999, it stand adjourned until 2 
     p.m. on Tuesday, January 19, 1999.

  The concurrent resolution was agreed to.
  A motion to reconsider was laid on the table.

                          ____________________
```
