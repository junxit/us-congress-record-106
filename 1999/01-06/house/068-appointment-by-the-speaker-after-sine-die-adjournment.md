# APPOINTMENT BY THE SPEAKER AFTER SINE DIE ADJOURNMENT

```
         APPOINTMENT BY THE SPEAKER AFTER SINE DIE ADJOURNMENT

Proliferation of Weapons of Mass Destruction Act of 1996, amended by
```
