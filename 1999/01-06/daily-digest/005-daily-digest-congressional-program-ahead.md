# Daily Digest/CONGRESSIONAL PROGRAM AHEAD

```
                      CONGRESSIONAL PROGRAM AHEAD

                   Week of January 4 through 9, 1999

                             Senate Chamber

  On Thursday, Senate will proceed to consideration of the articles of 
impeachment against President Clinton.
  On Friday, Senate's program is uncertain.


                           Senate Committees

  No committee meetings are scheduled.


                             House Chamber

  The House is not in session on January 7 and 8.


                            House Committees

  Committee on Commerce, January 7, to meet for organizational 
purposes, 10:30 a.m., 2123 Rayburn.
  Committee on Education and the Workforce, January 7, to meet for 
organizational purposes, 10:30 a.m., 2175 Rayburn.
  Committee on International Relations, January 8, hearing on 
Nipping Democracy in the Bud: The New Crackdown on Dissidents in 
China, 10 a.m., 2172 Rayburn.
  Committee on Small Business, January 7, to meet for organizational 
purposes; followed by a hearing and mark up of a measure concerning 
technical amendments to the Small Business Investment Company (SBIC) 
program, 10 a.m., 2360 Rayburn.
  Committee on Transportation and Infrastructure, January 7, to meet 
for organizational purposes, 2:30 p.m., 2167 Rayburn.
```
