# UNANIMOUS-CONSENT AGREEMENT--CONFERENCE REPORT TO ACCOMPANY H.R. 2490

**Speaking:** Shelby, Richard C. (S000320) [R-AL]

**Measures:** H.R. 2490

```
 UNANIMOUS-CONSENT AGREEMENT--CONFERENCE REPORT TO ACCOMPANY H.R. 2490

  Mr. SHELBY. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that at 10 a.m. on 
Thursday, September 16, the Senate proceed to the consideration of the 
conference report to accompany H.R. 2490, the Treasury-Postal 
appropriations bill.
  I further ask consent that the reading be waived and that there be 10 
minutes of debate equally divided in the usual form.
  I finally ask consent that following the debate, the Senate proceed 
to a vote on the adoption of the conference report with no intervening 
action or debate.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
