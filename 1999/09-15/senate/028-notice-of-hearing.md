# NOTICE OF HEARING

**Speaking:** Murkowski, Frank H. (M001085) [R-AK]

```
                           NOTICE OF HEARING


               committee on energy and natural resources

  Mr. MURKOWSKI. Mr. President, I would like to announce that on 
Thursday, September 23rd, the Committee on Energy and Natural Resources 
will hold an oversight hearing titled, ``Y2K--Will The Lights Go Out?'' 
The purpose of the hearing is to explore the potential consequences of 
the year 2000 computer problem to the Nation's supply of electricity. 
The hearing will be held at 9:30 a.m. in room 366 of the Dirksen Senate 
Office Building in Washington, D.C.
  Those who wish further information may write to the Committee on 
Energy and Natural Resources, U.S. Senate, Washington, D.C. 20510.

                          ____________________
```
