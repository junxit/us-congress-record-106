# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 1402, CONSOLIDATION OF MILK MARKETING ORDERS

**Measures:** H.Res. 294, H.R. 1402

```
    REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 1402, 
                 CONSOLIDATION OF MILK MARKETING ORDERS

  Mr. DREIER, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 106-324) on the resolution (H. Res. 294) providing 
for consideration of the bill (H.R. 1402) to require the Secretary of 
Agriculture to implement the Class I milk price structure known as 
Option 1A as part of the implementation of the final rule to 
consolidate Federal milk marketing orders, which was referred to the 
House Calendar and ordered to be printed.
```
