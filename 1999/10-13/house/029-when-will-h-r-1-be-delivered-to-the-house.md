# WHEN WILL H.R. 1 BE DELIVERED TO THE HOUSE?

**Speaking:** Ose, Doug (O000164) [R-CA]

**Measures:** H.R. 1

```
              WHEN WILL H.R. 1 BE DELIVERED TO THE HOUSE?

  (Mr. OSE asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute.)
  Mr. OSE. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to inquire when are we going to 
get H.R. 1 delivered to this House? When I arrived here in January, one 
of the things we did out of respect for the administration was reserve 
H.R. 1 for the President's plan on Social Security. It is now the 
middle of October, and the President's plan is still absent.
  When can we expect the delivery of H.R. 1 from the administration?

                          ____________________
```
