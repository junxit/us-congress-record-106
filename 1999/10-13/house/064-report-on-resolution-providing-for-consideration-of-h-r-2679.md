# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 2679, MOTOR CARRIER SAFETY ACT OF 1999

```
 REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 2679, MOTOR 
                       CARRIER SAFETY ACT OF 1999

  Mr. SESSIONS, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 106-381) on the resolution (H. Res. 329) providing 
for consideration of the bill (H.R. 2679) to amend title 49, United 
States Code, to establish the National Motor Carrier Administration in 
the Department of Transportation, to improve the safety of commercial 
motor vehicle operators and carriers, to strengthen commercial driver's 
licenses, and for other purposes, which was referred to the House 
Calendar and ordered to be printed.
```
