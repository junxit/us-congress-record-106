# ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, OCTOBER 14, 1999

**Speaking:** McConnell, Mitch (M000355) [R-KY]

```
                 ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, OCTOBER 14, 1999

  Mr. McCONNELL. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until the hour of 9:30 
a.m. on Thursday, October 14. I further ask consent that on Thursday, 
immediately following the prayer, the Journal of proceedings be 
approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, the time for the 
two leaders be reserved for their use later in the day, and the Senate 
then begin 60 minutes of debate on the conference report to accompany 
the Defense appropriations bill, as provided under the previous order.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
