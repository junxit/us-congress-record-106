# PROGRAM

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. McCONNELL. For the information of all Senators, the Senate will 
begin consideration of the Defense appropriations conference report at 
9:30 a.m. tomorrow. By previous consent, there will be 60 minutes of 
debate on the conference report, with a vote scheduled to occur at 4 
p.m. tomorrow. For the remainder of the day, the Senate will resume 
debate on the campaign finance reform bill. Amendments to the bill are 
expected to be offered, and therefore Senators may anticipate votes 
throughout the day. The Senate may also consider any other conference 
reports available for action.
```
