# INTRODUCTION OF BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

**Measures:** S. 1642, S. 1643, S. 1644

```
              INTRODUCTION OF BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

  The following bills and joint resolutions were introduced, read the 
first and second time by unanimous consent, and referred as indicated:

           By Mr. COCHRAN (for himself and Mr. Dodd):
       S. 1642. A bill to amend part F of title X of the 
     Elementary and Secondary Education Act of 1965 to improve and 
     refocus civic education, and for other purposes; to the 
     Committee on Health, Education, Labor, and Pensions.
           By Mr. GRASSLEY (for himself and Mr. Harkin):
       S. 1643. A bill to authorize the addition of certain 
     parcels to the Effigy Mounds National Monument, Iowa; to the 
     Committee on Energy and Natural Resources.
           By Mr. ABRAHAM (for himself, Mr. Schumer, and Mrs. 
             Feinstein):
       S. 1644. A bill to provide additional measures for the 
     prevention and punishment of alien smuggling, and for other 
     purposes; to the Committee on the Judiciary.
```
