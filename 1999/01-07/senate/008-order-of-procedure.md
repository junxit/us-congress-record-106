# ORDER OF PROCEDURE

```
                           ORDER OF PROCEDURE

  Mr. LOTT. Mr. President, I am about to suggest the absence of a 
quorum. For the information of all Senators, this will be a live 
quorum, and we will under the previous order meet at 1 p.m. to proceed

with the swearing in of the Chief Justice of the United States and all 
Senators.
```
