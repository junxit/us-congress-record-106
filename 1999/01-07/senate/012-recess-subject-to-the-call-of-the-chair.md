# RECESS SUBJECT TO THE CALL OF THE CHAIR

```
                RECESS SUBJECT TO THE CALL OF THE CHAIR

  Mr. CRAIG. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate go 
into recess subject to call of the Chair.
  There being no objection, the Senate, at 2:05 p.m., recessed subject 
to the call of the Chair; whereupon, the Senate, at 8:08 p.m., 
reassembled when called to order by the Presiding Officer (Mr. Crapo).
```
