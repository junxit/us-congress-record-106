# ENROLLED BILL AND JOINT RESOLUTION SIGNED

               ENROLLED BILL AND JOINT RESOLUTION SIGNED

resolution of the House of the following titles, which were thereupon 
signed by the Speaker:

     Baumel, a United States citizen, and other Israeli soldiers 
     missing in action.
