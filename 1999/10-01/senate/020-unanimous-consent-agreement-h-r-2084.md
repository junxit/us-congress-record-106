# UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--H.R. 2084

```
                 UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--H.R. 2084

  Mr. LOTT. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that at 5:30 p.m. on 
Monday, the Senate proceed to the Transportation appropriations 
conference report, the conference report be deemed to have been read, 
and statements by Senators Shelby and Lautenberg be placed in the 
Record and a vote occur immediately on adoption of the conference 
report at 5:30 p.m. on Monday.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. LOTT. I ask unanimous consent that after Senators Akaka and 
Murkowski speak--Senator Akaka is going to speak next and then Senator 
Murkowski--Senator Leahy be recognized to speak as in morning business.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. LOTT. I yield the floor.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Senator from Hawaii.

                          ____________________
```
