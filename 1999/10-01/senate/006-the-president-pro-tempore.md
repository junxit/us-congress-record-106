# THE PRESIDENT PRO TEMPORE

**Speaking:** Specter, Arlen (S000709) [R-PA]

```
                       THE PRESIDENT PRO TEMPORE

  Mr. SPECTER. Mr. President, let me comment at the outset what a great 
pleasure it is to see you opening the Senate again this morning, 
looking hale and hardy. We keep moving the time earlier and earlier; 
but no matter how early it is, you are always here first.
  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. I thank the Senator very much.
```
