# CELEBRATING THE BIRTH OF JORDYN MACKENZIE MOUDY

```
            CELEBRATING THE BIRTH OF JORDYN MACKENZIE MOUDY

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. RONNIE SHOWS

                             of mississippi

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 1, 1999

  Mr. SHOWS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to announce the grand arrival of 
Jordyn Mackenzie Moudy. She's a new little Democrat of the 4th 
congressional district in Mississippi.
  The proud parents are Jerry and Kristi Moudy from Terry, Mississippi. 
Grandparents include Joe and Annette Gallaspy from Clinton, 
Mississippi. Annette happens to be a member of my staff in my Jackson 
office.
  Granny Annette reports that Jordyn arrived on September 29, 1999, at 
5:30 p.m., weighing in at 7 pounds, 7 ounces and 19 inches long, and 
sporting lots of black hair. Mother and daughter are doing fine but 
Annette can barely contain herself and I do not know when she will 
return to earth.
  I send a hearty ``welcome'' to Miss Jordyn, and my best wishes go out 
to the Moudy and Gallaspy families.

                          ____________________
```
