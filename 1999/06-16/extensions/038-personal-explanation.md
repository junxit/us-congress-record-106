**Speaking:** Shows, Ronnie (S001147) [D-MS]

**Measures:** H.R. 1400

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. RONNIE SHOWS

                             of mississippi

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, June 16, 1999

  Mr. SHOWS. Mr. Speaker, because inclement weather delayed my 
connecting flight from Jackson, Mississippi, on Monday, June 14, 1999, 
I was unable to cast a recorded vote on rollcall No. 204.
```
