                           HON. RONNIE SHOWS

connecting flight from Jackson, Mississippi, on Monday, June 14, 1999, 
I was unable to cast a recorded vote on rollcall No. 204.

and pass H.R. 1400, the Bond Price Competition Improvement Act of 1999.
