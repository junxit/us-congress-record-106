# APPOINTMENT AS MEMBER OF COMMISSION ON INTERNATIONAL RELIGIOUS FREEDOM

```
 APPOINTMENT AS MEMBER OF COMMISSION ON INTERNATIONAL RELIGIOUS FREEDOM

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Without objection, and pursuant to section 
201(b) of the International Religious Freedom Act of 1998 (22 U.S.C. 
6431) and upon the recommendation of the minority leader, the Chair 
announces the Speaker's appointment of the following member to a 2-year 
term on the Commission on International Religious Freedom on the part 
of the House:
  Rabbi David Saperstein, Washington, DC.
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
