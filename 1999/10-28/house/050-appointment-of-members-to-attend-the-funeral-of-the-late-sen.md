# APPOINTMENT OF MEMBERS TO ATTEND THE FUNERAL OF THE LATE SENATOR JOHN H. CHAFEE

**Measures:** H.Res. 341

```
 APPOINTMENT OF MEMBERS TO ATTEND THE FUNERAL OF THE LATE SENATOR JOHN 
                               H. CHAFEE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to the provisions of House 
Resolution 341, the Chair announces the Speaker's appointment of the 
following Members of the House to the committee to attend the funeral 
of the late John H. Chafee:
  Mr. Kennedy of Rhode Island;
  Mr. Weygand of Rhode Island;
  Mr. Shuster of Pennsylvania;
  Mr. Waxman of California; and
  Mr. Petri of Wisconsin.
```
