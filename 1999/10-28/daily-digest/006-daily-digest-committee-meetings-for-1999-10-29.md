# Daily Digest/COMMITTEE MEETINGS FOR 1999-10-29

**Measures:** H.R. 887, H.R. 1089

```
                     COMMITTEE MEETINGS FOR FRIDAY,

                            OCTOBER 29, 1999

        (Committee meetings are open unless otherwise indicated)


                                 Senate

  Committee on Foreign Relations: to hold hearings on the nomination 
of Joseph R. Crapa, of Virginia, to be an Assistant Administrator of 
the United States Agency for International Development; Willene A. 
Johnson, of New York, to be United States Director of the African 
Development Bank; and Alan Phillip Larson, of Iowa, to be Under 
Secretary of State (Economic, Business and Agricultural Affairs), 10 
a.m., SD-419.


                                 House

  Committee on Commerce, Subcommittee on Finance and Hazardous 
Materials, hearing on Increasing Disclosure to Benefit Investors, 
focusing on the following bills: H.R. 887, to amend the Securities 
and Exchange Act of 1934 to require improved disclosure of corporate 
charitable contributions; and H.R. 1089, Mutual Fund Tax Awareness 
Act of 1999, 10 a.m., 2123 Rayburn.
  Committee on Government Reform, Subcommittee on Government 
Management, Information, and Technology, and the Subcommittee on 
Technology of the Committee on Science, joint hearing on Y2K 
Contingency and Day 1 Plans: If Computers Fail, What Will You Do? 10 
a.m., 2154 Rayburn.
  Committee on Resources, Subcommittee on Energy and Mineral 
Resources, oversight hearing on Limitations on Patenting Millsites 
under the Mining Law of 1872 and the Approval of Mining Plans of 
Operations Pursuant to the Federal Land Policy and Management Act, 
10 a.m., 1324 Longworth.
```
