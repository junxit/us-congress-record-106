# COMMENDING SENATOR BENNETT

```
                       COMMENDING SENATOR BENNETT

  Mr. GRAHAM. Mr. President, I have a statement to make on legislation 
which I will introduce this evening. But prior to that, I express my 
gratitude for the eloquence of the remarks the Senator from Utah has 
just delivered on behalf of our friend, John Chafee1. Many of us have 
attempted to reach into our souls and express the depth of our 
affection for this special man. The Senator from Utah has succeeded in 
that effort. I commend him and thank him for sharing those emotions.
  Mr. President, I have a second item before I turn to my remarks for
```
