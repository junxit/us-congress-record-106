# SCHEDULE

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. CRAPO. Mr. President, the Senate will be in a period of morning 
business until 10:30 a.m. Following morning business, the Senate will 
resume consideration of the pending Ashcroft amendment to H.R. 434, the 
African trade bill. As a reminder, there will be a cloture vote on the 
substitute amendment 1 hour after convening tomorrow. It is still hoped 
that an agreement can be reached to allow the Senate to complete action 
on this trade bill by the end of the week. The Senate may also consider 
any legislative or executive items cleared for action.
  I thank my colleagues for their attention.

                          ____________________
```
