# ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, OCTOBER 29, 1999

```
                  ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, OCTOBER 29, 1999

  Mr. BENNETT. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until the hour of 9:30 
a.m. on Friday, October 29. I further ask consent that on Friday, 
immediately following the prayer, the Journal of proceedings be 
approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, the time for the 
two leaders be reserved for their use later in the day, and the Senate 
then begin 30 minutes of debate equally divided between the two leaders 
on H.R. 434, the African trade bill. I further ask consent that the 
cloture vote occur at 10 a.m. on Friday.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
