# ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

```
                ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Debate has concluded on the first three 
motions to suspend the rules.
  Pursuant to clause 8 of rule XX, the Chair will now put the question 
on each motion to suspend the rules on which further proceedings were

entertained.

  H. Con. Res. 84, as amended, by the yeas and nays;
  H. Con. Res. 88, by the yeas and nays; and
  House Resolution 157, by the yeas and nays.
  The Chair will reduce to 5 minutes the time for the third electronic 
vote in this series.
```
