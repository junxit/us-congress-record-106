# PROGRAM

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. GRAMM. For the information of all Senators, the Senate will 
convene on Wednesday at 9:30 a.m. and will immediately proceed to a 
rollcall vote on adoption of S. Res. 94. Following the vote, the Senate 
will be in a period of morning business until 11 a.m. At 11 a.m., the 
Senate will resume consideration of Senator Sarbanes' substitute 
amendment to S. 900, the Financial Services Modernization Act, with a 
vote on the Gramm motion to table occurring at approximately 12 noon. 
Additional amendments are expected, and therefore Senators can expect 
votes throughout Wednesday's session of the Senate.

                          ____________________
```
