# APPOINTMENT AS MEMBER TO COMMISSION ON CIVIL RIGHTS

```
          APPOINTMENT AS MEMBER TO COMMISSION ON CIVIL RIGHTS

  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Shimkus). Without objection, and 
pursuant to section 2(b) of Public Law 98-183, and upon the 
recommendation of the minority leader, the Chair announces the 
Speaker's appointment of the following member to the Commission on 
Civil Rights on the part of the House, effective May 4, 1999, to fill 
the existing vacancy thereon:
  Mr. Christopher F. Edley, Jr., Cambridge, Massachusetts.
  There was no objection.
```
