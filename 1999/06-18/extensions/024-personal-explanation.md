# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Kolbe, Jim (K000306) [R-AZ]

**Measures:** H.R. 1501

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                             HON. JIM KOLBE

                               of arizona

                    in the house of representatives

                         Friday, June 18, 1999

  Mr. KOLBE. Mr. Speaker, on June 17, 1999 the House debated the 
Consequences for Juvenile Offenders Act (H.R. 1501). Following the 
vote, I was dismayed to see that I was listed as not voting on rollcall 
vote No. 223. I was on the floor and am positive I put my card in the 
voting device. Had my vote been recorded, it would have been ``nay.''
```
