# TITLE AMENDMENT TO H.R. 1501, CONSEQUENCES FOR JUVENILE OFFENDERS ACT OF 1999

**Measures:** H.R. 1501

```
 TITLE AMENDMENT TO H.R. 1501, CONSEQUENCES FOR JUVENILE OFFENDERS ACT 
                                OF 1999

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Without objection, the amendment to the 
title of H.R. 1501 proposed in amendment No. 36 in Part A of House 
Report 106-186 is adopted.
  There was no objection.
  The text of the amendment to the title is as follows:

       A bill to amend the Omnibus Crime Control and Safe Streets 
     Act of 1968 to provide grants to ensure increased 
     accountability for juvenile offenders; to amend the Juvenile 
     Justice and Delinquency Prevention Act of 1974 to provide 
     quality prevention programs and accountability programs 
     relating to juvenile delinquency; and for other purposes.

                          ____________________
```
