**Speaking:** Bryant, Ed (B000996) [R-TN]

**Measures:** H.R. 1501

```
AUTHORIZING THE CLERK TO MAKE CORRECTIONS IN ENGROSSMENT OF H.R. 1501, 
            CONSEQUENCES FOR JUVENILE OFFENDERS ACT OF 1999

  Mr. BRYANT. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that in the
```
