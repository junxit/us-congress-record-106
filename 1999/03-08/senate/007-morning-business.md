# MORNING BUSINESS

**Speaking:** Grams, Rod (G000367) [R-MN]

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, there will now be a 
period for the transaction of morning business not to extend beyond the 
hour of 2 p.m. The time between 12 noon and 12:30 p.m. shall be under 
the control of the Senator from Minnesota, Mr. Grams, or his designee.
  The Senator from Minnesota is recognized.
  Mr. GRAMS. I thank the Chair. I also expect to be joined in a few 
minutes by Senator Tim Hutchinson of Arkansas and also Senator Robert 
Smith of New Hampshire, and I will yield time to them as they come to 
the floor this morning.

                          ____________________
```
