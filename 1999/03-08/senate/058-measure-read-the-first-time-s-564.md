**Measures:** S. 564

```
                  MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--S. 564

  Mr. FEINGOLD. Mr. President, I understand that Senate bill 564, 
introduced earlier today by Senators Murray, Kennedy and Daschle is at 
the desk, and I ask for its first reading.

time.

       A bill (S. 564) to reduce class size, and for other
```
