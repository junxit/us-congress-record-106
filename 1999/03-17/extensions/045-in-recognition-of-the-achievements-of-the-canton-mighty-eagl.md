# IN RECOGNITION OF THE ACHIEVEMENTS OF THE CANTON MIGHTY EAGLE HIGH SCHOOL BAND

**Speaking:** Hall, Ralph M. (H000067) [D-TX]

```
  IN RECOGNITION OF THE ACHIEVEMENTS OF THE CANTON MIGHTY EAGLE HIGH 
                              SCHOOL BAND

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. RALPH M. HALL

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                       Wednesday, March 17, 1999

  Mr. HALL of Texas. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to acknowledge the hard 
work, discipline and deserved achievements of the Canton High School 
Band--from my Fourth District in Texas. Besides numerous awards and 
recognitions, the Mighty Eagle Band has been chosen to represent the 
State of Texas, today, St. Patrick's Day, by performing in Dublin, 
Ireland--in that city's St. Patrick's Day Parade.
  The young men and women of this band have participated in and won a 
multitude of competitions every year since 1993. More recently, the 
Canton Band was named the third overall band in the State of Texas. 
Along with this honor, came an invitation to perform in Dublin, 
Ireland, on St. Patrick's Day. As if the many hours of sacrifice and 
discipline exhibited by these young men and women--was not enough--they 
managed to raise an amazing $200,000 in order to pay for their trip.
  Mr. Speaker, as evidenced by their many achievements and awards, the 
Canton ISD music program emphasizes responsibility, accountability and 
service to others. Obviously, these youngsters have internalized these 
characteristics in their search for success. As we adjourn today, let 
us do so in honor of the Canton Mighty Eagle Band and their numerous 
merited accolades.

                          ____________________
```
