# ENROLLED BILL SIGNED

**Measures:** S. 494, H.R. 540

```
                          ENROLLED BILL SIGNED

  Mr. THOMAS, from the Committee on House Administration, reported that 
that committee had examined and found truly enrolled a bill of the 
House of the following title, which was thereupon signed by the 
Speaker:

       H.R. 540 (S. 494).--To amend title XIX of the Social 
     Security Act to prohibit transfers or discharges of residents 
     of nursing facilities as a result of a voluntary withdrawal 
     from participation in the Medicaid Program.
```
