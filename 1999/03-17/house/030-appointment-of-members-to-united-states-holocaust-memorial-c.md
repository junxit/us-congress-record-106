# APPOINTMENT OF MEMBERS TO UNITED STATES HOLOCAUST MEMORIAL COUNCIL

```
   APPOINTMENT OF MEMBERS TO UNITED STATES HOLOCAUST MEMORIAL COUNCIL

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Without objection, and pursuant to the 
provisions of Public Law 96-388, as amended by Public Law 97-84 (36 
U.S.C. 1402(a)), the Chair announces the Speaker's appointment of the 
following Members of the House to the United States Holocaust Memorial 
Council:
  Mr. Gilman of New York;
  Mr. LaTourette of Ohio; and
  Mr. Cannon of Utah.
  There was no objection.
```
