# THE BIRTH OF VERONICA KAY VOINOVICH

```
                  THE BIRTH OF VERONICA KAY VOINOVICH

  Mr. VOINOVICH. Mr. President, I want to bring to the Senate's 
attention the fact that we welcomed a new citizen into Ohio last night 
at 11:57, and that new citizen is my second grandchild, Veronica Kay 
Voinovich. Veronica is our second grandchild. Her grandmother and I 
welcome her and so do her other grandparents, Warren and Alice Fish. I 
apologize for not being in Cleveland last night for her birth, but it 
was necessary for me to be here to do the work of the Senate and to 
represent the people of Ohio and, hopefully, through those votes do 
something for her and the rest of the citizens of our great State.

                          ____________________
```
