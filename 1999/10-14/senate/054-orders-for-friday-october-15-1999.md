# ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, OCTOBER 15, 1999

**Speaking:** Santorum, Rick (S000059) [R-PA]

```
                  ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, OCTOBER 15, 1999

  Mr. SANTORUM. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until the hour of 9:15 
a.m. on Friday, October 15. I further ask unanimous consent that on 
Friday, immediately following the prayer, the Journal of proceedings be 
approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, the time for the 
two leaders be reserved for their use later in the day, and the Senate 
then begin the vote on the conference report to accompany the VA-HUD 
appropriations bill.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
