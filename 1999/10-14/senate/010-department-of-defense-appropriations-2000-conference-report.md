report accompanying H.R. 2561, which the clerk will report.

       Conference report accompanying H.R. 2561, making 
     appropriations for the Department of Defense for the fiscal 
     year ending September 30, 2000, and for other purposes.

  Mr. DOMENICI. Mr. President, I ask for the yeas and nays.

report. The yeas and nays have been ordered.

Kennedy) and the Senator from Massachusetts (Mr. Kerry) are necessarily 
absent.

Massachusetts (Mr. Kennedy) would vote ``aye.''
