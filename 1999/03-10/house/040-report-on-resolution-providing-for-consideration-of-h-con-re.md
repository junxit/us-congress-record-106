# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H. CON. RES. 42, PEACEKEEPING OPERATIONS IN KOSOVO RESOLUTION

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 42, H.Res. 103

```
 REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H. CON. RES. 42, 
              PEACEKEEPING OPERATIONS IN KOSOVO RESOLUTION

  Mr. DIAZ-BALART, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 106-48) on the resolution (H. Res. 103) providing for 
consideration of the concurrent resolution (H. Con. Res. 42) regarding 
the use of United States Armed Forces as part of a NATO peacekeeping 
operation implementing a Kosovo peace agreement, which was referred to 
the House Calendar and ordered to be printed.
```
