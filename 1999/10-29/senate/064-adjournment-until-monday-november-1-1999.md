# ADJOURNMENT UNTIL MONDAY, NOVEMBER 1, 1999

**Speaking:** Hutchison, Kay Bailey (H001016) [R-TX]

```
               ADJOURNMENT UNTIL MONDAY, NOVEMBER 1, 1999

  Mrs. HUTCHISON. Mr. President, if there is no further business to 
come before the Senate, I now ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
stand in adjournment under the previous order in further memory of our 
dear colleague, Senator John Chafee.
  There being no objection, the Senate, at 4:56 p.m., adjourned until 
Monday, November 1, 1999, at 12 noon.
```
