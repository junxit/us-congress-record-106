# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Myrick, Sue Wilkins (M001134) [R-NC]

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 194, H.R. 754, H.R. 2303

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. SUE WILKINS MYRICK

                           of north carolina

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 28, 1999

  Mrs. MYRICK. Mr. Speaker, I missed 4 recorded votes while I was 
working in my district. If I had been present, I would have voted as 
follows: Rollcall vote 536, on the motion to suspend the rules and pass 
H. Con. Res. 194 to recognize the contributions of 4-H Clubs and their 
members to voluntary community service, I would have voted ``yes.''
  Rollcall vote 535, on the motion to suspend the rules and pass H.R. 
2303, The History of the House Awareness and Preservation Act, I would 
have voted ``yes.''
  Rollcall vote 534, on the motion to suspend the rules and pass H.R. 
754, the Made in America Information Act, I would have voted ``yes.''
  Rollcall vote 533, on approving the Journal, I would have voted 
``yes.''
```
