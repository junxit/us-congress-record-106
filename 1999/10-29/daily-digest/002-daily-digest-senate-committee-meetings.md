# Daily Digest/Senate Committee Meetings

```
Committee Meetings
(Committees not listed did not meet)
NOMINATIONS
Committee on Foreign Relations: Committee concluded hearings on the 
nominations of Joseph R. Crapa, of Virginia, to be Assistant 
Administrator for Legislative and Public Affairs, United States Agency 
for International Development, Willene A. Johnson, of New York, to be 
United States Director of the African Development Bank, and Alan 
Phillip Larson, of Iowa, to be Under Secretary of State for Economic, 
Business and Agricultural Affairs, after the nominees testified and 
answered questions in their own behalf. Mr. Crapa was introduced by 
Senators Feingold and Kohl, and Representative Obey.
```
