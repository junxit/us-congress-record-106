                              THE CALENDAR

  Mr. CRAIG. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
now proceed to the consideration, en bloc, of the following bills 
reported by the Environment and Public Works Committee: Calendar No. 
53, S. 67; Calendar No. 56, S. 437; Calendar No. 57, S. 453; Calendar 
No. 58, S. 460; Calendar No. 59, H.R. 92; Calendar No. 60, H.R. 158; 
Calendar No. 61, H.R. 233; and Calendar No. 62, H.R. 396.
  I further ask unanimous consent that the bills be considered read a 
third time and passed, the motions to reconsider be laid upon the 
table, and that any statements relating to any of these bills be 
printed at the appropriate place in the Record, with the above 
occurring en bloc.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
