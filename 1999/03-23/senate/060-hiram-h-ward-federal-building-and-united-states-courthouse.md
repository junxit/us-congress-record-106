# HIRAM H. WARD FEDERAL BUILDING AND UNITED STATES COURTHOUSE

**Measures:** H.R. 92

```
      HIRAM H. WARD FEDERAL BUILDING AND UNITED STATES COURTHOUSE

  The bill (H.R. 92) to designate the Federal building and United 
States courthouse located at 251 North Main street in Winston-Salem, 
North Carolina, as the ``Hiram H. Ward Federal Building and United 
States Courthouse,'' was considered, ordered to a third reading, read 
the third time, and passed.
```
