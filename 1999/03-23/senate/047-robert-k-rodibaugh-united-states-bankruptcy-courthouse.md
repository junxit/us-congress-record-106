# ROBERT K. RODIBAUGH UNITED STATES BANKRUPTCY COURTHOUSE

```
        ROBERT K. RODIBAUGH UNITED STATES BANKRUPTCY COURTHOUSE

  The bill (S. 460) to designate the United States courthouse located 
at 401 South Michigan Street in South Bend, Indiana, as the ``Robert K. 
Rodibaugh United States Bankruptcy Courthouse,'' was considered, 
ordered to be engrossed for a third time, and passed; as follows:

                                 S. 460

       Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of 
     the United States of America in Congress assembled,

     SECTION 1. DESIGNATION OF ROBERT K. RODIBAUGH UNITED STATES 
                   BANKRUPTCY COURTHOUSE.

       The United States courthouse located at 401 South Michigan 
     Street in South Bend, Indiana, shall be known and designated 
     as the ``Robert K. Rodibaugh United States Bankruptcy 
     Courthouse''.

     SEC. 2. REFERENCES.

       Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, 
     or other record of the United States to the United States 
     courthouse referred to in section 1 shall be deemed to be a 
     reference to the ``Robert K. Rodibaugh United States 
     Bankruptcy Courthouse''.

                          ____________________
```
