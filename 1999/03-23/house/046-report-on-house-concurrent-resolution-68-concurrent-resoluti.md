# REPORT ON HOUSE CONCURRENT RESOLUTION 68, CONCURRENT RESOLUTION ON THE BUDGET--FISCAL YEAR 2000

```
REPORT ON HOUSE CONCURRENT RESOLUTION 68, CONCURRENT RESOLUTION ON THE 
                        BUDGET--FISCAL YEAR 2000

  Mr. SHAYS (during consideration of House Concurrent Resolution 37) 
from the Committee on the Budget, submitted a privileged report (Rept. 
No. 106-73) on the concurrent resolution (H. Con. Res. 68) establishing 
the congressional budget for the United States Government for fiscal 
year 2000 and setting forth appropriate budgetary levels for each of 
fiscal years 2001 through 2009, which was referred to the Union 
Calendar and ordered to be printed.
```
