# ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

```
                ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. LaHood). Debate has concluded on all 
motions to suspend the rules.
  Pursuant to clause 8 of rule XX, the Chair will now put the question 
on each motion to suspend the rules on which further proceedings were 
postponed earlier today in the order in which that motion was 
entertained.
  Votes will be taken in the following order:
  H.R. 70, by the yeas and nays;
  H. Con. Res. 56, by the yeas and nays;
  H. Con. Res. 37, by the yeas and nays.
  The Chair will reduce to 5 minutes the time for each of these three 
votes.
```
