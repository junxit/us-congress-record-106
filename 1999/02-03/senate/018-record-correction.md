# RECORD CORRECTION

```
                           RECORD CORRECTION

  Mr. REID. On rollcall vote No. 8, the Senator from Maryland, Ms. 
Mikulski, was necessarily absent because of illness. In the 
Congressional Record of January 28, her vote was erroneously announced 
as ``aye.'' Her vote on rollcall vote No. 8 should have been announced 
as ``no.'' I ask unanimous consent that the Record be changed to 
reflect this correction.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
