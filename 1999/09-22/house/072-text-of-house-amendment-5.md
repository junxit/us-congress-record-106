# Text of House Amendment 5

**Speaking:** Davis, Danny K. (D000096) [D-IL]

**Measures:** H.R. 2506

```
H.R. 2506

                   Offered by: Mr. Davis of Illinois

       Amendment No. 5: Page 7, after line 14, insert the 
     following subsection:
       ``(g) Annual Report.--Beginning with fiscal year 2003, the 
     Director shall annually submit to the Congress a report 
     regarding prevailing disparities in health care delivery as 
     it relates to racial factors, socioeconomic factors, and 
     disease prevalence in priority populations.
```
