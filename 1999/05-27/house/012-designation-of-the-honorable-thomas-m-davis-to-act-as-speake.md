# DESIGNATION OF THE HONORABLE THOMAS M. DAVIS TO ACT AS SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE TO SIGN ENROLLED BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS THROUGH JUNE 7, 1999

```
  DESIGNATION OF THE HONORABLE THOMAS M. DAVIS TO ACT AS SPEAKER PRO 
 TEMPORE TO SIGN ENROLLED BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS THROUGH JUNE 7, 
                                  1999

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following 
communication from the Speaker:


                                               Washington, DC,

                                                     May 27, 1999.
       I hereby appoint the Honorable Thomas M. Davis to act as 
     Speaker pro tempore to sign enrolled bills and joint 
     resolutions through June 7, 1999.
                                                J. Dennis Hastert,
                          Speaker of the House of Representatives.

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Without objection, the designation is agreed 
to.
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
