# ADJOURNMENT

**Speaking:** Goss, Porter J. (G000336) [R-FL]

**Measures:** S. 35

```
                              ADJOURNMENT

  Mr. GOSS. Mr. Speaker, as the designee of the majority leader, I move 
that the House do now adjourn.
  The motion was agreed to.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to the provisions of Senate 
Concurrent Resolution 35, 106th Congress, the House stands adjourned 
until 12:30 p.m. on Monday, June 7, 1999, for morning hour debates.
  Thereupon (at 12 o'clock and 27 minutes p.m.), pursuant to Senate 
Concurrent Resolution 35, the House adjourned until Monday, June 7, 
1999, at 12:30 p.m., for morning hour debates.

                          ____________________
```
