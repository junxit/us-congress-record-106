# COSPONSORSHIP OF THE MOTOR VEHICLE RENTAL FAIRNESS ACT

**Speaking:** McCain, John (M000303) [R-AZ]

**Measures:** S. 1125, S. 1130

```
         COSPONSORSHIP OF THE MOTOR VEHICLE RENTAL FAIRNESS ACT

  Mr. McCAIN. Mr. President, yesterday, I introduced two bills, S. 
1130, the Motor Vehicle Rental Fairness Act and S. 1125, the 
Telecommunications Merger Review Act of 1999. Later in the day, I asked 
that Senator Connie Mack be

[[Page S6277]]

added as an original cosponsor to the Motor Vehicle Rental Fairness 
Act. Despite the fact that my request specifically stated ``the Motor 
Vehicle Rental Fairness Act'', the Bill Clerk's office inadvertently 
added Senator Mack as a cosponsor to the Telecommunications Merger 
Review Act. It is my understanding that this error has been corrected. 
I want the record to reflect that Senator Mack was an original 
Cosponsor of the Motor Vehicle Rental Fairness Act.

                          ____________________
```
