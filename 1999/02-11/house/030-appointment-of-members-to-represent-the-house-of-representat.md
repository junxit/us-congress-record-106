# APPOINTMENT OF MEMBERS TO REPRESENT THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES AT CEREMONIES FOR THE OBSERVANCE OF GEORGE WASHINGTON'S BIRTHDAY

```
  APPOINTMENT OF MEMBERS TO REPRESENT THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES AT 
     CEREMONIES FOR THE OBSERVANCE OF GEORGE WASHINGTON'S BIRTHDAY

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to the order of the House of today, 
the Chair announces the Speaker's appointment of the following Members 
to represent the House of Representatives at appropriate ceremonies for 
the observance of George Washington's birthday to be held on Monday, 
February 22, 1999:
  Mr. Wolf of Virginia and,
  Mr. Moran of Virginia.
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
