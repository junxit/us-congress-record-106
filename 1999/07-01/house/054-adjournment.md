# ADJOURNMENT

**Speaking:** Schaffer, Bob (S000112) [R-CO]

**Measures:** S. 43

```
                              ADJOURNMENT

  Mr. SCHAFFER. Mr. Speaker, pursuant to Senate Concurrent Resolution 
43, I move that the House do now adjourn.
  The motion was agreed to.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to the provisions of Senate 
Concurrent Resolution 43, 106th Congress, the House stands adjourned 
until 12:30 p.m. on Monday, July 12, 1999, for morning-hour debates.
  Thereupon (at 12 o'clock midnight), pursuant to Senate Concurrent 
Resolution 43, the House adjourned until Monday, July 12, 1999, at 
12:30 p.m. for morning-hour debates.

                          ____________________
```
