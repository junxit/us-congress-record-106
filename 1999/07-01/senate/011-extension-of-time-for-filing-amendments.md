# EXTENSION OF TIME FOR FILING AMENDMENTS

**Speaking:** Specter, Arlen (S000709) [R-PA]

```

  Mr. SPECTER. Mr. President, I have been asked to request on behalf of 
the leader that the deadline for failing first-degree amendments on the 
Treasury-Postal appropriations bill be extended until noon.

  Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
