                   EXECUTIVE AND OTHER COMMUNICATIONS

  The following communications were laid before the Senate, together 
with accompanying papers, reports, and documents, which were referred 
as indicated:

       EC-4035. A communication from the Assistant Secretary, 
     Legislative Affairs, Department of State, transmitting,

     Committee on Foreign Relations.

     Finance Board, transmitting, pursuant to law, the management 
     reports of the twelve Federal Home Loan Banks and the

     Committee on Governmental Affairs.
       EC-4039. A communication from the Secretary of Energy,

       EC-4045. A communication from the Secretary of Veterans

       EC-4046. A communication from the Assistant Secretary,

       EC-4048. A communication from the Secretary of the
