# ORDERS FOR MONDAY, JULY 12, 1999

**Speaking:** Gorton, Slade (G000333) [R-WA]

```
                    ORDERS FOR MONDAY, JULY 12, 1999

  Mr. GORTON. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business, it stand in adjournment until 12 noon on 
Monday, July 12. I further ask that on Monday, following the prayer, 
the Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be 
deemed to have expired, the time for the two leaders be reserved for 
their use later in the day, and the Senate then proceed to a period of 
morning business until 1 p.m.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
