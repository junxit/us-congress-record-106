# PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE

```
                          PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE

  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. Senator Hutchison is designated to lead 
the Senate in the Pledge of Allegiance.
  The Honorable Kay Bailey Hutchison, a Senator from the State of 
Texas, led the Pledge of Allegiance, as follows:

       I pledge allegiance to the Flag of the United States of 
     America, and to the Republic for which it stands, one nation 
     under God, indivisible, with liberty and justice for all.

                          ____________________
```
