# NOTICE OF HEARING

**Speaking:** Murkowski, Frank H. (M001085) [R-AK]

```
                           NOTICE OF HEARING


               committee on energy and natural resources

  Mr. MURKOWSKI. Mr. President, I would like to announce for the 
information of the Senate and the public that a joint oversight hearing 
has been scheduled before the Committee on Energy and Natural Resources 
and the Committee on Indian Affairs.
  The hearing will take place on Wednesday, July 14, beginning at 9:30 
a.m. in Room SH-216 of the Hart Senate Office Building.
  The purpose of this hearing is to receive testimony on the Report of 
the General Accounting Office (GAO) on the Interior Department's 
Planned Trust Fund Reform.

[[Page S8159]]

  For further information, please contact the Committee on Indian 
Affairs at 202-224-2251.

                          ____________________
```
