# ORDER FOR REVISION OF STANDING RULES OF THE SENATE AND PRINTING OF A SENATE DOCUMENT

```
 ORDER FOR REVISION OF STANDING RULES OF THE SENATE AND PRINTING OF A 
                            SENATE DOCUMENT

  Ms. COLLINS. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the 
Committee on Rules and Administration be directed to prepare a revised 
edition of the Standing Rules of the Senate and that such Standing 
Rules be printed as a Senate document. I further ask unanimous consent 
that beyond the usual number, 2,500 additional copies of this document 
be printed for the use of the Committee on Rules and Administration.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
