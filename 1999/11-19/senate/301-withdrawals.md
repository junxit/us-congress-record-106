# WITHDRAWALS

```
                              WITHDRAWALS

  Executive messages transmitted by the President to the Senate on 
November 19, 1999, withdrawing from further Senate consideration the 
following nominations:


                    united states parole commission

       timothy earl jones, jr., of georgia, to be a commissioner 
     of the united states parole commission for a term of six

     to the senate on july 19, 1999.
       marie f. ragghianti, of maryland, to be a commissioner of 
     the united states parole commission for a term of six years, 
     vice edward f. reilly, term expired, which was sent to the 
     senate on july 19, 1999.
```
