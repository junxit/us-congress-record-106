# WILDERNESS BATTLEFIELD LAND ACQUISITION

```
                WILDERNESS BATTLEFIELD LAND ACQUISITION

  The bill (H.R. 1665) to allow the National Park Service to acquire 
certain land for addition to the Wilderness Battlefield in Virginia, as 
previously authorized by law, by purchase or exchange as well as by 
donation, was considered, ordered to a third reading, read the third 
time, and passed.
```
