# UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT

```
                      UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT

  Ms. COLLINS. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senator 
from Iowa, Mr. Grassley, be recognized for approximately 10 minutes, if 
that is sufficient for the Senator.
  Mr. GRASSLEY. I think it is.
  Ms. COLLINS. I also ask unanimous consent that he be followed by the 
Senator from New York, Mr. Schumer, for not to exceed 5 minutes, and 
that I be recognized to transact legislative business.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The Senator from Iowa is recognized.

                          ____________________
```
