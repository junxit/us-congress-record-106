# UNANIMOUS-CONSENT AGREEMENT--EXECUTIVE CALENDAR

```
            UNANIMOUS-CONSENT AGREEMENT--EXECUTIVE CALENDAR

  Mr. LOTT. Mr. President, as in executive session, I ask unanimous 
consent that all nominations received by the Senate during the 106th 
Congress, first session, remain in status quo, notwithstanding the 
November 19, 1999 adjournment of the Senate, and the provisions of rule 
XXXI, paragraph 6 of the standing rules of the Senate.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
