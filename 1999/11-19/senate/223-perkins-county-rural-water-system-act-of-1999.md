# PERKINS COUNTY RURAL WATER SYSTEM ACT OF 1999

**Measures:** H.R. 970

```
             PERKINS COUNTY RURAL WATER SYSTEM ACT OF 1999

  The bill (H.R. 970) to authorize the Secretary of the Interior to 
provide assistance to the Perkins County Rural Water System, Inc., for 
the construction of water supply facilities in Perkins County, South 
Dakota, was considered, ordered to a third reading, read the third 
time, and passed.
```
