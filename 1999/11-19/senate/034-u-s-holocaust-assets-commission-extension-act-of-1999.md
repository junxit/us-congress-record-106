# U.S. HOLOCAUST ASSETS COMMISSION EXTENSION ACT OF 1999

```
         U.S. HOLOCAUST ASSETS COMMISSION EXTENSION ACT OF 1999

  Ms. COLLINS. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent the Banking 
Committee be discharged from further consideration of H.R. 2401, and 
the Senate proceed to its immediate consideration.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered. The clerk 
will report the bill by title.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (H.R. 2401) to amend the U.S. Holocaust Assets 
     Commission Act of 1998 to extend the period by which the 
     final report is due and to authorize additional funding.

  There being no objection, the Senate proceeded to consider the bill.
  Ms. COLLINS. I ask unanimous consent the bill be read the third time 
and passed, the motion to reconsider be laid upon the table, and any 
statements relating to the bill be printed in the Record.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The bill (H.R. 2401) was read the third time and passed.
```
