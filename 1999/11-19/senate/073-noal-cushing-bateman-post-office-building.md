# NOAL CUSHING BATEMAN POST OFFICE BUILDING

```
               NOAL CUSHING BATEMAN POST OFFICE BUILDING

  The bill (H.R. 1251) to designate the United States Postal Service 
building located at 8850 South 700 East, Sandy, Utah, as the ``Noal 
Cushing Bateman Post Office Building,'' was considered, ordered to a 
third reading, read the third time, and passed.
```
