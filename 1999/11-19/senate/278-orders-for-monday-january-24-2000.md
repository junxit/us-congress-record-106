# ORDERS FOR MONDAY, JANUARY 24, 2000

```
                  ORDERS FOR MONDAY, JANUARY 24, 2000

  Ms. COLLINS. Mr. President, I believe we have now completed our 
business today. When the Senate completes its business today, it will 
stand in adjournment under the provisions of H. Con. Res. 235 until the 
hour of 12 noon on Monday, January 24, 2000, for the opening of the 
second session of the 106th Congress.
  I ask unanimous consent that following the quorum call and the 
routine housekeeping matters, the Journal of the proceedings be 
approved to date, the morning hour be deemed to have expired, the time 
for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the day, and the 
Senate then begin a period for the transaction of routine morning 
business until 2 p.m., with Members permitted to speak for up to 10 
minutes each.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
