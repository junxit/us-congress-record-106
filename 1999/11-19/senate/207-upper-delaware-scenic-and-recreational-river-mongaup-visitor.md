# UPPER DELAWARE SCENIC AND RECREATIONAL RIVER MONGAUP VISITOR CENTER ACT OF 1999

```
UPPER DELAWARE SCENIC AND RECREATIONAL RIVER MONGAUP VISITOR CENTER ACT 
                                OF 1999

  The bill (H.R. 20) to authorize the Secretary of the Interior to 
construct and operate a visitor center for the upper Delaware Scenic 
and Recreational River on land owned by the State of New York, which 
had been reported from the Committee on Energy and Natural Resources, 
was considered, ordered to a third reading, read the third time, and 
passed.
```
