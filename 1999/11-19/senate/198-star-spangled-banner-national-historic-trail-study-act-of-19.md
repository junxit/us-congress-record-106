# STAR-SPANGLED BANNER NATIONAL HISTORIC TRAIL STUDY ACT OF 1999

```
     STAR-SPANGLED BANNER NATIONAL HISTORIC TRAIL STUDY ACT OF 1999

  The bill (H.R. 791) to amend the National Trails System Act to 
designate the route of the War of 1812 British invasion of Maryland and 
Washington, District of Columbia, and the route of the American 
defense, for study for potential addition to the national trails 
system, was considered, ordered to a third reading, read the third 
time, and passed.
```
