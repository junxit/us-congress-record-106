# ARIZONA STATEHOOD AND ENABLING ACT OF AMENDMENTS OF 1999

**Measures:** H.R. 747

```
        ARIZONA STATEHOOD AND ENABLING ACT OF AMENDMENTS OF 1999

  The bill (H.R. 747) to protect the permanent trust funds of the State 
of Arizona from erosion due to inflation and modify the basis on which 
distributions are made from those funds, was considered, ordered to a 
third reading, read the third time, and passed.
```
