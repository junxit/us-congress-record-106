# COMMUNITY FOREST RESTORATION ACT

```
                    COMMUNITY FOREST RESTORATION ACT

                                 ______
                                 

                      BINGAMAN AMENDMENT NO. 2805

  Mr. DASCHLE (for Mr. Bingaman) proposed an amendment to the bill (S. 
1288) to provide incentives for collaborative forest restoration 
projects on National Forest System and other public lands in New 
Mexico, and for other purposes; as follows:

       At the end of the bill add the following:

     ``SEC. 9. AUTHORIZATION OF APPROPRIATIONS.

       There are authorized to be appropriated $5,000,000 annually 
     to carry out this Act.''

```
