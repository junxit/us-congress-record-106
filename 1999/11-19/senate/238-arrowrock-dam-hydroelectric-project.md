# ARROWROCK DAM HYDROELECTRIC PROJECT

**Measures:** S. 1236

```
                  ARROWROCK DAM HYDROELECTRIC PROJECT

  The bill (S. 1236) to extend the deadline under the Federal Power Act 
for commencement of the construction of the Arrowrock Dam Hydroelectric 
Project in the State of Idaho, was considered, ordered to be engrossed 
for a third reading, read the third time, and passed; as follows:

                                S. 1236

       Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of 
     the United States of America in Congress assembled,

     SECTION 1. EXTENSION OF TIME FOR FEDERAL ENERGY REGULATORY 
                   COMMISSION PROJECT.

       Notwithstanding the time period specified in section 13 of 
     the Federal Power Act (16 U.S.C. 806) that would otherwise 
     apply to the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission project 
     numbered 4656, the Commission may, at the request of the 
     licensee for the project and after reasonable notice, in 
     accordance with the good faith, due diligence, and public 
     interest requirements of that section and the Commission's 
     procedures under that section,

[[Page S15164]]

     extend until March 26, 2005, the time period during which the 
     licensee is required to commence construction of the project.

                          ____________________
```
