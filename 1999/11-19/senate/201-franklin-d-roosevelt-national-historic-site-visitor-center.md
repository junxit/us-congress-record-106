# FRANKLIN D. ROOSEVELT NATIONAL HISTORIC SITE VISITOR CENTER

```
      FRANKLIN D. ROOSEVELT NATIONAL HISTORIC SITE VISITOR CENTER

  The bill (H.R. 1104) to authorize the Secretary of the Interior to 
transfer administrative jurisdiction over land within the boundaries of 
the Home of Franklin D. Roosevelt National Historic Site to the 
Archivist of the United States for the construction of a visitor 
center, was considered, ordered to a third reading, read the third 
time, and passed.
```
