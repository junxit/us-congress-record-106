# CONDEMNING THE VIOLENCE IN CHECHNYA

```
                  CONDEMNING THE VIOLENCE IN CHECHNYA

                                 ______
                                 

                        HELMS AMENDMENT NO. 2791

  Ms. COLLINS (for Mr. Helms) proposed an amendment to the preamble of 
the resolution (S. Res. 223) condemning the violence in Chechnya; as 
follows:

       In the second whereas clause of the preamble, strike ``is'' 
     and insert ``are''.
```
