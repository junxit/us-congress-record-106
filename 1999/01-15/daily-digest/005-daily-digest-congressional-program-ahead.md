# Daily Digest/CONGRESSIONAL PROGRAM AHEAD

```
                      CONGRESSIONAL PROGRAM AHEAD

              Week of January 18 through January 23, 1999

                             Senate Chamber

  On Monday, Senate will not be in session.
  On Tuesday, Senate will continue to sit as a Court of Impeachment to 
consider the articles of impeachment against President Clinton; also, 
Senate will meet in joint session with the House of Representatives to 
receive the President's State of the Union message.
  During the balance of the week, the Senate expects to continue 
consideration of the articles of impeachment against President Clinton.
  (Senate will recess on Tuesday, January 19, 1999, from 11:30 a.m. 
until 1 p.m., for respective party conferences.)


                           Senate Committees

        (Committee meetings are open unless otherwise indicated)
  Committee on the Budget: January 19, to hold hearings on certain 
Social Security issues in the 21st Century, 10 a.m., SD-608.

  January 20, Full Committee, to hold hearings on Federal tax policy 
in the Year 2000, 10 a.m., SD-608.
  January 22, Full Committee, to resume hearings on certain Social 
Security issues in the 21st Century, 9:30 a.m., SD-608.
  Committee on Commerce, Science, and Transportation: January 20, 
business meeting to consider pending committee business; to be 
followed by a hearing on proposed legislation authorizing funds for 
the Federal Aviation Administration, Department of Transportation, 
9:30 a.m., SR-253.
  Committee on Environment and Public Works: January 20, business 
meeting to consider pending committee business and its rules of

consider pending committee business, 10 a.m., SD-342.
  Committee on Indian Affairs: January 19, briefing, to discuss

  Committee on Labor and Human Resources: January 20, business

  January 20, Full Committee, to hold hearings on group health plan

a.m., SD-430.
```
