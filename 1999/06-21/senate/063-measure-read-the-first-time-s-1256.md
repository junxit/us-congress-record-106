**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

**Measures:** S. 1256

```
                  MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--S. 1256

  Mr. SCHUMER. Mr. President, I understand that S. 1256, introduced 
earlier today by Senator Daschle, is at the desk. I ask for its first 
reading.
```
